## Primary prior work and official code

**L1. Belrose et al., NeurIPS 2023, LEACE:** [full paper](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/d066d21c619d0a78c5b557fa3291a8f4-Paper-Conference.pdf), Definitions 2.1–2.3, Section 3 and Section 7. Linear guardedness is scoped to linear classifiers and convex loss. Footnote 5 on p.8 already discusses leakage from stacking a classifier on multiclass softmax output. A nonlinear reader is not a refutation. **L2. Official implementation** version0.2.4, commit **9b18b3d5c73f552798212c51d6533d649fa434cd**: [concept_erasure/leace.py:26–47](https://github.com/EleutherAI/concept-erasure/blob/9b18b3d5c73f552798212c51d6533d649fa434cd/concept_erasure/leace.py#L26). Feature/concept-labelled owner fitting produces an affine eraser. Neither API nor theorem establishes a recipient's auxiliary-label acquisition pathway.

**F1. Jovanović et al., ICML 2023, FARE:** [full paper](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v202/jovanovic23a/jovanovic23a.pdf), Sections 1 and 3–5, Appendix D.1. The producer/consumer separation and malicious downstream consumer are explicit. The finite-representation certificate bounds demographic-parity distance for any downstream classifier of that representation. It is not directly the AUC used in our audit. **F2. Official implementation**, commit **89cb1b66ed268c16659cbf7428c43e60da2df641**: [tree/main.py:99–128](https://github.com/eth-sri/fare/blob/89cb1b66ed268c16659cbf7428c43e60da2df641/code/src/tree/main.py#L99) constructs cell-median embeddings, [scripts/eval_embeddings.py:38–80](https://github.com/eth-sri/fare/blob/89cb1b66ed268c16659cbf7428c43e60da2df641/code/src/scripts/eval_embeddings.py#L38) evaluates downstream families. Our one-hot cell is a re-encoding of the cell index, not a claim of byte-identical official embeddings. An own-head output computed only from that cell is still a function of it. Appending an untreated output is different access. Sensitive labels in evaluation files do not establish recipient access in practice.

**S1. Song & Shmatikov, ICLR 2020, Overlearning Reveals Sensitive Attributes:** [full paper](https://www.cs.cornell.edu/~shmat/shmat_iclr20.pdf), Section 3.1/Figure1 (p.3), Section 4.2 (p.5). The inference attacker sees target representation but not target input, and receives labelled auxiliary inputs plus a black-box encoder oracle to obtain their representations. Auxiliary size is 50% of target training size, and the auxiliary sample need not be the same dataset. Model repurposing in Section 3.2 is a separate model-access attack. Official inference code was not independently audited in this meeting task. This is precedent for the adversary family, not evidence for our identical service or auxiliary budget.

**A1. Jia & Gong, USENIX Security 2018, AttriGuard:** [full paper](https://www.usenix.org/system/files/conference/usenixsecurity18/sec18-jia.pdf), Section 3.2 (pp.516–517), Section 5.1 (p.520). Public profiles and disclosed sensitive attributes provide an explicit auxiliary-data scenario. The experiment assumes a randomly selected90% disclose lived cities. This is a benchmark assumption, not observed disclosure prevalence. Official code was not audited here. Public profiles differ from our target-input-withheld setting.

These source checks establish antecedents and scope. They do not establish a novel adversary or a real joint deployment of all our assumptions.

## Corrections and unresolved assumptions

**R1. Corrected adversary/injectivity record:** `results/pcrl_mechanism_design_v1/{ADVERSARY_AND_MOTIVATION.md,CORRECTIONS.md}` in worktree `pcrl-mechanism-design-correction-v1`, and its published patch v2 above. Sections1 and6 correct the injectivity and adversary-motivation reasoning. D1 independently distinguishes clean auxiliary data from target, parameter and repeated-query access. No later negative method campaign is presented as a successful algorithm. The ACS8,001/3,000 budget is not imported into these experiments.

**UNRESOLVED: motivation.** FARE documents a producer/consumer research setting, and Song/AttriGuard motivate labelled auxiliary attacks. None establishes our combined output-only service, its same-population sample size and matching releases, or coalition access. A common provider's shared identifier, or both recipients knowing the same person, are illustrative routes to linkage. Perfect linkage is granted by the benchmark. A recipient also holding raw inputs is a distinct plausible threat model, not ruled out or called illegitimate.

**UNRESOLVED: generalization.** All four experiments reuse previously analysed data. Registered endpoints are development evidence, not fresh confirmation. Intervals condition on fitted encoders/heads/readers. A finite bank's failure to recover is not optimal-recovery protection. FARE certificates at these sizes are unresolved, not refuted. No empirical result determines the utility the intended service actually needs.
