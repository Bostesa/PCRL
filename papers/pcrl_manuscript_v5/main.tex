\title{Adding a reusable feature channel beside a prediction service you cannot change:\\
an evaluated release problem, one confirmed effect, and five negative method results}

\small Study~5 integrated from evidence commit \texttt{a56bcc7f}; a sixth, utility-first programme has

\begin{abstract}
An organisation already publishes a prediction service---fixed probability vectors that downstream
recipients consume and cannot be asked to re-accept. We formalise and evaluate what happens when it
adds a \emph{second}, reusable feature channel for one recipient without altering a published number,
while trying to limit what that recipient, and that recipient colluding with another, can \emph{newly}
infer about sex and race. The contribution is an implemented release interface with a measurement
contract---per-recipient and coalition views, independently fitted attackers, incremental disclosure
always reported beside absolute disclosure---and what five studies measured through it. (i)~Exact
preservation of the published outputs is structural and holds bitwise on every released view; it does
not imply stable service accuracy. (ii)~On a locked, previously unused survey year, a penalty built from
the coalition's joint view beats an equal-strength and an equal-total-mass local control under
simultaneous $95\%$ intervals in 14 of 16 sensitive cells, none worse; its residence cost is bounded by
a point rule only. (iii)~Four successive development studies failed to make the mechanism competitive
with the strongest channel $J$: a nonlinear penalty, an exact rotation-invariant repair that disclosed
significantly \emph{more}, direct adversarial refinement from a weaker channel, and---in the fifth
study, new here---$J$-initialised adversarial training and a coalition-conditioned rank-reducing
projection of $J$. \textbf{No prospectively selected release in the fifth study met its declared
competitive conjunction.} Its selection rule returned untouched $J$ for both neural nominees; the
projection nominees lowered coalition sex recovery against $J$ at a significant residence cost and did
not separate from matched local-only projections. (iv)~Adapted closed-form erasers (LEACE, SPLINCE)
applied to channels this project already had beat the developed mechanism and are not separated from
$J$. We report an empirical and problem-formulation contribution, list every claim of ours this
revision withdraws or narrows, and state---before any outcome---how a utility-first extension will be
judged.
\end{abstract}

\section{What the paper does}
\label{sec:contribution}

Suppose a company already publishes two prediction services: a credit-style model returning
probabilities to one business partner, and a coverage model returning probabilities to another. Both
partners built systems on those exact numbers, so they cannot be revised. The company now wants to give
the first partner a compact reusable feature vector built from what the published predictions leave
unexplained, while ensuring that the partner, and the two partners pooling what they hold, cannot use it
to infer an applicant's sex or race better than the published predictions already allow. This paper
implements and measures that problem.

\begin{itemize}\itemsep1pt
\item \textbf{The interface.} A mechanism appends a channel $Z$ to one recipient's published output and
modifies nothing, so preservation is an identity checked bitwise, not a bounded perturbation.
\item \textbf{The comparisons it enables.} Heterogeneous mechanisms---spectral moment penalties,
adversarially trained neural channels, closed-form erasers, and projections of frozen channels---are
compared at matched width on the same wire by one independently fitted attacker slate, with $H$-only
recovery always subtracted and also shown unsubtracted.
\item \textbf{One confirmed effect.} On a sealed year, a coalition-view penalty lowers measured recovery
relative to two matched local-only controls (\S\ref{sec:study1}).
\item \textbf{Five negative method results.} Everything developed afterwards to reach the strongest
comparator failed (\S\ref{sec:study24}, \S\ref{sec:study5}); adapted published erasers beat the developed
mechanism. The fifth study is new in this revision and is also negative.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{This is an empirical and problem-formulation paper, not a method paper.} We do not present a
deployable mechanism. We offer no new mathematics: concept erasure, eigenvalue-sign rank selection,
minimax adversarial training and conditional-moment characterisations are prior work, and adversarially
protected transferable representations are not new \citep{madras2018laftr}. The fifth study's projection
is the hard-removal limit of this project's own residual spectral eigensystem applied to a different
input channel---an adaptation of an existing eigensystem, not new eigenvalue mathematics. What we did
not find, after a bounded search, is a prior \emph{evaluation} of recipient-specific and combined-access
policies over an already-published output that the mechanism may not touch (\S\ref{sec:related}); that
is absence of evidence, not a priority claim.

\section{The interface, and four distinctions we hold to throughout}
\label{sec:interface}

Each person has covariates $X$, sensitive attributes $S$ (sex, 2 classes; race, 9 classes) and task
labels. Two services are frozen and published first: $H_A(X)\in[0,1]^4$, the income-over-\$50k and
employment probability vectors released to recipient~A, and $H_B(X)\in[0,1]^2$, the coverage vector
released to B. A mechanism may add $Z\in\mathbb{R}^{16}$ \emph{for A only}; A sees $(H_A,Z)$, B sees
$H_B$, the coalition AB sees $(H_A,Z,H_B)$. The policy authorises A for income, employment and
residence and B for coverage and commute, and forbids sex and race everywhere plus nine cross-role
combinations. The adversary holds labelled data from the release year, fits fixed attack families on its
own pool, selects on a validation pool, and sees only its legal view. $A$'s inference path uses only
A-side inputs; $H_B$ enters fitting and auditing, never inference.

\textbf{$A_0$} is an unprotected 16-dimensional neural channel; \textbf{$J$} is a 16-dimensional
adversarially trained channel recovered from earlier work, the strongest comparator in the study, and
\emph{internal}. \textbf{$L1$}/\textbf{$L2$} are local controls using only A's view, matched to the
candidate on penalty strength and on total mass, so a coalition effect cannot be confused with ``more
penalty''; \textbf{$C1$} adds a penalty from the coalition's joint view.

\begin{description}\itemsep1.5pt
\item[\normalfont\textbf{Preservation is architectural; accuracy is empirical.}] $Z$ is concatenated to
$H_A$, never mixed in. That does not keep the service accurate: on identical released vectors,
employment log loss rises by $0.0138$ nats and income falls by $0.0145$ between years.
\item[\normalfont\textbf{Added capability is measured against the correct $H$-only view.}] A channel
adds capability only if independently fitted probes do better with $(H_A,Z)$ than with $H_A$.
\item[\normalfont\textbf{Disclosure is a finite-auditor gain, not a bound.}]
It is the improvement a validation-selected attacker achieves over the same family restricted to $H$: a
floor on what is recoverable, never a ceiling.
\item[\normalfont\textbf{Recipients have different permitted tasks}], so AB's audit roles are not either
singleton's.
\end{description}

\textbf{Scope limits.} Only recipient~A receives a channel; nothing here is a multi-channel design.
Residence and commute labels are excluded from every representation fit and selection, but residence
outcomes have been inspected repeatedly across this line of work, so residence is \emph{reserved from
training}, not a pristine unseen task.

\section{The mechanism family, and what is a theorem here}
\label{sec:method}

Let $T$ be a frozen PCA32 teacher. Build $\phi(T)$ from standardised $T$ and 96 random Fourier features
\citep{rahimi2007random}; residualise against a degree-2 polynomial basis in the released service
probabilities and eigen-whiten to $V$. Utility is $U=C_{VR}C_{RV}$ for the residualised teacher $R$;
protection matrices $P_j$ are built from household-grouped cross-fitted nuisances $m_j(H)$
\citep{chernozhukov2016dml} and residuals $\mathrm{onehot}(S_j)-m_j(H)$, aggregated over local roles,
coalition roles and a marginal variant. The release is the top-16 eigenvectors of $U_{\mathrm{norm}}-\lambda P$.
Studies~2--5 vary this construction (\S\ref{sec:study24}, \S\ref{sec:study5}).

\paragraph{What is proved, and over what.} Four elementary statements are proved in
Appendix~\ref{app:math}; nothing else here is a theorem. (1)~Appending $Z$ without modifying $H$
preserves $H$ exactly. (2)~An unrestricted recipient holding $[H,Z]$ can ignore $Z$, so its optimal
\emph{population} log loss is no worse than with $H$ alone; fitted models need not respect this, and some
measured increments are negative. (3)~Under Bayes prediction and log loss the population risk difference
between $H$ and $(H,Z)$ equals $I(S;Z\mid H)$; finite validation-selected attackers bound that quantity in
neither direction \citep{mcallester2020formal}. (4)~The Study~3 repaired penalty is exactly invariant to
orthogonal reparameterisation (measured to $5.6\times10^{-17}$). Ky Fan's principle \citep{fan1949weyl}
gives a restricted optimum for the original fixed-matrix family only. The moment condition is a finite
fitted subset of the $L^2$ characterisation of conditional independence used by kernel CI tests
\citep{zhang2011kci,strobl2019rcit}; vanishing fitted moments establish no conditional independence, and a
misspecified $m(H)$ can manufacture a penalty more than ten times the oracle value under \emph{exact}
conditional independence.

\section{Relation to prior work}
\label{sec:related}

SARL \citep{sadeghi2019sarl} takes eigenvectors of a utility-minus-$\lambda$-dependence matrix; OptNet-ARL
\citep{sadeghi2021optnetarl} embeds closed-form players in a learned encoder; K-TOpt
\citep{sadeghi2022ktopt} adds random features and evaluates on Folktables \citep{ding2021retiring}; U-FaTE
\citep{dehdashtian2024ufate} adds a conditional penalty. Our marginal arms are SARL's construction on
whitened residuals, verified by bitwise rebuild. Adversarial fair and transferable representation learning
is \citet{madras2018laftr}. Linear concept erasure \citep{belrose2023leace,holstege2025splince} supplies an
exact guarantee against \emph{linear} adversaries on a representation the releaser may edit; adversarial
removal leaves recoverable information \citep{elazar2018adversarial}; least-privilege learning has
fundamental limits \citep{stadler2024leastprivilege}. \textbf{Reusing a known solver, a conditional-moment
characterisation or a rank rule is attribution, not innovation.} What differs: we condition on a released
continuous artifact $H$ via cross-fitted residuals; utility is reconstruction of a residualised teacher;
and local and coalition penalties act over a fixed prior output with only one recipient receiving a
channel. The first two are adaptations. For the third, the nearest candidate, the multi-consumer
information-theoretic line \citep{sankar2013utility}, designs one sanitised release for many users; we could
not confirm a per-recipient release or a collusion term there and mark it ambiguous
(Appendix~\ref{app:related}).

\section{Evidence status and chronology}
\label{sec:design}

California ACS one-year PUMS, ages 19--34, three household splits, disjoint pools for representation
fitting, source validation, task fitting and validation, attacker fitting and validation, and evaluation.
Attacks are logistic regression, restarted MLPs, gradient-boosted trees and a random-feature kernel family,
plus every legal projection of $H$'s own attacks into each view. Uncertainty is a paired household-cluster
bootstrap with simultaneous intervals inside each pre-declared family. Two weightings (unweighted and
person-weighted) are computed from the same predictions and reported separately throughout.

\section{Study 1: the confirmed coalition effect, and the rule it did not establish}
\label{sec:study1}

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/forest_primary.pdf}

its two local controls; grey A/sex rows are \emph{unresolved}. \textbf{Right, F3:} $C1$ against $J$.}
\label{fig:forest}

On the sealed 2017 partition, $C1$ has a simultaneous-interval advantage over both $L1$ and $L2$ under
both weightings: of 16 sensitive cells, \textbf{14 have adjusted intervals entirely below zero, none
above, two unresolved} ($C1$ versus $L1$ on A/sex unweighted, $[-0.00585,+0.00020]$; versus $L2$,
$[-0.00568,+0.00052]$). Frozen 2018 attacks reproduce the ordering. \textbf{The paired residence rule is a
point criterion only}: seed-mean$(C1-\text{comparator})\le0.001$ passes (values $0.00010$--$0.00059$), but
zero of four comparisons support interval noninferiority at that margin, and zero support equivalence.
On the two unresolved cells up to $5\times10^{-4}$ nats of extra local sex recovery is not excluded.
\textbf{And the mechanism is not competitive with $J$}: $C1$ is significantly worse than $J$ on all four
sensitive endpoints under both weightings and better only on residence, $-0.0093\,[-0.0150,-0.0036]$. Since
$J$ supplies less residence capability, neither Pareto-dominates, and $J$ is internal, so this is evidence
about $J$, not the state of the art.

\section{Studies 2--4: three refinements, all negative}
\label{sec:study24}

\textbf{Study~2} (nonlinear penalty, rank reduction) moved recovery in the intended direction but failed all
eight registered coordination cells; its best candidate stayed significantly worse than $J$ on local sex
($+0.01065$ unweighted, $+0.01210$ person-weighted), and at rank~8 with $L1$ the nonlinear penalty made
A/race significantly \emph{worse}, $+0.00862\,[+0.00168,+0.01556]$. After evaluation it was diagnosed that
the penalty was \emph{coordinate-dependent}. \textbf{Study~3} repaired that exactly (rotation-only share
$1.4\times10^{-15}$ over 18 cells, verified before any score was read)---and the repaired releases disclosed
\emph{more}: 8 of 48 sensitive cells significantly worse, none better (Appendix~\ref{app:study23}). The repair
changed five ingredients at once, so no single cause is isolated.

\textbf{Study~3 also ran the first external comparisons.} LEACE \citep{belrose2023leace} and SPLINCE
\citep{holstege2025splince} were applied to the 16-coordinate channel alone---never to the concatenated
wire, since editing it would change the published output---so their linear guarantee covers that fitted
erasure, not nonlinear prediction from the view an attacker holds; a fixture shows a quadratic feature
still recovering after linear $R^2<10^{-8}$. Adapted LEACE, OptNet-C1 and OptNet-L2 recover significantly
less than the repaired mechanism on all four sensitive endpoints under both weightings, SPLINCE on seven of
eight cells (Table~\ref{tab:extrep}). LEACE's residence cost is unresolved against the repaired arm
($+0.0036\,[-0.0028,+0.0100]$) and significant against the channel it transformed
($+0.0098\,[+0.0036,+0.0159]$), so it dominates nothing. Against $J$, the external arms are \emph{not
separated} at three seeds---which is not equivalence (Appendix~\ref{app:external}). Protected-class support
in a fitting pool determines the realised width of an erased channel ($6,6,7$ across seeds; one race class
has counts $1,1,0$), and an unsupported class is unmeasured, not protected.

\textbf{Study~4} trained the channel directly against a refreshed attacker ensemble. All 114 trajectories
started from $A_0$, not $J$. Across its frontier family 189 cells were significantly worse and 18
significantly better, all 18 on residence utility; its coalition advantage held robustly only against the
weak control $L1$ and not under the simultaneous correction against the strength-matched $L2$. 63 of 114
trajectories published their bitwise-$A_0$ step-0 checkpoint although every one had moved: a selection
outcome, not stasis. Its scope correction showed an unmatched attack opportunity crediting an
\emph{identical} channel with $0.0155$ nats of apparent protection---an evaluation correction, not a gain.
Tables and figures are in Appendix~\ref{app:study4}.

\section{Study 5: $J$ initialisation and coalition-conditioned projection}
\label{sec:study5}

\paragraph{What was run.} Study~5 (evidence commit \texttt{a56bcc7f}; protocol locked before any new
outcome) asked two questions Study~4 left open. \emph{Track N} (168 nominal slots) is a factorial of
adversarial training: initialisation from $A_0$ or from $J$'s actual trained weights (loaded and asserted
bitwise identical to $J$'s released wire), a common frozen $A_0$ teacher with coefficient
$\gamma\in\{0,.01,.1,1\}$, penalty $\beta\in\{1,3\}$ (plus $\beta=0$ continuations), and policies $L1$,
$L2$, $C1$. \emph{Track E} (204 slots) is post-processing of a frozen channel ($A_0$ or $J$): whiten, rank
directions by a local ($L$), coalition ($C$) or feature-count-matched expanded local ($LX$) cross-moment
kernel built from cross-fitted service-only nuisance residuals, and remove the top $k\in\{1,2,4,6,8\}$ of 16,
with marginal, PCA and random-subspace controls and ordinary LEACE/SPLINCE on $J$. A panel of two
configurations per family was nominated on the \emph{validation} split, without residence or commute, and
committed before any panel test table. The registered \emph{competitive conjunction} required a sensitive
endpoint significantly better than $J$ \emph{and} than one of \texttt{leace\_A0}, \texttt{splince\_A0},
\texttt{optnet16\_C1}, with residence noninferior at $0.001$ (one-sided adjusted bound), every other sensitive
endpoint noninferior at a practical $0.001$-nat tolerance, and source eligibility, under both weightings,
at one candidate-wide correction level (family P, $m=400$).

\paragraph{Denominators.} 372 nominal slots (168 + 204) were all fitted; they resolve into 294 distinct
audited 2018 releases and 78 exact bitwise duplicates (66 Track N units whose selection returned the
untouched start, 12 constant full-span maps), each inheriting the canonical audit. The six references
(\texttt{ref\_A0}, \texttt{ref\_J} per anchor) are \emph{outside} the 372 and are not double-counted. The
stronger attack ran on 54 interfaces (17 releases plus the $H$-only view, three anchors; six of the 17 are
neural arms bitwise equal to $J$), and 18 Track E units (six releases, three anchors) were transported to
2017. Zero audits were quarantined (Appendix Table~\ref{tab:s5accounting}).

\paragraph{Verdict.} \textbf{No prospectively selected release established the declared competitive
conjunction}, under either weighting, with $J$ taken either as the exposure-matched \texttt{ref\_J} or as the
historical $J$ audit. We recomputed that verdict from the committed interval ledger rather than copying it.

\emph{The neural nominees are untouched $J$.} At $\gamma\in\{0,.01\}$ the registered equal-budget fresh-probe
selection returned step~0---$J$ itself---on every anchor of every $J$-initialised arm: fine-tuning moved the
channel, but no moved checkpoint scored better on the monitor fold. At $\gamma\in\{.1,1\}$ the $A_0$ teacher
pulled $J$ back toward $A_0$ and the selected channels leak more than $J$. So \textbf{actual $J$-initialised
training was tested here}---which no earlier study did---and under this objective and selection rule it did
not improve on $J$: low-$\gamma$ selection returned $J$, and stronger $A_0$ preservation moved it toward $A_0$.
Family-X cells where $J$-initialisation beats $A_0$-initialisation (36 of 192) are $J$'s head start, not an
improvement over $J$.

\emph{The projection nominees trade residence for coalition sex protection, as ordinary erasure does.}
Removing 8 coalition-ranked directions of $J$ (\texttt{E\_J\_C\_k8}) lowers $AB$/SEX recovery against $J$ by
$-0.0091\,[-0.0158,-0.0025]$ unweighted (significant under both weightings) and raises residence loss by
$+0.0101\,[+0.0023,+0.0179]$ unweighted (significant; person-weighted $+0.0085\,[-0.0009,+0.0178]$,
unresolved): its residence gain over $H$ is $0.0114$ against $J$'s $0.0215$. Lower recovery with lower
capability is a trade-off, not dominance. Its point estimates are within $\pm0.0011$ of ordinary LEACE
applied to $J$ on every endpoint unweighted (up to $0.0020$ person-weighted); \textbf{that is point-estimate
proximity, not statistical equivalence}---no equivalence test was run and the residence interval is
$[-0.0089,+0.0091]$. \texttt{E\_J\_C\_k6} separates from $J$ on no endpoint. Against its matched local
controls $L$ and $LX$, neither nominee is significantly better on any endpoint
(Figure~\ref{fig:s5contrasts}): \textbf{coalition projection on $J$ did not establish an advantage over matched
local projections}. Removing linear directions is not certified conditional privacy: the projection's only
exact property is the fixed-offset stationarity statement below, on training rows, within one linear
correction family.

\paragraph{What changed $A_0$-based results, and why that is not a method.} Lowering the teacher coefficient
from $1$ to $0$ ($A_0$ initialisation, $C1$, $\beta=1$) cut $A$/RAC1P recovery from $+0.0201$ to $+0.0038$ and
$AB$/RAC1P from $+0.0182$ to $+0.0078$ at a similar residence gain; in the exploratory whole-grid family
($m=3900$), 22 of 288 sensitive cells improve on $\gamma=1$ and one worsens. That is a coefficient change in an
existing objective, and every $A_0$-initialised arm remains significantly worse than $J$ on at least one
endpoint. Coalition conditioning in neural training beat $L1$ in 21 of 128 sensitive cells and the
strength-matched $L2$ in \textbf{0 of 128}.

\paragraph{An exploratory coalition effect on the weak channel.} On $A_0$ at $k=2$, coalition projection
lowers race recovery relative to both the local ($-0.0202$ on $A$/RAC1P, $-0.0253$ on $AB$/RAC1P) and the
expanded-local projection ($-0.0182$, $-0.0217$), with intervals below zero under both weightings
\emph{in the exploratory family X}, where it was found; it was not nominated and is not in family P. The
residence difference is unresolved (e.g.\ $-0.0018\,[-0.0068,+0.0032]$); \textbf{absence of a significant
residence difference is not evidence that utility is preserved}. And the release is far from competitive:
$A$/RAC1P $+0.0223$ against $J$'s $+0.0000$. It is a mechanism observation on a channel with residual race
signal to remove; whether it survives on a channel as strong as $J$, which has almost none, is open.

\paragraph{The stronger attack was weaker.} The prespecified $\mathrm{MLP}[256,256,128]$, frozen before any
panel outcome, recovered \emph{less} than the same recipe on the $H$-only view on both sex endpoints for every
interface---on untouched $A_0$, $-0.092$ on $A$/SEX where the standard audit finds $+0.032$---and about half
the standard audit's race increment on $A_0$. It stressed nothing, was not re-tuned, and \textbf{supports no
claim of stronger-attack survival}.

\paragraph{2017, exploratory.} All 18 transported units were scored (2018 maps applied to frozen 2017
channels; only fresh attackers and probes fitted; no intervals). Both projections leak less than $J$ on
$AB$/SEX and $A$/RAC1P in all three anchors, but the matched local controls show the same pattern, so 2017
shows no coalition-specific signal. \texttt{E\_J\_C\_k6} is descriptively interesting: unweighted residence
gain $0.0187$ against $J$'s $0.0189$. Person-weighted it is $0.0159$ against $0.0171$, a deficit that already
exceeds the $0.001$ margin at the point estimate, and its per-anchor residence difference changes sign.
It is a hypothesis for a fresh prospective evaluation, not a result, and it does not rescue the 2018
verdict.

\paragraph{A protocol change after outcomes, reported as such.} The registered identity check required
\texttt{ref\_A0}/\texttt{ref\_J} to reproduce the historical $A_0$/$J$ audits exactly; it \textbf{failed} on
three cells, because the historical audits carried an extra derived-candidate space no new release had.
Amendment~4, declared \emph{after} the 2018 panel intervals were computed, replaced the asymmetric historical
$J$ comparison by the exposure-matched \texttt{ref\_J}. Both versions are reported and the verdict is identical;
an unchanged negative verdict does not erase the amendment's timing.

\paragraph{What the projection is, mathematically.} With the channel whitened to $v$, the release is
$z_{\mathrm{out}}=\mu+v(I-U_kU_k^\top)S$: the utility-free, infinite-penalty limit of the project's own
residual spectral eigenproblem, applied to a frozen neural channel---a limiting form of an existing
eigensystem on a different input, not new eigenvalue mathematics. The rank-deficient case we flagged before
the study (whitened projection deletes off-support components of new rows that LEACE's subtractive form keeps)
was resolved by measurement: the supported rank is 16 of 16 in all 204 Track E fits with zero dropped
directions, so the whitened and subtractive forms coincide on training and transported rows. The
stationarity statement is proved and tested only for a \emph{fixed-offset} multinomial logit with the
cross-fitted offset that defines the residual, the same masks, a correction linear in $b(h)\otimes
v_{\mathrm{out}}$, and \emph{no free intercept} (whose gradient cross-fitting does not zero); because partial projections leave non-zero
training cross-moments, it does not cover the partial nominees at all, and it says nothing about retrained
baselines, nonlinear auditors or population quantities (Appendix~\ref{app:mathv5}).

\section{Two forms of competitiveness, and the pending utility-first programme}
\label{sec:pending}

A release beside $H$ can be competitive in two legitimate ways: \emph{lower recovery at comparable utility},
or \emph{higher utility at comparable protection}. Studies~4 and~5 registered the first. A sixth programme
registers the second as a \emph{new development objective}; it is not a correction to any earlier numerical
result, and no earlier negative becomes a success under it. \textbf{It has produced no outcome integrated
here.} We state now how its results will be read.

The intended release is $[H_A,Z_J,R]$ with $H$ and $J$ fixed, where $R$ predicts residual teacher information
not captured by a frozen decoder from $(H_A,Z_J)$ to $A_0$. Six statements bound what it can show.
\textbf{(a)}~Appending $R$ preserves every old output, but by statement~(2) of \S\ref{sec:method} it
\emph{cannot decrease} optimal population sensitive recovery: the aim is useful extra capability at bounded
added disclosure, never removal of information already available in $J$. \textbf{(b)}~The old predictor
remains available in population, but finite probe fitting needs explicit baseline candidates; exact
preservation of $J$ does not make every freshly fitted probe score identically. \textbf{(c)}~Residual
reconstruction is a proxy for transfer; no residence or commute label enters fitting or selection, and a
validation proxy gain is not the final utility claim. \textbf{(d)}~Cross-fitted residual targets are training
artifacts: the deployed extension must be a fixed function of permitted A-side inputs that cannot look up a
person's out-of-fold residual or depend on a sensitive label. \textbf{(e)}~Sensitive increments over $H$ and
over $J$ are different quantities and both will be shown; a small $J$-relative increment can coexist with
non-zero total recovery. Baseline attackers condition on the $J$-containing view and coalition attackers
also see $H_B$; local, coalition and equal-opportunity roles all remain, and a larger input must not buy
apparent protection from worse auditor fitting. \textbf{(f)}~A finite adversarial objective with baseline
subtraction, clipping and frozen nuisances is not a mutual-information bound or independence guarantee.

A utility-first claim would require that increased recovery be \emph{bounded} on every declared endpoint by
one-sided adjusted intervals---not merely non-significant---with the practical $0.001$-nat allowance named as
an allowance, not zero harm; comparison with every strong external comparator and local control; and a
separation of competitive utility gain from coalition specificity: an ordinary (non-coalition) extension
that performs equally well would be a counterexample to any coalition-specific claim. The programme's pilot
gate screens reconstruction, source eligibility and validation sensitive increments without residence
outcomes; its expansion is conditional, slot counts are cumulative, and a tier that does not run will be
reported as \emph{not triggered}, not as missing evidence. 2016 stays sealed; no fresh-year claim follows from
a 2017 row.

\section{What this evidence does not establish}
\label{sec:limits}

\textbf{No attack maximum is an upper bound}; a stronger attacker we did not build would move any recovery
number upward, and Study~5's attempt to build one produced a weaker attacker. \textbf{Nested
validation-selected models need not produce non-negative gains}; we report negative increments unclipped.
\textbf{No detected difference is not equality}: unresolved cells are never read as parity, and point
agreement within $0.0011$ is not equivalence. \textbf{Ratios are scale-dependent; paired differences are
not}, so we quote differences. \textbf{Nothing here is a certificate}: removing linear dimensions, or vanishing
fitted moments, imply neither $Z\perp S\mid H$ nor any bound on $I(S;Z\mid H)$. \textbf{Development against
confirmation}: only the 2017 transport of Study~1 is confirmatory; 2018 and the spent 2017 partition are
development for Studies~2--5, and \textbf{ACS 2016 remains sealed and unscored}. \textbf{Sampling and design}:
three seeds are three fitted systems on one cohort; bootstraps cover people, not retraining or Census design
variance; one state, one age band. \textbf{Multiplicity is only as honest as its level}: Study~5 declared one
decision level (candidate-wide, family P); its $A_0$ race effect is reported at the exploratory level where it
was found. \textbf{A tolerance is not zero harm}. \textbf{Numerical integrity}: transient probability-row
faults in Studies~3--4 were caught by validation and recomputed; Study~5 quarantined none, but its compaction
deleted attacker weights a later stage needed (incident~1), repaired by seeded bitwise-verified regeneration.

\section{Conclusion}

A service that has already published predictions can add a reusable channel beside them without changing a
released number, and can steer that channel away from directions a recipient or colluding pair would use to
infer sex and race. On a locked year, coalition conditioning beats both of its local controls in 14 of 16
sensitive cells, at a residence cost the data cannot bound below $0.001$ nats. Everything built afterwards to
make the mechanism competitive with $J$ failed. The fifth study, the most direct test so far, started
adversarial training from $J$ itself and projected coalition-conditioned directions out of $J$: its selection
returned $J$, its projections traded residence for coalition sex protection much as ordinary LEACE on $J$
does, and neither beat matched local-only projections. Reducing teacher strength improved $A_0$-based training,
and coalition projection showed an exploratory race effect on $A_0$; neither is a competitive method.

\textbf{The defensible contribution is the release problem and the evaluation design}: fixed published outputs
as a hard constraint, recipient-specific and coalition-specific audit roles, fresh and frozen attackers never
pooled, absolute and incremental recovery side by side, negative increments unclipped, denominators beside
every count, per-endpoint results never averaged, and a sealed temporal transport with multiplicity fixed in
advance. That contract is what made five negative method results legible rather than ambiguous. The mechanism
family, as tested, is not competitive. Whether a utility-first extension of $J$ can add capability at bounded
added disclosure is the open question the next programme addresses; nothing here anticipates its answer.

\section{Mathematical review of the Study 5 projection, as resolved}
\label{app:mathv5}
\paragraph{Form.} With row vectors, $v=(z-\mu)R$ and $z_{\mathrm{out}}=\mu+vP_kS$ compose to
$z_{\mathrm{out}}=\mu+(z-\mu)M$, $M=RP_kS$, idempotent and oblique (as is LEACE's $P^\star$). $RS$ is the
orthogonal projector onto the covariance support. Before the study we noted that under rank deficiency this
whitened form deletes off-support components of new rows that LEACE's subtractive form passes through, and
that the form must be declared. The study declared the whitened form and measured supported rank 16 of 16 on
both channels, every anchor, at every tolerance from $10^{-14}$ to $10^{-6}$, in all 204 fits; the two forms
therefore coincide, including on transported rows. Were a channel rank-deficient, the declared form would delete
off-support directions; that case did not arise. The moment uses a globally centred $v$ on declared masks.
\paragraph{Aliases and full span.} $L2=2K_{\mathrm{local}}$ is an exact eigenspace alias of $L$ and was registered,
not fitted. On these channels the moment Gram is full rank, so the full-span map is the constant $\mu$,
informationally equivalent to $H$ (made exactly zero by amendment~2 so floating residue could not be amplified).
\paragraph{Stationarity, exact scope.} For $\mathrm{logits}(h,z)=\ell_{0,j}(h)+\Theta\cdot(b_j(h)\otimes v_{\mathrm{out}})$
with $\ell_{0,j}$ fixed, average cross-entropy is convex in $\Theta$ with gradient $-G_j^{\mathrm{out}}$ at zero; so
$G_j^{\mathrm{out}}=0$ makes zero correction globally optimal \emph{within that family on those rows}. Necessary
conditions: the offset is the same cross-fitted $\ell_{0,j}$ that defines the residual; the same masks and
denominator; no free per-class intercept (its gradient $-\mathrm{mean}(e_j)$ is not zeroed by cross-fitting, tested
non-zero); linearity in $b\otimes v_{\mathrm{out}}$. It says nothing about retrained $H$-only baselines, nonlinear
auditors or population leakage; a tested counterexample has all linear moments near zero and $S$ recovered by a
quadratic probe. Partial projections generally leave non-zero training cross-moments (tested), so the statement
does not apply to the partial nominees at all; it applies to full-span removal, which on these channels yields the
constant map. \textbf{Nothing here
equates removing linear dimensions with conditional privacy.}
\paragraph{Relation to earlier work.} \texttt{acs\_residual\_spectral.py} builds per-role
$\sum_cG_cG_c^\top=GG^\top$ matrices exactly equal to $K_{\mathrm{local}}$, $K_{\mathrm{coal}}$ and the marginal
kernel here, up to the input space and nuisance family; its release is the top eigenvectors of $U-\lambda P$, and
as $\lambda\to\infty$ at retained rank $r-k$ these converge (off degeneracies) to the bottom $r-k$ eigenvectors of
$P$. Study~5's projection is that limit on a different input channel.
