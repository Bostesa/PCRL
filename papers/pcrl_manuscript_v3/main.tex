\title{Adding a reusable feature channel beside a prediction service you cannot change:\\
an evaluated release problem, one confirmed effect, and three negative method results}
\author{}
\date{Integrated revision of 2026-09-18.
Branch \texttt{research/pcrl-manuscript-integrated-v3}.}

\begin{document}
\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
An organisation already publishes a prediction service---fixed probability vectors that downstream
recipients consume and cannot be asked to re-accept. We formalise and evaluate what happens when it
adds a \emph{second}, reusable feature channel for one of those recipients without altering a single
published number, and tries to limit what that recipient, and that recipient colluding with another,
can \emph{newly} infer about sex and race. The contribution is an implemented release interface with
a measurement contract---per-recipient views, a coalition view, independently fitted attackers, and
incremental disclosure always reported beside absolute disclosure---together with what three
completed studies of one mechanism family measured through it. Four findings. (i) Exact preservation
of the published outputs is structural and holds bitwise on all 210 released views; it does not imply
stable service \emph{accuracy}, which moves by up to $0.015$ nats across one year. (ii) On a locked,
previously unused survey year, a penalty built from the coalition's joint view beats both an
equal-strength and an equal-total-mass local control under simultaneous $95\%$ intervals: 14 of 16
sensitive cells strictly better, none worse, two unresolved. The paired residence constraint is a
\emph{point} rule, and interval non-inferiority at the same margin holds in $0$ of $4$ comparisons.
(iii) Two successive refinements of the mechanism---a penalty nonlinear in the channel, then an
exactly rotation-invariant repair of that penalty---both fail. The repair is verified exact to
$10^{-15}$, and the release it produces discloses significantly \emph{more} than the defective
objective it repairs on $8$ of $48$ sensitive cells, with none significantly less. (iv) The first
executed external-method comparisons put adapted LEACE, SPLINCE and two OptNet-ARL policies below
the developed mechanism on every sensitive endpoint they resolve---$31$ of $32$ arm $\times$
endpoint $\times$ weighting cells; adapted LEACE also costs $0.0098$ nats of authorised
residence capability against the channel it transformed, a significant loss. We therefore report an
empirical and problem-formulation contribution and three negative method results, and we list every
claim of our own that this revision withdraws or narrows.
\end{abstract}

\section{What the paper does}
\label{sec:contribution}

Suppose a company already publishes two prediction services: a credit-style model that returns
probabilities to one business partner, and a coverage model that returns probabilities to another.
Both partners have built systems on those exact numbers, so the company cannot revise them. It now
wants to give the first partner something more useful---a compact reusable feature vector built from
what the published predictions leave unexplained---while making sure that the partner, and the two
partners pooling what they hold, cannot use it to work out an applicant's sex or race any better than
the published predictions already allow. That is the problem this paper implements and measures.

\paragraph{Contribution, precisely.} We build the interface and the measurement contract, and we
report what a bounded programme of experiments measured through them.

\begin{itemize}\itemsep1pt
\item \textbf{The interface.} A mechanism appends a channel $Z$ to one recipient's published
output and modifies nothing. Preservation is then an \emph{identity}, not a bounded perturbation,
and is checked bitwise on every released view.
\item \textbf{The comparisons it enables.} Because $Z$ is separable, heterogeneous mechanisms become
directly comparable at matched width on the same wire: spectral moment penalties, an adversarially
trained neural channel, and closed-form linear erasers, all evaluated by the same independently
fitted attacker slate against the same recipient-specific and coalition-specific audit roles, with
$H$-only recovery always subtracted and always also shown unsubtracted.
\item \textbf{The empirical finding.} On a sealed year, a penalty built from the coalition's joint
view lowers measured recovery relative to two local-only controls matched on penalty strength and on
total penalty mass. Everything the project then developed to close the remaining gap to the strongest
comparator failed, and three adapted published methods beat it.
\end{itemize}

\textbf{This is an empirical and problem-formulation paper, not a method paper.} We do not present a
deployable mechanism. We are not offering new mathematics: known concept erasure, eigenvalue-sign
rank selection, minimax adversarial training and conditional-moment characterisations are prior work,
cited as such. Adversarially protected representations that transfer to new tasks are not new either
\citep{madras2018laftr}. What we did not find, after a bounded search, is a prior \emph{evaluation}
of recipient-specific and combined-access policies over an already-published output that the
mechanism may not touch (\S\ref{sec:related}); that is absence of evidence after a bounded search, not
a priority claim.

\section{The interface, and four distinctions we hold to throughout}
\label{sec:interface}

Each person has covariates $X$, sensitive attributes $S$ (sex, 2 classes; race, 9 classes) and task
labels. Two services are frozen and published before anything here happens: $H_A(X)\in[0,1]^4$, the
full income-over-\$50k and employment probability vectors released to recipient~A, and
$H_B(X)\in[0,1]^2$, the coverage vector released to recipient~B. A mechanism may add
$Z\in\mathbb{R}^{16}$ \emph{for A only}. So A sees $(H_A,Z)$, B sees $H_B$, and the coalition AB sees
$(H_A,Z,H_B)$. $H$ without a subscript means the no-channel interface. The policy authorises A for
income, employment and residence and B for coverage and commute, and forbids sex and race everywhere
plus nine cross-role combinations---eleven forbidden roles in all. The adversary holds labelled data
from the release year, fits fixed attack families on its own pool, selects on a validation pool, and
sees only its legal view.

Three names recur. \textbf{$J$} is a 16-dimensional adversarially trained channel recovered from
earlier work and never retrained; it is the strongest comparator in the study and it is
\emph{internal}. \textbf{$L1$} and \textbf{$L2$} are local controls whose penalty uses only A's own
view, matched to the candidate on penalty strength and on total penalty mass respectively, so that a
coalition effect cannot be confused with ``more penalty''. \textbf{$C1$} adds a penalty term built
from the coalition's joint view; it is the candidate.

Four distinctions carry the argument and are never merged.

\begin{description}\itemsep1.5pt
\item[\normalfont\textbf{Preservation is architectural; accuracy is empirical.}] $Z$ is concatenated
to $H_A$ and never mixed into it, so $H$ is preserved by construction---a property of concatenation,
not a theorem. It does \emph{not} follow that the service stays accurate: on identical released
vectors, employment log loss rises by $0.0138$ nats and income falls by $0.0145$ between the two
years (Appendix~\ref{app:study1}).
\item[\normalfont\textbf{Added capability is measured against the correct $H$-only view.}] A channel
``adds capability'' only if independently fitted probes do better with $(H_A,Z)$ than with $H_A$.
\item[\normalfont\textbf{Added disclosure is a finite-auditor log-loss gain, not an information
bound.}] Every recovery number is the improvement a validation-selected attacker achieves over the
same attacker family restricted to $H$. It is a floor on what is recoverable and never a ceiling
(\S\ref{sec:limits}).
\item[\normalfont\textbf{Recipients have different permitted tasks.}] AB's authorised union is not
either singleton's permission set, and the audit roles differ accordingly.
\end{description}

\textbf{Two scope limits bound every claim below.} Only recipient~A receives a channel: nothing here
demonstrates joint optimisation of channels for several recipients, and the coalition penalty is a
constraint on one channel given another recipient's fixed view, not a multi-channel design. And
although residence and commute labels are excluded from every representation fit---the sense in which
residence is held out---residence outcomes have been inspected repeatedly across this line of work,
so residence is not a pristine unseen task and we do not claim unseen-task generality.

\section{The mechanism family, and what is a theorem here}
\label{sec:method}

Let $T$ be a frozen PCA32 teacher. Build $\phi(T)$ from standardised $T$ and 96 random Fourier
features \citep{rahimi2007random}; residualise against a degree-2 polynomial basis in the released
service probabilities and eigen-whiten to $V$ with $V^\top V/n=I$. Utility is $U=C_{VR}C_{RV}$ for the
residualised teacher $R$: for $W^\top W=I$, $\operatorname{tr}(W^\top UW)$ is exactly the training
reduction in least-squares reconstruction error of $R$. Protection matrices $P_j$ are built from
household-grouped 3-fold cross-fitted nuisances $m_j(H)$ \citep{chernozhukov2016dml} and the
residuals $\mathrm{onehot}(S_j)-m_j(H)$, aggregated over local roles, coalition roles and a marginal
variant. The released map is the top-16 eigenvectors of $U_{\mathrm{norm}}-\lambda P$. Inference needs
only $T$ and $H_A$; $H_B$ enters fitting and auditing and is never an inference input.

Study~2 replaced the fixed penalty matrix by one computed from degree-two monomials of the channel
output, so the objective is no longer $\operatorname{tr}(W^\top AW)$ for any $W$-independent $A$, and
added a predeclared rank-8 variant. Study~3 replaced that penalty again with two blocks that are
exactly invariant under $W\mapsto WQ$ for orthogonal $Q$: a Frobenius-weighted quadratic moment block,
for which rotation acts as $M\mapsto Q^\top MQ$, and an exact average of three Gaussian kernels on a
frozen $512$-row subset, which sees the data only through pairwise distances.

\paragraph{What is proved, and over what.} Four statements are elementary and we prove them in
Appendix~\ref{app:math}; nothing else in this paper is a theorem.

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep1pt
\item Appending $Z$ without modifying $H$ preserves $H$ exactly. This is a property of concatenation.
\item An unrestricted recipient holding $[H,Z]$ can ignore $Z$, so its optimal population log loss is
no worse than with $H$ alone. This bounds the \emph{population optimum}, not any fitted model, and
our measured increments are sometimes negative.
\item Under unrestricted Bayes prediction and ordinary log loss, the population risk difference
between $H$ and $(H,Z)$ \emph{equals} $I(S;Z\mid H)$. Our validation-selected finite attackers do not
attain those infima, so the reported differences bound that quantity in neither direction, and
mutual-information estimation has its own formal limits \citep{mcallester2020formal}.
\item For the repaired quadratic block, orthogonal reparameterisation preserves the Frobenius norm,
and an exact radial kernel is invariant to the corresponding distance-preserving rotations. Both hold
identically, subject to stated finite-precision conditions; measured, the objective moves by at most
$5.6\times10^{-17}$ under random rotations.
\end{enumerate}

Ky Fan's maximum principle \citep{fan1949weyl} gives a restricted global optimum for the
\emph{original} fixed-matrix family only. The refinements have no fixed matrix to take a spectrum of,
so they inherit no optimality statement whatever, and a suboptimal solution remains a live
explanation for their results. Eigenvalue-sign rank selection is SARL's Theorem~3, OptNet-ARL's
Theorem~4.1 and K-TOpt's Corollary~4.1 \citep{sadeghi2019sarl,sadeghi2021optnetarl,sadeghi2022ktopt};
we fixed $r=16$ to match the width of the neural comparator, which is a comparison choice, not an
optimum. The moment condition is a finite fitted subset of the $L^2$ characterisation of conditional
independence used by kernel CI tests \citep{zhang2011kci,strobl2019rcit}---Lemma~2(v) of
\citet{zhang2011kci}, attributed there to Daudin---quantified over $f\in\{Z_\ell b_k(H)\}$ rather than
over every square-integrable $f$. Vanishing fitted moments therefore establish no conditional
independence, and a non-zero moment may reflect nuisance error: one of our own fixtures shows a
misspecified $m(H)$ manufacturing a penalty more than ten times the oracle value under \emph{exact}
conditional independence.

\section{Relation to prior work}
\label{sec:related}

The closed-form line of Sadeghi, Dehdashtian and Boddeti already solves this shape of problem. SARL
\citep{sadeghi2019sarl} takes eigenvectors of a utility-minus-$\lambda$-dependence matrix; OptNet-ARL
\citep{sadeghi2021optnetarl} keeps closed-form ridge players inside a learned encoder and states in
one sentence that it generalises to several sensitive attributes; K-TOpt \citep{sadeghi2022ktopt} adds
random Fourier features and evaluates on Folktables \citep{ding2021retiring}; U-FaTE
\citep{dehdashtian2024ufate} adds a \emph{conditional} penalty. Our marginal arms are, up to
parametrisation, SARL's construction on whitened residuals---verified by bitwise rebuild
(\S\ref{sec:external}). Adversarial fair \emph{and transferable} representation learning is Madras et
al.\ \citep{madras2018laftr}, and we claim nothing new for that combination. Linear concept erasure
\citep{belrose2023leace,holstege2025splince} supplies an exact guarantee against \emph{linear}
adversaries on a representation the releaser owns and may edit. Adversarial removal is known to leave
recoverable information \citep{elazar2018adversarial}, and least-privilege learning has fundamental
limits \citep{stadler2024leastprivilege}. \textbf{Reusing a known solver, a conditional-moment
characterisation or a rank rule is attribution, not innovation.}

Three things differ, with their weight stated honestly. \emph{What we condition on:} U-FaTE's
conditional measure is stratification over a discrete label, and $H_A$ is a pair of continuous
probability vectors, so we substitute cross-fitted nuisance residuals; $H$ is also a \emph{released
artifact} rather than the label to be predicted. \emph{What utility means:} reconstruction of a
residualised teacher rather than dependence with a label. \emph{Recipient structure:} separate local
and coalition penalties over a fixed prior output, with only one recipient receiving a channel. The
first two are adaptations of known components. For the third, the nearest candidate we found is
weaker prior art than its title suggests: the multi-consumer information-theoretic line
\citep{sankar2013utility} designs one sanitised release consumed by many users, and we could not
confirm from its text that it carries a per-recipient release or a collusion term---we mark that
entry ambiguous rather than assert it. Appendix~\ref{app:related} gives the full comparison table over
five features (fixed pre-existing output, reusable extra features, recipient-specific permissions,
coalition-aware training, independent attack evaluation), with every entry we could not verify from a
full text marked as unverified rather than guessed.

\section{Evidence status, and a chronology that keeps four studies apart}
\label{sec:design}

California ACS one-year PUMS, ages 19--34, three household splits throughout, with disjoint pools for
representation fitting, source validation, task fitting and validation, attacker fitting and
validation, and evaluation. Attacks are logistic regression, two restarted MLPs at 120/360 epochs,
two gradient-boosted trees and a random-feature kernel-ridge family, plus every legal projection of
$H$'s own attacks and of singleton candidates into the coalition view. Uncertainty is a paired
household-cluster bootstrap with single-step max-$|t|$ simultaneous intervals inside each pre-declared
family.

Study~2 numbers in this paper remain numbers for the defective objective and are labelled so; the
Study~3 numbers are numbers for a different construction, not corrected versions of Study~2's.

\section{Study 1: the confirmed coalition effect, and the rule it did not establish}
\label{sec:study1}

\begin{figure}[t]
\centering
\includegraphics[width=\textwidth]{figures/forest_primary.pdf}
\caption{Paired differences with simultaneous $95\%$ intervals inside each pre-declared family, fresh
2017 attacks, both weightings, candidate minus comparator in nats. \textbf{Left, F1:} $C1$ against
its two local controls. The two grey A/sex rows are \emph{unresolved}---the comparison is undecided,
not the endpoint unaffected. \textbf{Right, F3:} $C1$ against $J$.}
\label{fig:forest}
\end{figure}

On the sealed 2017 partition, $C1$ has a simultaneous-interval advantage over both $L1$ and $L2$
under both weightings, and the registered \texttt{advantage} rule fired in all four contrast $\times$
weighting cells. The effect is real and not uniform: of the 16 sensitive cells in family F1,
\textbf{14 have adjusted intervals entirely below zero, none is above zero, and two are unresolved}
($C1$ versus $L1$ on A/sex unweighted, $[-0.00585,+0.00020]$, and versus $L2$, $[-0.00568,+0.00052]$).
Frozen 2018 attacks reproduce the ordering, so it is not an artifact of refitting attackers on the new
year. Every augmented interface clears the $0.01$-nat residence reference over $H$ in every seed,
weighting and mode.

\textbf{The paired residence rule is a point criterion and only a point criterion.} As implemented it
is $\text{seed-mean}(C1-\text{comparator})\le 0.001$ on residence log loss; the four values are
$0.00029$, $0.00010$, $0.00059$ and $0.00017$, so it passes. It does \emph{not} follow that the cost
is below $0.001$: \textbf{zero of four comparisons support interval non-inferiority at that margin},
missing it by $2\%$ to $35\%$ even under the most permissive procedure we computed, and zero support
two-sided equivalence (Appendix~\ref{app:study1}). ``No statistically detected cost'' and ``a cost
bounded below $0.001$'' are different statements and only the first is supported. The same applies to
the harm side: the \texttt{advantage} rule requires that no sensitive endpoint be significantly worse,
which is an absence-of-evidence condition, and on the two unresolved A/sex cells up to
$5\times10^{-4}$ nats of \emph{extra} local sex recovery is not excluded.

\textbf{And the mechanism is not competitive with $J$.} $C1$ is significantly worse than $J$ on all
four sensitive endpoints under both weightings and on both source probes, and better only on
residence, $-0.0093\,[-0.0150,-0.0036]$. That ordering survives matching the two channels on average
utility in both directions (Appendix~\ref{app:study1}). It does \emph{not} make $J$ dominant: $J$
carries materially less residence capability than every spectral arm, so neither channel
Pareto-dominates the other, and $J$ is an internal channel, so this is evidence about $J$ and not
about the state of the art.

\section{Studies 2 and 3: two refinements, both negative}
\label{sec:study23}

Study~2 asked whether that gap closes if the penalty acts on nonlinear functions of the channel, or
the released rank is reduced, or both. Both factors moved measured recovery in the intended direction
and they act on different attributes---the nonlinear penalty most on sex, rank-8 compression most on
race---taking additional A/race recovery from $0.0274$ to $0.0055$ against $J$'s $0.0071$ while
holding residence gain at $0.0259$ against $J$'s $0.0215$. But \textbf{all eight registered
coordination cells failed}, the best candidate \texttt{nlr8-C1} remained significantly worse than $J$
on local sex ($+0.01065$ unweighted, $+0.01210$ person-weighted; adjusted intervals exclude zero), an
exploratory cross-year look widened rather than narrowed that gap, and the nonlinear penalty was not
harmless: at rank 8 with the $L1$ policy it made A/race recovery significantly \emph{worse},
$+0.00862\,[+0.00168,+0.01556]$ unweighted. Denominators are in Appendix~\ref{app:study2}; most of
the grid is simply undecided at three seeds.

Study~2 then diagnosed, after those maps had been evaluated, that its penalty was
\emph{coordinate-dependent}: summing degree-two monomials $a\le b$ with equal weight computes
$\sum_a M_{aa}^2+\sum_{a<b}M_{ab}^2$ rather than $\|M\|_F^2$, and only the latter survives
$M\mapsto Q^\top MQ$. A separately optimised, information-preserving rotation of the original
solution's subspace reaches a mean $0.628$ of the refinement's training-objective gain (range
$0.367$--$0.907$ over 18 conditions). That share is \textbf{attained, not a supremum}---every rotation
search stopped on budget exhaustion or a failed line search---and it is a property of the objective,
not an accounting of what the optimiser did.

\textbf{Study~3 repaired it exactly, and the repair made the measured outcome worse.} The repaired
objective is a function on the Grassmannian: rotation-only share $1.4\times10^{-15}$ over 18 cells
against a registered forecast of $0.01$, and $|L(WQ)-L(W)|\le5.6\times10^{-17}$ on real data. This
gate was computed after fitting and \emph{before} any 2018 or 2017 score was read, which is why the
repair claim is supportable at all. Under the repaired objective---the only common yardstick---the
repaired map attains a lower value than the defective one in 18 of 18 matched cells. And its releases
disclose \emph{more}: over six matched contrasts $\times$ four sensitive endpoints $\times$ two
weightings, \textbf{8 of 48 cells are significantly worse and none is significantly better}
(Table~\ref{tab:repair}). The largest is $+0.0107\,[+0.0034,+0.0180]$ on local race at rank 16;
none of the twelve residence cells resolves in either direction, so the extra disclosure buys no
measured capability. Of Study~3's seven registered
forecasts, three were wrong, and the authors' own registration of the central one was ill-posed;
Appendix~\ref{app:study3} reproduces all seven.

\textbf{What this does and does not show.} It refutes the prediction made from the rotation
diagnostic---that removing the provably inert slack would let the same compute buy more protection.
It does \emph{not} show that rotation invariance is harmful, and it does not isolate a cause. The
repair changed five things at once: Frobenius weighting, a single scalar block scale in place of
per-feature standardisation, no centering, an exact radial kernel in place of a finite Fourier bank,
and frozen per-role $512$-row subsets. No ablation separates them, and the kernel substitution
changes the estimand---the new block averages over at most $512$ rows and carries a measured $1/m$
V-statistic floor. Surrogate--attacker mismatch remains the live explanation; ``coordinate dependence
was an implicit regulariser'' is a hypothesis this design cannot test, and we do not assert it.
Nor does four failed coordination cells in one construction show that nonlinear conditional-dependence
penalties cannot satisfy a coordination rule. Finally, the repaired arms are significantly
\emph{worse} than their own linear-moment controls on A/race at rank 8 in three contrasts, so ``the
repaired arms beat their own controls''---true of exploratory 2017 seed means---is not true of the
2018 intervals.

\section{The first executed external comparisons}
\label{sec:external}

Studies 1 and 2 ran no published method. Study~3 did, and the answer is unambiguous in one direction
and carefully bounded in the other.

\begin{table}[t]
\centering\footnotesize
\input{tables/external_vs_repaired.tex}
\caption{Adapted published methods against the repaired mechanism at rank 16 with the coalition
policy. Every arm transforms or replaces \emph{only} the auxiliary channel and appends it to
unchanged $H_A$. \noteExternalVsRepaired}
\label{tab:extrep}

\paragraph{What each adaptation is, and what it is not.} LEACE \citep{belrose2023leace} and SPLINCE
\citep{holstege2025splince} are applied to the 16-coordinate auxiliary channel \emph{alone} and the
transformed channel is appended to unchanged $H_A$. They are deliberately \emph{not} applied to the
concatenated $[H_A,Z]$ wire: LEACE's $P^\ast$ is a single oblique projection over all coordinates and
nothing constrains it to act as the identity on $H_A$, so editing the joint wire would generally
change the published service output. The consequence for interpretation is the important part.
\textbf{Their empirical linear guarantee applies to the fitted erasure of that channel, on the moments
it was fitted on. It is not a guarantee about arbitrary nonlinear prediction from the concatenated
service-plus-channel view an attacker actually holds}---LEACE's own authors conjecture that
nondestructive editing against a general nonlinear adversary is intractable, and a fixture in this
study confirms the boundary concretely: after erasure a linear probe reads $R^2<10^{-8}$ while a
quadratic feature of the same erased channel still recovers. SPLINCE additionally preserves covariance
with the \emph{authorised training} tasks, not with the held-out residence task; that is an
adaptation, and an asymmetry in SPLINCE's favour on utility, since no other arm sees a task label
during representation fitting. SPLINCE also shares LEACE's kernel, so under its own Theorem~2 the two
coincide after refitting an unregularised unique-minimiser model---any difference measured here comes
from attacker regularisation and nonlinearity, never from a stronger guarantee. OptNet-ARL's
multi-$\lambda$ multi-attribute form is an \textbf{extrapolation}: the paper asserts multi-attribute
generalisation in one sentence with no equation, no normalisation rule and no code. And the project's
marginal spectral arms turned out to \emph{be} SARL-style rebuilds---reconstructing them from the
derivation reproduces the stored maps bitwise---with one measured mismatch that is not absorbable into
$\bar\lambda$: per-attribute trace reweighting, moving the selected subspace by projector Frobenius
distance $0.10$--$0.20$. They are credited and reused, and six duplicate fits were avoided.
\textbf{Shared policy coefficients do not imply equal effective regularisation across methods.}

\paragraph{The result.} Adapted LEACE, OptNet-C1 and OptNet-L2 recover significantly less than the
repaired spectral mechanism on all four sensitive endpoints under \emph{both} weightings, and
adapted SPLINCE on seven of those eight cells (Table~\ref{tab:extrep}; its coalition-sex cell is
unresolved under person weighting). \texttt{OptNet-L1} separates on none.
\textbf{A closed-form linear eraser from 2023, applied to a channel this project already had, recovers
less than the mechanism this line of work developed across three studies.} That is the consequential
finding, and it is a finding about our mechanism.

The utility side must be stated with equal care, and the incoming study's own summary overstated it.
LEACE's residence-loss difference against the repaired arm is $+0.0036\,[-0.0028,+0.0100]$ unweighted
and $+0.0040\,[-0.0031,+0.0110]$ person-weighted. That is \emph{unresolved}, not costless: the interval
admits a cost of up to $0.010$ nats, larger than the recovery advantage LEACE wins on three of the
four endpoints, and it contains SPLINCE's $+0.0089$, which the same analysis calls significant. Against
the channel LEACE actually transformed, the cost is real: $+0.0098\,[+0.0036,+0.0159]$ unweighted and
$+0.0078\,[+0.0011,+0.0145]$ person-weighted, with mean residence gain over $H$ falling from $0.0318$
to $0.0220$. \textbf{Linear erasure of this channel buys its protection with measurable authorised
capability.} We therefore do not say LEACE dominates anything.

Against $J$, LEACE, SPLINCE, OptNet-C1 and OptNet-L2 are \emph{not separated} at three seeds on any of
the five family endpoints, while the repaired spectral arm is significantly worse on three of four
(Table~\ref{tab:extJ}). ``Not separated'' is not equivalence: no equivalence or non-inferiority test
was run for these cells, and all three external point estimates on local sex lean towards $J$, two of
them missing significance by less than $0.0007$. \texttt{OptNet-L1} is significantly worse than $J$ on
local sex, so the OptNet family is not uniformly indistinguishable from it either.

\paragraph{One structural finding worth keeping.} Linear erasure deletes exactly
$\operatorname{rank}(\Sigma_{XZ})$ directions, so the realised width of the released channel is
$6,6,7$ across the three seeds. Seed~2 differs because one race category has \emph{no support in that
seed's own representation-fitting pool} (counts $1,1,0$); the category is not absent from California,
and a class without support is unmeasured, not protected. \textbf{Protected-class support in the
fitting pool determines the realised width of a released channel} (Appendix~\ref{app:study3}).

\section{What this evidence does not establish}
\label{sec:limits}

Stated once, in one place, rather than scattered.

\textbf{No attack maximum is an upper bound.} Every recovery number is what a
\emph{validation-selected} member of a finite attack collection achieved. It is a floor. Our own data
contain a clean demonstration: when $J$ is given additional attack candidates, its measured absolute
recovery \emph{falls}, because selection on validation loss can pick a candidate that generalises
worse (Appendix~\ref{app:scope}). A stronger attacker we did not build would move any of these numbers
upward.

\textbf{Nested validation-selected models need not produce non-negative gains.} Statement~2 of
\S\ref{sec:method} is about population optima. In finite samples, 22 of 429 unweighted role-cells
have \emph{negative} increments, and $J$'s own local-sex increment is negative under person weighting
with an interval excluding zero. We report these unclipped, because clipping them would manufacture
protection out of a selection artifact. $H$'s own attack, routed onto an augmented wire, remains
executable, so a negative increment removes nothing an $H$-only attacker already had.

\textbf{No detected difference is not equality.} Most of the grid in every attribution family is
unresolved at three seeds. We mark such cells blank and never read them as parity.

\textbf{Ratios are scale-dependent; paired differences are not.} $C1$ against $J$ on A/race is
$6.0\times$ on increments and $2.6\times$ in absolute terms; the paired difference is identical on
both scales because the shared $H$ baseline cancels inside a paired contrast. We therefore quote
differences. This also makes every headline contrast in this paper invariant to the choice of attack
scope, even though the $H$-relative \emph{levels} are not (Appendix~\ref{app:scope}).

\textbf{Nothing here is a certificate.} Vanishing fitted finite moments imply neither $Z\perp S\mid H$
nor any bound on $I(S;Z\mid H)$; no Type-I control is inherited from KCI or RCoT; and a misspecified
nuisance manufactures a penalty more than ten times the oracle value under exact conditional
independence.

\textbf{Development against confirmation.} Only the 2017 transport is confirmatory. The 2018 pools
have been used repeatedly; the 2017 seal is spent, so Study~3's cross-year pass is development,
carries no intervals by design, and excludes the erasure and OptNet arms entirely. \textbf{ACS 2016
remains unscored}: admitted on provenance and schema with a prospective label-blind split prepared,
with no label, output, transform or performance figure inspected. A positive development result may
motivate a confirmation; it can never inherit a sealed year's status.

\textbf{Sampling and design.} Three seeds are three fixed fitted systems on one cohort. The bootstraps
cover people, not retraining variability and not Census design variance. Transport is between public
survey years of the same state and age band, and cross-year person distinctness cannot be established
from public identifiers. Income is nominal and unadjusted and its estimand differs again in 2016.

\textbf{Numerical integrity.} Study~3's exploratory 2017 stage aborted four times with a probability
row summing to exactly zero inside an otherwise well-formed array. Every occurrence was detected by
full-schema validation and aborted its unit before any metrics file was written; the four affected
units were quarantined and recomputed by the unpatched pipeline, so no corrupted array reached any
table. The machine was at swap capacity throughout. \textbf{Memory pressure was the observed
condition, not a demonstrated cause}, and nothing here claims the machine is now proved reliable. One
document in the incoming package records that no such fault occurred; it predates the stage in which
they did, and we follow the run ledger instead (\texttt{CORRECTIONS.md} B10).

\section{Claims this revision withdraws or narrows}
\label{sec:withdrawn}

Seventeen items were corrected in the previous revision and are carried forward in
\texttt{CORRECTIONS.md}. Fourteen are new here. The substantive ones:

\begin{enumerate}\itemsep1pt
\item \textbf{``LEACE dominates the repaired mechanism with no statistically detectable residence
cost.''} The recovery half holds. The utility half does not: the difference is unresolved with an
interval admitting $0.010$ nats, it contains SPLINCE's significant cost, and against the channel LEACE
transformed the cost is significant at $+0.0098$. \emph{Dominates} is withdrawn.
\item \textbf{``LEACE, SPLINCE and OptNet-C1 are statistically indistinguishable from $J$.''} Narrowed
to unresolved differences. No equivalence test was run and all three point estimates lean towards $J$
on local sex.
\item \textbf{$J$ is not Pareto-dominant.} It recovers less and supplies less residence capability
than several spectral arms, significantly so against \texttt{riv16-L1}. It is a trade.
\item \textbf{``Coordinate dependence was acting as an implicit regulariser.''} Narrowed to a
hypothesis. Five ingredients changed together and no ablation separates them.
\item \textbf{The $63\%$ rotation share} is an attained diagnostic value, not a supremum and not a
causal decomposition of training benefit.
\item \textbf{``Zero population support''} for a race category is a \emph{fitting-pool} count
($1,1,0$ by seed). A category missing from one pool is not absent from the population.
\item \textbf{The two carried numerical corrections are kept}: the \texttt{nlr8-C1} versus $J$ local-sex
deficit is $+0.01210$ person-weighted, not $+0.0098$; and ``no endpoint significantly worse'' for the
nonlinear-penalty factor stays withdrawn, the counterexample being $+0.00862\,[+0.00168,+0.01556]$
unweighted.
\item \textbf{``Terminal 1's experiment does not exist''} was stale. Study~3 is complete, committed
and integrated here at \texttt{73903b7f}.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Conclusion}

A service that has already published predictions can add a reusable channel beside them without
changing a released number, and can steer that channel away from directions a recipient or a colluding
pair would use to infer sex and race. On a locked, previously unused survey year, the
coalition-conditioning knob does what it claims against both of its local controls---14 of 16 sensitive
cells strictly better, none worse, two unresolved---while costing residence capability by an amount the
data cannot bound below the $0.001$ nats the registered rule used. Everything built afterwards to make
the mechanism competitive failed: a nonlinear penalty failed all eight of its coordination cells and
made one endpoint significantly worse; an exact repair of that penalty's coordinate dependence---the
one intervention the diagnostic predicted would help---produced significantly \emph{more} disclosure on
eight of forty-eight cells and less on none; and the first executed external comparisons put three adapted published
methods below the developed mechanism on every sensitive endpoint.

\textbf{The defensible contribution is the release problem and the evaluation design}: fixed published
outputs as a hard constraint, recipient-specific and coalition-specific audit roles, fresh and frozen
attackers never pooled, absolute and incremental recovery reported side by side, negative increments
retained unclipped, denominators printed beside every count, per-endpoint results never averaged into
one score, and a sealed temporal transport with the multiplicity fixed in advance. The measurement
contract is what made three successive negative results legible instead of ambiguous. The mechanism
family is not competitive, and we say so.

\section{Reproducibility}

Deterministic seeds throughout; every unit writes a hash-bound completion record. All 90 pre-declared
Study~1 endpoints were regenerated from stored per-person predictions by an independently written
scorer agreeing to $1.1\times10^{-15}$. Study~3's own independent score replay reports a maximum
absolute difference of $4.4\times10^{-16}$ over 3{,}264 checks, and $H_A$/$H_B$ parity is bitwise on
all seven pools for every arm at release time and again at evaluation time. Every table and figure
here is generated by two scripts from committed evidence; the Study~3 assets are read at a pinned
commit through the object store, so each printed digit traces to a (commit, path, sha256) triple in
\texttt{MANIFEST.json}. No number was typed by hand. Commands and file map:
\texttt{REPRODUCE\_PAPER.md} in \texttt{results/pcrl\_manuscript\_review\_v3/}.
