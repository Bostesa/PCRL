Linear erasure deletes exactly $\mathrm{rank}(\Sigma_{XZ})$ directions. A centred joint one-hot over sex (2), race (9) and coverage (2) has rank $1+8+1=10$, leaving 6 of 16. \textbf{Seed 2 differs because one race category has no support in \emph{that seed\textquotesingle s own fitting pool}}, so the centred one-hot has rank 9 and the channel keeps 7 dimensions. Support counts refer to fitting pools, not to the population: a category missing from one pool is not absent from California.%
