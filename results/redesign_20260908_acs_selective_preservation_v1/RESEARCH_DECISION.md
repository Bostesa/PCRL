# Selective preservation and reconstruction — research decision, 2026-09-08

**Real-attribute preservation gives a useful but limited tradeoff: it improves mean residential transfer over shuffled-label preservation, while its apparent attribute-specific protection largely weakens under catch-up.** Removing PCA32 reconstruction reduces recovery, with a residential cost. Learning restores source utility lost by the fixed erased teacher, but also restores protected-label predictability. These are informative component-design results, not a general protection advance or a solution to purpose-specific/combined-access coordination.

All **30 new final models, six reused raw-teacher models, and six direct E/S teachers** are complete across seeds0/1/2. Every prescribed fresh/catch-up trajectory ran once to360 with nested120 selections. Full scientific process wall was **678.83 seconds (11.31 minutes)** on one M4 Pro numerical thread; no fitting was skipped, retried or invalidated. Original households remain **DEVELOPMENT EVALUATION**. Three seeds share one cohort; their sample SDs are descriptive, not population uncertainty. **Teacher selection does not establish dominance over both controls.** Relative to R, E-D reduces pooled SEX and race recovery in every unweighted seed at either rho, while worsening residence in every seed. Relative to S, every matched E condition improves *mean* residence under both weights. However, E has **more pooled SEX recovery in allfour unweighted matched means**; only E/rho0/C reverses that comparison under PWGTP. Race benefits shrink and vary by seed: E/rho0/D has lower mean race gain than S/rho0/D, but its race and residential advantages occur only in seed0 under unweighted scoring. E/rho.1/D even has slightly higher mean unweighted pooled race gain than its S control.

The shuffled control cannot be dismissed as having removed a different number of dimensions. E/S both retain projection ranks7,7,8 and have normalized fitting distortion.5625,.5625,.5, matching to float32 rounding. Their erased directions and real-label covariance differ; full spectra are reported. The direct E teacher itself has better mean residence and lower independent SEX/race gains than direct S, with worse source losses; the learned students do not preserve a general pooled SEX advantage. [Teacher construction and static results](TEACHERS.md), [paired evidence](PAIRED.csv).

**Strongest positive intervention result: removing reconstruction helps recoverability even without D.** In E-C, rho0 versus rho.1 lowers pooled SEX gain by **.007613 / .007531** and race gain by **.006864 / .006609**, in every seed under both weightings. Residence worsens **.002427 / .002623**, and civilian-at-work loss rises **.005178 / .004661**, within the original source allowance. E-D also lowers race gain in every seed under both weights; its SEX effect has mixed seed signs. This supports a reconstruction/utility/recovery tradeoff, not cost-free protection.

The effect is larger than the raw-teacher control in mean race recovery, but is not exclusive to real-label erasure. S-C reconstruction removal lowers unweighted SEX/race gains by.005664/.005832; S-D's SEX reduction is larger than E-D's. Thus generic reconstruction removal and compression account for part of the favorable pattern. [Controlled contrasts and interactions](ANALYSIS.md) retain each seed and weighting.

**D adds modest benefit with reconstruction and little pooled SEX benefit without it.** At E/rho.1, D−C lowers unweighted pooled SEX/race gains by.005231/.001760, in allthree seeds; residential changes have mixed signs and an almost-zero mean. At E/rho0, the pooled SEX difference is only **−.000045 unweighted / +.000012 weighted**. Race gain falls.001347/.001449 on average, with mixed seeds. Civilian-at-work utility improves relative to E0-C, while income/public-coverage losses rise. An independent-only SEX benefit therefore should not be presented as a robust pooled benefit.

**Learning adds useful source performance beyond copying E, at substantially greater leakage.** E0-D lowers direct E's income/civilian/public-coverage losses from.356662/.414513/.529162 to.289863/.287607/.497025 and improves residence by.005293 unweighted/.005782 weighted. It is not merely a copy of the teacher. But even matched independent audits recover more attributes from E0-D than from direct E, and catch-up raises its gains further. Against historical beta0 D_init, E0-D has worse residential and race means under both weights; its tiny unweighted SEX improvement reverses under PWGTP. These references prevent mistaking source-floor compliance for an overall advance.

**The strongest counterexample to the favorable interpretation is the audit-scope reversal.** For E0-D versus S0-D, independent SEX gains are.014994 versus.021506, apparently favoring E. Catch-up-inclusive gains are **.024669 versus.021009**, favoring S. Race's corresponding E advantage shrinks substantially. Catch-up starts from each release's own observer and inherits representation-fitting exposure, so it is reported separately rather than relabeled independent. Three of60 new catch-up trajectories win pooled selection at epoch0 (seed1 SEX: E/.1/D, E/0/C, E/0/D); their advantage is already present before additional catch-up optimization. The separate saved-observer candidate is excluded, while epoch0 remains eligible within the unchanged catch-up trajectory rule. Increasing the new noncontrol trajectories from120 to360 changes no candidate-selected predictions, release rankings or numerical margin decisions. The reused exposed-control MLPs have their separately reported historical budget changes. All204 new fresh/catch-up trajectories select by120; this does not establish saturation over other attackers or privacy. [Audit-budget and saved/fresh/catch-up evidence](AUDIT_BUDGET.md).

**Coordinate matching does not stop recovery of erased structure.** The direct E teacher's mean per-seed development affine qE error/prior ratio is.9992. Learned E finals have ratios.6711–.6982: appreciable removed structure is linearly recoverable despite small teacher-matching error. With rho0, this recovery increases from W to both finals in every seed. E0-D actually reconstructs qE slightly *better* than E.1-D while leaking fewer labels. The removed residual is not pure sensitive information. Retained E structure remains accurately reconstructable. Final E teacher/reconstruction gradients are not generally opposed; local source/protection opposition does not prove causation. [Mechanism evidence](MECHANISM.md) separates these diagnostics from task utility and attribute audits.

Original residential-retention and attribute-halving margins remain unmet for most/all E seeds; no E final passes pooled SEX halving on development under either weighting. Allnine race categories remain reported, and absent racecode4 attacker-fitting/attacker-validation support leaves the full assessment unassessable. Nothing here establishes universal privacy, impossibility, novelty, or information destruction across tasks.
