# Research decision

**Verdict: `INCOMPLETE_MATCHED_COMPARISON` for this frozen capacity bank.** The required task-utility gate failed on both prescribed seeds before there was a permissible candidate/control pair. No SEX recovery, equal-union, sequential, or joint-design effect was measured. There is no method win or algorithmic novelty claim.

The real fits trained five deleted-group masks and one full-input F head for each task and seed, with equal inherited 64×64 heads and fixed held-out global calibration. On group-aware FIT validation, all 20 mask-task-seed rows failed U0 and Ucal mean-utility caps. Zero masks were feasible for either task on either seed; all ten rule slots per seed were invalid. The full-input F head itself exceeded every U0 allowance in both tasks and seeds. Its U0 NLL excess was 0.044753–0.060306 nats against a 0.010 allowance. This is the strongest adverse result: a common training/reference gap exists before deleting a feature.

The most favorable limited finding is that the native-country and marital-status deletion masks met all F-relative utility caps for both tasks and both seeds. They still failed the mandatory U0/Ucal conjunction. It would be incorrect to promote them or to report their unmeasured privacy as a benefit.

M5 independently restored all 24 validation packages and reproduced all 20 rows and U0/Ucal/F margins exactly. This verifies the no-go arithmetic and selection branch, not the effectiveness of a release. No FIT SEX, ATTACK_FIT_NEW, INNER_SELECTION, old assessment, or barred HEAD_VALIDATION label arrays were read by the successful scientific/verifier workers. No fresh attacker fit, V2 control, confidence interval, or selected-pair deployment occurred. The planned sequential and union-matched controls exist in runnable source but have no valid fitted arm to compare. An identical-mask control tie cannot be assessed.

The next step is a separately frozen full-input training/reference feasibility study: identify why a head trained under the present role split and inherited architecture misses both U0 and Ucal, and require F to meet those caps before paying for another mask or privacy search. Any revised head, capacity, or criterion is a new development design, not an amendment to this result.
