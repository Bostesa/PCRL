# Baseline matching: every panel member vs the nested mixture (NM)

Owner: baseline/audit agent. Written 2026-09-24 before any science fit and before any outcome was seen. Code: `experiments/pcrl_shared_context_release_v1/{rd.py, adv.py, poscontrol.py}` (competitors and positive controls), `fit_nm.py` (NM, T32, DET_SEL; method implementer), `audit_panel.py` (independent audit; infrastructure). Spec: `DESIGN_SPEC.md` §2–§5 + Amendments M1, M2, M4 and M5 (`PROTOCOL_AMENDMENTS.md`).

The goal of this file is to make the competitors strong and to list every way the comparison can be unfair, in either direction. A control that wins is reported as a baseline win (spec §4).

## 3. Registered choices made to keep the competitors strong

1. **Pure-law receivers for RD selection.** The coverage-rule decoders ((law + D17 + U)/3) price tokens for the cost oracle, which needs every token defined. They are not used to *score* RD candidates. A deterministic policy that re-assigns most people would be understated by a decoder trained half on D17 semantics. Each RD candidate is scored by an AR standard slate fitted on its own pure law, which is what the audit's utility probe does. D17 is scored the same way (`preflight_M1_4`).
2. **D17-shrinkage grid for RD_TASK.** On the 30% smoke with the pre-M2 oracle, tau = 0.002 moved 82–89% of people off D17, and the inner task loss was no better than D17's. M2's paired oracle cuts this, but tau still does not scale with oracle error (M2 known limitation). RD_TASK therefore also evaluates tau ∈ {0.01, 0.03, 0.1, 0.3}, which keeps only the largest predicted paired gains. On the pre-M2 smoke, tau = 0.1 was best (4.5% of people changed). The grid is fixed before any candidate fit, is chosen on inner_selection only, and nests the spec recipe (tau = 0.002). `--switch-tau 0.002` reproduces the literal spec.
3. **RD_PRIV oracle and feasibility fallback.** Following M2, the priced cost u − mu·(λ·a) is paired against D17. Because paired targets are linear in mu, RD_PRIV fits two paired oracles (task and price) once and combines them as Δ̂_u − mu·Δ̂_p (`MuPairedOracle`, a `policies.PairedOracle`), instead of refitting one oracle per bisection step (≤ 100 refits per unit). This differs from a direct fit of the combined target only through boosting shrinkage and early stopping. Fallback: The spec's mu path (u − mu·p) prices tokens with *frozen* round-0 attackers. Frozen attackers cannot read new token semantics, so p rewards moving people away from D17, which is the opposite of what refit attackers punish (MATH_REVIEW §3.3). On the smoke, round 0 was feasible at mu = 0 on the frozen bank, and after one best response it needed mu = 77.6, moved 95% of people, and still failed its own refit (+0.008 nats). The registered fallback applies only when no mu in the bracket is feasible. It keeps the least-violating traced mu and bisects the D17-switch threshold tau upward (≤ 16 bracket expansions + 12 bisections). Deviation sets are nested in tau, and tau → ∞ is D17, which is always feasible, so the search always ends at a feasible policy on the current bank. If no round survives the final bank, RD_PRIV is D17 and is flagged as an alias.
4. **ADV strength.** Exact 17-token expectation (no sampling noise). Adversaries are warm-started and take 5 steps per encoder step. The encoder is warm-started at the feasible reference. Up to 300 epochs, with early stopping on the inner_selection feasibility and task key. Two betas. Fixed seeds, one thread (deterministic: `test_adv_is_deterministic_under_fixed_seed`). Learning rate and weight decay (3e-4, 1e-3) were set from a smoke diagnostic at beta = 0 (inner rows of the 30% a0 subsample only; no inner_check, no outer). At 1e-3/1e-4, inner_selection task loss rose 0.475 → 0.502 in 15 epochs while training-row loss fell 0.43 → 0.39 (overfitting). At 3e-4/1e-3 it stayed within 0.474–0.483. This is disclosed as tuning on inner_selection rows, the same role that selects checkpoints.
5. **M1(d) for every RD/ADV candidate.** Feasibility is judged against attackers refit on the candidate itself (a closing best response for the last RD_PRIV round and for every ADV shortlist entry), not only against the frozen bank.

6. **ADV input and calibration fixes (smoke-driven, disclosed).** Without the D17(T0) one-hot, the encoder could not reproduce D17 from X_A, r, p and risk: its warm-start law put 47% of its mass off D17. With the one-hot it puts 3% off D17, and its inner task loss matched D17's (0.464 vs 0.464 on smoke). The warm starts are early-stopped on inner_selection because the 300-step warm starts at lr 1e-2 left the adversaries worse than the class prior on inner_selection (AB/SEX CE 0.90–1.14 vs ln 2). After the fix they sit at 0.683–0.70.
7. **P-route ADV units (M5, `adv --select privacy`).** Under the task rule, ADV picked its warm start (≈ D17 + 3% uniform) in every smoke run, so ADV_B* is effectively a U-route competitor. The registered ADV_B*_P units:
   - early-stop on the inner_selection privacy key;
   - rank checkpoints by AB/SEX slack against attackers refit on each checkpoint, not by the co-trained adversary;
   - apply the P task cap relative to the exact D17 witness;
   - use distinct seeds;
   - record any fallback explicitly.
8. **Exact D17 witness (M4).** RD_PRIV and ADV include the exact D17 witness in their selection set and apply their own rule among feasible members. The minimum-violation fallback is removed. A witness selection releases the exact one-hot D17 law, which the audit collapses as an alias.
9. **Common receiver seed.** Every RD candidate and D17 is scored with the same receiver seed, so identical laws score identically. With per-candidate seeds on the smoke, exact-D17 candidates scored up to 0.0023 nats *better* than D17 (receiver MLP-initialization noise), and one RD_PRIV round moved by 0.0045 between runs. That is receiver noise of the same size as the route margins. It is removed within a unit, but not across units or methods.

## 4. Unavoidable or registered differences, and which way they cut

| # | Difference | Favors |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | NM re-solves an LP with *all* retained cuts every round (adaptive prices). RD_PRIV keeps the round-0 dual *direction* and only moves mu or tau (spec). `--direction augmented` (prices the best-response cuts too) exists but is not the default | NM |
| D2 | NM gets 6 alternation rounds. RD_TASK and RD_PRIV get 3 (spec) | NM |
| D3 | NM's selected round may be round 6, whose law never faced attackers refit on itself (AR rule: round r's attackers are fit on round r−1). Every RD_PRIV round and every ADV shortlist entry must also pass a best response refit on itself (M1(d)) | NM (the stricter feasibility test is imposed on RD/ADV only) |
| D4 | NM mixes up to 5 frozen policies per context and can put weight on the columns that verify on coefficient_split. RD commits to one deterministic map | NM (by design: the attribution question) |
| D5 | RD_TASK gets a 4-value tau grid and RD rounds get pure-law receiver slates (16 + 1 extra decoder slates). NM columns use fixed tau = 0.002. More inner_selection choices also mean more winner's-curse optimism, but only in RD's own inner score, not in the audit | RD_TASK |
| D6 | ADV trains its encoder and decoder on nuisance_train ∪ coefficient_split (spec), 2.25× the policy-learning rows of NM/RD (nuisance_train only). Its checkpoint feasibility is then checked on coefficient_split, which is in-sample for ADV. NM's LP is also in-sample on coefficient_split, but NM policies are not | ADV |
| D7 | Stochastic laws (NM, ADV) raise the per-person token support. The inherited exact `hist_gb_{20,5}` auditors scale min_samples_leaf by the maximum support (up to 17×), which weakens those two auditors for stochastic releases. The sampled_hist_gb, logistic and MLP auditors are unaffected. The support is recorded per release (`audit_panel.hist_gb_record`). ADV prunes entries below 1e-3, but a softmax still has support ≈ 13–16 on smoke | NM, ADV (vs deterministic RD/D17) |
| D8 | ADV's inner task statistic uses its own jointly trained MLP decoder. RD uses the standard-slate receiver. NM uses the AR retained-decoder rule. Only the audit's utility probe is common | mixed, inner selection only |
| D9 | ADV adversaries are MLPs (2×64). The audit also uses logistic and tree auditors, which the encoder never trained against | NM, RD (ADV privacy may not transfer) |
| D10 | ADV adds an exact D17-imitation warm start. NM starts from Q_hist (AR). RD starts from d_1 | neutral / ADV |
| D11 | RD_TASK is not constrained by any bank. It is the richer-input utility ceiling and the M1.4 preflight. If it passes the privacy guards in the audit, that is a baseline win | — |
| D12 | Bisection assumes monotone feasibility in mu or tau. The trace records every evaluation, so non-monotonicity is visible, but a feasible mu below the bisection bracket could be missed | NM (RD_PRIV could be slightly suboptimal) |
| D14 | RD_PRIV keeps the task key for its final choice because it serves both routes (M5). Its mu or tau is bisected to the minimal feasible value, so it is a minimally protective feasible policy. NM*_P and ADV_B*_P choose by the largest AB/SEX slack | NM on the P route |
| D15 | The ADV witness is scored with the warm-start decoder. The RD_PRIV witness uses a pure-D17 receiver with the common seed. NM uses its own rule. Each family scores its witness with its own inner statistic, so a witness win is family-internal | neutral |
| D13 | DET_SEL (method implementer) is evaluated under the NM final decoder and bank of the same K. RD_PRIV is the independently trained deterministic comparator. Only DET_SEL is a same-bank exact selector, so a DET_SEL loss attributes gain to randomization; an RD_PRIV loss does not | — |

## 5. Alias and identity handling

Deterministic RD laws that equal D17 on the admitted rows (for example the RD_PRIV fallback) are exact aliases. `audit_panel.canonicalize` hashes each release's per-person law on audit_fit, inner_selection and inner_check plus the row identity, audits each distinct law once, and keeps every declared name in `ALIAS_LEDGER.json`. RD_TASK round 0 equals NM's `task_only` column by construction when tau = 0.002 is selected. That is a deliberate construction identity, not an independent benchmark.
