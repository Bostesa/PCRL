# Mechanism map and attribution boundary

This is an exploratory score-only transfer of known randomized quantization and privacy-funnel ideas. It begins with frozen task scores, fine cells and a held-out-calibrated public bank; the operation that changes information is sampling a **different public bank code** from a class-preserving conditional law. The person receives one code/vector draw, not an averaged vector or a fine cell with a revised decoder.

The central provider reads both U0 score vectors and private fine cells. SEX is a permitted FIT label for protected channel design, never an inference input. Recipient 1 receives only income's sampled globally canonical code/probability/decision and fixed metadata; recipient 2 receives only occupation's corresponding wire. The aligned coalition combines the two. Public bank, action map, gate weights and hashes are defense-aware context, but private per-person U0 scores, fine cells and random coins are not given to a recipient.

The predecessor portfolio A prototype used two local stochastic matrices `Q_r(code_r|fine_cell_r)` with independent local draws. It retained distinct output token IDs even when decoded vectors were equal. Its ten Adult source-bank preprocessing units completed, but it never ran a protected Adult channel and fresh complete-view attack comparison. This proposal globally canonicalizes output identities and learns a small **central two-score gate** over at most 16 joint actions. R01's actual fixed-bank audit found **zero duplicate decoded outputs** among the 16/321 T* codes in either Adult seed, so canonical merging changes no Adult bank size here. It remains necessary to prevent a source-specific ID leak and to define a correct public output law. The fixed bank and original mean utility caps remain; the old H-GLOBAL decoder is not refitted. These are different parameterization and service-scope choices, not proof of a new generic information-theoretic channel. The earlier ACS stochastic Q was a finite frozen-code/decoder-law design; its negative result does not rule out this paired raw-score-conditioned gate, and this gate does not claim to be fundamentally different simply because it has neural parameters.

Known relevant alternatives are task-only rate compression, deterministic quantization, local protected channels, central product channels, and both freeze-first design orders under the same source bank and utility caps. A favorable JOINT-STOCH versus T* alone would not isolate the central gate, randomization or joint design. An empirical method lead requires improvement beyond the strongest complete-view matched control with both tasks useful and no adverse local shift. A stochastic-component lead requires the optimized JOINT-DET arm to be completed and beaten. A joint-design lead additionally requires central product and both freeze-first orders to be completed and beaten. Every joint output law factors into either **sampling** order, so no result can establish an inherent advantage over unrestricted sequential sampling.

Fitted-law Bayes SEX accuracy is an exact finite empirical objective on public code identities. It is not fresh nonlinear attack AUC, mutual information, differential privacy, a pointwise distortion guarantee or population protection. Exact expected NLL/Brier are likewise properties of the fixed FIT law and fixed bank. Report sampled INNER utility and recovery over the three precommitted one-release realizations, with person/group pairing, as development evidence only.
