Stage-two sequential training receives **one precommitted cached first output per FIT unit**, sampled only after stage one's checkpoint is frozen; duplicate members share the first output. Its first-stage checkpoint, private FIT-draw stream and stage-two start are recorded before the second fit. The final complete pair's utility/privacy is additionally replayed under the **exact full frozen first-stage law**, at most four first outputs per person; selection requires final exact-cap feasibility, not merely feasibility on the sampled training draw. Both sequential orders get the same total tuning/restart/update/CPU opportunity as JOINT-STOCH, with allocation rule sealed before fits. The mathematical factorization of JOINT-STOCH into either **sampling** order is also checked by tiny exact compiler/replay, but those identical-law aliases are not scientific controls.
