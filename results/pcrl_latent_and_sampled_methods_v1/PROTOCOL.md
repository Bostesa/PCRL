## Scope and disclosure

Two separate service contracts are screened. D is a feature-code release: recipient i receives only its own ten hard bits, a probability vector and decision computed solely from those bits, and globally fixed model/version data. The coalition receives both complete recipient views. D never transmits soft bits, latent features, teacher scores or raw predictors. A is a score-only release: recipient i receives a globally canonical sampled public output code, its fixed decoded probability/decision and public metadata; the coalition receives both. A provider may read both frozen task score vectors, a disclosed input-scope advantage also given to matched central controls. A and D are ranked within their own service contract, never across unlike interfaces.

## Endpoints and selection

Every recipient and the aligned coalition are scored on their complete transmitted view. Common point utility caps are inclusive: each task accuracy loss <= 0.010; mean natural-log NLL excess <= 0.010 nats; summed multiclass Brier excess <= 0.005, against both original U0 and Ucal. Candidate comparisons to matched T and compatible F are also reported. Useful class recall and gain over a FIT-majority/constant predictor are reported. Coalition SEX AUC benefit over every eligible matched control must be at least 0.020; each local AUC worsening must be <= 0.010. A low-leakage but task-useless or hidden-fallback release is ineligible. D decisions may change and must earn accuracy; A preserves input predicted class by its channel support.

The fixed attack slate and its ATT-only fitting/tuning are bound before releases. It includes linear, nonlinear tabular and small neural readers; categorical/bit/code lookup and defense-aware readers when appropriate; coalition readers that ignore either recipient; null, shuffled-label and planted-leak controls. An attacker sees all public code, probability, decision and variable metadata, but no private per-person provider input. The strongest fixed eligible reader and strongest matched control use the sealed selection rule. Extra attacks are reported separately.

Nomination ranking within a family is frozen: valid admission/required controls; both-task point utility in both screen seeds; local guards against all eligible comparators; largest worst-comparator coalition benefit; simpler method and deterministic ID tie break. At most one finalist per family. No best seed or random draw. If confidence bounds cross a cap, point-feasible nomination is `PRECISION_LIMITED`, not a full statistical PASS. Missing required controls make the dependent competitive claim incomplete.

Before a family fit, its stage lock fixes a simultaneous paired-group interval family over the entire finite candidate/control registry, both tasks/references, three views, both seeds and any optional candidate maximum. The same 2,234 ordered INNER groups and resampling indices are used across arms; model seeds are not independent populations. PASS requires accuracy lower bound >= -0.010, NLL/Brier excess upper bound <= 0.010/0.005, coalition benefit lower bound >= 0.020 and local worsening upper bound <= 0.010. A bound crossing is UNRESOLVED. A point-only screen cannot claim statistical establishment. Fitted-model conditional intervals exclude adaptive historical search and population transport.

## Engineering and claims

Hard-code training uses an explicitly documented straight-through surrogate; it is not an exact hard-quantizer derivative. MMD is a dependence objective, not a privacy guarantee. Score-channel exact expected loss is over actual sampled public outputs, not an averaged probability. A cached single sample per person is the modeled query contract; repeated fresh-sample protection is not claimed. A joint conditional law can be compiled into either sequential sampling order, so no inherent joint-sampling expressiveness claim is allowed. Prior work, implementation changes, empirical gains and genuine novelty remain separate.

R04 independently verifies source/exposure, effective seals, representative deployed releases, selected endpoints, intervals, controls and claims without importing runner scoring or candidate implementations. Public files contain aggregates and hashes; rows, labels, predictions, checkpoints and private random coins remain private. Local git seals are effective for authorized development work but not external preregistration. Remote or off-device custody problems do not stop admitted local science and remain explicit in handoff.
