# Unit accounting integration

`UNIT_STATUS.csv` is the validated physical R03 queue and separate-charge ledger. Its CPU values are the only values summed for the campaign budget. `A_PLANNED_UNIT_STATUS.csv` is the 220-row sealed A producer graph, including 136 selected-policy descendants that were never triggered or were blocked by incomplete parents. `A_FIXED_ATTACK_PHYSICAL_ATTEMPTS.csv` preserves the 20 actual fixed attack attempts: 18 completed (six original, 12 R1) and two failed originals. The 12 corrected physical attempts are aliases of 12 logical cells; their CPU is counted once in R03, not added again from the A plan table.

The planned table is derived from R19's immutable proposal SHA-256 `63fe6909dfc738e5b467d64cfe3501c9c436c8cd3a4d2dba520ecbbca9fe5cca`, with exactly two `scientific_status` edits: `A-FIT-A_SEQ21-S0` and `A-FIT-A_SEQ21-S1` read `FAILED_AFTER_INFEASIBLE_STAGE1` instead of R19's `INCOMPLETE_RESOURCE`. Their original queue status `failed`, resource numbers and hashes are untouched. Both seeds completed two 150-update stage-one starts without a feasible checkpoint; stage two did not run. There was no observed CPU, wall-time or memory cutoff, as recorded in R03's all-16 FIT closeout SHA-256 `beb24a699c696adf0c0f7b483c7a1c9e074510393907e05a710d1fac892a9d39`. This integration wording corrects the reason label, not the underlying scientific or physical result.
