# Prior-art map for latent hard codes and sampled score channels

Status: scoped primary-source review for R00/R19, 2026-10-09. This maps the two **current proposed mechanisms** and their internal predecessors before making a new-method claim. It does not assert that any proposed arm is utility-feasible or empirically private. The current D specification is `latent_repair_v1/design/PROPOSED_D_LOCK.md`; the current A specification is `sampled_channel_v1/design/MECHANISM_SPEC.md`. The longer mathematical audit is `r05/MECHANISM_MATH_PRIOR_REVIEW.md`.

It is **not** ordinary adversarial C, and a D-K lead over it alone would not isolate the kernel, hard coding, or joint objective. The current D proposal does not register D-C as an initial candidate. |
| **Ordinary D adversarial C** | [Bertran et al. 2019](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/bertran19a.html) already learn utility-preserving obfuscating representations through an adversarial neural game; their [author code](https://github.com/MartinBertran/AIOI) is linked by PMLR. | The current C adapts adversarial training to the same frozen latents, ten-bit mapper, local plus pair critics, and code-only task heads as D-K. Bertran's published transformation and application are not an exact ten-bit Adult implementation. | C is an established-method control. Beating a serious matched C supports a relative empirical result; it is not a claim that adversarial representation learning was invented here. |
| **A central sampled score gate** | [Chen et al. 2025, PASS](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v267/chen25h.html) study stochastic data substitution with an information-theoretic privacy objective. [Cai et al. 2024](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v244/cai24a.html) optimize randomized quantization by LP for an **unbiased differential-privacy** formulation. PCRL's earlier ACS and Adult Team A designs already used finite stochastic token channels. | A uses frozen Adult two-task scores, a fixed calibrated **global** public probability bank, at most four same-class choices per recipient, and a small **central two-score** gate over at most sixteen joint actions. Cai's DP/unbiasedness requirements are different from A's empirical SEX-recovery endpoint. A lead over a serious same-bank deterministic arm is bounded evidence for this stochastic policy; over central product and both freeze-first design orders, bounded evidence for its **joint design procedure**. Neither result establishes stochastic-channel or joint-sampling novelty. |
| **A/D sequential controls** | [Taylor, Vippathalla and Coon 2026 v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21859v2) formulate online collusion-aware sequential releases, protecting the record with mutual-information constraints. Their BA-style algorithm has global expected-distortion scope in its specified finite known-law problem and only local scope for mutual-information utility. | A's controls freeze a first learned score release before fitting the second; D's controls freeze a first hard-code release before second-stage training, while keeping deployment local. It cannot prove that joint output laws are inherently more expressive than unrestricted sequential sampling or inherit Taylor's optimality. |

## Boundaries that must travel with any result

1. **The changed information is the public wire.** D must evaluate transmitted hard code identities and every code-determined output; A must evaluate the sampled globally canonical code, fixed probability/decision and all metadata. Internal soft bits, private fine cells, teacher scores, unsampled means and private coins are not the release. A leaked action/source-specific ID or row-varying flag changes the law and invalidates a code-only objective.
2. **Objective values are not attack guarantees.** D-K's finite biased MMD is nonnegative and can be zero locally for an XOR coalition clue; the aligned product kernel detects that clue. Small finite empirical MMD does not bound held-out attacker AUC. A's fitted-law Bayes `G_v=Σ_o max(P_v(SEX=0,o),P_v(SEX=1,o))` is exact for the **specified finite law on public IDs**, but neither estimates population recovery without uncertainty nor equals a fresh finite-reader AUC. The learned A gate optimization is nonconvex; successful local optimization is not a global optimum.
3. **A's utility cost belongs to the sampled vector.** Exact fitted expected NLL is `Σ_z q(z|x)[-log p_z(y)]`; exact fitted Brier is `Σ_z q(z|x)||p_z-e_y||²`. Both differ from loss at `Σ_z q(z|x)p_z`. Expected FIT feasibility does not imply every cached realization or INNER people meet caps. The decision-only privacy floor can be utility-ineligible.
4. **Input advantages must be matched.** A's provider sees both scores, unlike the earlier local `Q_r` service. Central product and freeze-first controls need the same score inputs; local controls answer a narrower locality question. D deployment maps must each use only their own latent, even when training uses aligned pairs. Public mechanism weights and fixed action maps are attacker side information. Publishing a new FIT-derived fine-state prior/table would add public model context and requires prospective disclosure/attack treatment; the current A specification does not include one.
5. **No novelty inference from a positive comparison alone.** A full empirical claim needs utility-eligible strongest controls, fresh complete-view attacks, paired seeds/realizations, honest uncertainty and independent confirmation beyond repeatedly reused Adult development rows. Exact aliases are ties. If a simple control wins, report it. Neither family currently has a generic algorithmic novelty claim.

The paper/author links above are primary sources. [MMD-B-Fair official code](https://github.com/namratadeka/mmd-b-fair), [SoFaiR author code](https://github.com/Gitiauxx/SoFaiR), and [Bertran author code](https://github.com/MartinBertran/AIOI) were identified from their paper pages. None of the named external implementations is a drop-in matched Adult two-recipient score/latent control; any adapted implementation must disclose its change of inputs, output wire, utility metric, protected variable, optimization scope and capacity. This review does not treat an absent direct code comparison as evidence of priority.
