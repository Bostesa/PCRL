## Promotion rule for ledger rows

1. **Inherited** means pinned predecessor protocol/result or established primary method; state its original service and population.
2. **Newly implemented** requires an exact source/lock hash, unit receipt and successful deployment restore. Mark drafts as drafts.
3. **Tested task-feasible** requires both tasks, both references and every paired screen seed at the declared point gate. Interval status is a separate field.
4. **Demonstrated matched recovery benefit** requires complete local/pair views, fresh fixed attacks, all eligible mandatory controls, paired uncertainty and independent replay; record the exact weakest clause if it fails.
5. **Explained by compression** applies when equal-capacity task-only T or deterministic control ties or wins. Keep that useful simpler release.
6. **Joint or randomness claim** requires its specific structural ablation and both stated sequential design orders where applicable. Do not infer it from a central arm beating an unmatched local control.
7. **Untested** covers missing permitted input, no fit, missing attack, incomplete control, failed technical gate or budget cut. Name the reason and unit IDs.
