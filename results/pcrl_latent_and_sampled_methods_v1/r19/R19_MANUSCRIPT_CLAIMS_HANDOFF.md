# R19 manuscript and claims handoff

**Role:** R19 integration and claims ledger. **Use:** scoped handoff to R00; the shared `RESEARCH_DECISION.md` and R04 independent receipts govern any manuscript text. This is exploratory development on repeatedly used Adult people. The old assessment was not opened, and no new confirmation population was used.

## Claims the evidence supports

- **Real feature-code tradeoff:** Two paired seeds yielded actual ten-bit task-only T and kernel-repaired D-K releases, code-derived task outputs, and fresh complete-view recipient and coalition attacks. D-K's pair SEX AUC was `0.805905/0.793778`, versus T's `0.832582/0.835960`. T-minus-D-K was `0.026676/0.042181` by seed, equal-seed mean `0.034429` with simultaneous 95% interval `[0.013719, 0.055138]`. This is a descriptive recovery reduction; its lower bound does not reach the registered `0.020` benefit target.
- **Utility cost:** D-K's mean occupation NLL excess over U0 was `0.032253 [0.010368, 0.054137]` against the `0.010` cap. Its mean occupation Brier excess over Ucal was `0.014797 [0.006994, 0.022600]` against the `0.005` cap. Both lower bounds establish violations. All six ten-bit D arms fail the full both-task/both-reference point utility gate. The continuous F control passes `24/24` task point clauses on its wider wire, but `9/36` simultaneous utility clauses remain unresolved and its pair AUC is higher (`0.869335/0.862196`).
- **Bounded sampled-channel no-go:** Fourteen of sixteen A score-channel searches completed without a feasible selected policy. Both reverse sequential jobs ended after infeasible stage-one starts and did not complete stage two. No protected A policy or sampled protected release was selected. All 18 *fixed historical/diagnostic* attack cells had fresh readers and independent full-view/AUC replay. T* pair AUC was `0.846745/0.829728`; inherited P* was `0.802702/0.803579` and met point task caps in both seeds; class-only was `0.736103/0.734125` but failed utility. These fixed cells provide context, not an A sampled-method comparison.

## Claims to withhold

| Claim | Boundary shown by this campaign |
| --- | --- |
| Competitive D method or kernel benefit | D-K and every capacity-matched ten-bit control fail the full point utility gate; there is no eligible hard-code method comparison. C has lower pair AUC than D-K in both seeds, also with worse utility. |
| D joint-design benefit | Local L and both sequential orders were measured, but no utility-eligible hard-code set supports attribution. |
| A competitive, joint or randomness benefit | No protected sampled policy reached release or attack. Fixed references and class-only diagnostics cannot stand in for a selected protected policy. |
| Feasibility or privacy theorem | A is a bounded search with two incomplete reverse sequential fits. The D intervals condition on fitted models/readers and reused development people. Neither finite-reader AUC nor a sampled-law objective proves information removal, differential privacy or population protection. |
| Fresh confirmation | Both seeds reuse the same `2,235` INNER people (`2,234` groups); prior development and Ucal family selection already exposed these outcomes. A new lock, agent count and paired bootstrap do not restore independence. |

**Paste-ready claim:** “On reused Adult development rows, a ten-bit kernel-repaired purpose representation reduced measured coalition SEX recovery relative to a matched task-only ten-bit release, but violated occupation confidence limits. A separate bounded sampled-score channel search selected no feasible protected policy. Neither experiment established a competitive, joint-design or randomness advantage.”
