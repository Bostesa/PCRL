# R19 public queue-status proposal helper

Run `python3 results/pcrl_latent_and_sampled_methods_v1/r19/queue_unit_status.py` from the campaign worktree to print a **proposed**, read-only CSV snapshot of R03's current enqueued units. R00 owns the shared `UNIT_STATUS.csv` and decides when to integrate rows. The helper does not call the scheduler, launch jobs or alter queue state.

The only CSV columns are `record_kind`, `record_id`, `unit_id`, `charge_id`, `stage`, `status`, `direct_child_cpu_s`, `charged_cpu_s`, `wall_s`, `sampled_tree_rss_peak_bytes`, and `receipt_sha256`. `record_kind=unit` covers every enqueued unit, including failed and superseded attempts. A finished unit's hash is recomputed from its R03 `RECEIPT.json` after checking unit identity, stage, status and the four resource values against queue state. A superseded unit's `SUPERSESSION.json` bytes, identity and hash are verified; its old CPU charge is included. Queued, held and running units have blank finished-resource and receipt fields. `record_kind=charge` covers each separate R03 ledger charge, with `charge_id` populated and `unit_id` blank. Its immutable receipt reference is hash-checked without publishing its path. `charged_cpu_s` is R03's conservative charge, which may equal elapsed wall time rather than process CPU.

The exporter constructs every output row from an explicit whitelist. It never emits `argv`, `cwd`, source or output paths, per-person data, private logs, PID data, hold reasons, or output-path keys. It validates safe IDs and known stages/statuses; inconsistent or incomplete state rejects the entire snapshot with no CSV output and a generic error. Unit and separate-charge rows stay distinguishable, so a setup charge cannot be mistaken for a scientific fit. R00 still reconciles the overall CPU ledger and scientific planned/run/skipped/aliased accounting.

The CSV is a live proposal, not a campaign closeout receipt. Rerun it after queue changes; do not treat an earlier queued or held row as a final scientific unit status. The helper verifies the unit receipt inventory against queue state, so an unaccounted final/supersession receipt fails closed. Its three targeted synthetic tests and read-only live runs passed without private path markers. The queue changed from 59 to 60 total unit/charge rows during R19's work; do not freeze a count from this draft. No full research test suite or numerical fit was run for this integration helper.

For **planned A units that have no queue row**, use the separate [220-unit A plan join](A_220_STATUS_HELPER.md). It distinguishes ten null-policy descendants from other planned controls while this general helper preserves historical failed attempts and separate charges.
