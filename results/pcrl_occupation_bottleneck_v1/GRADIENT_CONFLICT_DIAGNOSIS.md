## Interpretation and next test

The evidence supports batch-dependent LOCAL_TRAINING_CONFLICT and leaves SURROGATE_MISMATCH as a competing explanation. It does not identify a causal population bottleneck, a zero-gradient problem, a universal task/privacy trade-off or a failed nomination rule that concealed a verified useful winner.

The pre-INNER next choice is a direct occupation-only safeguarded refinement feasibility test from RAW with income frozen, using the implemented actual-displacement QP and finite NLL/Brier checks. Its controls must include unchanged RAW, ordinary continuation, a matched local twin and task-only continuation, plus row-space disclosure for interpretation. A new identity/lock is required because the present automatic trigger was unmet. The test asks whether heterogeneous adverse updates can be handled; it does not assume that a stable conflict has already been proved.
