# Advisor brief

The diagnostic campaign supports a practical interface change and a specific next training test, while the original competitive target remains unmet. Predictions alone recover SEX strongly; additional features substantially worsen income-recipient recovery. Occupation recovery remains high after ordinary compression. The explanation for the training trade-off is mixed: adverse sensitive-versus-task directions occur on some fixed batches, but favorable directions recur too.

This is an exploratory assessment on repeatedly used Adult development people. Models saw all FIT, the motivating source comparisons were known, and both fitted seeds share the same people. INNER labels opened only after the reader, reporting and supplementary applicable-reader freezes. No method was chosen or retrained using these assessment outcomes.

## Answers to the research questions

**Where is the leak?** Both predictions and features. For RAW .3 epoch 5, income SEX AUC falls from 0.751910 with the full wire to 0.689458 with the projected wire. Occupation falls from 0.838102 to 0.828298, and pair recovery from 0.856824 to 0.846138. RAW's logits/output-only occupation and pair AUCs are already 0.821197 and 0.841485. Its decisions alone have pair AUC 0.723088. These finite reader measurements distinguish interfaces; they do not decompose mutual information or establish a universal privacy floor.

**When does occupation usefulness pay for protection?** The first saved transition, epoch 0→5, already raises occupation NLL/Brier in both RAW seeds. At strength .3 the FIT2 NLL increases are 0.005611 and 0.004915; Brier increases are 0.002673 and 0.002334. Mean confidence falls in both seeds at this first transition. Costs continue at later saved states; confidence itself is subsequently nonmonotone. Across the fixed core paths, the independent replay places the first observed task-loss cost at epoch 5. This is coarse checkpoint timing and uses diagnostic FIT2 values, not an independent assessment gate.

**Was a useful repair opportunity found?** Ordinary head-row-space disclosure is a useful task-preserving baseline. It preserves actual decisions and probabilities to the declared tolerance and substantially lowers local income recovery. It retains strong occupation/pair prediction leakage. Its effective dimensions are one and five for the two heads, so this is ordinary compression under a narrower future-task contract. A new privacy-training or equal-capacity representation result was not established.

**Did the pilot run?** No. Status is NOT_TRIGGERED_DIAGNOSIS_UNRESOLVED, with zero defense fits. Only two of four prelocked starting-checkpoint batches were adverse, below the three of four trigger. The alternative interface trigger required pair reduction at least 0.015 in both ATT seeds; the actual reductions were 0.005787 and 0.017258. The pilot implementation was engineering-tested, but it has no Adult repair result and no earned advantage over controls.

**How far are the anchors from the target?** Each RAW/JOINT anchor and its projection passes all24 original point utility requirements. Each nevertheless has 5 PASS / 19 UNRESOLVED / 0 VIOLATION under K864 simultaneous utility bounds. In supplementary all-applicable-reader K18, RAW projection gains 0.010686 [0.000388, 0.020984] pair AUC versus full RAW; the point shortfall to0.020 is0.009314 and the target is UNRESOLVED. JOINT projection gains0.008585 [−0.001563, 0.018732], a SHORTFALL. Full JOINT worsens income recovery by0.063252 [0.045004, 0.081501], exceeding the0.010 guard. Later/stronger RAW states obtain lower recovery while becoming point-ineligible; historical RAW remains a diagnostic, ineligible control.

**What should change next?** The pre-INNER choice is a directly authorized occupation-only safeguarded refinement experiment from RAW, with income frozen. Test actual rounded parameter displacement and finite occupation NLL/Brier, preserving the implemented QP safeguard. Compare unchanged RAW, ordinary continuation, a matched local twin and task-only continuation; retain ordinary row-space disclosure as an interpretation comparator. Frame it as a feasibility test of handling heterogeneous adverse updates. Give it a new identity and locks because this campaign's automatic trigger was unmet. The new study must earn benefit over its strongest eligible matched control and preserve both local guards; favorable results would be an exploratory repair lead, with general joint-design and novelty claims requiring further evidence.

## Evidence and limits

The mandatory pool contains72 packages,36 paired configurations. The campaign performed1818 new reader fits and reused486 exact readers; four independently fitted U0/RAW anchor readers were also run. The independent implementation replayed all72 FIT2/INNER useful-head metrics, all36 point gates, frozen recovery envelopes, label tables, all-release forwards, fixed gradient tensors and the three interval families. Real-data control gates passed for the frozen dependencies.

Keep the families separate: original utility K864, original base9 privacy K108, and supplementary all18-reader anchor privacy K18. The supplemental audit strengthened U0-projection pair recovery and JOINT-projection income recovery; it did not change the original registered family. Each uses2048 shared duplicate-group draws, seed2026101301, and Bonferroni Student-t bounds. Utility preserves seed identity; privacy averages both seed contrasts inside each shared draw. Bounds are approximate conditional coverage for fixed models/readers on this exploratory population.

C3 true-label tables trained on FIT01 achieve INNER AUC 0.604363 for income,0.670317 for occupation and0.750508 for the true label pair. They quantify association and remain interpretation-only. Sparse true-task strata are unavailable under the frozen32-groups-per-SEX rule. Labels never enter recipient wires or primary attacks.
