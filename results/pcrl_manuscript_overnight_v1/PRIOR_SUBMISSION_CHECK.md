# Prior-review appendix: does it apply?

SaTML 2027 requires that a paper previously submitted elsewhere append that venue's reviews, anonymized
but unedited, with a description of how the feedback was addressed.

**Determination: it does not apply, on the records available.**

What exists in this repository and was checked:
* `MOCK_REVIEWS_2026-07-24.md` — three reviews written by **fresh agents acting as reviewers**, explicitly
  described in the file itself as mock/context-free. Not a venue. Not usable as prior reviews, and
  presenting them as such would be a fabrication.
* Those mock reviews are also about a **different paper** ("Outputs Leak What They Use", AAAI27 draft 38),
  not this manuscript.
* `results/reviewer_dropins/` — internal paper-paste material for that other project, not reviews.
* No HotCRP/OpenReview identifier, submission number, decision notice or reviewer report for **this**
  manuscript was found anywhere in the repository.

**Therefore:** no prior-review appendix is included, and none may be invented. If the author knows of an
actual prior submission of this manuscript, that changes the answer, and the reviews must be appended
verbatim and anonymized.
