# Audit budget, saved observers and composed witnesses

Increasing the audit trajectory from 120 to 360 epochs changes three selected bank endpoints and no main-model endpoint. It changes one limited SEX ranking, but no predeclared numerical inequality or feasibility flag. The larger difference comes from admitting each main model's inherited observer: catch-up wins 34 of 48 pooled main-model selections at either budget. These are distinct comparisons on DEVELOPMENT EVALUATION, not evidence of privacy.

## The two budgets are nested

Every new final release has the same five independent candidates: logistic regression, two fresh 64/32-ReLU MLP initializations, and the two fixed boosted-tree configurations with minimum leaf sizes 20 and 5. Each MLP runs one 360-epoch trajectory; the best validation checkpoint through epoch 120 and through epoch 360 are retained separately. Main C/D releases also have their own direct-coordinate observer catch-up trajectory, with Adam reset and epoch zero eligible. Banks have no observer or catch-up candidate. Logistic/tree candidates are unchanged between budgets.

All choices use unweighted attacker-validation log loss, with the original deterministic ties. PWGTP scores exactly those same selected predictions. Actual terminal model, Adam and NumPy schedule states at epochs 120 and 360 are saved separately from selected states. Independent replay checked 168 nested trajectories and 336 terminal checkpoints; 333 terminal states differ from their selected states. Thus the 360-epoch runs do not rewind to an earlier validation winner and call that a continuous trajectory. See [SCORE_REPLAY.json](SCORE_REPLAY.json), [FITTING.csv](FITTING.csv) and [all learning curves](CURVES.csv).

## What changed at 360

The candidate IDs stay the same in all three changed selections; only the selected checkpoint changes. Losses are nats. Lower loss means better attribute recovery. The listed development and PWGTP changes are 360 minus 120.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---:|---:|
| E bank | SEX | MLP 1 | 120 → 130 | .688858 → .688446 | .684104 → .683769 | −.000335 | −.000210 |
| S bank | SEX | MLP 0 | 105 → 195 | .681585 → .680269 | .686070 → .687952 | +.001882 | +.001121 |
| S bank | RAC1P | MLP 1 | 120 → 220 | 1.259184 → 1.257903 | 1.266989 → 1.264255 | −.002734 | −.000041 |

Seven individual bank MLP candidates change their selected predictions, including these three selected endpoints. Four changes do not win their bank/attribute selection. All main-model candidates, not just their winners, retain the same selected predictions at both budgets; a separate archive check found all 672 validation/development prediction-array pairs byte-identical. The 24 changed selected rows counted in [summary.json](summary.json) are these three endpoints × four scoring splits/weightings × two selectors; banks' independent and pooled selectors coincide because catch-up is unavailable. They are not 24 distinct changed experiments. [AUDIT_BUDGET.csv](AUDIT_BUDGET.csv) retains every candidate, split and weighting, including unchanged and unavailable entries.

The worse development result for S-bank SEX creates a small apparent lower-recovery ranking reversal. Among the ten new release types, its mean unweighted independent SEX loss rises from .682142 to .682769, crossing E-K-C (.682587) and E-K-D (.682310). Its apparent signed gain falls from .010545 to .009918; E-K-C and E-K-D stay at .010100 and .010377. The corresponding seed-0 ordering also changes. This is a validation-selected checkpoint generalizing worse on development, not evidence that extra fitting must reveal more information. No other per-seed or mean ordering changes among the ten new release types in the development/PWGTP, independent/pooled comparisons.

No source/residential condition, attribute-halving numerical inequality, feature-versus-bank numerical inequality, combined numerical condition, support flag, or defined/undefined assessment changes between budgets, across either selector or weighting. This was checked against every paired budget record in [criteria.json](criteria.json) and [bank_comparisons.json](bank_comparisons.json). Original PCA32 remains the 120-epoch parent; its allowance and denominators are unchanged. Direct E/S teachers, PCA16 and the unprotected rich banks retain compatible historical 360-epoch evidence. Historical full-input models remain contextual references, not substitutes for the newly matched F controls.

## Saved, fresh and catch-up are different evidence

At either budget, catch-up is the pooled winner for 34 of the 48 main release/seed/attribute endpoints. The standalone `saved_adversary` row is diagnostic and excluded from selection. One pooled catch-up winner, E-K-D / seed 0 / RAC1P, selects epoch zero: its development loss is 1.208921796 before and after catch-up. Its advantage over the fresh candidate cannot be attributed to additional catch-up optimization. All main-model catch-up candidates choose checkpoints by epoch 20; all main independent winners choose by epoch 50. Their subsequent fitting does not improve the observed validation selections.

Each saved observer already saw the representation-fitting protected labels during 20 observer warmup passes plus three updates per mapper step over 80 continuation epochs: 260 row passes along the evolving representation. Catch-up adds the declared attacker-fitting trajectory on the final frozen release. D also receives the real-protected-label mapper gradient; C does not. E is already label-informed through its frozen eraser. These exposures are recorded separately in [SOURCE_EXPOSURE.json](SOURCE_EXPOSURE.json), [CATCHUP.csv](CATCHUP.csv) and candidate metadata. Independent-only comparisons remain the shared audit scope across teachers, main releases and banks; pooled results add inherited observer exposure only where that observer exists.

This distinction matters to the decision. For E-C, K reduces mean independent race gain from .056080 to .051189, but pooled race gain changes from .074529 to .077666. For E-D the independent gains are .055042 versus .051889, while pooled gains are .075159 versus .075270. A lower independent gain therefore does not establish a lower pooled race endpoint.

All 456 K-C/K-D/bank candidate witnesses are also retained as teacher-only compositions. The 24 global teacher/seed/attribute/budget choices were frozen from validation losses before their development scores were read. Full prediction arrays reproduce the stored-release paths. The composed family adds mapper/source-head depth and source-label training; D and inherited observers add distinct protected-label exposure. It is an expanded teacher witness family, not a matched-budget replacement for the teacher's direct audit. See [COMPOSED_ATTACKS.md](COMPOSED_ATTACKS.md) and [the frozen choices](COMPOSED_SELECTION.json).

## Controls and unresolved limits

The fitting-prior and exposed-label controls are reused from the matching [historical nested audit](../redesign_20260908_acs_preservation_v1/extended/seed_0/metrics.json), with [seed 1](../redesign_20260908_acs_preservation_v1/extended/seed_1/metrics.json) and [seed 2](../redesign_20260908_acs_preservation_v1/extended/seed_2/metrics.json). Priors are unchanged; signed gains remain prior loss minus attack loss, including negative gains. The exposed control reaches perfect observed SEX classification and perfect observed race classification in seeds 0 and 1, but does not repair absent support. RAC1P code 4 (zero-based class index 3) is absent from attacker fitting and validation in every seed. It is absent from development in seeds 0 and 1; seed 2 has one such person, and exposed-label recall is zero at both budgets, including PWGTP scoring. Its very high overall accuracy and class AUC do not establish recall for that unsupported category. The full nine-category assessment remains unassessable.

Within these fixed trajectories, the selected main endpoints are stable and the changed bank checkpoints occur by epoch 265. This does not establish global audit saturation: other function classes or training paths could recover more, and longer fitting cannot manufacture missing category support. No 720-epoch pass, new restarts, new attack family or additional fitting search was launched.
