# Exact purpose coordination — definitions frozen before enumeration

This is a finite rational calculation, separate from ACS fitting. It requires no learned representation, sampled data, random draw, numerical library, or fitted attacker. It is not a novelty claim. The source, this protocol, and `purpose_coordination_exact_config.json` are hashed before calculation by `scripts/verify_purpose_coordination_exact.py --freeze`; the calculation refuses a mismatched freeze.

Let U,V be independent uniform signs and S=UV. One paired issue releases R1=U N1 and R2=V N2. The joint noise law is public and independent of U,V; its realized noise and the ground-truth task signs are not disclosed. Purpose1 receives only its R1 history and may estimate U; purpose2 receives only its R2 history and may estimate V. The coalition receives both complete histories and may estimate U,V, but S remains prohibited. Exact task recovery in both views contradicts this coalition prohibition because their product is S.

The fixed laws, in (++),(-+),(+-),(--) order, are: independent `(9/16,3/16,3/16,1/16)`; coordinated `(1/2,1/4,1/4,0)`; noiseless `(1,0,0,0)`. The first two have identical marginal error1/4. “Independent” means product randomization within each paired issue, not every independently designed channel. No law, marginal, or expected value will be changed to fit the result.

Enumerate k=1,2,4 paired issues for the SAME fixed U,V. Under **fresh**, draw noise pairs independently across time using the chosen within-pair law. Under **cached**, draw once and return the identical pair on every repeated request. These are issuance policies, not privacy claims under arbitrary side information or practical guarantees of reliable caching. No unpaired calls, restarts, changing task bits, extra noise seed disclosure, or other access is part of the calculation.

For each of18 law/policy/k cases preserve exact rational P(U,V,history) and P(S,history), including an explicit zero-cell convention. Compute coalition Bayes accuracy `sum_history max_s P(s,history)`; compute each authorized Bayes decoder using ONLY its own history. Verify individual-history independence from S using full joint probabilities, not covariance. Verify the two authorized histories have the same marginal task channels for independent/coordinated laws. Check the full joint by an independently implemented inverse-likelihood enumeration over histories and U,V, in addition to forward latent/noise enumeration.

Fixed expectations, checked rather than imposed on calculations: fresh independent coalition accuracies `(5/8,5/8,377/512)`; fresh coordinated `(1/2,5/8,107/128)`; noiseless1. The two noisy mechanisms' authorized own-history accuracies are `(3/4,3/4,27/32)` under fresh draws. Cached repeated access supplies no additional information: accuracies remain their k1 values. Every individual history is independent of S. Coordinated k1 coalition independence must hold for its full joint distribution; repeated fresh coordination is not assumed to preserve it.

For arbitrary purpose decoders with errors q1,q2, their product gives coalition accuracy at least `max(1/2,1-q1-q2)` when S is balanced. Verify the disjoint-error construction `(1-q1-q2,q1,q2,0)` attains this bound for every admissible pair on the predeclared grid `{0,1/8,1/4,3/8,1/2}²` with q1+q2≤1/2. This is15 fixed pairs, not a parameter search. Save full grid joint distributions, utility errors, bound and equality checks.

Floating point is used only to print decimal conveniences and place the SVG plot. No approximation is used to pass a check. Exact enumeration stops after these18 history cases and15 grid cases.
