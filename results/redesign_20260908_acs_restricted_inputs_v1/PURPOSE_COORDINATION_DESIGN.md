# One proposed experiment: jointly constrain two purpose interfaces

**Proposed only; not executed.** The next finite experiment would test whether a coalition-aware training objective improves two purpose interfaces, and whether learned feature outputs earn their extra interpretation burden against equally coordinated prediction outputs. The exact sign calculation already establishes that coordinated predictions can improve a declared coalition tradeoff. It gives no reason to require learned representations. Its fresh-repetition reversal also rules out assessing a repeated-access policy using one-call scores alone.

The smallest useful empirical question still missing is whether either purpose needs a *reserved member of its task family* that a strong coordinated source-prediction bank cannot support at comparable utility and attribute recovery. Existing ACS tasks can falsify that requirement on this cohort. They do not establish that a real recipient needs a reusable feature interface. If the reserved tasks show no advantage, this experiment should end with prediction outputs as the sufficient interface for this benchmark, not a new task or coefficient search.

## Stipulated benchmark policy and access

Use exactly the existing sampled ACS cohort, seeds 0/1/2, original household pools and original PCA32 inputs/preprocessing in all four learned conditions. Keep the saved E teacher as a fixed direct control. E was fit with representation-fitting SEX/RAC1P labels and is not guaranteed to remove their nonlinear recovery. No new state/year, households, features or outcome definitions are needed. All reported test outcomes remain **DEVELOPMENT EVALUATION**.

The following grouping is an explicit research policy, not a claim that these labels identify natural institutions or an established deployment requirement.

| Recipient | Authorized family | Labels used to train its utility objective | Reserved utility label, used only after releases freeze | Forbidden recovery from this recipient's release |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A: economic/residential benchmark | Income, civilian employment, residential stability | Income >50k; civilian employed and at work | Same residence | Both B-family targets: public coverage, commute >20 minutes; additionally SEX and RAC1P |
| B: coverage/mobility benchmark | Public coverage, commute duration | Public coverage | Commute >20 minutes | All three A-family targets: income >50k, civilian at work, same residence; additionally SEX and RAC1P |
| A+B coalition | Union of the five authorized tasks | No new coalition utility labels | Both reserved tasks remain unavailable to representation fitting | SEX and RAC1P |

The two source-label sets form a partition of the existing three binary source tasks. Each purpose's opposing reserved target is an audit target only after the releases freeze.

An individual receives its own frozen interface and public inference rule. The coalition receives both interfaces for the same person, their purpose/version identifiers and the public joint policy. Neither receives raw inputs, E coordinates separately, unreleased fitted-model hidden states, same-person true task labels, or a randomization seed. Model parameters may be public: the auditor may compose their deterministic inference with a released interface when that interface supplies all required inputs. Fit-time training labels and their exposure are disclosed separately from information delivered about an evaluated person.

This access restriction matters. In the sign example, exact U and V predictions imply exact S=UV for a coalition. If a future task policy has the same logical implication, no mechanism can both authorize those exact answers and hide that S. The present ACS policy instead asks for a measured approximate tradeoff; correlation can impose a cost even without a known deterministic implication. None of the finite margins below supplies an arbitrary-side-information guarantee.

## Four fixed learned conditions, plus direct controls

Run only these four paired conditions for each of the three existing seeds:

| Condition | A/B released interface | Individual protection gradient | Additional coalition SEX/RAC1P gradient |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| F-separate | Each branch's 16 feature coordinates | Yes | Zero; coalition observers train diagnostically |
| F-joint | Each branch's 16 feature coordinates | Yes | Yes, into both branches |
| P-separate | A's two / B's one positive-class source probabilities | Yes | Zero; coalition observers train diagnostically |
| P-joint | A's two / B's one positive-class source probabilities | Yes | Yes, into both branches |

P-joint is the mandatory **coordinated prediction-only** comparator. Comparing F-joint only with P-separate would conflate interface type and coordination. F-joint versus F-separate is the primary intervention; P-joint versus P-separate establishes whether the same benefit needs a feature interface at all. All four models receive the same input/label information and have the matched forward-model capacity specified below. No independent baseline is forbidden from knowing the other purpose's policy, schedules or training specification: “separate” means absence of the coalition objective, not forced ignorance of public design information.

Include the frozen direct E interface for each recipient, with coalition input `(E,E)`. It is a fixed teacher control, not a matched trainable-capacity arm; duplicating E creates no second independent information source. Also show existing unprotected neural/tree source banks and the current three-probability source-only banks as named references. Their richer label schemas or different parameter budgets remain explicit, so they cannot substitute for the matched four-condition comparison. No new teacher, beta, architecture or noise-law sweep is part of this proposal.

## Matched inputs, capacity, initialization and updates

Both branches accept **the same original PCA32 input**, standardized using the original saved PCA32 fitting means/scales. No reconstruction target, skip connection, private extra feature or teacher side channel is supplied. A branch uses a 32→64 ReLU→16 mapper. A has exactly two affine binary source heads, for income and employment; B has exactly one, for public coverage. Every condition fits exactly these same three source heads. The prediction interface is precisely A’s two positive-class sigmoid probabilities and B’s one probability, with public order `[income_binary,civilian_at_work]` and `[public_coverage]`. There are no duplicate task heads, hidden coordinates or auxiliary labels in the prediction interface.

Each branch has 3,152 mapper parameters. A adds 34 head parameters and B adds 17, totaling **6,355 trainable forward parameters** in every condition. F recipients get 16 float32 scalars each; P recipients get two and one respectively. Coalition wire widths are therefore 32 and three. This difference is the interface intervention, not a claim of a matched transmitted-information budget. Input features, fitting labels/rows, total forward-model capacity, optimizer exposure and numerical precision are matched. Released entropy, mutual information, numerical rank and ease of decoding need not match. Report output widths and ranks explicitly. An apparent feature benefit can simply reflect retaining more information for a future task; that becomes useful only if the fixed reserved-task and recovery requirements are met.

Before any fitting, freeze original PCA32 and direct E map/preprocessing identities and one set of starting mapper/head tensors per seed. Initialize each mapper to the original deterministic raw-PCA16 function: first-layer weights `[I32;−I32]`, zero first bias, output weights selecting the first sixteen coordinates with the saved scales and opposite signs, and output bias equal to their saved means. Verify numerical parity on the fixed fitting/source-validation rows. All 32 input coordinates have a trainable route, including the initially unused final sixteen output readouts. Use the same initialization for both purposes and all four conditions; reuse the genuine original initialization checkpoint’s three source-head tensors, assign income/employment to A and coverage to B, and clone those exact assigned tensors to every condition. Record checkpoint and tensor hashes rather than regenerating them after constructing the wider training graph. Verify equal initial F-separate/F-joint released functions and equal P-separate/P-joint released functions, not merely equal distributions of weights. F and P intentionally expose different functions of these same tensors; do not claim parity between feature values and probabilities.

Fit the common two-branch source warmup **once per seed** for 60 epochs, then clone it to F and P interfaces. Within each interface, run 20 observer-only epochs, freeze the complete mapper/head/observer/Adam state and clone it to separate/joint continuations. This gives byte-identical starting states and batch schedules within each causal pair. The F and P forward-model tensors also match before their interface-specific continuation gradients. F and P observe different released values, so their observer states after warmup may differ; that difference is part of the declared interface comparison. Source-label masks and optimizer exposures remain equal. Reuse the current mapper batch-schedule helper and its seed argument unchanged. For observer initialization, fix target order `(SEX,RAC1P,income_binary,civilian_at_work,public_coverage,same_residence,commute_over20)` and view indices A=0, B=1, coalition=2; use seed 20260911+100·study_seed+10·view_index+target_index, keeping it identical across all conditions that fit that target/view.

The per-branch utility loss averages its distinct source tasks: two for A, one for B. The base loss averages the two purpose losses; this deliberately gives each purpose equal weight, identically in every condition. Adam is .001, batch 256, original defaults, with 80 continuation epochs and the fixed final iterate. There is **no preservation or reconstruction term** in any of the four conditions. The raw PCA16 release is the common starting function. Direct E is a fixed erased-teacher control, not a fitting target. Direct teacher comparisons are contextual because the learned arms receive the larger original PCA32 input; the matched F/P comparison does not have that information-access difference.

Train 64/32 ReLU observer adversaries on each individual interface for its opposing *source* labels and SEX/RAC1P: three targets for A and four for B. Train two coalition observers on the concatenated interfaces for SEX/RAC1P. All four conditions have the same nine target/view observer roles, three detached observer updates per forward-model update and the same row/batch budgets. Hidden widths and optimizer schedules match. Input dimensions differ with the interface (16 versus two/one individually; 32 versus three jointly), so report actual observer parameter counts rather than claiming identical auditor parameter totals. The ordinary individual protection term is −.1 times the mean prior-entropy-normalized CE within each purpose, averaged across purposes. The extra joint term is −.1 times the mean normalized SEX/RAC1P coalition CE in joint arms and zero in separate arms. Record each coefficient and exposure separately. Observers for the opposing reserved tasks are fit only during the post-freeze audit; their labels never enter mapper training.

Coordination here is the shared coalition-gradient objective acting on both purpose outputs. There is no private shared random seed, stochastic dropout, additional shared encoder, extra labels or fresh randomization at inference. Public initialization and batch-seed reuse are matched experimental controls, not a secrecy resource. The causal question is consequently about joint objective design, not the benefit of a hidden shared-noise construction.

## Utility, coalition auditing and repeated access

After both interfaces of each condition are frozen, fit the existing independent logistic/MLP utility candidates with the same 2,048-label caps, fitting/validation households, 40-epoch MLP schedule and validation-selection rule. A utility heads may use only A's output and B heads only B's output. Evaluate all five authorized tasks; reserved residence and commute scores must not select a mapper, seed, epoch, coefficient or interface width. Keep each fixed native source-head score separate from these independent probes, with no post-hoc choice of native versus probed output. Individual forbidden-task auditors never receive the other interface; coalition auditors always receive the complete concatenation.

Use the current fixed independent audit families: logistic, two fresh 64/32 MLP initializations with nested 120/360-epoch checkpoints, and the two boosted-tree settings with minimum leaves 20/5. Every saved training observer also receives the declared direct-coordinate catch-up, with Adam reset and first-prediction fidelity verification. Epoch zero is eligible inside catch-up; a standalone saved-observer candidate is only diagnostic. Independent, saved, catch-up and pooled results remain separate, because inherited fitting exposure differs. Extend the same independent candidates to each opposing reserved target only after frozen releases, using the original attacker pool and masks. Preserve prior/exposed controls, all candidates and all nine RAC1P categories; absent categories stay unassessable. Extend the existing fresh-audit seed rule to 1260000+100·study_seed+10·view_index+target_index, with the existing +10000 second-restart offset; use 1300000+100·study_seed+10·view_index+target_index for catch-up. Across all conditions, row schedules for a given view/target are identical. The existing fitting/validation checkpoint rule and preprocessing are unchanged.

The proposed issuance policy is one deterministic, version-fixed A/B pair per evaluated person. For k=1,2,4 calls, return the same respective value. Preserve and audit the whole individual or coalition history and verify exact duplicate identity. Since the history is a deterministic copy of the first pair, a first-pair attack can be replayed exactly on every history and no additional randomness accumulates. This is an explicit benchmark policy, not an operational claim that caching solves repeated-release privacy. New model versions, changing person values, independently refreshed outputs or arbitrary additional releases are outside this experiment; publishing them would require a different access audit. The separate exact calculation already demonstrates why fresh paired noise cannot inherit this conclusion.

## Decision rule and the strongest alternative explanation

The primary outcome is the paired change in coalition SEX and RAC1P signed gain from F-separate to F-joint, under fixed authorized utility requirements. Lower gain means worse predictive recovery. Report each seed, means/sample SDs, raw task losses and same-prediction PWGTP sensitivity. Use independent 360-epoch selection as the cross-interface primary audit and catch-up-inclusive results separately. Keep the existing original-PCA32 source allowance of .01 nats and the existing residential-retention denominator and margins; no favorable weighting, support omission or denominator replacement can make an arm pass.

Evidence for a *representation-specific* benefit requires all of the following, assessed descriptively rather than as a privacy or noninferiority test:

1. F-joint reduces coalition recovery relative to F-separate without violating the fixed source and residential utility requirements, and does not merely move forbidden recovery into an individual output.
2. Each purpose's reserved task has at least a .01-nat log-loss advantage over **both** its P-joint interface and the direct E interface, on the same independent utility candidates and labels. Apply the same test beside PWGTP; retain raw failures. This extends the existing feature/bank advantage magnitude to a predeclared second reserved task, not a retrospective commute-retention denominator.
3. F-joint does not exceed the corresponding coordinated-prediction/direct-teacher coalition gain by more than .005 nats for either attribute under matched independent audits; pooled comparisons must not overturn an apparent benefit without being disclosed. Full race feasibility remains unassessable if the original support gap persists.

If the teacher or prediction bank already supports these two reserved tasks, lack of an F advantage is a substantive negative answer. If F wins only against small downstream probes but not against composed controls or stronger prediction banks, the strongest alternative is finite fitting/conditioning advantage rather than additional information. Every F/P output is a deterministic function of the same original PCA32 input with frozen public parameters, so it cannot create information absent from that input about a new example. Unlike the completed K arms, these proposed functions are not generally computable from E alone; do not present their auditors as composed E witnesses. A reusable feature interface might change practical fit efficiency; that is the claim to measure.

The existing ACS policy has not demonstrated a natural, consequential future task family that needs representations. If both reserved advantages fail, the exact missing condition is a documented family member for which coordinated predictions/direct teacher are insufficient at matched utility and recovery. This fixed five-task comparison is the smallest available test of that condition in the present cohort. The proposal does not authorize inventing another task, collecting another dataset or launching a follow-up sweep after it fails.

## Relation to existing evidence and prior work

The [exact calculation](PURPOSE_COORDINATION_EXACT.md) supports a coordination question independently of ACS: one-call dependence design can help even with matched marginal channels, while repeated fresh access can reverse its ranking. It does not establish novel theory or the need for representations. The completed restricted-input experiment motivates choosing original PCA32 for **every** arm of this proposed comparison: every new K condition fails the unweighted all-source floor in all three seeds. For E-C, restricting F to K raises mean source losses from .2949/.2920/.4986 to .3242/.3562/.5129 (income/employment/coverage). A teacher-only experiment would inherit a demonstrated admission-floor problem before testing coordination. E-K-C does beat its source-only bank on residence (.505432 versus .521901), but not commute (.687176 versus .686863). Its smaller independent race gain also reverses against F-C under pooled audits: .07767 versus .07453. These are measured tradeoffs, not evidence for a general preservation or protection success. [Current tables](TABLE.md), [research decision](RESEARCH_DECISION.md).

The K composition result establishes that source training can make a function of E more accessible to an auditor without creating information absent from E. It does not resolve useful representation access beyond source predictions: the current advantage is residence-specific and source floors fail. Nor does this completed study contain two actual purpose interfaces. The next matrix therefore changes the scientific question to joint purpose objectives with equal original inputs and retains P-joint as the central alternative explanation, rather than adding a K/F factor or another preservation coefficient.

Prior work already studies adversarially learned transferable representations, including separately trained classifiers on held-out tasks after freezing an encoder. The reserved-family requirement therefore needs empirical evidence against competitive prediction outputs, not a novelty claim from adversarial training alone. [Madras et al., Algorithm 1 and §6.2](https://www.cs.toronto.edu/~zemel/documents/laftr-icml.pdf). Conditional information-leakage composition distinguishes unconditional independence from access conditioned on earlier outputs; the exact sign calculation uses a particular declared target rather than maximal leakage over all targets. [Issa, Wagner and Kamath](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1807.07878). Longitudinal randomized-response work analyzes permanent versus changing reports with explicit qualifications; the deterministic repeated-access policy above is narrower. [Erlingsson, Pihur and Korolova](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1407.6981).

Execute seed 0, then 1, then 2. Within each seed freeze the common base, train the F-separate, F-joint, P-separate and P-joint continuations in that order, and freeze all four A/B pairs before evaluating reserved utilities. Then complete their utility and audit evidence in that same condition order. Do not pick a subset of favorable seeds or weaken an auditor if the budget becomes tight.

This is one proposed twelve-pair learned matrix: four conditions × three seeds, plus fixed direct/reference controls. Before any future execution, freeze this task/access matrix, source dependency hashes, exact initial tensors, seeds, output orders, audit budget and a 60-minute scientific-compute / four-hour total-work ceiling with complete ordered units and explicitly partial reporting if necessary. Do not launch it as part of the completed overnight study.
