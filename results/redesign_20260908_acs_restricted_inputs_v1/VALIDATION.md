It does not count 24 distinct changed release/attribute endpoints. [AUDIT_BUDGET.md](AUDIT_BUDGET.md) identifies the changed checkpoints, limited SEX ranking reversal, unchanged predeclared inequality/feasibility flags, inherited catch-up exposure and unsupported race category.

Teacher map algebra, paired permutations, complete-case supports and cross-covariance diagnostics were replayed from the original artifacts without refitting LEACE. Small float64 covariance residuals and the declared analytic projection rank do not erase published float32 residuals or establish nonlinear privacy. The affine replays use original PCA16 scales, fitting-only intercepts, rank tolerance 10⁻¹², and undefined ratios for negligible fitting/evaluation variance or prior error. qE is not pure protected information; high affine error does not rule out nonlinear recovery.
