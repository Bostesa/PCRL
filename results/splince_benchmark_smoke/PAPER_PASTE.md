## Section 5.3 paragraph (paste-ready)

To situate the variance-constrained PCRL trainer relative to a contemporary closed-form baseline, we replicate the head-to-head from Holstege et al. (NeurIPS 2025) by applying SPLINCE — their oblique projection that erases linear concept predictability while preserving the cross-covariance with a target task — to the same PerPurposeLoRAEncoder backbone our paper uses, with one projection fit per (purpose, sensitive attribute, seed) cell on the same training fold. Across the 8 cells of the variance-constrained benchmark grid, SPLINCE achieves linear-erasure compliance on 8/8 cells under our $R^2_\text{1H} < 0.05$ certificate, against 8/8 for the PCRL R5/R7 baseline; tightening the criterion to also require a post-hoc auditor margin of $\Delta_\text{aud} < 0.02$ takes SPLINCE to 5/8, and the full strict criterion that further demands a per-dimension standard deviation $\bar\sigma_\text{dim} \geq 0.5$ and effective rank $\geq 2$ leaves SPLINCE at 0/8. This last drop reflects the canonical SPLINCE failure mode — when the covariance subspace of the sensitive attribute and that of the task label are nearly aligned, the oblique projection becomes ill-conditioned and the projected representation collapses toward a one-dimensional manifold even as $R^2_\text{1H}$ goes to zero. We saw 1 such cells fall back to the LEACE limit by our $\mathrm{cond}(U^\top V) > 10^6$ guard. Linear-probe task accuracy on the same SPLINCE-projected representations degrades by a mean of 7.4 percentage points relative to the unconstrained backbone, with the worst single-cell drops on the purposes whose primary task and sensitive attribute share covariance directions (most notably employment\_analysis/race on Adult and underwriting/race on HMDA). Read together, the comparison says that the closed-form path can match or beat PCRL on the linear-erasure ledger when the geometric alignment is benign, but pays for that erasure with a representation that the rank-and-variance health checks reject; PCRL, by contrast, trades a slightly higher $R^2_\text{1H}$ ceiling for a representation that satisfies all four certificates simultaneously.
