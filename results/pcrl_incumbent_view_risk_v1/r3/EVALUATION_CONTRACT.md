## Complete simultaneous family

The immutable family has K=948 potential two-sided intervals:

- 192 seed-specific exact-law utility slots: 8 arms × 2 seeds × 2 tasks × 2 references × 3 metrics.
- 756 recovery difference slots: all 28 unordered arm pairs × 3 views × 9 summaries (six seed/draw, two within-seed three-draw means, one equal-seed grand mean).

Every possible overall nomination, strongest-control comparison, local guard, claim A/B/C and structural attribution is an orientation of these pair slots. Both directions share the same interval by sign reversal. Excluded, aliased, unrun and incomplete arms retain their slots; K never shrinks. Equal-seed utility means and realized utility are descriptive only, without extra simultaneous intervals.

Use 2048 paired duplicate-group bootstrap draws with PCG64DXSM(20261010); the inherited INNER count is 2234 duplicate-group IDs and 2235 people. Draw one group-index array shared across arms, views, references and seeds. Sample groups with multiplicity, retain every person within a selected group, and use person-weighted means. Fixed draw recovery and exact utility condition on the fitted law/readers and use group resampling only. Three-draw recovery means additionally resample one whole-release tuple of three realization IDs per seed and bootstrap replicate, shared across every arm/view. The same people remain paired across seeds. Deterministic arms broadcast their single release; no duplicate attack fits or independent-population claim.

Intervals are point ± t critical × paired-bootstrap standard deviation, alpha=.05, critical t(1-alpha/(2K),df=2233) for group-only quantities and df=2 for three-draw recovery means. With three releases, mechanism-variance precision is coarse. Utility PASS: accuracy lower >= -.010; NLL/Brier upper <= .010/.005. VIOLATION: accuracy upper < -.010 or loss lower > cap. Otherwise UNRESOLVED. These conservative approximate intervals are conditional development summaries, not post-selection confirmation.

## Nomination and eligible controls

Checkpoint choice is FIT/internal only. Before final attacks select one overall V-family, the same family for both seeds, excluding P0 and O_J. Among families with all24 original point clauses and complete internal-reader receipts, minimize equal-seed mean sensitive-CE gap `CE_constant_prior - min(CE_retained_pair_readers including constant_prior)`. Constant priors are fitted on their allowed training folds; CE is evaluated out of fold on all FIT people, aggregating the three fixed duplicate-group-disjoint folds by actual action law; the exact fold manifest hash is pinned in each reader receipt. Exact ties use V_J,V_P,V_D,V_L,V_S12,V_S21. If all otherwise eligible families are exact P0 aliases on both seeds, retain P0 and mark claim A untested. No INNER SEX or final attacker result selects that nominee.

V_J is the separate fixed joint nominee when point eligible. Claim A compares preselected overall V nominee with freshly attacked P0, benefit lower >= .020 and local worsening upper <= .010. Claim B compares O_J minus V_J pair AUC, lower strictly >0, while preserving original utility and local guards. Claim C is a conjunction over EVERY eligible required V_P,V_D,V_L,V_S12,V_S21: each pair benefit lower >= .020, each local guard upper <= .010, and candidate/control original utility conjunctions. An unavailable required sequential order blocks a joint claim as INCOMPLETE or UNTESTED. The strongest point control is reported but does not substitute for all required simultaneous bounds. O_J is also reported as a required objective control. Strongest-control selection means smallest equal-seed mean pair AUC in the preregistered eligible pool, ties by the fixed full arm order. All possible pool members and contrasts were included before scoring. Overall competitive reporting considers every other eligible fixed arm, excluding the nominee itself.

A local guard is candidate AUC minus P0 AUC and candidate minus the actual required comparator, upper <= .010. Pair benefit is control minus candidate. Missing, control-failed or ineligible comparisons are UNTESTED/INCOMPLETE; a point-eligible candidate with unresolved utility bounds is not a complete pass. Identity ties are aliases, not gains. Claims have separate PASSED/NOT_ESTABLISHED/UNTESTED/INCOMPLETE statuses.
