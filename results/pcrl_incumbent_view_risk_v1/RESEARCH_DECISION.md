# Research decision

Exact P0 preserved useful predictions and confidence at the point estimates in this narrow development search; simultaneous confidence preservation remains unresolved. Complete-view training did not establish the required improvement. No objective, joint-design or randomness component earns competitive empirical credit. Raw83 deployment, independent numerical replay and snapshot-only restore are complete; fresh-population confirmation and repeated-query protection remain untested.

## Tested comparison

The registered comparison used two paired source seeds and eight arms: exact frozen P0, inherited-objective O-J, complete-view joint V-J, product V-P, separately hard-trained deterministic V-D, local V-L, and freeze-first sequential V-S12/V-S21. Fourteen real policy fits ran. V-D returned exact P0 in both seeds after its full training budget; this is a tie, not protection progress. The remaining twelve fits selected nonidentity policies. No support, threshold, architecture or checkpoint sweep followed the outcomes.

V-L was nominated using the fixed FIT reader rule before fresh attacks. The eligible control pool and every contrast were frozen before attack scoring. Its strongest control under the predeclared equal-seed/draw point-AUC rule is V-S21. V-J remained the separate joint diagnostic. All arms use the score disclosure track; no feature-sharing claim is made.

## What earns evidence

The mechanical diagnosis demonstrates two preceding restrictions: the W_TSTAR bank/support excluded every exact P0 probability state, and P0 failed six of twelve old B0-relative proxy clauses robustly while preserving original point utility. No observed old-input conflict was found; that does not prove learnability or population routing. The inherited finite-law Bayes objective already uses actual public codes. The new actual-wire reader package is a tested implementation of established constrained-reader ideas; no new solver or information-removal theorem is claimed.

The study supplies a reproducible, well-scoped negative for this conservative neighborhood and optimizer budget. It establishes exact incumbent restoration, useful point-eligible channels, complete-view attack machinery and honest comparator selection. It supplies no population impossibility claim.

## Closeout and next step

Close this particular objective/support recipe. The next different hypothesis should test a wider class-preserving output support, with the same useful references and explicitly measured utility cost, to isolate the demonstrated support limitation from an objective change. This is a later design recommendation, not a promised gain or automatic extension.
