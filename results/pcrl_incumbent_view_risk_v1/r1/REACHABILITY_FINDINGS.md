# P0 reachability findings

The new queued source admission restored the exact historical P_STAR
incumbent from raw83 for both source seeds. Every ATT and INNER original
public coarse ID, probability, decision and metadata-bearing JSON wire hash
matches the historical R12 release. The actual historical `hcal.deploy`
CLI matches the new route on the fixed 32 FIT rows for both recipients.
This establishes executable source identity, not independent utility or
privacy evidence. R3 and R5 own those separate gates.

## Old policy inputs

No identical old continuous-score input on the admitted FIT, ATT or INNER
rows required different P0 outputs, for either seed or either recipient.
There were 4,234 repeated central-score rows on FIT, 579 on ATT and 217 on
INNER, with zero conflicting input keys. Local income-score repeated rows
were 4,298/601/230; occupation counts were 4,234/579/217. These counts are
per source seed and are not distinct populations.

The old central gate received both frozen U score vectors plus their
deterministic entropy/margin summaries; its private support lookup used
fine cells routed from those scores. The fixed P0 router also routes from
those same complete scores. Consequently the old inputs mechanically
determine P0's routing. This observation does not show that the bounded
shared gate can learn or optimize that map. It does not rule out further
capacity, optimization or numerical restrictions.

## Old bank and bounded actions

| Source seed | P0 income states absent from old bank | P0 occupation states absent from old bank |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 14 / 14 | 270 / 270 |
| 1 | 13 / 13 | 299 / 299 |

Here equality means exact frozen float64 probability bytes, including their
strict decisions. Every admitted row's required P0 probability state was
also absent from the old per-row support: 15,434 FIT, 4,064 ATT and 2,235
INNER rows per task and seed. The old bank-channel space could not emit
exact P0 probability states under its fixed decoder. Original complete
wire identity was additionally impossible under its different ten-bit
code and metadata contract. No approximation error lower bound, privacy
impossibility, or exclusion of a different beneficial channel follows.

The new support is one fixed identity-plus-three-nearest same-class
neighborhood on P0's original global IDs. Adding this bank and changing
the anchor alters the mechanism space; O-J and V-J receive both changes.
A comparison with the old W_TSTAR experiment cannot isolate the objective.
All 14/13 income tokens have four actions in seeds 0/1. Occupation has one
singleton class token and 269/298 four-action tokens in seeds 0/1. Thus
the paired action list has at most 16 entries; a pair involving the
occupation singleton has four. These counts are fixed table properties,
not observed person-level frequencies or evidence of utility feasibility.

## FIT proxy and independent source replay

R3's separate fixed-row calculation found exact P0 violates six of twelve
old B0-relative FIT cost caps. Seed 0 fails occupation NLL and Brier; seed 1
fails both tasks' NLL and Brier. Its original-reference INNER point gate
passes all 24 clauses across both seeds. The former is a historical
incremental-proxy restriction; it does not invalidate the newly specified
P0-relative candidate limits. R5 independently replayed P0 routing and
source costs and reported PASS, including its numerical precision checks.
These results still do not assign the prior campaign's outcome to the
objective alone. R2 separately recovers the inherited objective and its
actual-wire reader counterpart.

No HEAD labels, old assessment, task labels or SEX labels were read by this
R1 mechanical admission. New artifact array exports are role-only and
remain private; public receipts carry hashes and aggregates.
