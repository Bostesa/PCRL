# Reachability and old fitting-budget audit

Exact P0 is the historical H-CLASS-TEMP release, independently restored from raw83 predictors, with original IDs and full wire preserved. No partition, decoder or teacher was refitted.

## Policy inputs

On every admitted FIT, ATTACK_FIT_NEW and INNER row, independently inspected duplicate old continuous-score inputs have zero conflicts in the P0 outputs they require. This does not prove learnability or population representability. The old router consumes those frozen scores; there is no demonstrated input impossibility on these observed rows.

## Output support

All P0 probability states (14/270 seed0;13/299 seed1) are absent from the old W_TSTAR bank, and every admitted row's bounded old support lacks its exact P0 state. This demonstrates exact action-space exclusion; it does not rule out approximation. Code and metadata contracts also differ, so probability-state support and complete wire identity are reported separately.

## Objective and assessment

The inherited optimization minimizes empirical finite-law Bayes SEX accuracy G_pair+.5(G_r1+G_r2), summing SEX action mass per actual global public state/tuple. Fixed probabilities, decisions and within-unit metadata are functions of those IDs. It is a fitted-law objective on known FIT people, not a bound on fresh nonlinear AUC. The previous study's best new product arm improved fresh pair AUC by.005951 and joint did not beat it, while P0 was stronger. Those receipts do not isolate an objective cause and no causal regression is fitted.

The new comparison changes the anchor and bounded neighborhood for BOTH O-J and V-J. It then tests a cross-fitted actual-wire CE reader package versus the inherited G objective under common guards, support, update capacity and point utility targets. Known restrictions are exact action exclusion and old proxy exclusion. Optimizer/generalization and the effect of the objective remain empirical questions.
