# Manuscript claims handoff

This follow-up is exploratory development on repeatedly used Adult rows. It does not revise the locked empirical paper claim ledger, create a new confirmation cohort, or open the old assessment. The prior latent-and-sampled study and this campaign must retain distinct source pins and result labels.

## Admissible statements

- Two inherited frozen-score public banks, compiled as global ten-bit code IDs including predicted class, met all 24 current INNER point task utility caps against U0 and Ucal. They show that the stated score-routing service can retain useful confidence at ten bits on these rows. They are not a new purpose-feature representation.
- A newly fitted teacher-guided latent hard-code learner failed the sealed two-seed utility gate at both ten and sixteen bits (13/24 and 21/24 point clauses). This is a recipe-specific failure. It is not an information-capacity lower bound.
- The full fixed family used 2,376 simultaneous conditional development clauses. The 144 estimable utility clauses had 41 PASS, 103 UNRESOLVED, and zero bound VIOLATION; 2,232 dependent clauses were not estimable. These intervals do not undo the predeclared point-based stage stop.
- The prior sampled-channel identity audit found no admitted exact identity that simultaneously satisfied the old fitting-domain utility constraints and common-role point criteria under the original stochastic parameterization. The fixed bank action did pass common-role INNER point utility but failed FIT occupation NLL. No impossibility theorem follows.

## Claims this campaign does not support

No new kernel privacy comparison, fresh SEX attacker result, competitive method improvement, joint-design advantage, stochastic channel win, or confirmation claim was produced. The R3/R4 downstream fit and attack units were stopped at their declared dependencies. Historical kernel AUC contrasts belong to the earlier study and must retain that study's citation and development status.

The paper may use this as an engineering and design diagnostic: a public score code can preserve task confidence on reused rows, while the tested latent mapper did not find an equally useful service under the locked training recipe. A new bank-targeted latent learner would require its own prospective design record and gate before privacy fitting.
