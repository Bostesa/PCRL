# PCRL code utility witness: start here

This is a bounded exploratory study on reused Adult data. The old assessment and new HEAD_VALIDATION labels stayed closed. Its result is a useful **historical public-code witness**, plus a precise failure of the new latent learner's utility prerequisite. It does not establish a new privacy or joint-design method.

## What was tested

1. Recomputed the old U0/Ucal/F/T/D-K task losses and restored two inherited score-routed banks on the same admitted development rows. Both `W_Q` and `W_TSTAR` transmit a ten-bit global code per recipient (16 income states, 321 occupation states including class) and meet all 24 point utility caps. They are inherited mechanisms, not new latent repairs. See [loss table](LOSS_DECOMPOSITION.csv) and [bank witness](BANK_WITNESS.json).
2. Fitted one teacher-guided hard-bit latent learner at the two sealed capacities, ten and sixteen bits, on two paired seeds. Ten bits passed 13/24 utility point clauses; sixteen bits passed 21/24. The three sixteen-bit misses are seed-one occupation NLL versus U0/Ucal and Brier versus U0. See [task-only table](TASK_ONLY_UTILITY.csv) and [capacity decision](CAPACITY_LADDER_DECISION.json).
3. Applied the full 2,376-clause simultaneous development inference family. Of 144 estimable task utility clauses, 41 passed and 103 remain unresolved; 2,232 other clauses are null because their dependent arms or privacy attacks did not run. Independent replay matched all estimable bounds. See [interval table](UTILITY_INTERVALS.csv) and [verifier receipt](r5/UTILITY_INTERVALS_INDEPENDENT_REPLAY.json).
4. Audited the prior sampled-channel identity. Its exact bank identity passes the common INNER point utility check but fails the old FIT occupation NLL constraint in both seeds. No new channel search was admitted. See [identity audit](r2/R2_UTILITY_AND_CHANNEL_AUDIT.md).

## Decision

The locked ladder required every task, seed and U0/Ucal point clause to pass before new kernel privacy training. Neither learned capacity did. No new kernel, local, sequential, ordinary adversarial, or fresh SEX attack fit ran. Thus this follow-up has no new competitive privacy result and no joint-design attribution. The [research decision](RESEARCH_DECISION.md) gives the strongest favorable and adverse comparisons and the next study design.

The public source, hash pins, frozen tables, fitted private service packages and strict recipient-local deployment adapters are preserved. The [quickstart](QUICKSTART.md) describes actual commands and restore scope. Models, per-person samples and releases stay private. A local snapshot and off-device status are recorded in the custody receipts when closeout completes.
