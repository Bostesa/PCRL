# Proposed only: history-aware protection for one genuine model refresh

**One finite next method experiment; not launched.** The present study finds a conditional SEX benefit from coalition training, but race orderings depend on legal access and B retains considerable forbidden recovery. Its repeated-output checks contain no new values. The next useful question is whether protection trained on the *complete permitted release history* limits additional disclosure when an already issued model is replaced. Existing disclosure cannot be retracted by training the replacement.

This is a test of incremental disclosure under a specified refresh policy. It is not a proposal to solve the current race-support gap, to claim a representation advantage over prediction delivery, or to establish a deployment need. The observed residence advantage over P motivates retaining a feature comparison; no analogous .01-nat commute advantage has been established.

## Falsification, limits and stopping

The strongest alternative explanation is that apparent coordination benefit reflects the effective observer input/function change or stronger local pressure, rather than useful control of new joint disclosure. Matching initial functions and stored capacity, retaining J_current/Iplus_history, and comparing real F/P outputs make those explanations reviewable without claiming to eliminate every one. V0 already contains recoverable attributes; the objective cannot lower the true recovery floor imposed by retaining it. A lower selected development score alone cannot demonstrate removal of its information.

A useful result would reduce incremental history recovery under the declared complete audit pools, preserve the measured authorized utility, and survive the stronger local history comparison in a clearly specified task/attribute sense. A result that only changes a fresh auditor, harms source utility, moves leakage between individual and AB views, or loses its apparent benefit after saved/public/projection candidates enter is a negative or conditional result. Full race support and privacy remain unresolved even if numerical references pass. Three optimizer replicates are descriptive conditional on one cohort and one V0, not population uncertainty.

This is the smallest refresh test with a genuine second release, a current-only baseline, a targeted stronger-local control, and a matched prediction interface: one fixed old version, one new version, three fixed regimes, two interfaces, no extra dimension/coefficient/data factor. Measure the first complete replicate before expansion, predeclare a 90-minute scientific and four-hour total ceiling if this design is later authorized, and stop after the fixed matrix. No automatic third version, new attacker family, support-repair dataset, or follow-up sweep. **No part of this proposed learned study has been executed.**
