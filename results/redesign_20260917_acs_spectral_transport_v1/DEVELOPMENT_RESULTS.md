# Development results (California 2018; exhausted cohort; not confirmatory)

Source: the completed development study [`redesign_20260910_acs_residual_spectral_v1`](../redesign_20260910_acs_residual_spectral_v1/DECISION_REPORT.md) (commit `ad2c088`). The prompt called for 14 interfaces x 3 seeds = 42 interface instances; all 42 were evaluated there, 24 of them newly fitted spectral maps and 18 recovered historical systems (not retrained). Every table below is generated by `scripts/summarize_spectral_development.py` from that study's committed aggregate CSVs (`PUBLICATION_EVIDENCE/PER_SEED.csv.gz`, `PAIRED.csv.gz`). The 2018 cohort had already been used in full by earlier studies. These are **development** numbers; three seeds are three fitted systems on one cohort, not population replications.

All endpoints are in nats. "Residence gain" is H's selected residence probe loss minus the interface's (higher is better). "Additional recovery" is the interface's absolute recovery (prior loss minus selected attack loss) minus H's, in the same scope, seed and weighting (lower is better; negative values are retained). Main scope: `kernel_expanded_catchup` at budget 360, which contains the common random-feature kernel family, all legal H and singleton projections, public auxiliary heads, and inherited catch-up attacks.

## Answers to the development questions

1. **Does reconstruction-driven S0 retain more reserved-task capability than the old source-head-trained channel at comparable capacity?** No material difference. S0 − A0 residence loss is −0.0001 unweighted and −0.0000 PWGTP (seed values straddle zero). Both are 16-coordinate channels, but S0 is float64 and A0 is float32 promoted losslessly. S0 also leaks slightly more SEX/race than A0 (A/race +0.0040 unweighted, +0.0092 PWGTP).
2. **Do conditional penalties help beyond marginal penalties?** Partly. At equal strength, L1 − M1 lowers A/SEX (−0.0094), AB/SEX (−0.0042) and A/race (−0.0093) recovery in every seed, but raises AB/race recovery (+0.0033 unweighted, +0.0050 PWGTP) and costs about 0.002 nats of residence. The marginal penalty itself (M1 − S0) mainly reduces race recovery (A/race −0.0180, AB/race −0.0159).
3. **Does C1 improve on both L1 and equal-total-mass L2?** Against L1, yes on every sensitive column, in every seed for A/SEX, AB/SEX and A/race, at a residence cost of 0.0023 / 0.0018 nats. Against L2, which applies the same total penalty mass to local terms only, the advantage nearly vanishes: AB/SEX −0.0003 / −0.0009 and AB/race −0.0006 / −0.0004, with mixed seed signs. The only consistent difference is A/race (−0.0053 / −0.0055), and residence is worse by about 0.002. The prospective coordination criterion failed. Most of C1's apparent coalition benefit over L1 is explained by its larger total penalty.
4. **Does spectral C1 offer a better option than frozen neural J?** No. C1 gains 0.0050 / 0.0047 nats more residence than J, but recovers substantially more SEX and race: A/SEX +0.018, AB/SEX +0.009 / +0.007, A/race +0.020 / +0.017, AB/race +0.017 / +0.013. Some of J's low additional recovery comes from attacks that generalize worse than H (J's A/SEX increment is negative: −0.0047 / −0.0052), so part of J's apparent protection is a selection artifact rather than a demonstrated property.
5. **Do apparent improvements survive the common nonlinear scope and the historical catch-up scope?** The seed-mean C1 − L1 sensitive-recovery difference is negative in all 96 scope × budget × weighting × endpoint cells (A/AB SEX and race, development `PAIRED.csv.gz`). The kernel family did not change any selection shown above. The unequal historical exposure remains: spectral arms have no saved observer, so their catch-up scope contains only routed H catch-up attacks.
6. **Are gains bought by worse local race or another role?** Relative to L1 and L2, C1 lowers local (A) race recovery. Relative to J, every spectral arm has much higher local race recovery (0.027–0.066 vs 0.007). The legacy source-probe allowance failed for every spectral arm in at least 4 of 6 seed × weighting cells (development `DECISION_REPORT.md`), even though the exact service outputs are unchanged.
7. **Does withholding of a simpler channel explain the frontier?** Under the full five-utility, six-sensitive directional rule at δ = .001, spectral arms dominated a withholding mechanism in 0 of 960 fixed comparisons, and withholding dominated a spectral arm in 6 of 960. No withholding mechanism dominated a spectral recipe in all seeds and both weightings. Utility and leakage move together along the withholding curves, so neither family is robustly better on the full vector.

## Development conclusion

Residual preservation adds residence capability beyond H: every spectral arm clears 0.01 nats in every seed and weighting, and S0 reaches about 0.032 nats, the largest of any interface. The conditional/coalition penalty lowers measured recovery relative to its equal-strength local control. It does not beat the equal-mass local control L2, and it leaks far more than the neural J channel under the same attack families. No spectral recipe met the prospective nomination rule. The locked 2017 transport tests whether these orderings, including J's apparent advantage, hold on a new survey year.
