# Adding a reusable representation beside a fixed prediction service: what conditional erasure buys, and what it does not

**Draft, 2026-09-17.** Research branch `ablations-facct-2026-07-24`. Every number in this draft is generated from the result files listed in §11 or checked against them.

## Abstract

An organization already publishes a prediction service: fixed probability vectors that downstream recipients consume. We ask whether it can add a *second*, reusable representation for one recipient without changing a single published number, while limiting what that recipient — and a coalition of recipients — can newly infer about sex and race. We study a residual spectral channel: it keeps the teacher information that the fixed service outputs do not explain, and chooses directions by maximizing linear reconstruction of that residual minus conditional-moment penalties, where the conditioning is on the *released service probabilities* and is estimated by cross-fitted nuisance models. Penalties can be local to a recipient or specific to a coalition's joint view. We evaluate eight penalty settings against five frozen adversarially trained channels and a no-channel baseline on California ACS, with independent post-hoc attacks (logistic, restarted MLPs, boosted trees, random-feature kernel ridge), and then transport the entire frozen 14-interface slate to a locked, previously unused survey year (2017). Three findings. (i) Exact service preservation is structural and holds bitwise on every released vector; it does not imply stable service accuracy after a year's shift. (ii) The coalition-conditioned penalty does what it claims: on the locked new-year evaluation, C1 reduces additional sensitive recovery against both its equal-strength local control and an equal-total-mass local control, under simultaneous 95% intervals and both weightings, at a residence-utility difference within the prespecified 0.001 band — a criterion the development year had failed for lack of resolution. (iii) The method still loses: under identical attacks, every spectral arm leaks several times more sex and race than a frozen adversarially trained channel of the same width, buying about 0.009 nats of extra residence utility for 0.013–0.038 nats of extra recovery. We report this as a qualified tradeoff and a negative method result, with the mechanism, its finite-sample scope, and the evaluation design offered as the contribution.

## 1. Contribution in two sentences

A service that already publishes predictions can hand one recipient extra features built only from what those predictions leave unexplained, and can steer those features away from directions that would let that recipient — or that recipient plus a colluding one — guess sex or race better than the published predictions already allow. We show the steering knob works on a fresh year of data, and that it is nonetheless not competitive with an adversarially trained channel of the same size, so the useful output of this study is the evaluation design and a negative method result rather than a new deployable mechanism.

## 2. Threat model and interface

Each person has covariates X, sensitive attributes S (sex, 2 classes; race, 9 classes) and task labels. Two frozen services exist: H_A(X) ∈ [0,1]^4 (income > $50k and employment probability vectors) released to recipient A, and H_B(X) ∈ [0,1]^2 (public coverage) released to recipient B. A mechanism may add a channel Z ∈ R^16 for A only. Views: A sees (H_A, Z); B sees H_B; the coalition AB sees (H_A, Z, H_B). The policy authorizes A for income, employment and residence, B for coverage and commute, and forbids sex and race everywhere, coverage/commute for A, and income/employment/residence for B — eleven forbidden roles in total. The adversary holds labeled data from the release year, fits the fixed attack families on its own pool, selects on a validation pool, and sees only its legal view; every published function, including the preprocessing, the spectral map and the neural arms' public auxiliary heads, is in scope, and coalition attacks may use any legal projection of A's and B's candidates.

## 3. Method

Let T be a frozen PCA32 teacher. Build phi(T) = [standardized T, 96 random Fourier features] (Gaussian kernel, median-heuristic bandwidth) [@rahimi2007random]. Let q_A be the intercept and all degree-2 monomials in A's two positive-class service probabilities; q_AB adds B's. Residualize by SVD least squares: V0 = phi − q_A B_phi and R = T − q_A B_T, then eigen-whiten V0 to V with V'V/n = I. Utility is U = C_VR C_RV: for W'W = I, tr(W'UW) is exactly the training reduction in least-squares reconstruction error of the residual teacher. Protection matrices use cross-fitted (household-grouped, 3-fold) multinomial-logistic nuisances m_j and, for each retained basis column b_k,

  G_jk = (1/n_j) Σ_i V_i b_k(H_i) (onehot(S_j)_i − m_j(H_i))ᵀ,  P_j = Σ_k G_jk G_jkᵀ (trace-normalized),

averaged over local roles (basis q_A: sex, race, coverage), coalition roles (basis q_AB: sex, race) and a marginal variant (constant basis). The released map is the top-16 eigenvectors of U_norm − λ·P, giving arms S0 (λ=0), M025/M1 (marginal), L025/L1/L2 (local) and C025/C1 (local + coalition). Inference needs only T and H_A; H_B enters fitting and auditing only. Z is appended to H_A, never mixed into it, so the published probability vectors are released bitwise unchanged.

## 4. Mathematical scope (what is a theorem, and what is not)

The eigenvector step is Ky Fan's maximum principle for a *fixed* empirical matrix [@fan1949weyl]; it certifies nothing about the choice of features, basis, nuisances, rank or attacker, and with a fixed r = 16 it can retain directions whose penalty exceeds their utility. The reconstruction identity min_D ||R − ZD||²/n = ||R||²/n − tr(W'U_raw W) is exact under whitening and orthonormality. The moment condition is a finite subset of the L2 characterization of conditional independence used by kernel CI tests [@zhang2011kci; @strobl2019rcit]: vanishing *fitted* moments over one basis and linear functions of Z do not establish conditional independence, and can reflect nuisance misspecification. Two counterexamples are carried in the code as fixtures: an XOR channel with zero marginal but perfect conditional recovery, and a zero-first-moment channel from which thresholding recovers S exactly. With population-optimal log-loss predictors, the H-versus-(H,Z) risk difference equals I(S;Z|H); our validation-selected finite attacks do not attain those infima, so reported differences bound it in neither direction — indeed some are negative — and MI estimation has its own formal limits [@mcallester2020formal]. A separate finite-source linear program certifies exact conditional privacy for a known small distribution; it is a diagnostic, not an ACS guarantee. For the withholding control, an independent visible branch B ~ Bernoulli(p) gives I(S; B, Z_B | H) = p·I(S;Z|H) and expected routed loss (1−p)L_H + p·L_HZ. Full statements and proofs: `METHOD_AND_SCOPE.md`.

## 5. Relation to prior work

The closed-form line of Sadeghi and Boddeti and co-authors already solves exactly this shape of problem: SARL [@sadeghi2019sarl] takes eigenvectors of a utility-minus-λ-dependence matrix, K-TOpt [@sadeghi2022ktopt] adds random Fourier features, and U-FaTE [@dehdashtian2024ufate] adds a *conditional* penalty (stratified on a discrete label, evaluated on Folktables ACS data [@ding2021retiring]). Our marginal arms are, up to parametrization, SARL's linear construction on whitened random-feature residuals. What differs here is what we condition on (continuous *released service predictions*, through cross-fitted nuisance residuals rather than label strata), what utility means (reconstruction of a residualized teacher rather than dependence with a label), and the recipient structure (separate local and coalition penalties, with only one recipient receiving a channel). We did not find prior work combining these three, but a bounded search cannot establish novelty and we make no priority claim. Linear concept erasure [@belrose2023leace; @holstege2025splince] addresses a different guarantee (exact linear erasure) on a representation the releaser owns, and least-privilege learning has known fundamental limits [@stadler2024leastprivilege]; adversarial removal is known to leave recoverable information [@elazar2018adversarial]. See `PRIOR_WORK_AND_NOVELTY.md` for the verified reference-by-reference relation and the contribution table.

## 6. Experimental protocol

California ACS one-year PUMS, ages 19–34. Development: 2018, 30,000-person cohort, three household splits (seeds), pools for representation fitting, source validation, task fitting/validation, attacker fitting/validation and development evaluation. 14 interfaces per seed — H (no channel), E, A0, L025, L20, J (frozen adversarially trained channels recovered without retraining) and the eight spectral arms — i.e. 42 interface instances, of which 24 are the new spectral maps, frozen before any reserved-label evaluation. Attacks: logistic, two restarted MLPs at 120/360 epochs, two gradient-boosted trees, and a random-feature kernel-ridge family, plus every legal projection of H's own attacks and of singleton candidates into the coalition view, plus, for the neural arms, their public auxiliary heads and saved-observer catch-up trajectories. Utility probes are fitted after the representation freeze. Both unweighted and PWGTP-weighted scores are computed on identical selected predictions.

Transport: California ACS 2017, admitted on provenance and schema alone, with a label-blind household hash partition (attacker fit 25%, attacker validation 15%, task fit 25%, task validation 15%, final evaluation 20%) fixed before any prediction existed. Mode A applies everything frozen. Mode B refits only probes, priors and attackers on 2017 fitting pools and selects on 2017 validation pools; representations, protection matrices and nuisances are never refitted. Before the final partition could be read, a lock hashed 17,639 inputs — protocol, comparison families, code, all interface objects, all 2017 fitting/validation releases, every fitted object and selection — and was committed and pushed. Uncertainty: paired household-cluster bootstrap (2,000 replicates over the 10,701 final households), with single-step max-|t| simultaneous intervals inside each predeclared family. Full protocol: `PROTOCOL.md`; data admission: `DATA_ADMISSION.md`.

## 7. Results

**Development (2018).** All eight spectral arms exceed the 0.01-nat residence reference in every seed and weighting; S0 reaches 0.032. No arm met the prospective nomination rule, and the coordination criterion failed: C1 beat its equal-strength control L1 on every sensitive endpoint but was indistinguishable from the equal-total-mass control L2 (AB/SEX −0.0003, AB/race −0.0006 unweighted). Full tables: `DEVELOPMENT_RESULTS.md`.

**Transport (2017), fresh attacks.** Residence capability transports for every augmented interface (spectral 0.025–0.030 nats, J 0.017–0.019, all seeds and weightings above 0.01). The coordination criterion now passes: C1 versus L1 gives AB/SEX -0.0059 [-0.0091, -0.0027] and A/race -0.0137 [-0.0174, -0.0100] unweighted; C1 versus L2 gives AB/SEX -0.0047 [-0.0080, -0.0014] and A/race -0.0074 [-0.0109, -0.0039], with the residence difference inside ±0.001 and no sensitive endpoint significantly worse. All 20 primary endpoints keep their development sign; the difference is resolution — the final partition has 15,924 people against 2,982 in development.

**But the method still loses to the frozen neural baseline.** C1 versus J: residence -0.0093 [-0.0150, -0.0036] (C1 better), A/race 0.0377 [0.0294, 0.0459] and AB/SEX 0.0070 [0.0015, 0.0124] (C1 much worse), plus significant income- and employment-probe costs. Frozen 2018 attacks (Mode A) reproduce the same ordering, so it is not an artifact of refitting attackers.

**Costs and controls.** C1's advantage over its local controls is paid in income-probe utility (about 0.0013–0.0019 nats, intervals excluding zero). No withholding mechanism dominates C1 across all seeds and weightings, and spectral arms dominate withholding in at most 3 of 180 fixed comparisons, so the frontier does not reduce to releasing a simpler channel at random. Service outputs are bitwise preserved on all 210 released 2017 views, while service accuracy moves with the year (income −0.015 nats, employment +0.014). The legacy source-probe allowance passes everywhere under fresh adaptation but fails for spectral arms under frozen transfer — a probe-transfer effect, not a change in the service. Complete tables, per-seed values and every fixed ancestor attack: `TRANSPORT_RESULTS.md`, `PER_SEED.csv.gz`, `AGGREGATE.csv.gz`, `FAMILIES.csv`.

## 8. What this study supports, and what it does not

| Claim | Status | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Exact preservation of the published service outputs | established (structural + replay) | 210/210 released views bitwise equal; `INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION.json` |
| The channel adds reusable residence capability beyond H, and this transports | established under the tested probes | ≥0.01 nats in every seed, weighting and mode |
| Coalition conditioning reduces measured recovery versus equal-strength and equal-mass local controls | established on the locked 2017 evaluation, under the tested attack families | F1, both weightings, simultaneous intervals |
| The spectral channel is competitive with an adversarially trained channel | **unsupported; contradicted** | F3: leaks 0.013–0.038 nats more on every sensitive endpoint |
| Small measured recovery implies small conditional mutual information | unsupported by construction | §4; negative increments observed |
| The mechanism generalizes to many recipients | unsupported | only A receives a channel |
| 2017 respondents are different people from 2018 respondents | unsupported | public identifiers cannot establish this |
| Service accuracy is stable after shift | not supported | employment degrades ~0.014 nats |

## 9. Limitations

Three seeds are three fixed fitted systems on one cohort, not population replications; the bootstrap covers people in the 2017 final partition only, not retraining variability or Census design variance. Attack strength is a floor, not a guarantee. Race class 3 has one person in the final partition and no 2018 attacker-fitting support; class-level race statements are withheld. Income is nominal and unadjusted across years. Transport is between public survey years of the same state and age band. The evaluation refits attackers but not the representation, so it does not test a mechanism retrained on 2017.

## 10. Reproducibility

Deterministic seeds throughout; every unit writes a hash-bound completion record; the lock refuses to open the final partition if any hashed input changed, and its two amendments are code-only and diffed. Commands, required local objects and software versions: `REPRODUCE.md`. Fitted objects, releases, per-person predictions and person arrays stay local; compact aggregates, code and reports are published.
