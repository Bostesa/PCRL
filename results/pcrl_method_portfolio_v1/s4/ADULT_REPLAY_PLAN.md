# S4 independent Adult replay plan

Status: B-TASK seed 0 and B-JVIB seed 0 screens and real control probes have
completed. Independent Adult numerical replay has not run. The B-only prefit
seal checks custody and admission; it does not verify a fitted result.

## Admission and custody

For each completed B screen, require the exact S3 unit in the effective lock,
exit 0, matching arm/seed, a completed postrelease real-control unit, and all
null, group-shuffle and planted-leak gates passing. Preserve failed and partial
attempts. Hash `ATTEMPT_INDEX.json`, its listed fit checkpoints, both saved
release files, calibration records, reader receipts, `ENDPOINT_INPUTS` files
and `AGGREGATE.json`. Join ordered `(row_id, unit)` independently to the
admitted role-only ATTACK_FIT_NEW and INNER_SELECTION files. Recompute the
fixed ATTACK_TRAIN/TUNE group split from role IDs and check counts, disjointness
and both SEX classes. Read no all-role or old-assessment person arrays.

## Complete release and readers

Parse each saved wire payload independently and reject extra or missing view
fields, duplicate person-purpose keys, a changed digest, row order, or a
metadata field that the attack view omitted. Rebuild both recipient views and
the aligned pair from actual transmitted 16D latent, logits, calibrated
probabilities, decisions and metadata. Check probability simplex, public
decision parity, public head logits from transmitted latent, and the fitted
global-temperature law from public calibration records. Recreate the exact
numeric attack matrices and defense-aware public transforms, then compare
their bytes and reader receipt hashes to the frozen ATT/INNER matrices. The
provider's private input hashes and person keys are alignment evidence, not
recipient inputs.

Load the locally trusted private fitted reader artifacts without importing
candidate, attack-evaluator, endpoint or selection modules. Recompute their
TUNE/INNER positive-class predictions from the reconstructed matrices and
compare to saved vectors. Check pair ignore-recipient aliases equal the
corresponding local vectors on identical people. Independently calculate
TUNE orientation and the validation winner; the primary INNER envelope still
maximizes over every fixed reader and both score orientations. Verify source
and fitted model/checkpoint identities for both purpose heads, sequential
frozen first draw where applicable, and the exact one-shot sampling contract.

## Endpoints and inference

Construct `SeedRows` from admitted INNER labels, U0/Ucal references, actual
saved releases and independently replayed reader vectors. Use S4's separate
`independent_endpoints.py` for accuracy, clipped natural-log NLL, summed
multiclass Brier, tie-correct AUC, and all twelve utility plus every registered
control's three privacy margins. Check every arm and both paired seeds, with
no best-seed or best-reader choice on INNER. Compare per-seed aggregates with
saved `AGGREGATE.json`; do not accept an aggregate alone as evidence.

After all required B arms and feature anchors are complete, independently
apply the locked control eligibility rule: candidate excluded from its own
pool, local AUC caps against the fixed raw83+Ucal anchor, point utility caps
against both U0/Ucal, then minimum coalition AUC with the locked tie order.
Recompute the fixed 2,048 same-group, paired-seed max-studentized bootstrap
with private seed custody. Report PASS, VIOLATION or UNRESOLVED for every
clause; retain all arm/seed results and any failed controls. A selected result
is exploratory on reused Adult rows, even when an interval clears a target.

## Queue and outputs

S4 numerical replay is a separately registered S3 queue unit, with its own
script hash, one numerical thread, explicit CPU/RSS ceiling, and private
person-array output. First run a single completed B arm/seed custody replay;
run the full endpoint/bootstrap replay once all required outputs exist. Public
`INDEPENDENT_VERIFICATION.json` records only aggregates, hashes, checks and
limits. No S4 code imports the candidate's evaluator or selector.
