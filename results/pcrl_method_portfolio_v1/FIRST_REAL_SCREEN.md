# First real end-to-end development screen

**Milestone: REAL_END_TO_END_SCREEN_COMPLETE, Team B seed 0.** The first admitted Adult comparison fitted a retrained stochastic joint bottleneck (`B-JVIB`) and its identical-architecture task-only control (`B-TASK`). Each completed defense fitting, held-out task calibration, actual feature releases for ATTACK_FIT_NEW and INNER_SELECTION, fresh recipient and coalition attacks, task and SEX-recovery endpoints, and private write-once custody. The same seed-0 units were reused in the complete two-seed screen. This is development evidence on repeatedly used Adult rows, not independent confirmation.

The first V1 shuffled-label control was mathematically unsound under max-orientation AUC and was superseded without deleting its receipts. The corrected V2 real null, shuffled and planted-leak controls passed on all retained B arms and anchors. S4 independently replayed the 16 B arm-seed releases and two shared anchors, including complete views, fresh readers and endpoints; 822 aggregate comparisons and the strongest-control selection also matched.
