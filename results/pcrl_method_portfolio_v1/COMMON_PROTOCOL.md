# PCRL method portfolio: common development protocol

## Status and exposure

This portfolio is a development search motivated by repeatedly used Adult outcomes. Four method families, source models and previous results are known. Any screen or finalist result is exploratory and does not provide independent confirmation. Agent count, new locks and new partitions do not reverse prior exposure. No old assessment or new confirmation population is opened.

## Questions and tracks

Compare four distinct operations: A randomizes a finite channel on frozen task scores; B jointly learns purpose-specific raw-input bottlenecks and task heads; C selects global purpose-specific groups of raw inputs and retrains heads; D repairs frozen task-trained latents through an information-changing nonlinear map and retrains heads. A operates on scores. B, C and D normally disclose purpose features and head outputs. Report within-track frontiers only. A score-only result is not a feature-sharing result.

The unit of comparison is an aligned person and a paired seed. Historical seeds do not create independent populations. All predictions released to a task recipient must be functions of the protected code or features in its view. The old clean teacher output cannot be appended. The two task recipients and their coalition are audited, including readers that ignore one view. Public maps, weights, tables, mask identities, flags, lengths and fallbacks belong to the attacker contract. Public weights do not grant private person-level provider inputs. For randomized releases, a fixed per-person draw is scored and disclosed; a mean probability is not substituted for the sampled output. A repeated-query claim requires a separate, predeclared query contract and attack.

## Utility, privacy and inference

Freeze U0 (original continuous U) and Ucal (continuous U with hcal global temperature) by exact identity and report both. A complete common-criterion pass requires every applicable task utility clause against both references, not only one. Accuracy difference must be at least -0.010; mean NLL excess at most 0.010 nats; mean multiclass Brier excess at most 0.005. Report useful prediction retention and per-class recall. Coalition SEX AUC benefit must be at least 0.020 relative to the strongest eligible matched control in the same track. Neither recipient's AUC may worsen by more than 0.010 relative to that control. Class-only score release is an ideal-reader privacy floor but is confidence-ineligible unless it happens to satisfy the actual criteria. This is not a privacy guarantee.

Boundary conventions are inclusive. For cost and local-AUC caps, PASS requires a simultaneous upper bound <= cap; VIOLATION requires a lower bound > cap; otherwise UNRESOLVED. For accuracy and coalition benefit, PASS requires a lower bound >= threshold; VIOLATION requires an upper bound < threshold; otherwise UNRESOLVED. Point-estimate feasibility can justify pilot promotion but is never a passed clause. Any structural equality applies only to the quantity and reference it proves. Uncertainty is conditional on fitted models, paired by person across seeds, accounts for release randomness, and does not correct for portfolio selection.

Controls have the same permitted input, interface, model/code/feature capacity where needed, fitting role, calibration family, search budget, and fresh nonlinear attack capability. Strongest control is selected by minimum coalition AUC among point-utility-eligible controls with local caps met, across required task-only/local/sequential/non-joint arms; tie within 1e-12 by lower summed NLL, then registered ID. An ineligible class-only floor remains reported but cannot be the competitive utility comparator. Each team must also show every required control individually. If all eligible controls are absent, the competitive claim is unavailable.

## Phase gates and selection

Phase I requires each team's hypothesis, primary prior work, method spec, strongest control, failure tests and exact planned units. Before algorithmic synthetic runs, S1 must freeze finite laws for feasible null, removable sensitive distinction, complementary coalition clue, no complementarity, and useful confidence versus class-only output. Analytic pre-lock premise checks are distinguished from algorithm results. At most two prototypes per family, ideally one method and one structural ablation. A duplicate family becomes an ablation, with identity and reason recorded.

Before any Adult fit, `CAMPAIGN_LOCK.json` must be committed, pushed and remotely verified. It binds candidate IDs, controls, seeds, role access, attacks, sampling, budgets and promotion. Two paired seeds are the screen default. Promotion is at most two total: one reserved for score and one for feature track. Within each track rank by valid complete units/controls; both-task useful point-utility feasibility; coalition gain without local worsening; seed consistency; best matched control; then complexity/reproducibility. An unused track slot may go to the next eligible candidate in the other track under that track's own ranking. No cross-track numerical ranking. Point-feasible wide intervals are PRECISION_LIMITED. Freeze finalist identities/configurations before three-paired-seed pilots and new attacks. New finalist results remain development evidence.

Stop a lane when its registered kill rule fires. Keep every attempted, aliased, failed, skipped and incomplete unit. Post-lock fixes preserve original outputs and require a dated pushed amendment before affected reruns. Scientific transfer after observing outcomes gets a new ID and budget. Independent S4 replay must not import candidate or evaluator scoring/selection code. A result with incomplete views, unsafe labels or unmatched controls is invalid for the competitive claim.

## Budget and resource queue

The whole campaign has 10 elapsed hours, 20 CPU-hours, zero cloud spend and at most two heavy processes across participating machine sessions. Suggested reservations: 3 setup, 6 screens, 8 finalists, 3 replay/closeout CPU-hours. A single durable semaphore and receipts are required for every heavy training, attack, preprocessing and verification job, including child processes and explicit numerical thread limits. Existing live jobs count. At a ceiling, checkpoint and stop this campaign's work only. No external acquisition or cloud resource launch.
