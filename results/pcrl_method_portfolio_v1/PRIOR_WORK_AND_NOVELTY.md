# Prior work and claim boundaries

This portfolio assembles established mechanism components. No algorithmic novelty is assumed before matched evidence.

| Lane | Primary antecedents and actual overlap | Permitted claim if later supported |
|---|---|---|
| A: finite score channel | [Privacy-aware randomized quantization](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v244/cai24a.html) uses randomized quantization and linear programming. PCRL's own earlier ACS adaptive-release study fitted a 32-state to 17-token stochastic `Q` with a deterministic control and no competitive gain (`research/pcrl-adaptive-release-v1`, local tip `2ce5d171f079f759eb755a21ab415765ea234479`). [General distortion privacy](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.05468) studies information-theoretic privacy under distortion. | An Adult two-recipient score-only transfer with mean NLL/Brier constraints and actual sampled complete-view recovery; no new finite-channel algorithm claim. A deterministic arm can earn the simpler result. |
| B: jointly trained bottleneck | [Deep VIB](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00410) supplies a variational rate bottleneck; the [privacy funnel](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.1774) supplies the utility/private-information framing. Ordinary raw adversarial training and PCRL's prior raw joint methods are required controls. | An empirical feature-track gain due to retrained rate-constrained encoders, only if task-only, deterministic, separate, sequential and raw-joint arms fail to explain it. A KL rate bound is not an AUC bound. |
| C: grouped feature selection | Purpose-specific grouped selection with a coalition-union objective is an instance of constrained feature selection, with established fairness/privacy-aware selection antecedents. The selected raw values are directly visible; head training adds no ideal-reader secrecy. | A transparent competitive baseline or useful method if it beats full, utility-only, independent, shared and sequential masks under raw-union attacks. Novelty requires an additional tested selection principle beyond standard selection. |
| D: post-hoc hard repair | [LEACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03819) guarantees erasure for linear concept readers, which does not settle nonlinear recovery. [Nonlinear density-matching erasure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12341) and quantization are prior components. D's hard VQ is many-to-one but code uniqueness can defeat practical privacy. | An empirical gain over frozen, linear, local, sequential and generic pooled compression at matched capacity **and distortion**. Without distortion matching, privacy-objective and joint-component attribution is unavailable. |

[Taylor et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21859) study adaptive sequential release under collusion using expected distortion or mutual information and an information-theoretic privacy criterion. Their reported optimality for expected distortion is specific to that formulation. PCRL's learned feature/score sequential controls are faithful within their own contract when implemented, but are not asserted to reproduce Taylor's setting or optimum. Both release orders must be reported.

[Hard distortion privacy](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.00063) uses a deterministic per-instance fidelity support. CCM failed under its pointwise guard. This does not rule out mean-loss channels in A or the other methods. Expected distortion, mutual information, maximal leakage, differential privacy, demographic parity, and fitted-attacker AUC are distinct quantities. A fitted finite-law constraint or information objective is not a held-out guarantee.

The incumbent hcal score partition has an exploratory 0.031920 coalition-AUC advantage over T* on reused assessment rows, with occupation NLL excess intervals crossing the 0.010 allowance against both U0 and Ucal. The privacy result repeats an old partition on old rows; shared calibration changes probabilities without removing token information. CCM closes its exact hard-guard geometry, and its independent verification remains incomplete because of a loader access failure. These historical findings motivate mechanisms but cannot be counted as new portfolio outcomes.

Claim hierarchy: an implementation improvement needs correct deployment and complete views; an empirical advantage needs matched eligible controls and fresh permitted attacks; joint design needs an additional gain over both sequential orders and the strongest non-joint arm; algorithmic novelty needs a mechanism or solver beyond the antecedents above. Synthetic laws demonstrate capability in those laws only. No Adult screen, finalist, joint-design or novelty claim is established at campaign setup.
