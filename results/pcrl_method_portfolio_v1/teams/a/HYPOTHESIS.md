# Team A — stochastic confidence channel

## Question and status

Can a class-preserving sampled score token retain useful task confidence under the original **mean** loss limits, while reducing SEX recovery from each recipient and their aligned coalition more than matched deterministic and sequential score releases? This is a score-track development hypothesis on repeatedly used Adult roles. It is not a claim about feature sharing, a population guarantee, or algorithmic novelty.

The operation changes what is transmitted. A local sampler takes its own frozen task-score bin and emits **one** bank token. The recipient sees that token and its public probability vector; it never sees the input bin or a clean teacher score. An unchanged input bin plus a new decoder would not remove information.

## Testable predictions, registered before any portfolio fit

1. The fixed output bank will admit at least one channel satisfying both tasks' point-estimate accuracy, mean NLL and mean Brier caps against **both** U0 and Ucal on INNER_SELECTION. If it does not, the lane stops; no utility allowance or bank is enlarged after outcomes.
2. Jointly designed local channels may reduce coalition recovery more than separately designed channels. The incremental benefit may be small because the existing deterministic sequential arms nearly tied the old JOINT release; a tie is a negative joint-design result.
3. Randomization may permit a lower fitted-law Bayes SEX accuracy at fixed mean utility than a one-hot row map. This is only a finite-law capability prediction. A fresh complete-view attacker may erase the apparent advantage.
4. The occupation task is the likely binding utility constraint. A protected channel may be utility feasible at a point estimate yet have an upper confidence bound above 0.010 NLL excess; that is PRECISION_LIMITED, not a passed clause or a violation.

The old hcal result is historical development evidence: its P* preserved decisions and reduced reused-row coalition AUC by 0.031920 against T*, but occupation NLL upper bounds crossed the 0.010 allowance against both references. Repeating its decoder or tempering it is outside this design. The score channel should be judged against the strongest **eligible** matched control, not only historical T*.

## Success and stop rules

Only S1's common protocol can define a campaign pass. Team A proposes no private success label. A worthwhile exploratory method lead needs utility-feasible point estimates on both tasks, meaningful fresh-attack coalition improvement within the score track, no adverse local shift, consistency across paired seeds, and an advantage over the strongest matched eligible control. It earns a **joint** claim only if it also beats both sequential orders and the strongest non-joint channel. A deterministic or task-only channel win is retained as the simpler result.

Stop at a failed premise, absence of a mean-utility-feasible protected channel, mostly raw fallbacks (the design permits none), failure under complete-view attack, or explanation by the matched deterministic or sequential arm. At most one protected channel design and one deterministic structural ablation are candidate prototypes. All other arms are required controls, not new Team A prototypes.

No new fit may start until S2 demonstrates a loader that never materializes forbidden old-assessment or HEAD_VALIDATION labels and S1 pushes CAMPAIGN_LOCK.json. The existing CCM transient-read defect is not considered cured by masking after load.
