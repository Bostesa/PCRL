## Scientific premise and prior identity

The proposed operation is a purpose-specific stochastic raw-input encoder, `q_i(z_i|x)`, with a task head trained on the resulting feature. Releasing one sampled feature per recipient changes the acquired representation and is distinct from recalibrating a frozen score. It is also distinct from the historical JCV implementation's mandatory final LEACE transform and projected adversarial update. JCV is a cautionary control: final LEACE alone lost 4.9 points of income and 3.7 points of occupation accuracy, and no protected neural arm met its utility gates (source branch `research/pcrl-joint-complete-view-method-v1`, tip `568f970683d11373d717ca112b619fbdde40d0b3`, `results/pcrl_joint_complete_view_method_v1/RESEARCH_DECISION.md`). The new design must avoid silently inheriting that eraser.

The component idea is established. A Gaussian variational bottleneck with a prior KL penalty follows [Deep Variational Information Bottleneck](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00410); optimizing a probabilistic privacy/utility mapping follows the [Privacy Funnel](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.1774); adversarial fair representation learning has a prior in [LAFTR](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v80/madras18a.html). Team B may establish an empirical advantage in this exact recipient/coalition contract. A bottleneck plus local/pair critics alone is not a demonstrated new mechanism or solver. Previous PCRL ACS bottleneck and raw-adversarial work must be acknowledged as different tasks/interfaces, not treated as failures of all raw stochastic representations.

An average `KL(q_i(z|x) || N(0,I))` upper-bounds `I(X;Z_i)` only for the law over `X` used in the expectation and the specified encoder; it is not an empirical bound on SEX AUC, a fitted-attacker result, or a population certificate. If the two noises are conditionally independent given `X`, a sum of the two *true* information terms bounds `I(X;Z_1,Z_2)`, but that does not establish the stated AUC target. Any shared noise changes this premise and needs its own derivation. A deterministic real-valued mean release has no information bound merely because its training objective contains a Gaussian KL term. The same caveat applies to a low-dimensional deterministic code: dimension alone does not limit disclosed bits.

## Disclosure and deployment checks

1. The **primary feature view** for recipient `i` must include its sampled `z_i`, decision, probabilities or logits actually served, and all per-person metadata; the coalition gets both aligned views. The head output must be computed from the exact released `z_i`. A frozen teacher score appended after the bottleneck bypasses it. Public encoder/head weights are known to defense-aware attacks, while private provider inputs are not automatically available.
2. `q_i` must use only the pinned named and ordered 83 permitted predictors at inference. A length-83 array check is insufficient to catch a swapped SEX or task-label column. The provider-side schema check should reject forbidden names, missing/extra columns, and reordered columns. SEX can train the protection objective but cannot route a deployment row.
3. The declared stochastic contract is one immutable draw per person and purpose. If the code only caches draws in memory, it must be an immutable batch builder whose saved batch is served unchanged; it must not imply a durable repeat-query guarantee. A new builder run on the same people is a new release. The provider-side private row key and RNG state must not be transmitted. If the mechanism permits repeated samples, register and test an aggregation attack at the declared query count.
4. Disclosing `z_i` makes its deterministic head output informationally redundant for an ideal reader, but the entire actual view still needs to be passed to finite attackers. Means, variances, raw `x`, raw teacher scores, and hidden fallback flags must not leak via files, logs, metadata or deployment adapters.

## Controls required before a competitive reading

- Task-only stochastic bottlenecks with the identical encoders, heads, feature width, distillation term, calibration and compute budget. Otherwise compression or architecture can explain the result.
- Separately privacy-trained local bottlenecks, evaluated as a coalition; sequential training in **both** orders with the same total optimizer/attacker exposure; and a strong ordinary raw joint/adversarial arm. The sequential arms need a faithful feature-interface implementation; an old score-only sequential result is contextual, not a matched substitute.
- A deterministic mean-release version with matched architecture and task/critic exposure. Its exact regularizer and whether log variance is trained must be stated. Include a task-only deterministic counterpart if needed to isolate randomness from compression and regularization.
- The same frozen input schema, training/calibration roles, task references, fresh nonlinear attack slate, checkpoint selection and search budget in every eligible arm. Keep historical JCV and any old ACS result as historical evidence unless their full interfaces and roles actually match. The strongest eligible control is selected by the common frozen rule, not by narrative preference.

For every candidate and control, report each paired seed, both task accuracies and per-class recall, actual-sample NLL and multiclass Brier against both U0 and Ucal, each local SEX AUC, and coalition SEX AUC including ignore-one-recipient readers. A loss computed on mean probabilities is not the loss of a recipient's sampled release. Protect against a misleading low AUC from nearly constant predictions by showing usefulness and class retention. Calibration and reference comparisons must use the common family; a method-specific calibrator changes the comparison.
