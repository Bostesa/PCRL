## Local prior evidence read

The checked-out `research/pcrl-joint-complete-view-method-v1` tip `568f970683d11373d717ca112b619fbdde40d0b3` contains `results/pcrl_joint_complete_view_method_v1/METHOD_CARD.md`, which trained separate 83→64→64→16 deterministic raw-input encoders, affine task heads, local and pair critics, and an official final LEACE map. Its `RESEARCH_DECISION.md` reports no utility-feasible protected neural arm. LEACE alone cost about 4.9 percentage points of income and 3.7 of occupation accuracy on its reused Adult assessment. The projected-gradient and plain-penalty joint arms did not establish a joint advantage. Team B therefore requires a genuine stochastic rate bottleneck and drops mandatory LEACE; a simple adversarial penalty on the old deterministic architecture would duplicate prior work.

The repository's `redesign_20260908_acs_bottleneck_v1` tested a 16-dimensional nonlinear ACS bottleneck with task supervision, reconstruction and an adversarial penalty. It found a modest SEX-attack-loss change without its full utility/transfer conjunction. That ACS schema, targets and verdict are distinct from Adult. Team B uses no input reconstruction and must not portray the ACS result as evidence that Adult confidence is preserved.

- **Implementation difference:** one-draw purpose-specific feature releases with head outputs constrained to depend on the released vectors, and explicit complete-view coalition auditing.
- **Possible empirical claim:** lower measured SEX recovery at matched utility and interface than the strongest eligible control on reused Adult development roles.
- **Possible joint-design claim:** only if it additionally beats both sequential orders and the strongest non-joint arm under the frozen within-track rule.
