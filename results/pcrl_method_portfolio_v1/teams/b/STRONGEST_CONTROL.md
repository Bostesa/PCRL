## Contract to match

Primary B candidate: two purpose-specific 16-dimensional Gaussian variational bottlenecks. Each encoder maps the same 83 permitted raw predictors through the same 83→64→64→(16 means, 16 log variances) architecture. One sampled latent `z_i` per person goes to the corresponding task head. Training uses task cross-entropy, frozen-teacher distillation, a fixed KL-to-standard-normal term, and local plus coalition SEX critics. Recipient `i` receives the **actual draw** `z_i`, the raw task logits produced from that draw, the calibrated task probability vector, its decision, and any sampling, calibration, fallback, or code metadata. A pair attacker receives both aligned views. Public weights and calibration parameters are known. True SEX, task labels, the other private input, and untransmitted teacher scores are unavailable at deployment. A provider may possess permitted `X`; that does not put `X` in the recipient's view. S2 must freeze the final view and query contract.

All new arms use identical role-specific fitting people, 83 predictors, train-only preprocessing, task-label availability, calibration family, latent width, head class, model/attacker seed identifiers, optimizer schedule, per-encoder update count, data order, checkpoint rule, two-seed screen, and (if promoted) three-seed pilot. Each arm gets the same shared attacker bank on its **complete** released interface. A higher score from an accidentally omitted component is invalid. No arm appends the clean teacher output after protection. Structural and historical differences that cannot be exactly matched are stated rather than called aliases.

## Matched release arms

| ID | Construction | Scientific role | Training jobs per seed |
|---|---|---|---:|
| `B-JVIB` | Jointly fit two stochastic VIBs with local and pair SEX critics. | Primary joint design. | 1 paired job |
| `B-DVIB` | Same Gaussian parameterization, KL coefficient, task/distillation losses, and critics as `B-JVIB`, but optimize and release `z=mu` throughout. | Deterministic structural ablation. Its Gaussian KL is a regularizer, **not** a deterministic information bound. | 1 paired job |
| `B-TASK` | Identical stochastic VIB and task/distillation/KL objective, with all SEX terms zero; two purpose models can share one scheduled job but have no cross-purpose gradient. | Strong task-only bottleneck at identical capacity. | 1 paired job |
| `B-RAW-TASK` | Deterministic 16D raw-input trunk/head with task/distillation objective, no KL and no SEX critics; same active mean path as `B-RAWJ`. | Strong deterministic task-only control. | 1 paired job |
| `B-LOCAL` | Each stochastic VIB is trained independently with its own local SEX critic, with the same per-encoder updates. Attack their aligned union afterward. | Separate local-private comparator. | 2 single-recipient jobs |
| `B-SEQ12` | Fit recipient 1 as `B-LOCAL`; freeze it. Fit recipient 2 with its local critic and a pair critic seeing the frozen first release. | Sequential order 1→2. | 2 stage jobs |
| `B-SEQ21` | Symmetric reverse order. | Sequential order 2→1. | 2 stage jobs |
| `B-RAWJ` | Matched 16-dimensional raw-input neural encoders and heads with task/distillation and local/pair adversaries, without sampling or KL. | Ordinary raw joint/adversarial comparator. Do not misname it Taylor et al.'s method. | 1 paired job |

The first stage of each sequential arm is a separately charged fit: `B-LOCAL` receives `2T` updates per local critic, while sequential stage 1 receives `T`, so no alias is planned. A future alias would require source/code, seed, rows/order, preprocessing, initialization, architecture, losses, critic steps, schedule, checkpoint, public interface and hashes to match exactly under an amendment. Stage 2 consumes a frozen one-draw release of the first encoder on defense-fit people; its checkpoint SHA, release SHA, ordered person and group IDs, feature schema and private sampler-plan digest must be verified before pair-critic training. It cannot silently resample first-recipient features. This adds two defense-fit first-stage forward units per seed. Shared source teachers and U0/Ucal are historical references, not substitutes for feature-track matched controls. Historical RAW-J, LEACE, FARE and hcal score releases can be shown descriptively only when their input/view differences are explicit; they are not automatic matched-control aliases.

Proposed matched exposure is `T` task optimizer updates **per encoder**, with `T=600` provisionally in `TRAINING_DESIGN.md` and S3 smoke/S1 prefit seal required. For `B-JVIB`, `B-DVIB` and `B-RAWJ`, update each local critic `T` times and the pair critic `2T` times total. For each sequential arm, its first-stage local critic gets `T`; its second-stage local critic gets `T` and its pair critic gets `2T` while the first encoder is frozen. Thus joint and sequential arms each use `2T` encoder, `2T` local-critic and `2T` pair-critic updates. The stages cannot be scientifically identical because the pair critic in a sequential arm sees a frozen first encoder. `B-LOCAL` has no pair objective; give **each** local critic `2T` updates, matching the total `4T` critic-update ceiling and providing a strong local comparator. `B-TASK` has no critic objective. All arms receive identical fresh-attacker fits. `B-RAWJ` is a deterministic 16-coordinate release with identical trunk width, head, inputs and `2T` encoder / `4T` critic updates; its Gaussian variance branch is inactive and its KL coefficient is zero. Report total and active parameter counts. These counts are training critic updates, distinct from the fresh post-fit attacker models.

Local protection coefficients also match per-purpose pressure: joint arms use `0.05 × mean(CE_1, CE_2)`, giving `0.025 × CE_i` to each recipient; each one-purpose LOCAL/SEQ stage uses `0.025 × CE_i`. A pair critic, where active, has coefficient `0.05`. Critic update counts above are separate from these loss weights.

`B-JVIB` evaluated with the sampler disabled at inference is a zero-training diagnostic (`B-JVIB/noise-off`), with a separate forward pass and attack bank if reported. It cannot enter candidate ranking as a stochastic release result. `B-JVIB` versus newly trained `B-DVIB` tests a stochastic **training-plus-release** bundle; the noise-off diagnostic can further isolate the deployed draw, if its added attack/forward cost is preregistered. A randomness claim requires `B-JVIB` to beat `B-DVIB` under matched complete-view attacks, not just its own noise-off diagnostic. `B-RAW-TASK` provides an additional deterministic task-only check. A joint-design claim requires advantage over `B-LOCAL`, **both** `B-SEQ12` and `B-SEQ21`, both task-only controls, and `B-RAWJ` where utility eligible.

## Utility, calibration, and attacker parity

Each arm fits the same single H-GLOBAL-TEMP calibration family on `CALIBRATION_HELDOUT` task labels: one scalar per recipient, no grid. Release scoring uses the calibrated vector actually sent. Show both U0 and Ucal task-reference conjunctions, with the complete conjunction requiring both. Compare task accuracy, mean NLL, multiclass Brier, retention and per-class behavior for **both** tasks. All accuracy, loss and AUC margins and simultaneous interval boundaries come from S1/S2's prefit common protocol; point-estimate eligibility is not a passed clause.

Fresh SEX attacks fit and tune **inside** `ATTACK_FIT_NEW`: S2 freezes a deterministic group-aware fit/tune split by stable ID, disjoint in both people and groups. Each of the four fixed model configurations per arm/seed/view is fitted on the attack-fit half and assessed on the attack-tune half. Provisional bank: LR (`C=1`), MLP (64×64), HGB (100 iterations, max 15 leaves), and a defense-aware MLP whose inputs include the full public transformation/weights and every transmitted field. S1/S2 must freeze exact hyperparameters, preprocessing, architecture and fit/tune membership before fits. Each fit's `P(SEX=1)` output and its complement are two **predeclared** score readers requiring one model fit; this prevents a reversed signal from masquerading as privacy. The validation-selected reader and orientation are frozen on attack-tune data before any `INNER_SELECTION` score. Primary recovery on INNER is the maximum AUC over the predeclared fixed-slate score readers; report every reader and the tune-selected winner too. No new reader or orientation is chosen from an INNER outcome. The pair slate also includes all local fitted score readers as ignore-the-other-recipient candidates. These are prediction aliases, with zero new model fits, only when rows, model, preprocessing, seed and interface hashes match exactly. S2 may choose a stronger common bank before seal; if the provisional bank misses planted leaks, revise **before** the lock.

Control eligibility is determined independently of the candidate's observed result: both-task point utility against frozen U0 and Ucal plus each control's local AUC cap against S1's fixed feature-track reference `FEATURE_REF_RAW83_U`. Both `B-TASK` and `B-RAW-TASK` are separately eligible task-only controls; neither is a mutable local-cap anchor. Among eligible controls, take minimum coalition recovery under the fixed-slate envelope, then lower summed NLL, then registered ID. Candidate local-worsening clauses compare against that chosen control. If no control is eligible, no competitive claim is available. S1 must freeze the exact global rule before real fits.

For paired uncertainty, resample the same person/group IDs jointly across all arms and model seeds. In **each** replicate recompute AUC for every fixed reader, take that replicate's maximum within each arm/view/seed, then average the paired seed endpoints. Bootstrap the strongest-control selection and resulting candidate contrast under S1's frozen inference rule if the reported interval purports to include comparator selection; otherwise label it conditional on the selected control. Never compute an interval for only the realized winning reader and call it an interval for the fixed-slate envelope. Stochastic draw variability receives separately predeclared draws or a stated conditional-on-draw scope.

The strongest eligible control is selected within the feature track under S1's frozen rule, based on inner rows only. Control eligibility requires valid deployment, useful predictions and point-estimate utility on both tasks against both references; unresolved confidence intervals are flagged. Compare all arms even if ineligible, never silently drop one or change the comparator after results. Show local and pair AUC, attacker CE, each seed, each task and the utility/recovery frontier.

## Budget and matching limits

The 16-dimensional transmitted latent is the common feature budget. Task-only, separate-private, sequential, joint, deterministic and RAW all use the same input predictors and head size. KL/sampling is the intended design difference between VIB and RAW, so compute equality does not imply objective equality. Hold **per-encoder** task update counts, data exposure and restart count fixed; record critic updates and wall/CPU time separately. An apparent joint advantage over a cheaper sequential arm is not credited unless both sequential orders receive their full registered training. The global scheduler grants at most two heavy processes across participating sessions, with fixed numerical threads and memory bound.
