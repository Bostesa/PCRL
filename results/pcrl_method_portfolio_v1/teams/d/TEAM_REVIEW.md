# Team D skeptical review — pre-fit, 2026-10-08

**Status:** Conditional premise approval for engineering and finite-law tests only. This review does not approve an Adult fit, establish a privacy result, or independently verify implementation. I reviewed the designer's stated proposal on 2026-10-08; the final locked `METHOD_SPEC.md`, implementation, and endpoint code still require a second pass.

## Proposal as reviewed

The revised pre-lock proposal freezes each task-trained purpose encoder `h_i(x)` and applies a pooled, hard vector quantizer `T` after it. Each recipient receives its own one-of-1024 finite code identity and the corresponding shared **standardized** centroid, probabilities and decision from a newly trained head that reads **only** that centroid, and all routing/quantization metadata. The privacy loss reads hard code identities: exact local code-frequency features and a fixed sketch of aligned pair IDs. The same group-blind codebook is fitted for both purposes, using protected SEX labels only during fitting. A deployment call for recipient `i` may read the permitted raw columns needed by `h_i`, its own `h_i(x)`, and the public quantizer/head. It may not read SEX, the other recipient's private per-person latent, a true task label, or old assessment data. The coalition observes the aligned pair of complete releases.

Hard quantization is many-to-one as a map on continuous latent space. That is a real change to transmitted information; an invertible nonlinear transform would not qualify. The first 4×256 product-code draft would have allowed 2^32 complete tuples per recipient and was appropriately dropped **before the lock or outcomes** because it was a weak bottleneck for about 15,434 fitting people. The 1024-code revision is substantially more plausible but does not imply SEX privacy: codes can remain group-pure, and a pair of 10-bit codes can have up to 2^20 possible joint identities. Report single-code and pair-code occupancy, singleton **person mass**, collision rates, and label/task composition on fitting and screening roles. A quantizer with raw-score/latent fallback has a different and potentially leaky interface; every fallback flag and value must be attacked and counted. No silent fallbacks.

## Premise and deployment gates before real fitting

1. **Source identity.** Pin both frozen task-trained encoders, the precise layer/tensor used as latent, dimensionality, preprocessing, permitted columns, seed, and hash. A post-LEACE or previously privacy-trained representation would change the question. Do not construct these latents through a loader that materializes old-assessment labels or arrays; wait for S2's role-specific guard. Task labels must be excluded from the 83 input columns.
2. **Exact map.** Freeze 1024 centers in 16 dimensions, metric and scaling, tie rule, assignment function, and deterministic/stochastic status before fitting. Per-purpose fitting-role μ/σ scaling is proposed for the separately trained 16-dimensional latent spaces; pin it and apply it to every matched arm. A pooled codebook assumes only a common coordinate *format*, not semantic alignment of independently trained latent axes. No SEX-specific codebook, SEX-gated branch, other-party latent, or per-person protected-label routing at inference. Source-safe Adult latent exports are not yet shown; their absence blocks a real-data fit rather than licensing a forbidden loader.
3. **Actual wire.** The public codebook, code ID, decoded vector, head weights, probabilities, decision, flags, lengths and fallback values are available to a defense-aware attacker. The code ID and decoded vector are equivalent only when unique code vectors have unique IDs; with duplicate vectors, IDs can disclose *more*. Score the full transmitted wire, including both recipients for the coalition. No original frozen teacher score or clean head output may be appended. Unit-test that output probabilities depend exclusively on the released code and fixed public head.
4. **Capacity and compute.** Task-only pooled VQ, local VQ, joint VQ, both sequential orders, and generic pooled compression need the same frozen latents, 10-bit per-recipient code budget, quantizer/head capacity, data roles, preprocessing, restarts, search/compute ceiling, calibration family, utility constraints and attack effort. The proposed separate-codebook control uses 1024 centroids *per purpose*, matching the wire budget while doubling total centroid parameters; this is a strong control and the parameter imbalance must be visible. An equal-total-centroid diagnostic would have a smaller per-recipient wire and cannot substitute for the primary control. An unchanged-latent retrained-head control is also necessary. LEACE and a strong ordinary adversarial control provide historical and compatible baselines, but their different capacity must be disclosed. If a standard pooled VQ or deterministic compression ties D, do not attribute a joint mechanism gain.
5. **Objective and sample support.** Specify exactly how local and pair SEX recovery enters quantizer fitting, how hard assignments are handled during optimization, and what is selected. If a soft assignment or training critic is used, the deployed hard-code view must be attacked afresh. Pair-code cells can be sparse even when local cells are well populated; describe smoothing/support handling without using screening labels to tune it. Fitted-cell entropy, MI, classifier loss or critic score is not a held-out attacker guarantee.
6. **Common protocol.** Check both purposes against *both* U0 and Ucal for accuracy, mean NLL and multiclass Brier. Report every seed, task, local view and coalition view; per-class recall and retention matter. Use S1/S2's strict boundary and simultaneous intervals. Accuracy or near-constant-code success alone is insufficient. If the code removes confidence distinctions, the NLL/Brier clauses should reveal the cost.

These gates can be inspected without real-data fits. Failure of the source/role guard or a mismatch between declared and deployed wire blocks D's Adult screen. A failure does not block unrelated finite-law tests.

### Findings on the revised pre-lock spec

The 2026-10-08 revised `METHOD_SPEC.md` resolves the 32-bit product-code concern by proposing one 1024-center full-vector code, freezes nearest-centroid ties, and states a code-only nonlinear head. The revision preceded the campaign lock and all Adult outcomes. The following implementation/control checks remain:

- **Codebook gradient — specification corrected, test pending.** With frozen upstream encoders, the usual VQ-VAE commitment term `||h - stopgrad(c)||²` has no gradient to codebook `C`, and a common straight-through decoder expression routes task gradient to `h` rather than `C`. The designer revised the spec to hold the selected integer argmin fixed only within backpropagation and differentiate through the selected centroid, including a true centroid reconstruction term. This resolves the mathematical ambiguity. The promised tiny fixed fixture must still show nonzero expected `C` updates from task and MMD terms independently before a real fit.
- **Decoded-MMD versus public ID — design corrected, runtime test pending.** The first draft compared SEX-conditioned *centroid vectors* although the wire contains exact centroid **indices**. For one-dimensional `h=-1` for SEX 0 and `h=+1` for SEX 1 with centers `-ε,+ε`, nearest-center IDs reveal SEX perfectly for every `ε>0`, while Gaussian-kernel MMD on decoded values tends to zero as `ε→0`. The designer revised the forward privacy loss to exact local one-hot code-frequency discrepancies plus a fixed 128-feature sketch of aligned pair IDs. A soft-distance surrogate supplies backward gradients to assignments. Static review of revised `trainer.py` confirms these operations are now coded. This targets exposed IDs, and the fixed-ID centroid-collapse example no longer fools the **forward** local loss. The pair sketch has a large nullspace and is not a privacy guarantee. The runtime fixture, assignment-change rate, ID-level contingency/recovery and full-view attacks remain required. If hard IDs remain unchanged, D's claimed information removal has failed even if another loss improves.
- **Coalition alignment — specification corrected, implementation pending.** The pair MMD must concatenate both released codes for the same person. The designer now specifies one minibatch of paired person indices, preserving registered group alignment across the two frozen encoders. Independently sampled purpose batches would estimate a product-of-marginals discrepancy and miss the complementary coalition clue. Verify the trainer actually follows the aligned-index rule.
- **Prototype parity.** The synthetic `prototype.py` scaffold has the 1e-6 scale floor and code-only heads. Revised `trainer.py` now statically matches the ID-level loss and aligned minibatch rules and includes `TorchDRelease` for fitted releases. It still accepts only supplied arrays, has no lawful Adult data loader or calibration/attacker pipeline, and must be described as synthetic-ready engineering rather than a complete Adult candidate. Runtime tests and role-specific deployment replay remain pending at this review point.
- **Numerical replay — code corrected, verification pending.** Initial training hard argmin used PyTorch float32 while the NumPy scaffold converted centers and latents to float64; near a Voronoi boundary this could change the transmitted code ID. The fitted `TorchDRelease` now repeats the PyTorch float32 normalization, argmin, GELU and softmax. Its static path matches training. Require exact training/deployment ID parity on each permitted scoring role, counts of ties/near ties, and a declared probability tolerance. A code-ID mismatch changes the privacy interface, so it cannot be waved away as ordinary rounding.
- **Generic control identity and distortion.** A task-only pooled VQ is a generic pooled compression arm at the same *capacity* and compute. Register one immutable alias if the extra generic-arm name denotes that exact fit. Its realized reconstruction distortion need not equal D's, however. The Team D handoff explicitly calls for generic compression at matched **distortion and capacity**. If S1's locked budget cannot include a predeclared distortion-matching control procedure, report the distortion mismatch and call this required comparison incomplete. D may still have an exploratory capacity-matched comparison; it cannot attribute a gain beyond compression or claim the required competitive comparison was complete.

## Attack and falsification checks

- Give attackers the raw code ID as a categorical variable, one-hot/cell lookup features, the public decoded code vectors, probabilities and decisions. Fit linear and fresh nonlinear readers with the common slate; include direct smoothed local-code and pair-code SEX readers when supported. A network on code vectors alone can be weaker than a simple lookup. Sparse pair codes can make lookup fitting unstable, so the adaptive attack also needs decoded vectors and marginal-code features on disjoint attack-fit people.
- Pair readers must include independently fitted recipient-1 and recipient-2 readers as options. For an ideal attacker, adding the second recipient cannot reduce available information; a reported pair AUC below the stronger local AUC reveals estimator/selection noise, not negative coalition leakage.
- Test complete-view bypass: reconstruct scores from public heads and codes; verify no continuous latent, teacher probability, assignment distance or residual norm travels outside the declared code wire. If an assignment confidence/distance is sent, it can reintroduce continuous information and needs its own matched control.
- Because probabilities and decisions are deterministic functions of the public ID, an ideal reader of the ID can reproduce their SEX signal. Report decision-only and probabilities-only recovery as disclosure-floor diagnostics; D cannot erase SEX information that its useful task decisions necessarily convey.
- Use separate finite laws for a removable SEX distinction, a complementary XOR-style pair clue, no complementarity, a feasible null, and useful confidence versus decision-only utility. Lock laws before algorithms run. A positive synthetic case shows mechanism capability on that law, not Adult prevalence.
- Run shuffled-SEX, constant/null-reader, and planted-leak engineering controls. A weak planted-leak or a persistent shuffled-label signal blocks the affected attack comparison. Inspect hard-code occupancy and train-to-screen shifts to detect cell memorization or overfit.
- Check both sequential orders. A joint arm has no joint-design advantage if either matched sequential arm or the strongest non-joint arm attains the same utility/recovery frontier. Local AUC may worsen while coalition AUC improves; enforce the local guard rather than averaging that harm away.

## Distinction and novelty

Team B's designer reports an end-to-end learned 16-dimensional diagonal-Gaussian latent with a KL-to-prior rate term. D's frozen-upstream, post-hoc hard VQ is currently a distinct operation and tests whether a **repair of acquired features** can work without relearning acquisition. If D later trains the upstream encoder jointly or B adopts the same VQ, make the overlapping component an ablation and record the identity amendment. Different quantizer strengths, codebook sizes, or critic schedules are not new families.

The components themselves are established. [LEACE](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03819) provides a closed-form linear erasure guarantee, which cannot certify nonlinear SEX protection. [Kernel-space adversarial concept erasure](https://aclanthology.org/2022.emnlp-main.405/) and [nonlinear density-matching erasure](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12341) already target nonlinear recovery; the latter uses a non-invertible projection. [Fairness repair by optimal transport](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v97/gordaliza19a.html) is related, but group-conditional transports cannot be copied here if they require true SEX at runtime. [The privacy funnel](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.1774) covers the established utility/privacy objective. D should claim an implementation or empirical advantage only after matched controls and fresh complete-view attacks; a new mechanism or solver claim needs a specific demonstrated difference from these methods.

[Taylor et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21859) study adaptive sequential releases under collusion with mutual-information privacy and expected-distortion or mutual-information utility, using a Blahut–Arimoto-style algorithm. D's sequential VQ orders are faithful controls for *D's own feature interface* if implemented as specified, but are not a reproduction of Taylor's finite-law algorithm or its optimality result. Label them by their actual construction.

The predecessor [matched removal benchmark](../../../../results/combined_matched_removal_benchmark_v1/RESEARCH_DECISION.md) found Adult nonlinear SEX AUC 0.815 untreated and 0.817 after official LEACE on its principal cell, despite LEACE's linear check passing; clean output probabilities preserved recovery after heavy noise. The prior [joint complete-view study](../../../../results/pcrl_joint_complete_view_method_v1/RESEARCH_DECISION.md) found no utility-feasible LEACE-based nominee and no established joint advantage. Those are historical development findings on different interfaces, not direct D controls or a prediction that D must fail.

## Current verdict

**Scientifically admissible as a distinct prototype, conditional on the six premise gates above.** The strongest possible favorable outcome would be both task-confidence conjunctions passing with a coalition AUC benefit at least 0.020 over the strongest eligible matched VQ/compression/sequential control and no local worsening above 0.010. The most important adverse alternative is that task-only pooled quantization or sequential quantization explains the same gain, or that complete pair-code readers recover the SEX clue. A lower AUC against an underpowered continuous-vector attacker cannot establish a D benefit.

This reviewer is independent of D's implementation but is a team reviewer, not the S4 independent replay auditor.
