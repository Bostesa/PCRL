# Team D prior work and claim boundary

Pre-lock primary-source review, 2026-10-08. These sources establish components and relevant controls; they do not establish a PCRL result. The review is bounded to the cited abstracts/method descriptions and the repository's prior study documents, so no priority claim follows from an absent example.

| Source | Relevant established result or setting | Consequence for D |
|---|---|---|
| [Belrose et al., LEACE, arXiv:2306.03819](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.03819) | Closed-form least-distorting **linear** concept erasure guards against linear recovery. | Keep a faithful LEACE comparator. Linear guardedness is not nonlinear SEX privacy. The old PCRL evidence already found nonlinear residual recovery. |
| [Ravfogel et al., R-LACE, ICML 2022](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v162/ravfogel22a.html) | Linear adversarial concept erasure on fixed pretrained representations. | Post-hoc repair of frozen features is established. D cannot claim that setup as new. |
| [Saillenfest and Lemberger, nonlinear concept erasure, arXiv:2507.12341v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.12341) | Learns a rank-reducing orthogonal projection using class-conditional density matching for nonlinear erasure, on pretrained embeddings. | A matched nonlinear distribution-matching projection is a relevant strong control when implementable. Its map is linear, its distribution objective targets nonlinear distinctions, and its rank makes it noninvertible. Calling all density matching novel would be false. |
| [van den Oord et al., neural discrete representation learning, NeurIPS 2017](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2017/hash/7a98af17e63a0ac09ce2e96d03992fbc-Abstract.html) | Vector quantization supplies a discrete latent and straight-through style training. | The hard code and its optimization are known tools, not a new solver. |
| [Champion et al., privacy-preserving speech representation learning with VQ, arXiv:2203.09518](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.09518) | Uses a VQ dictionary to trade task content against a sensitive speaker attribute. | Privacy-oriented VQ also exists. D's possible contribution is a tested two-recipient complete-view application and comparative result, subject to controls. |
| [Gretton et al., A Kernel Two-Sample Test, JMLR 2012](https://www.jmlr.org/papers/v13/gretton12a.html) | MMD compares distributions by an RKHS mean discrepancy, with efficient approximations. | Exact local code-frequency discrepancy and a fixed random pair-code sketch are training dependence objectives, not finite-attacker AUC, information guarantees, or fairness certificates. |
| [Makhdoumi et al., Privacy Funnel, arXiv:1402.1774v5](https://arxiv.org/abs/1402.1774) | Privacy and utility tradeoffs through a release mapping; the paper's log-loss setting relates to mutual information. | Compression can account for apparent privacy gains. Task-only quantization is indispensable. The present utility clauses use actual task NLL/Brier and are not equivalent to a mutual-information constraint. |
| [Alemi et al., Deep Variational Information Bottleneck, arXiv:1612.00410](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.00410) | End-to-end stochastic bottleneck representation learning. | Team B occupies this acquisition/retraining direction; D freezes upstream encoders and tests repair afterward. |
| [Taylor et al., Adaptive Privacy of Sequential Data Releases Under Collusion, arXiv:2601.21859](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21859) | Sequential collusion-aware releases under an information-theoretic model. | Compare both sequential orders, state whether our fitted second mechanism observes the first code, and make no Taylor-optimality claim for a nonadaptive empirical control. |

Fairness through group-conditional optimal transport is another known repair direction (e.g. [Chiappa et al., A General Approach to Fairness with Optimal Transport, AAAI 2020](https://ojs.aaai.org/index.php/AAAI/article/view/5771)). Group-specific transport that chooses a map using true SEX at deployment violates this portfolio's runtime contract, so such a map is not an eligible direct control. A group-blind transport or pooled map with equal output capacity can be a control, with the entire transformed feature included in attacks. An invertible transport alone does not erase information for an ideal reader.

The repository's earlier [nonlinear upstream pilot](../../../redesign_20260907_nonlinear_upstream_v1/PROTOCOL.md) and historical PCRL studies are development evidence in different settings, not Adult confirmation for this mechanism. The portfolio must compare D with the existing strong raw joint/adversarial feature controls and preserve their source identities.

**Claim ceiling before data:** established quantization plus established distribution matching, applied post-hoc to two purpose features. A matched performance advantage could be an empirical method lead. A joint-design lead additionally needs an advantage over both sequential orders and the strongest non-joint arm. No privacy theorem, population guarantee, or method novelty is presumed.
