# Frozen-release audit findings

The full audit matrix is complete. Catch-up changes the apparent J-versus-local ranking more than the 120-to-360 budget change does. The favorable F guarded-J comparison with the stronger guarded local penalty appears in the catch-up-inclusive pool; it does not hold under the independent attacks alone, and it still fails the primary utility-matching requirement. [Raw audit review](AUDIT_REVIEW.json), [scope contrasts](AUDIT_SCOPE.csv), [budget contrasts](AUDIT_BUDGET.csv) and [fixed utility comparisons](MATCHING_ANALYSIS.md) retain the contrary results.

For F G-J minus F G-L20, the mean unweighted development AB SEX gain difference is **+.006229** with standard or expanded independent attacks, and **−.005481** with catch-up included. Lower gain is better. The corresponding race differences are +.009755, +.006188 and −.007935 nats. These are different audit scopes with different inherited exposure. They do not show that optimization alone caused a change in information, and none makes the primary source-plus-residence comparison qualify at δ=.001.

A changed choice includes a later checkpoint from the same candidate trajectory, not just a different candidate name. Inherited singleton catch-ups are counted where they win an AB role; these are role selections, not distinct fitted trajectories.

No new F **AB** pooled prediction changes between budgets. Its sole F pooled change is seed 0 G-L025's B/race public-composition MLP: selected epoch 110 becomes 255, improving validation loss by .000846 but worsening development loss by .000626 unweighted and .000669 weighted. Across all 23 new pooled changes, development attack loss decreases in 15 and increases in eight unweighted; PWGTP reverses those counts to eight and 15 on the same choices. Neither validation fitting nor the weighting sensitivity supplies a uniform development ordering.

There is a numerical budget sensitivity in P seed 0 G-J versus G-L20 race under independent audits. G-J's selected AB candidate changes from a direct MLP at epoch 35 to the inherited B MLP at epoch 255. J-minus-local development gain changes from −.001177 at 120 to +.001756 at 360. The race directional qualifications that previously held disappear in all four utility panels; close qualifications at δ=.002 disappear too. This single contrast repeats across the two identical P independent scopes and the panel/δ rows—it is not 32 independent findings. The primary F/SEX result is unchanged. Full nine-category race assessment remains unassessable at both budgets.

Every new F/P system receives the same 11 forbidden-role audit. The five fresh candidates are logistic regression, two independently seeded 64/32 ReLU MLPs, and the two fixed boosted-tree configurations with minimum leaf 20/5. Each MLP runs one 360-epoch trajectory; the best validation checkpoint through 120 and through 360 come from that same path, and its real terminal optimizer/RNG checkpoints are saved. Each system also has nine own final training observers, including T. Their direct-coordinate catch-up resets Adam, preserves the observer's own final weights and class schema, verifies initial predictions, and allows epoch 0. A saved-only diagnostic is excluded from validation selection.

Report scopes separately: standard independent uses published wire inputs; expanded independent adds the legal public native-source-head compositions for F; expanded catch-up adds saved-start trajectories. Every A/B sensitive candidate is included in the same system's AB pool by exact coordinate projection. P's published probabilities already supply its native interface, so duplicate derived fitting is omitted. No candidate crosses condition, strength, seed or model-version boundaries. Pool inclusion guarantees a validation minimum no worse than its members; it imposes no ordering on development loss.

Fresh auditor parameters have attacker-fitting exposure only. F public-head compositions additionally inherit source-head fitting exposure, and saved-start catch-ups inherit the representation-fitting labels seen by their own observers. Source heads received 140 fitting passes and observers 260 passes under the fixed schedule; these exposures are not erased by resetting the catch-up optimizer. Epoch-0 selection favors the already trained observer and must not be described as an effect of additional catch-up optimization alone. T has its own trained observers and audits despite receiving no protection gradient.

Both audit budgets retain the same unweighted validation-selection rule. PWGTP is a sensitivity score on the same predictions, without a separate weighted winner. Signed prior-minus-attack log-loss gains remain signed. In particular, the reused P Iplus .025 seed-0 AB SEX tree still generalizes worse than the prior and contributes to that arm's negative mean gain. The result is retained, not clipped or reselected. Neither that failure nor a development reversal proves absence of recoverable information. Actual terminal checkpoints and nested prefixes are retained; this finite 360-epoch pass does not establish audit saturation.

All nine RAC1P categories remain in every class-schema report. Census code 4 is absent from independent attacker fitting and validation in all three splits. Representation-fitting support is 1/1/0, while development support is 0/0/1; the one seed-2 development person's PWGTP is 79. Inherited observers may therefore have different exposure in seeds 0/1, but this does not repair independent category support or establish a full nine-category assessment. [Reused exposed-label and prior controls](STATIC_CONTROLS.md) retain that class failure alongside their aggregate scores. Direct E, PCA32 and other historical references keep their actual original audit budgets and source identities. These are finite predictive audits on development households, not privacy certificates.
