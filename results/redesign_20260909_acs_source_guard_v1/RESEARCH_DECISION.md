# A correct local guard did not deliver a consistently source-feasible protected release

**The source-only feature control shows that the unweighted source floors are attainable; the tested guard does not consistently recover them while protecting the release.** F-T passes all three independent source-probe floors in 3/3 seeds on both validation and development evaluation, unweighted. Every guarded F/P condition fails at least one development seed under both weightings. In the primary F comparison, G-J has **0/3 closely matched and 0/3 directionally matched eligible seeds against either fixed guarded local control**, before requiring lower SEX recovery.

This is complete **DEVELOPMENT EVALUATION**, not a fresh confirmation. All 24 new interface systems, 24 matched reused systems, both audit budgets and all declared scopes are reported. U/PWGTP cells below score the same unweighted-validation-selected predictions; means use all three cohort-sharing seeds. Lower utility loss and lower measured recovery gain are favorable, but a negative gain can reflect attacker generalization failure. The strongest positive control is F-T: its all-seed unweighted source pass rules out an unavoidable architecture-level failure for that particular criterion and recipe. Within the guarded comparisons, F G-L20 lowers coverage probe loss by **.004552 U / .005773 PWGTP** relative to its own unguarded counterpart and increases source-pass counts from 1/3 to 2/3 under each weighting. These are specific benefits. They come with increased income loss and AB SEX recovery, and do not constitute a protected source-feasibility repair.

The strongest counterevidence to a coalition advantage is the complete G-J/G-L20 comparison. Mean AB SEX gain difference is **+.006229 U / +.002448 PWGTP under either independent scope**, but **−.005481 / −.008562 with catch-up included**. The favorable pooled ranking therefore depends on inherited saved-adversary exposure. It also carries **+.010705 U / +.006949 PWGTP B/race gain**, more A/SEX and A/race recovery, and no primary utility-matched eligible seed. G-L025 is also retained: G-J's unweighted pooled SEX difference against it is **+.001881**, not an improvement. The primary conclusion is not obtained by choosing the weaker local comparator.

The guard itself passed its declared numerical audit on 60,000 updates. It often improves the fixed native full proposal, yet some accepted finite-step task losses increase and independent probes do not reliably track native heads. [GUARD_DIAGNOSTICS.md](GUARD_DIAGNOSTICS.md) separates the ideal float64 projection, actual float32 cast bounds, current inherited Adam moments and the independently reconstructed source-only T diagnostic path. Correct local projection is not a guarantee of source generalization, transfer or privacy.

Both 120- and 360-epoch audits completed. The main F coalition pooled selections did not change with the longer budget; other roles, especially P, did change and can worsen on development even when validation improves. All nine race classes remain reported. Independent attacker fitting and validation lack race code 4, so full race and all-category protection remain **unassessable**. Catch-up starts have inherited representation-fitting exposure; an eligible epoch-0 winner does not establish a benefit from further optimization. See [AUDIT_FINDINGS.md](AUDIT_FINDINGS.md).
