# Fixed contrasts: local utility gains do not establish a guarded coalition advantage

All results are **DEVELOPMENT EVALUATION** on the existing cohort. U/PWGTP means use the same three fixed seeds and the same predictors selected by unweighted validation. Loss and signed recovery-gain differences below are in nats; negative favors the left system. Full paired means, descriptive sample SDs and every seed are in [PAIRED_AGGREGATE.csv.gz](PAIRED_AGGREGATE.csv.gz) and [PAIRED.csv.gz](PAIRED.csv.gz). ## Both fixed local controls remain in the primary comparison

At δ=.001, neither F G-J/G-L025 nor F G-J/G-L20 has a source-plus-residence eligible seed, close **or** directional, under either weighting. Source-only unweighted matching is also empty. These are fixed pairs across seeds, not comparisons between favorable means or different locals selected per seed.

This table uses expanded catch-up at 360 epochs. G-L025 is the unweighted pooled SEX counterexample to a blanket J advantage. G-L20 exposes a different cost: lower pooled coalition SEX/race recovery for J accompanies more B/race recovery. J also has higher mean A/SEX, A/race, A/coverage and B/income recovery than G-L20 under both weightings. No SEX/race average or mean-only Pareto claim hides those opposing directions.

The predeclared sensitivity panels do not turn this into a three-seed matched result. At δ=.002, F G-J/G-L20 has one unweighted close source-only qualifying seed, and one directional source-plus-residence qualifying seed, but still no close residence-qualified seed. Weighted G-J/G-L20 has one directional qualifying seed for source-only and source-plus-commute at δ=.001; at δ=.002 each also has one close qualifying seed. The weighted source-plus-residence panel remains ineligible. No fixed guarded pair qualifies across all three seeds under any declared panel, δ, audit scope or budget. The P G-J/G-L20 directional δ=.001 panel has one eligible development seed under each weighting, including all five tasks, but **zero** lower-SEX qualifications. These qualifications refer to the stated pooled scope; independent selections are not substituted. ## Guarding the same unguarded method changes the tradeoff in different directions

| F guarded minus its own unguarded counterpart | Coverage probe Δ U / PWGTP | Residence probe Δ U / PWGTP | AB SEX gain Δ U / PWGTP | AB race gain Δ U / PWGTP |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| G-J − U-J | −.000861 / +.000449 | −.002618 / −.000532 | +.006876 / +.003475 | −.006904 / −.007247 |
| G-L025 − U-L025 | +.000415 / −.000765 | +.005415 / +.004518 | −.000075 / +.002690 | +.003935 / +.004481 |
| G-L20 − U-L20 | −.004552 / −.005773 | −.001174 / −.001843 | +.004195 / +.007461 | −.004445 / −.002147 |

G-J has a real mean residence/race benefit versus its own unguarded counterpart under both weightings, but source-pass counts remain 2/3 and SEX recovery increases. G-L20 improves coverage, employment and residence but worsens income and AB SEX recovery; B/employment recovery rises by +.011128 U / +.012034 PWGTP. G-L025 worsens mean residence and commute under both weightings. Native/probe changes can disagree, as documented in [SOURCE_FEASIBILITY.md](SOURCE_FEASIBILITY.md). No single direction describes the guard across tasks or objectives.

P changes are generally smaller in utility, and no guarded P system recovers all source floors. A large P G-L025-minus-U-L025 SEX gain difference is particularly unsuitable as a direct information-loss story: the historical unguarded mean includes a negative gain driven by a seed-0 validation-selected tree's poor development generalization. The signed value is retained; it is not negative recoverability or privacy.

Against source-only F-T, all three guarded F systems have higher mean coverage, residence and commute loss under both weightings, while their pooled AB SEX/race gains are lower. For G-J, residence costs **+.016433 U / +.015048 PWGTP**, while AB SEX gains decrease **−.018807 / −.020355** and AB race gains decrease **−.047601 / −.042321**. All eleven mean forbidden gains decrease for F G-J versus F-T under both weightings. This is a broad measured recovery reduction purchased with utility costs, not a utility-matched protection advance or an all-category guarantee.

The .01 residence-loss advantage reference is met in 2/3 U and weighted G-J seeds, 3/3 U and 2/3 weighted G-L025/G-L20 seeds, and 3/3 T seeds under each weighting. No condition reaches the .01 commute-advantage reference in any seed under either weighting. This does **not** mean commute utility is guaranteed by the probability channel; it means this study does not establish that particular feature advantage.

Disclosure and capability must be checked together. At pooled 360, no guarded F/P residence comparison has all three seeds simultaneously meeting the .01 task-advantage and both .005 extra-attribute-gain numerical references, whether measured at A or AB. Isolated numerical passes remain visible: G-J seed 2 at AB under both weights, and G-L025 seed 0 at A weighted. They do not imply whole-policy feasibility or full race support. The F/P commute joint reference fails everywhere because its task advantage is absent. See [feature/bank criteria](feature_capability.json.gz), retaining the comparator identity; the direct E comparisons are separate rows, not extra P seeds.

All guarded final systems have 0/3 passing seeds on the original residential half-headroom reference under each weighting; F-T has 1/3 unweighted and 0/3 weighted. It is a named simple reference, not a source-feasible replacement.
