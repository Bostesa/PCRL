[REUSE_MANIFEST.json](REUSE_MANIFEST.json) binds the 18 matched unguarded systems, six ordinary-I controls, original data/PCA/subset identities, historical full optimizer forks and compatible static controls. No historical prefix, representation, utility head or auditor was refitted for this study.

New training artifacts are under `seed_N/training/CONDITION/`: `fork.pt`, `final.pt`, five fixed `diagnostic_epoch_*.pt` files, `training.json`, `frozen_releases.npz`, `release_freeze.json`, compact step diagnostics and immutable `complete.json`. The global [RELEASE_MANIFEST.json](RELEASE_MANIFEST.json) binds all 48 primary systems before new reserved-label fitting and permanently closes representation training.

Each new evaluation directory `seed_N/CONDITION/` contains the utility candidates, all fresh and catch-up auditor objects, actual terminal states separately from selected checkpoints, `predictions.npz`, `native_predictions.npz`, `releases.npz`, candidate/selection/parity records and `local_artifacts.json`. Its immutable completion record binds all fitted evidence. `seed_N/split_rows.npz` is necessary to align reconstruction and weighted score replay with the original household pools. Exact model/prediction replay requires these omitted objects; aggregate tables cannot substitute for them.

The original T all-step stream lacked displacement norms/dots. A separately frozen diagnostic reconstruction reproduced the three existing forward trajectories exactly, without fitting observers, selecting a replacement model or changing any original artifact. [The reconstruction record](t_source_reconstruction/REPLAY.json) and its compact all-step diagnostics are public. Duplicate verifier source snapshots remain local; canonical sources and their hashes are published. Its whole process cost is included in the scientific budget, while reconstructed steps are distinct from new model fits.

Per-unit `metrics.json` and `audits/audit_selection.json` are published as exact deterministic gzip siblings. [COMPACT_UNIT_EVIDENCE.json](COMPACT_UNIT_EVIDENCE.json) binds both original and compressed bytes. The original plain files remain local and can be restored losslessly for report regeneration. Aggregate compressed tables similarly retain the complete derived metrics, exclusions and lineage; redundant plain exports are omitted. Compact all-step guard records contain numerical diagnostics and batch hashes, without raw rows or vectors of person predictions.
