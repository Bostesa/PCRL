Task A: Theorem 2 label-shift validation
----------------------------------------
20 (dataset, purpose, disallowed-attr) cells across Adult / HMDA / Diabetes.
For every cell we computed the maximum of
    min{P(A=a), P(A=a')} * d_TV(pi_i^a, pi_i^a')
across pairs of attribute values, calling the maximum gamma_min.
  gamma_min >= 0.1   :  0/20  cells
  gamma_min >= 0.05  :  3/20  cells (all on Adult)
  gamma_min >= 0.01  : 12/20  cells
  raw range          :  0.0009 (Diabetes quality_research vs race)
                       0.0804 (Adult education_assessment vs income)
Honest finding: Theorem 2 is essentially vacuous on these benchmarks.
The marginal label distribution barely shifts across attribute groups,
so the bound permits arbitrarily tight invariance without predicting
any task-accuracy floor. The leakage we see in §5 is therefore not
the label-shift channel Theorem 2 covers; it is high-cardinality
residual structure in X that survives a low-rank LoRA adapter.
Outputs:
  theorem2_label_shift.json
  theorem2_label_shift_table.tex
  PAPER_PASTE_thm2.md
