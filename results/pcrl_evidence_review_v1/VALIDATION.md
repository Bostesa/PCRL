# Validation of the Terminal B audit

What was checked, by what, and what the check cannot establish.

## 1. Independent reproduction of the published evidence

| Check | Result |
|---|---|

Independence is in the estimator arithmetic, the selection rule, the household indexing and the
interpreter, not in the randomness: replaying the published intervals requires the protocol's
declared `default_rng(20260917)` and 2,000 replicates.

## 2. Conventions verified rather than assumed

Studentisation (`se` = bootstrap SD; `T_b = max_k |θ*−θ̂|/se_k`; 95th percentile, `method='higher'`);
degenerate endpoints excluded from the maximum (none occur in F1–F4); weighted means as ratio
estimators with the replicate's own weight denominator; one shared set of household draws across
every method, seed, task, weighting, mode and view; both rows of a household sharing a resample
count; equal 1/3 seed averaging applied before resampling; three seed predictions for one person
counted as one person; and the exact cancellation of the shared prior inside a paired contrast, which
makes the paired difference of absolute and additional recovery identical.

## 3. Tests

`tests/pcrl_evidence_review_v1/` — **21 passed**.

* 8 mathematical fixtures (exact enumeration, not simulation): XOR detected only by an interaction in
  `H`; the magnitude counterexample with `E[Z²(S−½)] = 0.75` exactly and *no* `H`-basis moment able to
  see it; conditional null with all moments < 1e-12 under a correct nuisance and > 0.05 under a
  constant one; free-rank optimum equal to the positive-eigenvalue sum; sign selection on an old
  matrix strictly suboptimal for a new one; and the invariance argument showing a squared-output
  penalty is not a trace form.
* 13 sealed-year loader tests: open without a lock refused; seal/open logs the access after the lock;
  tampering with a data input, deleting an input or editing the manifest all abort; a code-only
  amendment is accepted; amendments naming a data path, an unlocked path, or written out of band by
  hand are all rejected; re-sealing refused; sealing a non-existent input refused; the loader returns
  no label arrays; and one round-trip over published files of the **already spent** 2017 study.

## 4. Manuscript checks

`papers/pcrl_evidence_v1/main.pdf` — 12 pages, **0 overfull boxes**, **0 undefined references or
citations**, every `\cite` key present in `references.bib`. All five tables are `\input` from files
generated by `make_paper_assets.py`; all five figures are generated from the same evidence. No number
in the manuscript was typed by hand from a report. The compiled PDF was inspected page by page for
clipped tables, unreadable figures and broken references.

## 5. What this validation cannot establish

* That the attack families are strong. Every recovery number is a floor.
* That the moments certify anything. They do not; §4.1 of the manuscript and the fixtures show why.
* That 2017 respondents differ from 2018 respondents, or that 2016 respondents differ from either.
  Public identifiers cannot support this.
* That the surveyed prior work is exhaustive. A bounded search cannot establish novelty.
* That memory pressure caused the original numerical corruption. It is the observed condition.
* Anything about Terminal A's new mechanism. Its results were not available when this package was
  completed; see the pending addendum in `REVIEW_INDEX.md`.
