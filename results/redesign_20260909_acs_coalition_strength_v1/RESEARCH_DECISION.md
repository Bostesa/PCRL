# Coalition-strength research decision — 2026-09-09

The larger local-control grid weakens the claim that coalition training improves the protected tradeoff. **F J at β=.1 has lower mean coalition SEX recovery than every local point under the catch-up-inclusive audit, but no nonidentical F pair is source-feasible and close on all four primary utility tasks, even in one seed.** Every fixed system fails at least one source floor in seed 1. A stronger local point also matches J's SEX result under independent audits, while J increases several individual forbidden recoveries. The evidence supports a conditional SEX effect and a utility/disclosure tradeoff, not a demonstrated overall advantage.

The strongest positive finding is the reduction in pooled mean F coalition SEX recovery at J .1: .005406, versus .010475–.018875 across the five local points. This is not a monotone coefficient response: J .2 rises to .012550. The strongest counterevidence is the Iplus .2 comparison above, including its audit-scope reversal and individual race cost. [ANALYSIS.md](ANALYSIS.md) reports every local comparator, source failures, all-task checks and sensitivity results.

The original residential half-headroom reference also remains demanding: only F Iplus .025 and F J .05 pass in one unweighted seed each; only F J .05 passes in one weighted seed. Every other condition, including F J .1, all P conditions and direct E, passes in 0/3. A residence benefit over the prediction interface therefore does not mean retention of the original PCA32 advantage over the rich banks.

Negative signed attack gains are retained. In particular, P Iplus .025's negative mean SEX gain is driven by a validation-selected tree that generalizes worse than the prior on seed 0; it is evidence of auditor generalization failure, not privacy. Census race code 4 is absent from independent attacker fitting **and validation**; representation-trained observers see 1/1/0 examples across seeds. All nine categories remain reported, and full race or all-target certification is unassessable. Longer finite audits do not remove that limitation.
