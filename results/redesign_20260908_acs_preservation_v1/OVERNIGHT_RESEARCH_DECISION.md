# PCA16 preservation: overnight research decision — 2026-09-08 UTC

**Preservation improves residential transfer, but does not establish a robust protected utility/recoverability advance over beta=0.** All eight new final conditions improve mean residential loss over their corresponding no-preservation arm under both unweighted and person-weighted scoring. The strongest persistent penalty yields the best unweighted residential means, while retaining almost all linearly recoverable teacher structure and largely eliminating the *measured difference* between protection-on D and protection-off C. This is a useful training-objective result, not general information destruction, a privacy guarantee, or novel PCRL.

All results are **DEVELOPMENT EVALUATION** on the original households. Three seeds share one cohort; their SDs are descriptive. No mapper was selected using residential/commute performance. All24 new finals and the full predeclared360-epoch extension completed. Scientific unit/audit wall time was **672.27 seconds (11.20 minutes)** on one M4 Pro numerical thread, including the1.15-second failed preflight. **Strongest positive finding.** The beta.1 warmup-only C arm improves mean residence by .005422 unweighted/.001867 weighted versus C_init, while reducing mean pooled SEX gain by .003281/.003984 and race gain by .004835/.005329. These means survive360 epochs. This is a favorable limited comparison without a protection gradient; it is not complete dominance. Pooled race recovery worsens in seed0, weighted residential loss worsens in seed2, and source losses rise within the original allowance. Every new final has higher mean civilian-at-work and public-coverage losses than its beta0 counterpart.

For protected D, beta1 warmup-only improves residence by .004323 unweighted/.001272 weighted at nearly unchanged unweighted pooled recovery (SEX gain −.000233, race −.000016). Weighted SEX/race gains instead increase .001958/.002154. This is conditional evidence of a useful tradeoff, with mixed seeds and source costs; it is not dominance.

**Continuous preservation has task-dependent benefits.** Persistent minus warmup-only improves unweighted residence in all four matched mean comparisons. Under PWGTP, three signs reverse: beta.1 C +.001414, beta.1 D +.000098, beta1 C +.002538; only beta1 D improves both weights (−.004653 unweighted/−.000866 weighted). At beta1, persistence improves unweighted income loss but worsens civilian-at-work and commute losses; unweighted public coverage is essentially tied for C and worse for D. Weighted public coverage improves for C. Thus continuous preservation is not uniformly better. The [stage plot](utility_stages.png) and [stage/weighted analysis](ANALYSIS.md) also show W_beta improvements over I; beta1 W improves unweighted residence versus historical W while worsening its weighted mean by .001227.

**The stronger penalty changes the tradeoff rather than solving it.** With warmup-only preservation, beta1 D reduces SEX/race recovery and improves commute relative to beta.1 D, at some unweighted residential and income cost. Weighted residence improves for both C/D. Beta1 C instead reveals slightly more of both attributes than beta.1 C. With persistence, beta1 improves residence but increases both attribute gains. Beta1 persistent D gains .008976 unweighted/.002138 weighted residence versus D_init, while increasing pooled SEX gain by .009031/.013396 and race gain by .022158/.026226. Its D−C attack-loss differences are only .000523 SEX/.000549 race, versus .009650 SEX/.012559 pooled race for beta1 warmup-only. Actual protection mapper gradients remain nonzero; this observation does not identify a missing gradient or prove an optimization mechanism.

**Stronger audits did not change the verdict.** All216 non-control fresh/catch-up trajectories retained a best validation checkpoint from epochs5–80. Every corresponding candidate prediction/score—and every independent or pooled release ranking—was unchanged at360. All216 non-control360 endpoints had worse validation loss than their best checkpoint. The12 exposed-control MLP trajectories improved slightly and selected360, but selected control winners and category-support failures remained unchanged. There were no parent-halving or joint feature/bank numerical feasibility changes. This supports no further extension of these exact trajectories; it does not establish saturation over attack families or privacy. [Budget comparisons](AUDIT_BUDGET.md), [curves](EXTENDED_CURVES.csv), [rankings](AUDIT_BUDGET_RANKINGS.csv), and [saved/fresh/catch-up evidence](CATCHUP.csv) preserve the distinctions and inherited fitting exposure.

**Coordinate movement is not equivalent to losing recoverable teacher structure.** Beta1 warmup-only C has direct standardized teacher error1.498880 but affine reconstruction error.266758, versus fitting-prior error1.000353 on development. Beta.1 C moves *less* (1.318994) yet has *worse* affine error (.347024), reversing the direct-error ranking. Beta1 persistent C/D have direct errors.010723/.010945 and affine errors.008800/.008816: nearly all teacher structure remains linearly recoverable. All affine designs have rank17 including the fitted intercept. These fitting-only decoder results are also evaluated on disjoint source-validation inputs. They neither exclude nonlinear recovery nor guarantee transfer; the teacher itself is leaky. [Diagnostic plot](preservation_affine.png), [per-coordinate errors](COORDINATE_ERRORS.csv), and [frozen decoder records](beta_1/seed_0/affine_freeze.json) document this separately from utility and attacks.

No new final halves the original PCA32 SEX gain on unweighted development under either audit scope, and no unweighted development joint feature/bank numerical comparison passes. A few weighted numerical comparisons pass, but **race code4 lacks attacker-fitting/validation support**, so no complete all-attribute assessment is available. All nine classes, signed gains, failed margins, undefined quantities and exposed-control failures remain in [support evidence](SUPPORT.md). The margins are descriptive references, not privacy budgets or noninferiority tests.

**One next method experiment:** replace uniform matching to raw PCA16 with persistent distillation toward a **fixed jointly attribute-residualized PCA16 teacher**, while retaining the ordinary source/reconstruction objectives and a matched C/D comparison under the same360-epoch audits. Fit that teacher transformation only on representation-fitting inputs and SEX/RAC1P labels; residential/commute labels stay reserved. This tests whether selective preservation can retain transferable structure without insisting on reconstructing the teacher's attribute-bearing directions. Prior LEACE source losses and nonlinear residual leakage are explicit risks to measure, not reasons to assume success. The present limitation most relevant to design is that uniform teacher preservation rewards useful and protected structure together. This next experiment was **not launched**.
