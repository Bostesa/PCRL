# Nested audit budget sensitivity

Fresh MLP and direct-coordinate catch-up checkpoints at 120 and 360 are selected from each same 360-epoch trajectory by unweighted attacker-validation loss. Logistic/tree candidates remain fixed. Negative loss differences mean additional measured recovery. Validation gains need not transfer to development. Fresh and catch-up exposure remain separate; saved observers are diagnostic only.
