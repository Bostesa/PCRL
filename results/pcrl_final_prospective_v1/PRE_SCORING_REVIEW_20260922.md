# Pre-scoring protocol and implementation review — 2026-09-22

This review was completed while the 2016 fits were running and before the final-label cache or any final score existed. The requested background reviewer left no persisted review in the shared handoff. This is an independent review of the registered implementation, not a new selection step.

- The A, B and AB wires in `evaluation._wire` use H_A, H_B and [H_A,H_B], respectively. A release adds its own Z only to A and AB. The dependency scheduler admits same-role H candidates, same-release A candidates for AB and H's B candidates for AB. J is not an ancestor. The fixed residence decoder and public H_A global offsets are utility candidates only. The private code, Q row, household index and labels do not enter predictor features.
- `expected_token_loss` computes the weighted sum of per-token negative log probabilities with the registered 1e-9 floor. `score_unit` uses this function for selected, independent and fixed-decoder losses. It does not take log loss after averaging predictions.
- The ten frozen families and three anchors in `common.PANEL` match `PANEL.json`. All anchors share the one admitted 2016 household partition. `paired_household_bounds` resamples a common union of households and averages three anchor-level weighted ratios equally. It does not count anchors as independent datasets.
- Primary endpoints are exactly two ten-clause conjunctions: task differences M−J under two weightings with upper bound ≤ −0.003, plus J−M sensitive loss under four roles and two weightings with upper bound ≤ 0.001. The one-sided critical value is 1.959963984540054 at α=.025 per candidate. Secondary endpoints are 7 comparators × 10 = 70, with two-sided Bonferroni critical value 3.3840362518645613. Signs and denominators match the registration. No endpoint can replace a primary clause.
- Fitting selections use only the fitting pool and the role-specific validation pool, balanced validation loss and lexical ties. The final loader checks the evaluation lock for every final-label load. At review time the final-label cache and final score markers were absent on the Linux host.

Two pre-outcome engineering defects were corrected. `lock.py` originally hashed artifact manifests without checking their referenced weights and omitted the restored frozen objects. It now checks every fitted artifact and validation choice, binds the staged inputs and restored frozen objects, and refuses a lock if final labels or scores already exist. `independent_replay.bootstrap_se` originally used the first role's households as its entire bootstrap universe; it now uses the union across roles and anchors. These corrections do not alter the registered release panel, fitted models, scoring loss, estimands, bootstrap used for primary inference, thresholds or claim rules. Their red/green regression checks ran on the registered Linux host before final scoring.

The 2016 admission audit is recorded in `DATA_INDEPENDENCE.md`, `DICTIONARY_2016_VS_2018.json` and `ADMISSION_CHECKS_2016.json`. Exact H-output preservation is separately checked from the 2016 service log loss and accuracy. The earlier 2018 development results remain historical evidence, not a fresh-year outcome.
