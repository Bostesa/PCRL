# ACS 2016 prospective evaluation: research decision

The two predeclared releases **Q and D17 both fail the full registered primary claim**. Each passes all eight sensitive-recovery clauses against J, but neither passes either task-improvement clause at the registered −0.003-nat margin. This is a failure to establish the complete conjunction, not evidence that the task effects are zero or that either release causes excess sensitive disclosure.

The final pool has 23,684 people in 15,928 households. All three frozen anchors use these same households; the reported estimand averages three anchor-specific ratios and the common 10,000-draw household bootstrap pairs every comparison. The lock was created before the final labels were read and binds 15,268 files, including all 156 fitted units and validation selections. The aggregate inference SHA-256 is `09566ca36b3389cbba73757d306c576554cc32952276ed2a33ba897d774b75a9`.

Each task upper bound had to be at most −0.003. For the eight sensitive clauses, the estimand is `CE_J − CE_M`, and every upper bound for Q and D17 is below zero, stronger than the registered +0.001 cap for this measured attacker slate. The selected predictors' absolute task CE, averaged across anchors, is 0.494541 / 0.491551 for J, 0.491275 / 0.487555 for Q, and 0.489799 / 0.486409 for D17 (unweighted / PWGTP). These point improvements do not meet the uncertainty-adjusted task rule.

The 70 prespecified secondary endpoints use simultaneous two-sided 95% Bonferroni intervals (`z = 3.3840362518645613`). Q has lower task CE than the simple RR75 and W75 controls under both weightings: Q−RR75 is −0.011652 and −0.011170, and Q−W75 is −0.007055 and −0.006875. Against each simple control, however, Q has **strictly higher measured sensitive recovery in six of eight endpoints**; the simultaneous intervals do not establish the registered all-eight sensitive-cost cap. A complete task/privacy benefit over these matched simple controls is therefore **not established**. Q is also strictly worse on unweighted task CE than D17 and D33; its PWGTP task differences are unresolved by the simultaneous intervals. Q's sensitive recovery is not shown strictly lower than D17 or D33 on any endpoint. Direct supervised and E/S adaptation controls have better task CE than Q, with different measured disclosure costs. Passing against J alone cannot establish an optimization-method advantage.

The registered panel was completed without candidate replacement or outcome-driven stopping: 156/156 fit units and 156/156 final score units, zero recorded unit failures, zero invalid or quarantined final scores, and 10,000/10,000 accepted bootstrap draws. The result contains 20 primary, 70 secondary and 114 descriptive endpoints. The [service check](SERVICE_2016_FINAL.json) found exact H bytes in all 30 family/anchor interfaces. Its separate 2016 final-pool accuracy, averaged over anchors, was 0.87413 for income, 0.89052 for civilian work, and 0.75992 for public coverage (corresponding log losses 0.27862, 0.31151, and 0.50826). Byte preservation and year-shift accuracy are different findings. The private independent replay checked all 156 units with zero rows beyond tolerance and maximum per-person loss difference `3.55e-15`; all 204 endpoint point estimates matched within `1.39e-17`. A [post-lock verifier correction](POST_LOCK_VERIFIER_CORRECTION.md) affected only that independent sanity check, not registered scoring or inference. The persistent-token fixture repeated each sampled token exactly for the same secret and agreed with the exact mean loss within 1.03 Monte Carlo standard errors in all three tested units; it is secondary to the exact expected-loss estimator.

This prospective 2016 study is separate from the previously used, supervised 2018 development programme. It does not replace that programme's failed 706-endpoint adjusted decision. The frozen teacher/code and controls used task or sensitive labels during earlier development, so this is a fresh-year evaluation of a supervised, fixed release family, not an unseen-task or universally private guarantee. The measured privacy conclusion is limited to the registered access views, roles and attacker slate. Failure to reject a contrast is not equivalence, and failure to prove a non-inferiority cap is not proof of excess disclosure.
