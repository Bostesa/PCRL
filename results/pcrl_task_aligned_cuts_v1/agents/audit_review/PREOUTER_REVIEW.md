## Equations and signs

For fixed predictor `a` and state code `t_i`, the attack coefficient `A[t,z]=Σ_{i:t_i=t} (w_i/Σ_j w_j)(−log a(s_i|H_i,z))` makes `L_a(Q)=Σ_tz A[t,z]Q[t,z]`. It is affine only while `a`, its input route and coefficient rows remain fixed. The floor `L_a(Q)≥rho−delta` has the correct protection direction: positive delta tolerates an attack-loss reduction (more recovery) relative to the fitted D17 reference. A one-state fixture with cost `[0,1]` and cut `Q[0,1]≥.5` has LP objective .5 versus deterministic optimum 1. This checks signs and a genuine same-bank randomization gap. It says nothing about fresh attacks.

For the independently fitted audit, attack recovery of candidate versus comparator is `(CE_H−CE_candidate)−(CE_H−CE_comparator)=CE_comparator−CE_candidate`; negative favors candidate. Task contrast is `CE_candidate−CE_comparator`; positive harms task. A superficial `CE_candidate−CE_comparator` attack comparison would reverse the desired sign.
