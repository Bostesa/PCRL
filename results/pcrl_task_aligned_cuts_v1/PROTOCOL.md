# Registered development protocol — task-aligned constraint exchange v1

Registered before any new ACS candidate outcome was inspected. This is a new 2018 supervised development study, not an amendment to the closed Q or full-view protocols. The source and input pins, deadline and limitations are in [START_STATE.md](START_STATE.md). The owned branch is `research/pcrl-task-aligned-cuts-v1`; `PROTOCOL_LOCK.json` hashes this file and the deterministic queue before the first scientific unit.

## Contract and data roles

The frozen historical T0 has 32 states from allowed PCA32 X_A and four H_A service columns. Every initial channel has 17 historical token labels. One sampled token is sent beside unchanged H; A sees H_A+Z and AB sees H_A+H_B+Z. B alone sees its unchanged historical service. The individual hidden T, row Q[T], current Y/S, RNG state and unused draws are never released. A service-output parity check is mandatory. A repeated call for the same person must reuse its token under a server-side cache or keyed replay; the key is not public. The threat model is one persistent token per person. No repeated-query or population privacy claim follows.

Within each anchor, historical representation_fit teacher/codebook rows have already trained the frozen encoder; its household-disjoint mechanism rows estimate all channel coefficients. `downstream_fit` trains the common U1 task decoder and later fresh audit models; `attacker_fit` trains initial references and exchange best responses. Before any new candidate outcomes, the union of `downstream_validation` household IDs across all three anchors is sorted by `(SHA256(UTF8(household_id)), household_id)`; the first `floor(H/2)` unique households are `inner_selection` and the rest are `inner_pilot`. This global household-only rule is identical across anchors, which share households. Inner selection fits no model; it selects the U1 decoder, reference routes, candidate settings and audit routes. Inner pilot measures independent descriptive development risk but does not select these objects. `attacker_validation` is the software-locked **outer development assessment**, read only after selection lock. All historical pools are household-disjoint within anchor, but the same 2018 people can recur across anchors and past studies. The outer pool was historically used and is not an untouched scientific holdout. For each protected role and each weighting separately, reference attack selection minimizes that weightings exact expected-token loss on inner_selection over every legal fitted H-only/D17 ancestor candidate, with lexical tie-breaking. The selected predictors risk floor rho is then frozen on the same representation_fit mechanism coefficient rows, class schema, probability floor and weight normalizer used by candidate cuts. A selected bank may adapt to those coefficient labels across exchange rounds; no fixed-family population inference is claimed from that pool. The independent final audit fits on downstream_fit, selects on inner_selection, and scores the locked panel on attacker_validation. All protected classes retain their original schema; unsupported cells are reported, never deleted or filled using outer labels. Admission found RAC1P class index 3 absent from the allowed fitting and inner-validation resources; all RAC1P predictors must retain nine output coordinates with explicit prior smoothing, and no unsupported-class risk guarantee is claimed.

## Arms, targets and objective

For each candidate and role, exact per-token log loss is grouped by frozen T0 state. U and PWGTP coefficient matrices are separately normalized on the **same valid coefficient rows**; their average is the task objective. No second state-mass multiplication is allowed. U0 is the historical fixed 17-action H_A decoder. U1 is one shared audit-like H_A+token decoder trained on equal training mixture mass of historical Q, D17 and uniform-token coverage, then frozen before any crossed candidate fit. Its person-level expected token loss, not loss of averaged probabilities, defines the U1 cost. The same U1 decoder and supervision are used for all U1 controls. The unprotected U1 rowwise minimum D_U1 is computed before constrained U1 fits.

P0 is historical **A-only** empirical CMI under the original two H_A cells, with independent SEX/RAC1P and U/PWGTP constraints at budgets 0.005, 0.01 and 0.02 nats. AB remains audited. U0P0 at 0.01 reuses Q if exact object/contract hashes agree. P1 is fitted full-view attack-loss constraints for A/SEX, A/RAC1P, AB/SEX and AB/RAC1P, both weightings. For frozen attack a and normalized weights w on coefficient rows, `L[a](Q)=Σ_i w_i Σ_z Q[t_i,z](-log a(s_i|H_view_i,z))`; a cut is `L[a](Q)≥rho[role,weight]−delta`. Positive delta permits worse protection by lower sensitive loss; negative requires improvement. Pilot deltas are −0.002, 0 and +0.002 nats and are **not** CMI budgets. All P1 banks include compatible H-only ancestors; the same role/class/weights/rows/floor are used for rho and cuts. These are empirical fitted-risk constraints, not MI, DP or arbitrary-attacker bounds. P0 and P1 have different targets and equal numbers cannot be equated.

The nominal center panel has 4 crossed arms ×3 registered budgets =12 configurations at anchor 0. If promoted, the same 12 per anchor run on anchors 1 and 2 (36 nominal total). First required unit is anchor-0 U1P1 delta=0. Every release is audited; aliases are deduplicated by exact release hash but retained as named rows. An unsuccessful or infeasible point remains recorded. Before P1 minimization enumerate all 17 constants and solve a common-slack phase-I LP; if infeasible, preserve registered failure and fit one explicitly descriptive minimum-feasible-relaxation fallback. No floor or role is moved to obtain a registered pass. The initial optimization bank uses the inherited standard slate (logistic, exact/sampled tree and 120-epoch MLP) uniformly across H, D17, historical Q, coverage, A/AB protected roles and B H-only ancestors. Independent final audits use the full inherited catchup slate uniformly. P1 exchange retains all valid cuts, uses at most eight pilot rounds and twelve promoted rounds, checks fixed-bank violations at 1e-5 and separately records LP solver residual and heuristic oracle error. An initial-bank round-zero release is a technical checkpoint, not a completed exchanged or independently audited result. The search stopping without a new violated fitted attack is not a global guarantee.

## Matched controls and assessment

Controls include H, J, historical Q, D17, D33, D_U1, 17 constants, withholding and randomized-response rates 0.50/0.75/0.90 on both D17 and D_U1, restored LEACE/SPLINCE/OptNet if contract-compatible, a matched-gradient finite-channel adversarial adaptation, and a privacy-feasible deterministic MILP plus bounded search for each promoted P1 point. P1 LP and MILP use the same frozen union of cuts, data, task cost and compute budget; the MILP reports valid global lower bound and incumbent. A fixed-bank stochastic/deterministic gap requires a feasible LP upper objective below a valid MILP lower objective on that same bank. Timeout yields unresolved gap. P0 deterministic ACS optimum is not presumed from rounding; only feasible replayed search is reported. Ancestor model routing respects A versus AB service views and token semantics. Every panel arm receives compatible common audit slates and catch-up opportunity.

The outer assessment is opened once, only after `SELECTION_LOCK.json` pins at most two operating points, all candidate/control hashes, decoder/reference/audit route hashes and the endpoint family. If a scorer error is found afterward, keep both records and rescore the unchanged lock with a dated correction. No candidate adaptation after opening. Household-paired intervals resample shared households once across anchors/methods; anchors are not independent populations. Intervals condition on fitted models and do not cover design selection or full survey uncertainty.

The primary task/privacy screen for every candidate versus each matched comparator requires both task CE differences ≤+0.001, all eight sensitive-recovery differences ≤+0.001, and AB/RAC1P differences ≤−0.002 in both weightings. Task contrast is `CE_task(candidate)−CE_task(comparator)`; protected-recovery contrast is `R(candidate)−R(comparator)=CE_sensitive(comparator)−CE_sensitive(candidate)`, so negative favors the candidate. In addition, residence improvement over H must be ≥0.01 at point estimate, with uncertainty separate. The primary algorithmic comparison includes corresponding unprotected optimizer (D_U1 or D17), historical D17, validation-selected feasible deterministic control, and matched gradient adaptation; missing any comparator blocks the broad claim. J and external methods are reference frontiers with access disclosed. Selection uses development validation only. A script enumerates all selected slots × comparators × two task/eight sensitive weighting endpoints and computes family size before outer scores. Use simultaneous **two-sided** Bonferroni 95% intervals, `z=Phi^-1(1−0.05/(2m))`, and apply the same correction to favorable and unfavorable comparisons. A secondary task-first tradeoff may be reported descriptively with unchanged signs; no retroactive margin changes.

## Promotion and registered contingencies

Anchor-0 strict point screen versus D17 and D_U1: each task weighting ≤+0.001, no protected endpoint >+0.001, and AB/RAC1P ≤−0.002 in both weightings. If passed, run full crossed panel on anchors 1 and 2. If failed, still run the four center points (U0P0,U1P0,U0P1,U1P1) on anchors 1 and 2 before diagnosing. Then at most two contingency families and twelve extra stochastic maps total may run before the 17:00 UTC science cutoff. Decision order: integrity/oracle repair, then C2 decoder adaptation if fresh inner probe materially beats U1 or changes ranking, then C1 64-state same-residual code only if a ≥0.003 task deficit in both weightings remains and decoder checks do not explain it, then C3 bank diversity if useful task gains vanish against fresh attacks. C2 allows three matched alternating rounds; C1 only one 64-state family and two budgets, identical richer access to controls; C3 at most two predeclared new attack families/initializations with a positive-control check. No threshold, target, year or outer set changes. If no contingency applies, finish controls and audit rather than inventing a path.

Prior probabilities before outcomes: 0.7 that U1 improves its own fixed task surrogate over U0 at a comparable bank; 0.35 that this survives a fresh utility probe; 0.25 that P1 survives fresh full-view attacks at useful task utility; 0.10 that it beats a privacy-feasible deterministic control; 0.05 that it beats the matched adversarial adaptation in both task/privacy tradeoff and demonstrates a distinct algorithmic advance. These are falsifiable directional judgments, not evidence.
