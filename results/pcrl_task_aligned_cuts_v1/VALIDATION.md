## Frozen semantics checked before candidate scoring

- The independent scorer evaluates `Σ_z p_i(z) [-log q(y_i|H_i,z)]` per original person, with the inherited `1e-9` probability floor. It rejects malformed token laws and incomplete class-probability tensors. The synthetic smoke gives **1.203972804325936** nats; replacing it with `-log Σ_z p_i q(y_i|H_i,z)` gives **0.6931471805599453** and fails the fixture.
- A's predictor input is the four archived H_A service columns plus the token; AB sees those four, the two archived H_B columns, and the token. B alone sees H_B. A predictor never receives H_B, a hidden T0 code, a probability row, a seed, or the person's label.
- A withheld token is a **visible eighteenth token** when a control uses withholding. It is scored and fitted as a distinct category, not aliased to H-only. All 17-action releases retain 17 categories.
- Token enumeration does not create independent people or households. Candidate selection minimizes one validation balanced score, `0.5 U + 0.5 PWGTP`, with lexical tie break; the selected model is then replayed for both weightings.
- Every primary endpoint is enumerated from locked selected slots, distinct comparator identities, five roles and two weightings. Task orientation is `CE_candidate − CE_comparator`; sensitive orientation is `CE_comparator − CE_candidate`. The AB/RAC1P material target is `≤−.002`, other sensitive guards `≤+.001`, and task cost `≤+.001`.
- The paired inference wrapper uses one common household multinomial draw across endpoints and anchors, three anchor-specific weighted ratios, and an equal anchor mean. It rejects nonidentical original-person masks, households or weights in a pair. Anchors share households and are not treated as independent replicate populations.

## Independent counterexamples

- XOR: `Z=S xor H` is marginally independent of `S` but reveals `S` given H. The same construction with H_B shows the AB coalition can exploit information unavailable to A. A coarse privacy diagnostic does not certify the full view.
- Identical task and sensitive labels under the same view and predictor family have identical log-loss gains, so a selective win is impossible in that fixture.
- A constant token is independent of the sensitive label under an exact law. Fitted finite risk gaps need not be exactly zero; underfitting and sampling differences must be reported rather than cancelled by an arbitrary offset.
- A one-state two-action fixed-bank problem with costs `[0,1]` and attack cut `Q[0,1]≥0.5` has stochastic optimum `0.5` and deterministic optimum `1.0`. This is a true same-bank integrality gap and verifies the loss-floor direction. An unrelated rounded deterministic map cannot serve as the optimum.
