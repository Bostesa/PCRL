## Independent counterexamples

- XOR: `Z=S xor H` is marginally independent of `S` but reveals `S` given H. The same construction with H_B shows the AB coalition can exploit information unavailable to A. A coarse privacy diagnostic does not certify the full view.
- Identical task and sensitive labels under the same view and predictor family have identical log-loss gains, so a selective win is impossible in that fixture.
- A constant token is independent of the sensitive label under an exact law. Fitted finite risk gaps need not be exactly zero; underfitting and sampling differences must be reported rather than cancelled by an arbitrary offset.
- A one-state two-action fixed-bank problem with costs `[0,1]` and attack cut `Q[0,1]≥0.5` has stochastic optimum `0.5` and deterministic optimum `1.0`. This is a true same-bank integrality gap and verifies the loss-floor direction. An unrelated rounded deterministic map cannot serve as the optimum.
