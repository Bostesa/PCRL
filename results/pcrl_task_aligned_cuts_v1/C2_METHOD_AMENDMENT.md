`P_m,r(z | i) = (Q_m,r−1[t_i,z] + D17[t_i,z] + 1/17) / 3`.

The three own-decoder costs are generally different. Therefore **do not** subtract the own-decoder LP objective from the own-decoder MILP lower bound and call it an integrality gap. Only this same-cost LP/shadow-MILP pair can yield a fixed-bank stochastic/deterministic gap. The independently adapted deterministic track remains the empirical matched control even if the shadow problem has a gap. A finite-bank gap says nothing by itself about fresh probes or stronger attackers.

## Budget, selection and replay

At most three rounds on each of three anchors create at most **nine new LP candidate channels**, below the registered twelve-additional-candidate-map cap. Matched gradient control grid outputs and deterministic maps are retained as controls with their own counts and hashes; they are never relabeled as extra LP candidate nominations. Each output directory is immutable and has a full artifact receipt. A technical failure may be retried only under the existing bounded recovery rule with both attempts preserved. Phase-I infeasibility and descriptive fallback remain visible; no role/floor may be dropped to make a candidate eligible. Before fitting, a separate execution lock must pin all three anchors' completed source channels/banks, source tree, exact run IDs, argv, output paths, cost/decoder seed rule and sidecar byte SHA.

Use only `downstream_fit` for task-decoder and independent-probe fitting, `inner_selection` for decoder/route/hyperparameter/round selection, and existing mechanism rows for cost coefficients. `inner_pilot` remains descriptive and cannot choose a round; `attacker_validation` stays software-locked until the coordinator's final selection lock. `inner_selection` has already been used in this development study and will be adaptively reused; report that limitation. All three anchors share households, so choose **one round number for all anchors**, not one favorable round per anchor. The *sole* C2 nomination rule is [NOMINATION_RULE_AMENDMENT.json](NOMINATION_RULE_AMENDMENT.json), written before C2 outcomes: slot 1 is the fixed initial center; slot 2 requires registered-bank feasibility in all anchors and all eight protected-recovery mean-anchor contrasts versus center no greater than `+0.001` on inner_selection, then selects the eligible common round with lowest equal-anchor mean balanced independently fitted utility-probe cross-entropy (lexical unit-ID tie). If none is eligible, leave slot 2 empty and report the C2 curve. The primary task/privacy screens versus matched deterministic, gradient and other controls remain unchanged *reporting tests*, not a second nomination rule. The selected-panel cap remains two slots with the complete endpoint family.

Every selected C2 release and its matched controls require an independent inner-development audit with exact token expectation and identical compatible recipes. Preserve common-decoder, own-adapted-decoder and freshly refitted task losses side by side; do not treat surrogate improvement as fresh utility. Retain all four protected roles under U/PWGTP and H-only/transferable ancestors. Report fixed-bank feasibility separately from fresh-attack behavior. Do not open outer labels, extend the 17:00 UTC science cutoff, or interpret a negative C2 result as permission to alter the rules.
