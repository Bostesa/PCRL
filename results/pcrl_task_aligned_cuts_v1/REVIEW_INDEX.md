# Review index — 2018 task-aligned cuts development

Start with [RESEARCH_DECISION.md](RESEARCH_DECISION.md) for the supported result and [QUICKSTART.md](QUICKSTART.md) for the executable release. [PROTOCOL.md](PROTOCOL.md) and [PROTOCOL_LOCK.json](PROTOCOL_LOCK.json) contain the pre-candidate rules; [START_STATE.md](START_STATE.md) records prior evidence and the fixed 14:00 America/New_York deadline. This is not a continuation of the completed 2016 evaluation.

The machine-readable evidence is [CORE_FACTORIAL.csv](CORE_FACTORIAL.csv) (36 nominal configurations, ten aggregate endpoints each), [MATCHED_CONTROLS.csv](MATCHED_CONTROLS.csv) (declared anchor-0 simple, deterministic and gradient controls), [POINT_SCREENS.json](POINT_SCREENS.json) (registered descriptive point rules recomputed from those tables), [CROSS_ANCHOR_INFEASIBILITY_CERTIFICATE.json](CROSS_ANCHOR_INFEASIBILITY_CERTIFICATE.json), and [OPTIMIZATION_CERTIFICATES.json](OPTIMIZATION_CERTIFICATES.json). [RUN_STATUS.json](RUN_STATUS.json) distinguishes complete, fallback and not-triggered units. The historical continuous-output comparators are [context only](agents/controls_data/EXTERNAL_2018_CONTEXT.json) because their release contracts differ.

[EXCHANGE_PILOT_SCREEN.json](EXCHANGE_PILOT_SCREEN.json) separately compares the one anchor-0 exchanged channel with its same-bank deterministic control, round zero and the frozen validation-selected comparators. It is an independent inner-pilot diagnostic on used 2018 data, not an added core factorial arm or outer assessment.

[POST_EXCHANGE_INNER_PILOT_UNCERTAINTY.json](POST_EXCHANGE_INNER_PILOT_UNCERTAINTY.json) independently replays the exchanged channel against round zero and its same-bank MILP with one shared-household resample and descriptive family-20 intervals. Its fitted weights and original-person contributions remain in the private archive.

[INNER_PILOT_UNCERTAINTY.json](INNER_PILOT_UNCERTAINTY.json) gives descriptive one-anchor, 40-endpoint Bonferroni household intervals from the reused 2018 inner pilot. They are not the registered three-anchor outer inference; its private contribution/seed replay receipt stays outside Git.

[SELECTION_LOCK.json](SELECTION_LOCK.json) and [INFERENCE.json](INFERENCE.json) are explicit **not-triggered** records: the frozen all-anchor P1 target was infeasible before outer assessment, so neither is a completed evaluation lock or an inferential result. [C2_NOT_TRIGGERED.json](C2_NOT_TRIGGERED.json) preserves the decoder-ranking trigger and the separate feasibility reason that prevented the registered contingency. [NEXT_ACTION.md](NEXT_ACTION.md) proposes one new, separately registered calibration test; it does not amend this run.

Implementation and review live in `experiments/pcrl_task_aligned_cuts_v1/`, `analysis/pcrl_task_aligned_cuts_v1/`, and `tests/pcrl_task_aligned_cuts_v1/`. [METHOD.md](METHOD.md), [METHOD_DIFFERENCE.md](METHOD_DIFFERENCE.md), [VALIDATION.md](VALIDATION.md), and the [independent claims review](agents/audit_review/FINAL_CLAIMS_REVIEW.md) explain the coefficient signs, release contract, matched scope, tests and limitations. Private fitted weights, original-person contributions, frozen input objects, logs, local archive and restore receipts are retained outside Git; [TRAINED_MODEL_MANIFEST.json](TRAINED_MODEL_MANIFEST.json) and [REUSABLE_INPUTS_PINNED.json](REUSABLE_INPUTS_PINNED.json) give hash-pinned pointers without publishing those records.
