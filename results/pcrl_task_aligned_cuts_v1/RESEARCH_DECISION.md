# Research decision — task-aligned constraint exchange

This is a supervised **2018 development** study on historically used data. The anchor-0 task-aligned, one-round exchanged channel and its round-zero U1P1 checkpoint are trained, callable 32-state/17-token releases, but the registered primary algorithmic advantage is **not established**. Both private packages are labeled `EXPERIMENTAL_NO_ADVANTAGE`; deterministic controls remain separately identified baselines. The completed 2016 prospective decision is unchanged, and no 2016 person-level records were used here.

The main registered obstacle is exact finite-bank infeasibility across anchors. In anchor 1, an H-only A/RAC1P cut has frozen floor 1.2499300018261117 nats while its token-invariant loss is 1.2419135441045435. In anchor 2, an H-only AB/RAC1P cut has floor 1.2757213060109978 while its loss is 1.2700837606083266. Because every row of either coefficient matrix is constant across all 17 actions, **no channel or task decoder can change these losses**. The unavoidable gaps, 0.008016457721568226 and 0.00563754540267114 nats, match independently solved phase-I minima to numerical precision. Even the most permissive registered delta, +0.002, leaves gaps 0.006016457721568226 and 0.00363754540267114. [The hash-pinned certificate](CROSS_ANCHOR_INFEASIBILITY_CERTIFICATE.json) is a proof about this fitted bank, coefficient pool and frozen reference construction. It is not a general impossibility for privacy-constrained releases.

The actual inner-pilot comparison also gives no basis for a competitive claim. All 12 anchor-0 crossed configurations fail the registered material AB/RAC1P point screen versus D17 and D_U1 under the standard inner-pilot audit slate; 11 have zero measured AB/RAC1P increment and the remaining U0P0 budget-0.005 point has positive, unfavorable increment. This selected-slate result does not show zero true disclosure. The U1P1 center's fresh task loss is worse than D17 by 0.005342909 U / 0.005200352 PWGTP nats, worse than D_U1 by 0.003724572 / 0.004577716, and worse than the same-initial-bank deterministic MILP by 0.001244373 / 0.000890159. These are **descriptive inner-development** contrasts, not multiplicity-adjusted outer inference. The complete aggregate [factorial](CORE_FACTORIAL.csv), [matched-control table](MATCHED_CONTROLS.csv), and generated [point screens](POINT_SCREENS.json) retain every declared endpoint and status.

The gradient selector retained all 12 registered settings and chose GRADIENT_011 from five distinct bank-feasible maps by balanced **inner-selection** task loss only. Its rule was committed after the first four pilot control audits, so this remains a transparently timed development comparator, not a pre-pilot confirmatory choice. The center's better pilot task score versus this selected setting does not meet the protected-improvement conjunction or outweigh its losses to D17, D_U1 and the MILP.

The standard inner-pilot slate selected an H-only AB/RAC1P route for all 30 named control releases, giving zero measured token increment at that endpoint. This makes the registered material-improvement screen fail in the observed table, but it also limits what this slate can say about absolute race disclosure. No population non-disclosure or equivalence follows from those zeros.

An [independent household-paired descriptive replay](INNER_PILOT_UNCERTAINTY.json) used 1,494 original people in 1,004 households and one common 10,000-draw resample for all 40 center/control endpoints. For example, the center-minus-GRADIENT_011 U task contrast is −0.012102706 nats with a descriptive family-40 interval [−0.023848198, −0.000357214]; its AB/RAC1P contrast is exactly zero under the selected standard slate. These intervals condition on the fitted models and design choices and do not substitute for the unrun outer assessment, repeated selection uncertainty or full survey-design uncertainty.

On anchor 0's initial 312-cut bank, the stochastic LP objective is 0.4678505964229197 nats with replayed dual lower bound 0.4678505964229196. The same-bank deterministic MILP closed at valid floating-point lower bound and feasible objective 0.46797998599855606, a numerical integrality gap of about 0.000129389576 nats. This verifies a finite fitted-bank advantage on the **fixed common task objective** only. The fresh utility probe reverses that ordering, and protected routes selected H-only in the first independent pilot. A tiny fixed-bank gap does not establish a better independent task/sensitive frontier, a bounded best-response oracle error, algorithmic novelty, or population privacy. [Optimization certificates](OPTIMIZATION_CERTIFICATES.json) distinguish those claims.

One registered paired exchange round trained equal-budget response attacks against the stochastic center and initial-bank deterministic control, retained all old cuts, and added 12 violated SEX cuts. On the resulting common 324-cut bank, the stochastic LP objective is 0.4682873058695374 nats; the deterministic MILP has feasible objective 0.4686263337714318 and valid floating-point lower bound 0.46862633376208584. The numerical same-bank gap is at least 0.0003390278925484713 nats. Independently fitted standard inner-pilot slates found **no measured protected recovery change** between the exchanged channel, round zero, and the matched 324-cut MILP. The exchanged channel's fresh residence loss is higher than round zero by 0.000166236913 U / 0.002369563441 PWGTP nats and higher than its same-bank MILP by 0.000181427252 / 0.001164157832. This is a one-anchor development diagnostic, not a converged attack oracle, a new all-anchor eligible nominee or an outer evaluation. Further exchange rounds were not run after the all-anchor P1 impossibility proof; oracle error remains unbounded.

The hash-pinned [exchange pilot screen](EXCHANGE_PILOT_SCREEN.json) finds zero of six comparator point rules passing. Exchange-minus-comparator task CE, U / PWGTP, is +0.005509146 / +0.007569916 against D17, +0.003890809 / +0.006947280 against D_U1, +0.000181427 / +0.001164158 against its matched 324-cut MILP, +0.000166237 / +0.002369563 against round zero, and −0.011936469 / −0.011106201 against validation-selected GRADIENT_011. The positive task advantage versus that one gradient setting has no measured AB/RAC1P recovery benefit; every one of these AB/RAC1P contrasts is zero under the selected standard slate. The fixed-bank stochastic gap is therefore a verified numerical optimization result without an independently measured tradeoff win.

The separate [post-exchange paired replay](POST_EXCHANGE_INNER_PILOT_UNCERTAINTY.json) reproduces all 20 exchange-versus-round-zero and exchange-versus-matched-MILP endpoints on the same 1,004 households. Its descriptive family-20 intervals for task loss versus the matched MILP are [−0.007495311, 0.007858166] U and [−0.008261980, 0.010590296] PWGTP. These include zero and do not prove the small point-estimate task worsening; nor can they establish equivalence or rescue the absent material protected improvement. No three-anchor outer uncertainty was computed.

The common U1 decoder ranked the center ahead of the initial MILP and GRADIENT_000 controls while independently fitted utility probes ranked it behind them. This is evidence of a surrogate-to-audit ranking mismatch, not causal proof that decoder adaptation would have solved the transport failure. The registered C2 ranking trigger was documented, but C2's nine candidate rounds and matched adaptations were **not triggered** once the all-anchor H-only infeasibility was proved. No reference floor, delta, role, margin or outcome family was changed to recover a pass. The anchor-1/2 center P1 channels are explicitly descriptive phase-I fallbacks, not registered-feasible releases.

The historical J, LEACE, SPLINCE and OptNet releases were hash-verified and their [existing 2018 aggregate results](agents/controls_data/EXTERNAL_2018_CONTEXT.json) retained as context. Their continuous auxiliary outputs and training contracts differ from the present 17-token T0 interface, so their old audit tables are not paired matched controls. The registered common final catchup slate and fresh external-method audits were not triggered after the all-anchor feasibility gate failed; absence of those scores cannot be recast as a win over published methods.

The nominal 36-core matrix contains 20 fitted and audited configurations, 14 registered feasible under their **own** target (10 coarse P0, 4 P1), six descriptive P1 fallbacks, 16 not triggered, and zero failed fits/audits. The 360 factorial rows are ten aggregate endpoints per nominal configuration, not independent fitted releases. The historically reused outer 2018 assessment was not scored: [the selection record](SELECTION_LOCK.json) and [inference record](INFERENCE.json) truthfully contain zero nominated slots, zero assessment endpoints and no intervals or p-values. A historical monolithic prepared object transiently deserialized outer labels before sanitized copies were introduced, though no prelock outcome indexing or scoring was found; this is a software-seal limitation, and the 2018 sample cannot be described as untouched confirmation data.

The supported next action is a separately registered reference-calibration study that makes a declared public-contract reference feasible on the same coefficient rows before optimizing channels. It must then compare against the feasible deterministic MILP/search, matched gradient adaptation and visible-token randomization using fresh compatible development audits. [NEXT_ACTION.md](NEXT_ACTION.md) gives that constraint. This run does not silently restart under a changed floor or reopen the completed 2016 evaluation.
