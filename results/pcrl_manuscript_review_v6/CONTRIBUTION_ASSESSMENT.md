# CONTRIBUTION_ASSESSMENT — v6

**Strongest supported claim (unchanged).** On the locked ACS 2017 partition (Study 1), a penalty built from
the coalition's joint view lowers measured sex/race recovery against both an equal-strength and an
equal-total-mass local control: 14 of 16 sensitive cells better under simultaneous 95% intervals, none worse,
two unresolved. Its residence cost is bounded by a point rule only.

**Primary contribution (unchanged).** The release problem — an immutable published output plus a channel
appended for one recipient — and its measurement contract.

**What v6 adds.** A sixth study that changes the objective rather than the mechanism: instead of asking for
lower recovery at comparable utility, it asks for higher capability at bounded added disclosure, beside a
frozen J. Three results are worth keeping:

1. **A clean negative pilot with a measured reason.** The appended channel really does carry capability the
   frozen J channel lacks (11–45% of the residual teacher variance, every configuration, all anchors) and it
   carries sensitive information with it at every adversarial weight tried. Capability and disclosure fall
   together, monotonically, in all three policies. The declared .001-nat operating point was never reached;
   the closest configuration missed it (+0.0016 unweighted, +0.0021 person-weighted) at the lowest capability
   in the grid.
2. **The earlier evidence holds no utility-first candidate either.** Re-scoring the completed 372-slot study
   under the new criterion with a correctly enlarged family (m = 2480): 0 of 124 configurations pass against
   J, 0 against LEACE-on-A0. This closes the "maybe a near miss was hiding in it" reading without touching
   that study's own verdict.
3. **A third independent failure to find a stronger attacker.** 0 of 8 prespecified recipes beat the standard
   slate under a frozen adoption rule, with a passing positive control.

**Why the negative is informative rather than empty.** The two negatives now come from opposite directions.
Studies 1–5 asked for less disclosure at equal utility and could not get past J; Study 6 asked for more
capability at bounded disclosure and found that the extra capability is not separable from the extra
disclosure by this adversarial term.

**Corrected reading (see CORRECTIONS_V6 D7).** An earlier version of this assessment said the inseparability
appeared "on a proxy that never saw a residential label, which removes the usual 'the utility target leaked'
explanation". That inference is wrong and is withdrawn. Excluding residence labels keeps the *reserved task*
out of fitting; it does not make the target innocuous. The target is the residual of A0 — the unprotected
channel with the largest sensitive recovery in this line of work (+0.032 A/SEX, +0.051 A/RAC1P over H) — so
improving on the proxy can be achieved precisely by carrying sensitive-correlated directions. "The objective
itself rewarded sensitive information" therefore remains a live explanation of the pilot outcome, and the
study cannot distinguish it from "no extension of J in this family separates capability from disclosure".

**Not claimed.** Any bound on I(S; Z | H); protection from appending a deterministic channel (impossible by
the inclusion fact); equivalence from nonsignificance; confidence statements for the pilot (nothing was
nominated); anything about r ∈ {4,8}, a nominee comparison or a 2017 transport of the extension (never run);
fresh-year confirmation (2016 sealed).

**Remaining scientific gap.** Whether *any* family can separate capability from disclosure beside this
channel — the pilot rules out this one at r = 2 with these budgets, not the question. A larger β or a
narrower R on this design would be outcome-driven; it needs a fresh prospective protocol. Also open: whether
the A0 coalition race effect of Study 5 has an analogue on a channel with J-like utility.
