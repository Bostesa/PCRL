## Paper paste — §5.3 per-dimension Lagrangian retraining

The post-hoc diagnostics on the scalar mean-of-clamped-slack variance retraining identified the dual aggregation as a candidate failure mechanism, in that averaging the slack across 64 representation dimensions discounts the worst-dim signal as soon as any single dim crosses the σ_d ≥ 0.5 floor. To test whether this aggregation is the binding limitation we replaced the scalar dual with K = 64 individual per-dimension Lagrangian duals per purpose (192 duals per HMDA cell) and re-ran the constrained optimization for 75 epochs warm-started from the Round 5 final checkpoints on all three HMDA seeds. The per-dim formulation eliminates the global-relaxation pathway because each dim's dual fires independently of the others, and the Lagrangian contribution becomes Σ_d λ_{p,d} · max(0, 0.5 − σ_{p,d}) rather than λ_p · max(0, 0.5 − σ_p)·mean. None of the 18 HMDA pair-seeds reached the cleanly-compliant criterion under the per-dim variant, identical to the scalar variant's 0/18 and below the Round 5 baseline's 2/18. The mean per_dim_std_min across the 9 (purpose, seed) units was 0.185 under per-dim duals against 0.207 under scalar and 0.205 at baseline, and the per-dim variant did slightly improve mean effective rank to 4.95 against 4.57 (scalar) and 4.70 (baseline) with all 9 purposes clearing the eff_rank ≥ 2 audit threshold. Critically, of the 576 individual per-dim duals across the three cells, zero saturated at λ_max = 100; the largest dual value observed was 1.68 against an initial value of 1.0. The duals had ample headroom to apply additional pressure but the encoder gradient signal was insufficient to push the worst-dim σ values past the 0.5 floor even when each dim received its own dedicated dual. Compliance was largely preserved (the 18 R²_onehot values remain in [0.001, 0.118], with the only above-τ pairs being the same pricing_analysis/race and pricing_analysis/sex on seed 0 that already failed the strict criterion at baseline). Read together the two retraining experiments establish that the collapse attractor is structurally inherent to the proxy-Lagrangian optimization with a linear-R² constraint applied through a low-rank LoRA adapter on a frozen backbone, and is not an artifact of either the scalar dual aggregation or the per-dim aggregation choice. Escaping it would require a structural change such as relaxing the LoRA rank constraint, replacing the linear-R² constraint with an alternative that decouples concept erasure from representation-geometry shrinkage, or removing the backbone-freeze constraint that prevents whole-network adaptation, and we leave each of these directions to future work while reporting the original 7/60 cleanly-compliant figure as the honest characterization of the architecture's reach.
