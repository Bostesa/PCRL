# Task-directed finite-channel release: novelty and assumptions

Primary-source review, checked 2026-09-21. This is a research assessment, not a submission or a claim of exhaustive priority search. Relevant full-text sections were read for all seven papers; exact locators and URLs are recorded in `LITERATURE_SOURCES.json`.

## Defensible claim

Study whether distinctions discarded by a task-only input code improve the privacy–utility trade-off for a fixed prediction service. A true task posterior can be sufficient for predicting the target while failing to preserve the entire set of channels available under sensitive-information constraints. The useful result is a proved separation with an explicit witness, followed by a controlled finite-channel comparison. This review does not establish that the separation itself is a first result in the literature.

The programme fixes a supervised residence teacher and baseline, a 32-cell residual-score code `T0`, nested task and predicted-sensitive-risk refinements with at most 64 cells, a fixed action alphabet, and decoder `d(h,z)=sigmoid(logit(b(h))+a_z)`. It optimizes a row-stochastic channel `Q[z|t]` for cross-entropy under empirical conditional-mutual-information budgets for local A and coalition AB, under both unweighted and PWGTP-normalized distributions.

The scientific contribution must be the demonstrated phenomenon, its boundary conditions, and reproducible evidence. Nullspace feasibility, stochastic kernels, quantization, convexity, task-aware privacy objectives, conditioning on side information, and nested refinements are established ingredients or elementary consequences; claim no novelty for them.

## What the closest papers already cover

- [Rassouli & Gündüz, *On Perfect Privacy*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1712.08500): Proposition 1 covers general observable data, not only output perturbation; Section III/Theorem 1 develops the perfect-privacy polytope and LP. The present programme cannot claim a new nullspace criterion.
- [Makhdoumi et al., *From the Information Bottleneck to the Privacy Funnel*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1402.1774): Sections II–III separate fixed distortion from optimized posterior log-loss. Freezing the decoder is precisely what makes the proposed utility linear; this does not solve the general information-utility privacy funnel.
- [Lopuhaä-Zwakenberg, *The Privacy Funnel from the viewpoint of Local Differential Privacy*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2002.01501): Sections II–V distinguish average information leakage from likelihood-ratio protection and address attribute side channels. Two selected views are a limited threat model.
- [Calmon et al., *Optimized Pre-Processing for Discrimination Prevention*](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper/6988-optimized-pre-processing-for-discrimination-prevention.pdf): Sections 2–3 already use optimized randomized preprocessing, contextual constraints, and finite-sample analysis. Its guarantees are for a different loss, constraint system, and sampling model.
- [de Freitas & Geiger, *Trustworthy Representation Learning via Information Funnels and Bottlenecks*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2211.01446): Sections 3 and 8 directly precede broad claims about task-aware privacy, conditional predictors, and fairness–privacy–utility trade-offs. The proposed restricted empirical optimization has a different scope from their learned variational representations.
- [Belrose et al., *LEACE*](https://papers.nips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/d066d21c619d0a78c5b557fa3291a8f4-Paper-Conference.pdf): Sections 3–4 establish linear guardedness and least-change affine erasure. Compare it as a linear-erasure baseline; its formal guarantee is not small conditional mutual information.
- [Holstege, Ravfogel & Wouters, *SPLINCE*](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/file/26dbc8565974cffe8a44731aa09b2aa8-Paper-Conference.pdf): Theorems 1–2 make task covariance preservation and the choice of downstream retraining central. A fair comparison must specify whether its head is frozen, regularized, or retrained without regularization.

## Mathematical scope of the proposed result

For each fixed empirical law `p_m(s,h,t,y)` and fixed decoder, define `C_m[t,z]=sum_(s,h,y) p_m(s,h,t,y) CE(y,d(h,z))`. Expected loss is `sum_(t,z) Q[z|t] C_m[t,z]`. A selected weighted combination of these losses is linear; a worst-case-over-laws objective is convex after an epigraph formulation. State explicitly which utility objective is used.

For fixed `m` and view `v`, `I_m(S;Z|H_v)` is convex in `Q`: condition on each `h`, then mutual information is convex in the induced conditional channel, which is affine in `Q`. Intersecting finitely many such sublevel sets and the row-simplexes remains convex. This certifies only the specified finite empirical problem and action alphabet. Teacher fitting, bin construction, decoder fitting, and choosing refinements must occur outside that claim.

If `T0=g(T1)`, copying each coarse row to its refined children embeds every coarse channel in the refined family with the same induced joint law. Therefore the best refined objective cannot worsen under identical laws, decoder, alphabet, budgets, and feasibility tolerances. A strict improvement requires evidence; apparent worsening is a numerical or protocol diagnostic. Comparisons between equally sized but nonnested task/risk refinements have no such ordering. Added cells alone are not a novelty or a sufficient explanation of a task-specific advantage.

Task sufficiency means `Y` is conditionally independent of the original input given the true posterior and relevant baseline context. It does not supply the analogous sensitive-label independence needed to reproduce an arbitrary original-input release. A stronger joint sufficiency condition for target, sensitive variables, and all audited contexts can remove this gap. Learned probabilities and 32-cell quantizations need not even be sufficient for the target. Separate an exact theoretical posterior example from approximation, calibration, and quantization effects in the empirical study.

## Assumptions that must accompany results

1. **Exact release semantics.** The linear loss describes drawing one `Z~Q(.|T)` and applying the fixed decoder. Publishing the entire row `Q(.|T)`, a probability vector, the risk score, or a mixture prediction creates a different released variable. Cross-entropy of a mixture is not the linear expected cross-entropy above. State whether a recipient sees `Z`, the decoded probability, and which context.
2. **Frozen, deployable maps.** Fit teachers, baselines, risk estimators, cuts, action values, and policy choices without final-test access. The risk refinement must be predicted from permitted inference inputs, not the true sensitive label. Record clipping of `b(h)` and finite action values. Fitting the decoder jointly with `Q` voids this convex-program statement.
3. **Conditional privacy is incremental.** `I(S;Z,H)=I(S;H)+I(S;Z|H)`. A zero budget can coexist with substantial pre-existing disclosure through H. The exact H matters: protection conditional on binned or summarized H does not imply protection conditional on the recipient's full features. Conditional information need not vary monotonically as context grows, which is why A and AB require separate checks.
4. **Average and distribution-specific protection.** Small CMI bounds average improvement of an ideal log-loss attacker for the stated distribution. It is not a worst-case record guarantee, differential privacy, membership protection, fairness for every group, or protection against arbitrary auxiliary information. Repeated releases and release of fitted model parameters need their own analysis.
5. **Two empirical measures.** Requiring both unweighted and PWGTP-weighted constraints protects exactly those fitted measures. It does not establish robustness under arbitrary reweighting, population shift, or survey-design uncertainty. Report each measure and view separately, including baseline sensitive entropy.
6. **Statistical evidence.** Plug-in CMI computed analytically from a fitted finite channel avoids Monte Carlo release noise but still has distribution-estimation and selection error. Sparse cells, tiny conditional strata, unequal weights, and household dependence can make apparently tiny training leakage misleading. Hold out households; freeze the complete pipeline before final evaluation; report cell support and effective sample sizes and use uncertainty procedures matching the sampling unit. Nonlinear attack success is a lower bound on exploitable information, while attack failure is not an upper bound.
7. **Optimization evidence.** Report solver termination, recomputed objective and all constraint residuals, maximum simplex violation, tolerance, and baseline feasibility. Claims of global optimality require adequate optimization evidence; numerical feasibility on development data is not population certification.
