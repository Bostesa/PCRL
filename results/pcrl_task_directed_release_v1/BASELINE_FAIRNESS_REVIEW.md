# Source-only review of supervised fitting information

Reviewed before comparative ACS validation inspection on 2026-09-22. No outcomes were opened. Sources were `run.py`, `encoding.py`, `mechanisms.py`, `baselines.py`, `PROTOCOL.md` and `DATA_USE.md`.

The current methods share the same frozen residence teacher, label authorization and inherited preprocessing/service contracts. That makes the original supervised affine controls policy-matched, but not fully row/information-matched for adaptation. Original LEACE/SPLINCE moments use teacher-fit RF households only (nominally 48%), including teacher logits that are in-sample there. Q costs and protected laws use separate mechanism households (nominally 40%), where the teacher is out of fit. Trisk also fits protected risk predictors on teacher-fit households, so its protected supervision spans the nominal 88% union.

The direction of any performance effect cannot be determined from this difference alone. Original erasers have more protected fitting rows than T0/Ttask Q laws considered alone, but receive no analogous fresh mechanism-label adaptation; Trisk uses protected information from both slices. In-sample teacher logits are not test contamination, but their fitting covariances need not represent the same prediction regime as mechanism-row logits. The methods' objectives also differ: Q uses balanced task costs and both empirical privacy weightings, while canonical affine controls use unweighted covariance restrictions. That difference must remain disclosed rather than mislabeled as a shared objective or weighted affine guarantee.

The prospective repair is both mechanism40 and teacher-fit/mechanism-union88 affine pairs, retaining originals and identical audit competence. Mechanism40 isolates fresh adaptation; union88 addresses Trisk's broader protected-label information. Teacher-internal-validation rows are excluded from the union, and the frozen teacher is never retrained. All three settings in each affine family enter validation nomination together.
