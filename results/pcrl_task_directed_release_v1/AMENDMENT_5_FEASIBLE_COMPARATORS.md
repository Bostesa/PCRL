# Prospective amendment 5: feasible comparator sets and completed controls

Recorded 2026-09-22 00:49:58 UTC, before any comparative ACS outcomes have been inspected and while current-run evaluation remains sealed. No fitted model, audit budget, scientific margin, weighting, main historical-J screen, or candidate ranking changes.

This corrects a mismatch between implementation and the original requested Section 16 and committed protocol. Section 16 requires comparisons against “every nominated feasible family” and states that constant and unconstrained extremes remain visible but need not both be dominated simultaneously. The protocol likewise requires “every eligible family nominee” and adjusted superiority against “each-eligible-family”. An empty **comparison set** does not establish superiority; this does not mean every scientifically completed family must contain a feasible point at the prescribed operating caps.

## Availability and scientific eligibility are distinct

For each default control family, the original registered primary-17 settings are mandatory comparison attempts. All must have completed, valid three-anchor audits before competitive claims can use that family search. A missing, invalid, or incomplete mandatory attempt blocks the registered competitive claim. Custom required families default to requiring all their explicitly listed settings. Optional registered-33 additions remain on the full attempted frontier; incomplete unscheduled additions are visible and cannot nominate, but do not veto completed primary comparisons. The separate programme completion gate still requires every frozen scheduled unit.

A fully completed family whose every setting fails the route's unchanged validation operating constraints is **scientifically ineligible**. It remains visible with its attempted frontier and exclusion reason, but does not veto a competitive claim against the nonempty union of eligible nominated families. Every eligible family nominee remains a required adjusted comparison. If that union is empty, superiority is blocked. All candidate and nominated comparator evaluation caps and existing pairwise strict/non-inferiority requirements remain unchanged.

The previous all-required-families-must-be-eligible criterion is retained as the separately named stricter diagnostic `competitive_all_families`; it must not replace the registered feasible-family claim or be described as its prerequisite.

## Randomization attribution at common operating constraints

Retain the complete matched U/direct-code and three-point withholding/randomized-response attempts, including for a selected-33 dictionary. Missing required matched controls or incomplete simple-control grids block attribution. For its primary common-cap comparison, nominate only controls satisfying the route's unchanged validation constraints: both singleton U/direct-code controls are screened individually, and each simple-control family uses its existing eligible nominee. Completed but ineligible controls remain visible. The resulting feasible comparator set must be nonempty; every included comparator receives the existing adjusted direct-comparison and evaluated-cap conjunction.

All U/direct-code contrasts remain explicit descriptive/diagnostic endpoints even when those extremes are infeasible. The stricter preceding amendment-4 conjunction is retained separately as `randomization_all_controls`, requiring favorable comparisons with U/direct code and an eligible nominee in each simple-control family. Its failure does not negate a narrower supported common-cap comparison. No claim follows from unavailable controls or from an empty feasible set.

All diagnostic and primary endpoints remain in the single prospective multiplicity family. Reports distinguish incomplete mandatory comparisons, completed scientific ineligibility, primary adjusted claims, and stricter diagnostics. This amendment supersedes earlier implementation notes that treated any empty individual family as an automatic primary-claim veto; the empty-total-set prohibition remains intact. Synthetic counterexamples verify the distinction before outcomes are opened.
