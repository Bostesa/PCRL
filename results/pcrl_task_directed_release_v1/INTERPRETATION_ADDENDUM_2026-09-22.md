# Interpretation corrections, 22 September 2026

This dated source-review addendum corrects wording in this study's registration package without changing its fits, margins, selection rules, endpoint family, or data permissions. The referenced versions are preserved in Git at `dfc8d833eaab01cd0629c875a48710e69a41702c`. Validation has now been opened for the registered checks; current-run evaluation is still sealed. These are mathematical and source-code qualifications, not outcome-driven changes to a scientific gate.

1. In `NOVELTY_AND_ASSUMPTIONS.md`, the statistical-evidence paragraph says that nonlinear attack success is a lower bound on exploitable information. For the comparisons actually reported here, use the more precise statement: **measured recovery is an audited model's log-loss reduction, not a conditional-mutual-information estimate or bound.** A restricted predictor can improve after receiving a deterministic feature `Z=f(H)` even though `I(S;Z|H)=0`. Approximation error in both fitted predictors prevents the proposed information-bound interpretation. Failure of an attack also provides no upper bound. Source SHA-256: `5bdef54bf91d0214181883857e8d704238f1cb796372cdae62fb55fceb4af54f`.

2. In `PROTOCOL.md`, “causal decomposition” means **descriptive controlled loss decomposition** in this programme. Different fitted encoders, finite samples, action resolution, estimation and independently fitted probes can all affect the observed differences. The comparisons do not identify a general causal or information-theoretic loss at each stage. Source SHA-256: `d88320be37f47f093552828f0f89118c4b6ab2fd752446ddbcc5deabb1446956`.

3. In `AMENDMENT_6_BASELINE_FAIRNESS.md`, the union88 fitting-information statement describes the union of designated permitted pools. Its stronger wording, “at least the protected fitting information used by Trisk,” must be qualified by each method's missing-label mask: the risk models use attribute-specific valid-label rows, while the erasers use joint complete-case masks, with further task-label requirements for SPLINCE. The supplement provides **broader permitted fitting pools, subject to the disclosed method-specific masks**. A strict superset of actual observed labels may be asserted only if the recorded support diagnostics establish it. Source SHA-256: `ab27c3d42138a6b31977d47d8d07286b391e29de39cb61c9332ac3c5cc354ecd`.

4. In `DATA_USE.md`, “joint protected labels” refers to the concatenation of marginal one-hot concepts used for fitting. Neither this phrase nor the separate SEX and RAC1P constraints establishes protection of their joint intersection categories. Source SHA-256: `b5fabcbd323243fa4cf619a3a0c06dfb3b775b72a89a9bdb3003b759254c833d`.

The final addendum must retain the prospective residence supervision, historical use of the 2018 cohort, the full-H versus coarse-conditioning distinction, the single sampled-token contract, and the separate tests for task resolution, risk refinement, coalition constraints and randomization. A comparison with historical J alone cannot attribute a benefit to the new algorithm. Nothing in these corrections changes the original manuscript or its actual prior-review history.
