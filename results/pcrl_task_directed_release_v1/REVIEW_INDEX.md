# Review index

This index maps the requested scientific questions to the implemented checks and their evidence artifacts. It is a source-only navigation document, not a verdict. **Final selection, adjusted results, final verification, archive publication and closeout status remain pending here until their generated artifacts are reviewed and this index is updated.** A pre-existing filename or an intermediate report does not establish final completion. No ACS outcomes were read to prepare this index.

The scientific sample is historically reused 2018 development data. **2016 remains sealed.** See [DATA_USE.md](DATA_USE.md), [RELEASE_CONTRACT.md](RELEASE_CONTRACT.md), and the separately defined [fresh-year confirmation protocol](FRESH_YEAR_CONFIRMATION_PROTOCOL.md). Instructions for synthetic checks, preserved-model replay, historical fitting entry points and private archive restore are in [REPRODUCE.md](REPRODUCE.md).
