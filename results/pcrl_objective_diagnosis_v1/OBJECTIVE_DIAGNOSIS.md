# Post hoc diagnosis of Q versus D17

This diagnosis uses the completed 2016 **aggregate** inference only as context. All new channel calculations use frozen 2018 development objects. It does not alter the registered evaluation, select a new release, or claim a fresh confirmatory test. Source is pinned to `f4bdf4cd5bf74c634feeec50aef78bff249667e4`; the seven relevant executable source files are byte-identical in the completed evidence branch, as verified in [REUSABLE_INPUTS.json](REUSABLE_INPUTS.json). The three anchors reuse households and are descriptive replicates, not independent samples.

## Completed prospective fact

[PROSPECTIVE_AGGREGATE_REPLAY.json](PROSPECTIVE_AGGREGATE_REPLAY.json) reads only the immutable aggregate `INFERENCE_2016.json` (SHA-256 pinned there). Q and D17 each fail **both** registered task-improvement clauses at the −0.003 margin and pass **all eight** measured sensitive-recovery non-inferiority clauses against J. In the registered 70-endpoint secondary family, Q's unweighted task CE exceeds D17 by 0.001475758 (interval 0.000334056 to 0.002617459) and D33 by 0.001781746 (0.000429485 to 0.003134007). The PWGTP task intervals cross zero. None of Q's eight sensitive-recovery comparisons against either D17 or D33 establishes Q superiority. Failure to prove a task margin is not proof of equivalence; passing privacy non-inferiority versus J does not establish superiority to simple controls. Randomization has not established an algorithmic advantage.

## Frozen optimization actually solved

Within each 2018 anchor, the supervised task teacher takes PCA32 plus four H_A coordinates and predicts `same_residence`; its H_A-only baseline predicts the same label. T0 bins the clipped-logit residual `logit(p_task) − logit(p_HA)` into 32 quantile states. The H_B columns do not enter T0. The encoder object also carries protected-risk models for *other* refined codes; Q and D17 use T0. The frozen 17-action library is a zero offset plus 16 fitted logit offsets, trained against the teacher's soft probabilities on teacher/codebook rows. It gives an H_A-dependent binary task prediction `clip(expit(logit(p_HA)+offset_z), 10⁻⁵, 1−10⁻⁵)`. The action index is a token, not a task label. Task labels supervised the teacher and the mechanism cost, so the code and action design can carry task information through training; no person's hidden task or protected label is directly appended to the release.

The task cost for state `t`, action `z` is its **joint-mass-weighted** expected binary cross-entropy on the separate mechanism-estimation rows. `cost_U` normalizes by valid task-label count, `cost_W` by valid-label PWGTP sum, and the minimized matrix is `C = (cost_U + cost_W)/2`. Thus the objective is `Σ_tz C_tz Q_tz`, with no second multiplication by state mass. The decoder's probability clipping is part of the fixed action definition; the cost routine introduces no additional floor. The audit's independently trained utility probe is different: it sees H_A and the emitted token, selects from a registered predictor slate on development validation, and evaluates **expected token log loss** with its own 10⁻⁹ probability floor. It does not receive a person's probability row or hidden Q row. Likewise an A attacker sees H_A and token; AB sees H_A, H_B, and token. The H service columns remain exactly public across these releases.

The local privacy programme constrains four separate empirical conditional informations `I(SEX;Z|C_A)` and `I(RAC1P;Z|C_A)`, each under unweighted and PWGTP laws, to 0.01 nats. `C_A` is a two-cell KMeans partition of only H_A coordinates 1 and 3. The AB four-cell modeled laws are audited but **not** constrained for Q. No fitted constraint conditions on the recipient's full continuous H_A or H_A+H_B, and the two protected attributes are constrained separately. All 32 T0 states have mechanism people (99–167 per state across anchors); support tying is inactive. Q and D17 share the identical frozen teacher, T0 codes, 17 offsets, mechanism rows, 32×17 cost matrix, and all eight empirical privacy laws. D17 omits privacy constraints. D33 uses the same teacher, code, and rows but its separate 33-action dictionary and 32×33 cost matrix.

For an unconstrained fixed linear distortion objective over stochastic rows, `Σ_z C_tz Q_tz ≥ min_z C_tz` for every `t`; choosing one minimum-cost action per row attains the sum. This proves a rowwise deterministic optimum for **this fixed unconstrained objective**. D17 is exactly the first rowwise argmin in all 32 states at all three anchors, and its objective agrees with `Σ_t min_z C_tz` to floating precision. Q cannot beat D17 on that *same* objective, even if a different trained decoder could rank releases differently. This is not an impossibility theorem for privacy-constrained deterministic optimization: randomization may improve a constrained privacy–utility frontier relative to a separately optimized deterministic channel.

## Where Q changes the channel

Mass-weighted entropy is 0.690, 0.605, and 0.505 nats (effective action counts 1.99, 1.83, and 1.66). Q has 30, 30, and 25 materially nondeterministic rows, covering 94%, 94%, and 78% of mechanism state mass. Its mean total-variation distance from D17's rowwise cost minimizer is 0.676, 0.615, and 0.578. The minimum-cost action receives only 32%, 39%, and 42% mass on average. Every state is supported, no state has under 1% mechanism mass, and Q has no mathematically exact zero entries, though 376, 386, and 425 of its 544 entries per anchor are below 10⁻⁸. All rowwise fixed-cost premiums are nonnegative, as the theorem predicts. The top quartile of states by a precomputed coarse-privacy sensitivity has 25–28% of state mass and only 20–24% of weighted Q–D17 variation: changes do **not** concentrate there under this diagnostic. Sensitivity correlation is not causal attribution. Exact aggregate quantiles and counts are in [RANDOMIZATION_PROFILE.json](RANDOMIZATION_PROFILE.json).

The distribution-free, support-aware information-radius bound `I(S;Z|H) ≤ I(T;Z|H) ≤ Σ_t p_t KL(Q_t || Σ_u p_u Q_u)` is 1.384, 1.470, and 1.650 nats for Q, versus 2.586, 2.535, and 2.584 for D17 on the 2018 mechanism state masses. It confirms a broad token-information contraction. These loose bounds are far above 0.01 and do not certify privacy for a full-H recipient. No zero-probability smoothing was added.

## Procedural note

Before locating the archived four-cell files, an initial local diagnostic invoked the historical `ServicePartitions.fit(..., n_local=4)` on frozen 2018 teacher/codebook rows at all three anchors. This **was an encoder-component partition fit**, contrary to the instruction to perform no new encoder fits. It produced no candidate, altered no historical artifact, and its provisional outputs were replaced. The final calculations load hash-verified historical `fineC/tables.joblib` objects and check their empirical laws to 10⁻¹². No attacker, utility probe, release, or channel was fitted here; no cloud instance was launched. The deviation does not change the 2016 inference but is recorded rather than hidden.
