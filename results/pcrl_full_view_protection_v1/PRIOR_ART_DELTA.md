## Exact remaining question

The candidate mathematical increment would be a quantitative, verifiable bridge from a fitted finite/coarse law to `I(S;Z|H_r)` for continuous published H, or a mechanism property selective to S that improves useful information over all-input radius control under an operationally justified conditional envelope. Merely imposing the full-view constraints on a known finite law is a standard conditional-information adaptation. A finite uncertainty list gives an exact certificate for those listed laws only. Without a population envelope coverage proof or a matched empirical/computational gain, the outcome is a diagnosis and implementation package, not an algorithmic contribution.

The comparison target is the same encoder, action alphabet, costs, information access, full-view budget and uncertainty list. If the Diaz-style robust adaptation solves the same constrained convex programme, the two outputs are the same method. Calling their repeated solutions separate methods would be false attribution. A nominal full-view channel that fails the robust target is informative about sensitivity but is not a fair same-target operating-point baseline.
