# Amendment 2 — post-suite protected-prior sensitivity

2026-09-22, after the original eight-fixture suite. The original finite-list result and all checkpoints remain unchanged. This amendment adds a separate ninth, explicitly **post-suite exploratory** sensitivity case because the original perturbation list varied `P(T|S,H)` while holding protected priors fixed. It cannot turn a failed preregistered ACS gate into a pass.

Starting from the partly coupled fixture, take the Cartesian product of three conditional-input shifts `{-1/20,0,+1/20}` and three protected-prior shifts `{-1/20,0,+1/20}`. A prior shift u replaces `P(S=1|H_A=0)=1/4+u` and `P(S=1|H_A=1)=3/4-u`, with `P(H_A)=1/2`; the rest of the original conditional law, including `H_B=S xor H_A` and `P(Y|T)`, remains fixed. This produces nine coherent full joint laws, not nine unrelated role-specific tables. The nominal objective is the central law. The uncertainty set U is exactly these nine laws. Evaluate the same four budgets, controls, access and numerical policy as Amendment 1. The `+1/10` shift in both dimensions is outside U and evaluated diagnostically. Do not assert coverage for a continuous-H population.
