## Question and release contract

Can a single sampled auxiliary token beside unchanged service probabilities add residence-task information while bounding extra information about SEX and RAC1P available to A or AB under their *full* views? A holds `H_A` (four unrounded float64 service columns); AB holds `H_AB=(H_A,H_B)` (six columns). `B_r=b_r(H_r)` is only a coarse cell. `T` is the frozen residence-supervised T0 code computed from permitted PCA32 and H_A, and `Z` is one privately sampled token from public `Q(.|T)`. The coin is independent of `(S,Y,T,H)` conditional on the row; one persistent draw is served per person. The actual proposed interface is replacement `H+Z`, with J a comparator. If J was previously disclosed, this replacement cannot retract it and the view must instead include J; no such disclosure is evidenced by the release contract.

The mathematical target is `I(S_j;Z|H_r)` in nats for each role `r in {A,AB}` and attribute `j in {SEX,RAC1P}` under a stated joint law and release randomness. The historical `+.001` cap is a difference in fitted attackers' log losses against J, not this target. Main illustrative CMI budgets are `0.001, 0.01, 0.05` nats, predeclared for feasibility and shape, not for moving a margin after results. Zero and unconstrained endpoints are mandatory controls. No ACS parameter is selected on 2016 rows; 2016 is read-only evidence and receives no new score.

## Mathematical sequence and failure branches

1. Prove the chain-rule identity `I(S;Z|H)=I(S;Z|B)+I(H;Z|S,B)-I(H;Z|B)` for finite S,Z and standard conditional mutual information. Check a finite XOR construction with coarse zero and full-view log 2. Report whether the correction is positive or negative in each test; never infer its sign from the coarse value.
2. Prove the information-radius upper certificate `max_t KL(Q_t||r)` for any reference distribution `r`, with all deployment-allowed rows included and explicit zero-mass handling. Numerically find a good reference for the *unchanged* archived 2018 Q and D17; report its maximum row KL as an upper bound, while capacity maximization is a lower bound on the optimum radius. For a state-dependent mechanism, require the bound in every state already present in the view. Demonstrate `I(Y;Z|H)` obeys the same bound; distinguish Bayes log-loss gain from gain over an imperfect finite J probe.
3. Implement the ordinary radius-constrained expected-distortion mechanism at the three budgets, zero and unconstrained endpoints. It is a control, not a proposed algorithmic contribution.
4. Investigate the attribute-specific programme `min_Q E[c(T,Z,H_A)]`, subject to `sup_{P in U} I_P(S_j;Z|H_r)<=delta_(r,j)` and row simplex. Encoder, action set, costs, and `U` are fixed before optimizing. In finite examples `U` is the *enumerated finite list of entire joint laws* below. Enumeration certifies only that finite list; it is not a convex hull or a population confidence set. Each law uses one joint `(S,Y,T,H_A,H_B)` table, so local and coalition constraints are coherent. If a broader envelope is investigated, report an exact oracle or a separately proven analytic upper bound, never a scenario search as a global certificate.
5. For continuous H, attempt a conditional-law continuity theorem: if every true conditional joint law `(S,T)|H=h` is within a stated total-variation radius of a reference law at that same h, bound the true per-h CMI by its reference CMI plus an explicit finite-alphabet entropy-continuity term. Quantify the radius needed for `.01` and compare with `log|S|`; include numerical error. This is assumption-conditional unless an independent uniform-coverage proof is supplied. A sparse histogram, calibration, ensemble or bootstrap alone is not that proof. If the bound is vacuous on available ACS information, stop before new ACS fitting.

## Predeclared exact finite suite

All synthetic laws are finite, with rational table entries and output alphabet `{0,1}` unless the inherited fixture specifies otherwise. Task cost is Hamming loss `1{Y!=Z}` for binary Y. The same encoder/input T, public state access, output size, cost and privacy target apply to every comparison within a fixture. No mechanism uses H_B. Baselines are withholding/constant, randomized response mixtures, exhaustive deterministic maps for `|T|<=3`, nominal full-state CMI optimization, finite-list robust full-state CMI optimization, a matched published robust-information-privacy adaptation using the *same* list U, and the capacity control. The robust method and published adaptation may coincide; duplicate channels count once and trigger the no-novelty branch.

- **XOR trap:** independent fair S and H_A; H_B constant; T=Y=S xor H_A; B_A constant. A release of T has `I(S;Z|B)=0` and `I(S;Z|H_A)=log 2` when deterministic. This checks the algebra and rejects coarse-only certificates.
- **Safe task, two views:** independent fair S,Y; T=Y. Case a has independent fair H_A,H_B; case b sets H_A=S xor Y and H_B constant. Case a admits perfect task release with zero sensitive leakage; case b converts task information into sensitive full-view information. B is constant in both.
- **Partly coupled task:** fair H_A and S with `P(S=1|H_A=0)=1/4`, `P(S=1|H_A=1)=3/4`; H_B=S xor H_A; conditional on `(S,H_A)`, binary T has `P(T=1|S,H_A)=(1+2S+H_A)/5`, and Y=T flipped by independent Bernoulli(1/5). Use all four `(S,H_A)` cells. This is the main nontrivial tradeoff.
- **Inherited rational stochastic separation:** exactly the fixture at `experiments/pcrl_task_directed_release_v1/fixtures/pcrl_score_refinement_fixture.py`, SHA-256 `06800bb98a229429e97c8cb107ba499511dac1832cb04dacbdc3beffd32f8151`. H is constant. Its original X is T, original S and Y distributions are retained. Compare binary-action Hamming cost and report MI task information as a separate metric; no ACS inference follows from it.
- **Finite uncertainty perturbation:** for the partly coupled fixture, U consists of the nominal law and two entire joint laws formed by replacing every `P(T=1|S,H_A)` with its nominal value plus or minus `1/20`, clipped only if mathematically necessary (not expected here), holding `P(S,H_A,H_B)` and `P(Y|T)` fixed. Those three laws are the complete declared U. Also evaluate the `+1/10` law *outside* U without claiming protection there. This is scenario robustness over a known finite set, not an uncertainty confidence region.
- **Support/null cases:** repeat the partly coupled fixture with `P(S=1,H_A=0)=0` and with a rare protected cell mass `1/1000` (renormalize the remaining three cells proportionally); test a constant channel and a channel with one zero output column. No absent class may receive a zero uncertainty radius by default. A truly unsupported class yields a conditional-law uncertainty of the whole simplex for any population claim.

For the three privacy budgets, optimization feasibility tolerance is `1e-7` nats, row sums `1e-8`, nonnegativity `1e-9`, and objective comparison `1e-6`. Record solver residuals, exact re-evaluation of all declared laws, and primal/dual objective bounds where available. A numerical solver success flag alone is not an exact optimum certificate; if no certified lower bound is available, label optimum unknown. Null and deterministic cases use exact rational probabilities and exhaustive enumeration. No synthetic example is omitted from the final table.

## Decision to spend ACS compute

All are required: (i) chain rule, radius, continuity and finite solver claims pass independent proof/reviewer check; (ii) one predeclared nontrivial synthetic case has positive incremental task information and satisfies its stated attribute-specific full-view guarantee; (iii) a concrete advantage at the *same full-view target* over the matched existing robust adaptation, or an honestly bounded computational improvement, or a meaningful robust-feasible improvement over a coarse mechanism that is itself feasible at that target; (iv) tractable implementation and explicit ACS guarantee class. If the finite robust method coincides with published robust optimization and no concrete incremental question remains, do not run a 27-fit ACS grid. Finish the diagnosis and package. The nominal method violating the full-view target cannot count as a win by the robust method.

## Conditional 2018 pilot, fixed before any pilot outcomes

Only if every gate above passes: three anchors `0,1,2`, budgets `.001,.01,.05`, conditions nominal full-state model, proposed robust full-view model, closest matched published robust adaptation: at most 27 distinct release fits. The cost, T0, 17 actions, frozen H, decoder, inherited household splits, fitting data and selectors match the completed programme. If methods coincide, no duplicate fits. Training households fit nuisance laws; held-out development validation selects channels; repeatedly used 2018 test is development assessment. No 2016 rows are used.

Compare to D17 and matched published robust method first, J as reference. A useful operating point needs either task CE non-inferiority within `+0.003` nats with a sensitive-recovery improvement of at least `0.001` nats, or sensitive-recovery non-inferiority within `+0.001` nats with task CE improvement of at least `0.003` nats, under simultaneous one-sided 95% household-paired bounds for a family of the two task weightings and 8 sensitive role/attribute/weighting endpoints per primary comparator (20 per comparator, 40 total). These are pilot thresholds, not the original registered 2016 claims. Report all endpoints and raw estimates even if no conjunction passes. Same feature-based attacker/utility slate, selection and host for all candidates; full unrounded H; exact expected single-token loss; no Q row, coin or another method's hidden input to an attacker; no anchor multiplication in household count. H bytes and same-channel audit equality are mandatory checks. Any failed unit is counted, checkpointed and quarantined; retries are limited to two numerical repairs without changing targets.
