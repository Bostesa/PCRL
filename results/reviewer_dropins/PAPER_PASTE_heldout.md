## Paper paste — §6 held-out validation of the dual-update schedule

\paragraph{Held-out validation of the dual-update schedule.}
To partially address the post-hoc-tuning concern, we ran a held-out validation on Adult seed~3, which was not used during development of the $\lambda_{\min}$ floor or the warmup-skip rule (the R1+R2 probe touched seeds 0, 1, and 2). Retraining all 8 Adult pairs on seed 3 from a fresh LEACE warm-start under the Round-5 schedule, the held-out $R^2$-pass rate is $6/8$ versus $7/8$ on the headline Adult seed-2 run, and the cleanly-compliant rate is $1/8$ versus $0/8$. Mean per-cell $\Delta R^2_{\mathrm{onehot}}$ between held-out and headline is $\bar\Delta = +0.0111$ (std $\sigma_\Delta = 0.0372$, range $[-0.0395, +0.0824]$). The schedule generalises to a fold that was untouched during development, defusing the post-hoc-tuning concern. This is a single-fold partial validation on one of three datasets, not a full $k$-fold cross-validation; we recommend the latter as the rigorous follow-up.
