# Prior work and claim boundary

This campaign reuses finite stochastic output channels, fixed score quantization and constrained optimization. It begins with **no algorithmic novelty claim**. The source sampled-channel study already used a finite frozen-code/decoder law; this continuation changes the fitting reference to an exact useful W_TSTAR identity, repairs the emitted-ID bridge, and tests the actual release and matched controls. Those changes may improve implementation or empirical performance without creating a new channel family.

Primary settings checked:

- [Taylor, Vippathalla and Coon, *Adaptive Privacy of Sequential Data Releases Under Collusion*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.21859) studies adaptive sequential requests, colluding recipients, mutual-information privacy and expected-distortion or mutual-information utility. Its reported optimality is under its expected-distortion formulation; our freeze-first S12/S21 training comparison and finite-reader SEX AUC have a different objective and do not refute that result. A joint law can be sampled sequentially in either order, so an advantage here could only be attributed to a **training procedure** after both sequential controls are tested.
- [Liao et al., *Privacy Under Hard Distortion Constraints*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.00063) concerns deterministic per-release distortion fidelity and maximal alpha leakage. This campaign deliberately uses mean fitted task losses with no per-person all-label guard; it cannot inherit a hard-distortion guarantee.
- [Kalantari, Kosut and Sankar, *Information-Theoretic Privacy with General Distortion Constraints*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1708.05468) formulates random mappings under expected cost and leakage criteria including mutual information. Its information and asymptotic results do not certify finite fitted SEX attack AUC or this reused Adult population.
- [Cai, Zhang and Khalili, *Privacy-Aware Randomized Quantization via Linear Programming*](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v244/cai24a.html) designs unbiased differentially private discrete quantizers by linear programming. This release makes no differential-privacy or unbiasedness claim; the paper is evidence that randomized quantization itself is established prior work.

Interpretation tiers are kept separate. Exact B0 wire parity and constraint correctness are engineering evidence. A lower fresh complete-view AUC under fixed task limits would be an exploratory empirical method lead. A randomization component needs D to lose under matched conditions; a joint-procedure component needs P, L and both S12/S21 to lose, plus the strongest compatible inherited control. Novelty beyond these established channel ideas requires a separate theoretical and literature argument. Fitted-law Bayes G, Shannon information, differential privacy, old hard guards and fresh finite-reader AUC are different quantities.
