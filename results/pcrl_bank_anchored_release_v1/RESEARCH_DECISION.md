# Research decision — bank-anchored release (development)

**Decision:** The useful frozen ten-bit W_TSTAR bank survived as an executable identity service. Bank-relative fitting trained the seven registered alternatives on both source seeds. The fitted joint sampler J retained all 24 original common-INNER *point* utility clauses, but did not beat the strongest eligible score-release control. This campaign supplies a runnable bank service and a negative bounded method comparison, not a competitive privacy or joint-design claim.

This is exploratory development on repeatedly used Adult people. No fresh population, original assessment person-level array, or new HEAD_VALIDATION label was opened. The two model seeds reuse the same people. Original simultaneous utility and recovery bounds remain unresolved; a 24/24 point gate is not an established confidence guarantee.

## What ran

The frozen W_TSTAR public ten-bit code bank, decoder, decision rule and private teacher routing were admitted for both income and occupation. B0 is the exact unchanged bank. One fixed policy per arm and seed was fitted for: task-only T, deterministic privacy D, independent central-product P, local L, joint J, and freeze-first sequential S12/S21. Privacy protection changes which existing bank code is transmitted while keeping the public table fixed. W_Q and the older privacy-trained P* were restored as compatible references. Three cached complete releases per stochastic arm were attacked separately; a recipient receives one code per person, not their average probability or all three draws.

The new FIT proxy compared each policy with B0 on the same FIT people at 0.002 accuracy, 0.001 nats NLL and 0.0005 summed multiclass Brier. This fitting change was prospective and did not replace the original INNER utility limits. Several returned policies lie within about 6e-14 of a FIT NLL boundary; R3 and an independent scalar replay disagree at that floating-point edge. These clauses remain **NUMERIC_BOUNDARY_UNRESOLVED**. The unchanged common-INNER point gate admitted B0, T, D, P, L, J and S12 at 24/24 each. S21 passed 22/24 and was excluded from attacks. W_Q and P* passed 24/24 point clauses on the same development rows.

Fresh linear, nonlinear, token, probability, decision and public-aware attacks covered each recipient and their aligned pair. Thirty-eight effective release/attacker units (114 complete public views) passed null, shuffle and planted-leak gates. The first two P* H1 units failed technically before a valid reader fit and were preserved; the fixed H2 units completed. Public codes, probabilities, decisions and actually emitted metadata were in the attack view. Private teacher scores, fine cells and coins were not emitted. Independent replay checked all 38 attack units and 114 views to at most 2.22e-16 AUC difference. Four preregistered J/P HGB refits matched the common reader predictions exactly; a stronger fixed HGB diagnostic did not reverse the ranking.

The strongest favorable new-bank result is P versus B0: pair AUC falls by 0.005951, with seed-specific reductions 0.009509 and 0.002392. That is below the predeclared 0.020 target. Thus randomness and joint coordination have no distinct measured advantage over the matched central-product arm.

The strongest adverse comparison is eligible P* versus J: P* pair AUC is lower by 0.030645 overall and by 0.037764/0.023526 in the two seeds. J's recipient-2 AUC exceeds P* by 0.051984 at the point estimate. Under the frozen simultaneous family of 1,575 recovery slots, the P* minus J pair interval is [−0.868989, 0.807699], and the J minus P* recipient-2 interval is [−0.953712, 1.057680]. They cross the relevant boundaries and are **UNRESOLVED**, not interval-certified violations or benefits. Every new arm's complete simultaneous utility conjunction is likewise unresolved; the 72 W_Q/P* historical utility interval slots lack the dependency for this campaign and are marked not estimable.

## Attribution and next step

The evidence supports the exact bank identity foundation and working bank-relative channel implementation. It does not support a ≥0.020 recovery benefit, a competitive improvement over P*, or a joint/sampling novelty claim. This result is scoped to W_TSTAR, the inherited privacy objective, conservative B0-relative fitting tolerances, two seeds and the bounded search. It does not rule out other bank release objectives or a broader privacy–utility frontier.

The next scientific step is to **redesign the privacy objective against the observed strong P* complete-view attacker**, then freeze one new method and its controls before another development test. A later confirmation would need a separately authorized, genuinely unexposed population and prospective protocol. Do not promote these selected development intervals as confirmation.
