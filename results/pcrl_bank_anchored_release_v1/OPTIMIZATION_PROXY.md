# Prospective bank-relative fitting change

The predecessor compared sampled-channel FIT losses with U0/Ucal and rejected the existing useful W_TSTAR bank: B0's occupation NLL excess against U0 was 0.014312010568 for seed 0 and 0.015420555695 for seed 1, above the then 0.010 cap. Seed 1 U0 Brier excess was 0.005348076600, above 0.005. R5 independently recomputed these on 15,434 admitted FIT people per seed in `r5/PREFIT_FIT_B0_AND_OLD_REFERENCES.json`. This was a real result under that old rule.

This campaign prospectively compares each fitted policy with exact B0 on the **same FIT people**. For each task and seed, the inclusive limits are accuracy loss 0.002, mean NLL excess 0.001 nats and mean summed multiclass Brier excess 0.0005. Exact B0 is a feasible checkpoint with all three excesses zero. These are optimization tolerances, not guarantees relative to U0/Ucal on new people. The old FIT U0/Ucal margins will be reported for resulting policies as diagnostics, without gating this campaign.

The privacy fitting objective remains the inherited finite-law SEX Bayes accuracy `G_pair + 0.5(G_r1+G_r2)` for protected stochastic arms, with local G ceilings at B0's fitted-law values, and no required hard pair-improvement floor. T uses privacy-label-blind empirical `I(X;(Z1,Z2))`. D, P, L, S12 and S21 use their restricted versions of the inherited objective and fixed bank action support. The objective is a proxy; fresh complete-view attack AUC is the assessed recovery quantity. The numerical recipe and structural identity handling are specified in `r2/OPTIMIZATION_PROXY.md` and hashed in the effective lock.

The external development test is unchanged: on INNER_SELECTION, each task and seed must have accuracy within 0.010, mean NLL excess at most 0.010 and mean summed Brier excess at most 0.005 against **both** U0 and Ucal. Primary costs are exact expectations of the fixed decoded output under the fitted release law. Cached sampled outputs are scored as realized secondary diagnostics. No confidence interval or population guarantee is inferred from fitted-law constraints. Every point-eligible fixed arm enters fresh attacks; uncertainty is reported separately.

The output bank uses original deployed QPC token order. The predecessor's sorted-bank internal IDs differed for most states; R2's bridge preserves its action-neighbor construction but remaps actions into original public IDs before fitting, materialization and deployment. This is necessary to keep B0's actual transmitted bits and table meaning unchanged, not a change in the 16/321-state capacity. R1 and R5 check this bridge independently before a fit.
