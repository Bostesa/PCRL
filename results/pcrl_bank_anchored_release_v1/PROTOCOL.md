## Service and inputs

The selected service is inherited W_TSTAR for source seeds 0 and 1. The provider privately runs the frozen U model and bank router on the ordered 83 permitted predictors. It emits one global bank ID per recipient, the fixed bank-decoded probability, its first-tie argmax decision and fixed public arm/source metadata. Income has 2 classes and 16 public states; occupation_group has 6 classes and 321. Each state fits ten global bits including the decision class. R1's adapter and R5's independent saved-wire/raw replay must establish exact B0 identity before protection fits. W_Q and P_STAR are separate inherited reference services, each with its own pinned table. P_STAR is mandatory if its same-track output service and 24 original point utility clauses are admitted.

No recipient receives original predictors, private teacher scores, fine cells, input-local action positions, hidden coin values or SEX. The coalition receives both actual recipient wires, aligned by person, with all public metadata and tables. One sampled paired release per person is the stochastic query contract; three frozen realizations are three separate virtual deployments for uncertainty, never a three-query recipient view. SEX is available only to admitted fitting and attack roles. The runtime does not read SEX or true task labels.

## Fixed comparison

`B0` is immutable exact W_TSTAR identity. `T` is task-only channel optimization without SEX. `D` is deterministic centrally privacy-trained. `P` is central-product stochastic with independent recipient coins. `L` is recipient-local stochastic. `J` is central joint stochastic. `S12` and `S21` freeze the first complete release before fitting the conditional second release in the stated order. One selected policy per nonidentity arm and source seed, two fixed starts, no capacity, coefficient, temperature, bank or attacker grid. Every arm retains a runnable exact identity checkpoint. An identity return is an alias and a failed protection attempt, not a gain. ## Evaluation and nomination

The original common-INNER limits remain inclusive on each source seed, task and reference U0 and Ucal: accuracy difference candidate minus reference at least -0.010, expected NLL excess at most 0.010 nats, expected summed multiclass Brier excess at most 0.005. The primary stochastic task estimand is exact law-weighted expected cost of actual public table outputs, with NLL floor 1e-12 and Brier summed over all classes. Loss of mean probability is not scored. Realized cached-draw utility is secondary. Point eligibility requires all 24 original clauses; bounds crossing a limit are unresolved rather than proof of a pass. Every point-eligible fixed arm enters the attack stage. `J` is the main nominee only if J itself is point eligible on both seeds. Identity always remains in the table.

R4 fits fresh complete-view SEX readers on ATTACK_FIT_NEW only and reports INNER. The fixed slate has linear, nonlinear, code/cell-smoothed, probability/decision, defense-aware and transported ignore-other-recipient readers, plus null/permutation and planted-leak controls. Public masks/flags/length/fallback would be included if any arm emitted them; the declared adapter has none. Deterministic exact wire aliases may share attack fits only when source, rows, readers and view are byte-identical. P_STAR uses its own bank. A control failure makes the affected recovery comparison incomplete.

For J, select the strongest required eligible non-J competitor by lowest equal-seed-mean paired AUC, with the fixed arm order tie break. The required pair benefit is competitor AUC minus J AUC, at least 0.020. Each recipient's worsening J minus B0 and J minus the selected competitor must be at most 0.010. Also report every pairwise comparison to T, D, P, L, S12, S21, W_Q and P_STAR when admitted; a joint-procedure claim requires advantage over both sequential orders and strongest non-joint. A randomization claim requires advantage over D. Never promote a B0-only gain over a stronger eligible P_STAR.

The fixed inference family has 1,575 potential clauses as R3 defined: 360 utility and 1,215 pairwise recovery; unused conditional slots remain null. Duplicate INNER group IDs are the sampling units (2,234 IDs, 2,235 people). There are 2,048 paired resamples. Fixed-release clauses resample groups only; three-release means cross common paired group draws with a single three-ID realization tuple per seed/replicate shared across all arms/views. Both seeds remain paired repeated model runs on the same people. Two-sided Bonferroni at fixed K=1,575 and Student df 2,233 for group-only, df 2 for three-draw mean is the predeclared approximate simultaneous procedure. Accuracy PASS needs lower bound >= -0.010; NLL/Brier PASS needs upper bound <= 0.010/0.005; pair benefit PASS needs lower bound >= 0.020; local worsening PASS needs upper bound <= 0.010. A crossing bound is UNRESOLVED; a cost lower bound above its cap is VIOLATION. These intervals are conditional development summaries after an adaptive research history, not post-selection confirmation.
