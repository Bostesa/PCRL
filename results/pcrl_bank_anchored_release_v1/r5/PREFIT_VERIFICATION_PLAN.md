## Before fitting

R5 reviews R0's sealed source admission, optimization proxy, arm registry, cache/randomness convention, complete public view, candidate checkpoints and queue. R5 checks the following with independently written scalar arithmetic and synthetic inputs, without importing the channel runner's endpoint or selection implementation:

1. The exact B0 checkpoint emits its original bank ID, probability and decision. Its FIT B0-relative accuracy, NLL and summed multiclass Brier excess are exactly zero for both tasks.
2. Every row's aggregate action law is normalized after duplicate public-code pairs merge. The identity action is present in every required support. Expected NLL/Brier use the fixed emitted probabilities, not the loss of their average.
3. A central-product law factors with independent recipient coins, local arms read only their own declared scores, and each sequential arm freezes its complete first release. A joint law's sequential compiler is an alias, not a separate training comparison.
4. No varying branch flag, original fine token, action index, teacher score, private coin or source row key reaches either recipient. A defense-aware reader can use public parameters and bank tables. The aligned pair is the union of complete recipient views.
5. The fitting objective and local guards are source-bound, with bank-relative utility tolerances `0.002` accuracy loss, `0.001` NLL excess and `0.0005` Brier excess. The optimization reference differs from the unchanged common-INNER caps (`0.010`, `0.010`, `0.005` against both U0 and Ucal).

Engineering correctness does not require a synthetic privacy gain. Any failed or ambiguous prefit check blocks the affected fit until a prospective dated amendment is sealed.
