We attempted to extend PCRL's training procedure to BERT-base layer-12 [CLS] features with the linear-R² compliance constraint. Across five launches with documented rescues — static post-projection, online LEACE refit at K=50 steps observed both pre- and post-projection — R²(gender, h_concat) remained at 0.946 with seed-deterministic agreement to four decimals. The utility was preserved (task accuracy 0.80 vs vanilla 0.79) and the representation did not collapse (per-dim std 0.88, effective rank 380). The structural obstruction is rank deficit: rank-1 LEACE — even refit every K=50 steps — blocks one direction per refit, while the 768-dimensional LoRA reroutes the gender signal through the remaining directions. This is consistent with the mechanistic finding above showing gender enters block-1 through multiple block-0 heads whose writes are not LEACE-frame-aligned. Tightening the bound at this scale would require either cumulative rank-K null-space accumulation or non-linear adversarial constraints (HSIC), both of which are substantial method changes we leave to future work.
