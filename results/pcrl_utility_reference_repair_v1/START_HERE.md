# PCRL utility-reference repair and conditional feature-selection screen

**Status (2026-10-09):** The local bounded development study finished its two-seed method screen. It found a valid point-utility path to testing masks, but **no competitive privacy or joint-design lead**. These Adult people and INNER_SELECTION outcomes were previously used; this is exploratory evidence, not independent confirmation.

## What changed

The preceding focused study compared a validation-stage F trained on 11,574 or 11,575 OSF_DEFENSE_FIT rows with U0 trained on all 15,434, then scored the FIT rows F had held out. U0 had seen those rows during fitting. We preserved that study and made a new, common-role comparison on the existing INNER_SELECTION people, where FIT and calibration rows have zero direct row or group overlap. Historical Ucal family choice had used INNER outcomes and is disclosed. No old assessment person arrays or HEAD_VALIDATION labels were opened.

R1 and independent R3 restored U0 and Ucal with bitwise parity in all 48 admitted role/seed/task vector checks. FIT-only validation selected the existing A task learner over the source-style B recipe. A was refit on all 15,434 FIT people, calibrated on the fixed 2,000-row CAL role, and frozen before INNER task scoring. Its two tasks and two seeds met every unchanged **point** accuracy, NLL and Brier cap against both U0 and Ucal. The simultaneous 24-clause group bootstrap gave 10 PASS, 14 UNRESOLVED and no VIOLATION, so the full utility-preservation conjunction remains unresolved.

Two historical single-group masks, dropping native-country (42 encoded columns retained) or marital-status (76 retained), met the same point utility caps against U0, Ucal and the equally trained F on both tasks and seeds. The bounded five-mask bank, task-only/local/joint/sequential selection, matched raw unions, complete recipient releases and fresh ATTACK_FIT_NEW SEX readers then ran. All selected release and attack results were independently replayed.

## Method result

The joint rule J selected the **same mask pair as task-only T in both seeds**. Its paired mean coalition SEX AUC was **0.897277**. The frozen strongest eligible main control, sequential S21, had **0.895978**; control minus J was **−0.001299**, versus the required **+0.020** benefit. Seed 0 J and S21 reveal the same full 83-column canonical raw union; their 0.002598 measured AUC gap is a finite-reader difference on information-equivalent raw inputs. Seed 1 they are the same pair. There is no measured privacy win or joint component to promote to a third seed.

A separately sealed observational check scored the predecessor's saved F on the same INNER people. That old F also met all point task caps, despite its earlier exposed-validation failure. Old and new F have mixed task tradeoffs and unequal FIT exposure, so the new result cannot be attributed causally to the full-role refit alone.
