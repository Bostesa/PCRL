# R2 conditional feature-selection method plan

Status: prospective engineering plan. No new task head, mask, sensitive-label
search, release, or attack has run for this study. The former focused study is
retained as an exposed development diagnostic; its no-go is not revised here.

## Admission and ownership

R0's locally sealed full-input gate must identify one recipe selected solely
on defense-fitting internal validation. It must show point eligibility for
both tasks and both paired seeds against U0 and Ucal on the common admitted
comparison role. R2 will accept only R0's exact sealed gate receipt and R1's
hash-bound full-role F packages. An unselected recipe cannot replace a failed
selected recipe. No R2 worker opens FIT SEX, ATTACK_FIT_NEW, or INNER_SELECTION
SEX before this admission. Every worker uses the shared host semaphore, one
numerical thread, explicit memory/time cap, and a versioned output receipt.

R1 owns full-input training, recipe selection and reference parity. R2 owns
masked-head fitting, frozen mask rules, complete release construction and
fresh-reader fitting after conditional admission. R3 independently checks
gate, selection, releases and numerical endpoints; it does not import R2's
scoring or selection functions. R0 owns every shared lock, branch choice and
decision.

## First branch: two historical task-feasibility leads

Fit the unchanged `M_DROP_NATIVE_COUNTRY` (42 encoded columns) and
`M_DROP_MARITAL_STATUS` (76 columns) for each task and paired seed. They are
different realized capacities and are reported separately. Use the *same
selected recipe* as F, fit on the entire permitted OSF_DEFENSE_FIT role after
its internal selection, and apply the same fixed global-temperature procedure
using CALIBRATION_HELDOUT. The head receives only selected per-person raw
values. If the selected recipe requires an 83-column input, a public adapter
may fill absent columns with fixed constants learned only on defense fitting;
it cannot read withheld per-person values. Its constants, column order and
source identity are bound to the deployment package.
The deployment model identity also binds the exact masked-head checkpoint
SHA; a public model ID alone never authenticates weights. The release worker
must rehash the checkpoint and calibrator on restore.

For both tasks, compare accuracy, natural-log NLL and summed multiclass Brier
against U0, Ucal and equally fitted F on identical admitted common-comparison
people. Caps remain inclusive: accuracy loss <= 0.010, NLL excess <= 0.010,
Brier excess <= 0.005. A mask is a qualifying two-purpose lead only if all
applicable point clauses hold for both tasks in both seeds. Simultaneous
intervals are reported separately. This branch uses only task labels and
never calls utility measurement privacy protection.

If neither lead qualifies, the R2 conditional method stops. The result is
limited to those two masks and cannot be described as a privacy failure.

## Second branch: fixed bank and selection

If a lead qualifies, finish the old five-mask bank by fitting the three
remaining masks for both tasks and seeds with identical recipe, fitting rows,
calibration rule, architecture/compute ceiling and source schema. The bank is
exactly the five one-categorical-group exclusions under the inherited
4-numeric/4-categorical/max-77 profile. It has 25 ordered pair assignments
and six distinct raw unions. No new bank, capacity, mask, score threshold or
search restart is selected from INNER_SELECTION SEX or utility outcomes.

Before sensitive search, publish every head/package hash, task-only score,
utility margin and complete task-only T choice. Fit local and canonical
raw-union search critics on the permitted fitting role only: logistic and
histogram-boosted readers for each of five local masks and six unions, 22
reader fits per seed. Use one immutable 25-row table for J, task-only T,
independent I, both sequential orders, shared-mask, and valid equal-union
controls. Keep invalid rows and legitimate identical-mask aliases. The same
numeric utility caps against U0, Ucal and F govern candidate and control
eligibility. Primary method screening uses the admitted common role and is
fully disclosed as development selection.

The old geometry limits interpretation: any off-diagonal pair transmits the
full 83-column raw union. A diagonal pair's exact union width identifies its
mask within this bank. Thus off-diagonal raw-union information is identical,
and diagonal equal-union controls may be exact ties. A finite reader's lower
AUC on either geometry alone is not information removal.

## Releases, final readers and inference

The actual recipient view includes selected raw values, selected group/column
identity, task probabilities, decisions, head and calibration identity, flags
and all other public metadata. The coalition receives both local views and
their canonical raw union. A task output must use its masked head or public
constant adapter; no clean full-input F/U0 output may be appended. Reconstruct
raw unions in source order and transport a verified reader whenever the raw
inputs, people and reader source hashes match, particularly for full-83
off-diagonal coalitions.

Freeze final reader architecture and orientation on ATTACK_FIT_NEW, using its
group-disjoint tune partition. Use logistic, nonlinear MLP, histogram-boosted,
defense-aware, raw-union transport and ignore-recipient readers on each
selected complete view. Include null, shuffled-SEX and planted-leak controls.
Score only actual releases on INNER_SELECTION; keep per-person arrays private.
Select the strongest eligible main control and the strongest eligible
equal-union control by the sealed rule. Coalition AUC benefit must be >= 0.020
and neither local AUC may worsen by > 0.010. Joint attribution separately
requires advantage over both sequential orders. Use paired people and the
predeclared simultaneous inference; seeds are not independent populations.

## Upper-bound units before any alias savings

| Conditional stage | Two-seed maximum | Exact dependency |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Lead masked-head packages and calibrations | 8 + 8 | Full-input gate passes |
| Remaining bank masked-head packages and calibrations | 12 + 12 | A two-purpose lead qualifies |
| Fitting-role raw search readers | 44 | Bank completed |
| Pair table and rule selections | 2 tables, 20 rule slots | Search readers complete |
| Canonical raw-union reader fits | `2*U` per seed, `U <= 6` | Selected pairs |
| Complete-view and fixed V2 reader fits | `18*P` per seed, `P <= 10` | Selected distinct pairs |
| Releases and final complete views | `P` releases and `3*P` views per seed | Bound heads/controls |

The two-seed final reader ceiling is `2*(18*10 + 2*6) = 384` fitted
readers, before exact pair/union aliases. Ignore-recipient readers are verified
transports, not additional fits. A conditional third seed requires its own F
gate, 10 masked packages/calibrations for the full bank, 22 fitting search
readers, and at most 192 final reader fits; it runs only after a finalist is
frozen and the complete required control/replay budget remains. R0's actual
queue plan must include preprocessing, forward passes, calibration, attacks,
endpoint indexing, independent replay, failed starts and reserves within the
new 10-hour/20-CPU-hour ceiling. Unit caps are set from role-sized smoke work
before the conditional seal, not from these algebraic maxima.

The package counts above are purpose outputs, not an assertion that both
candidate architectures have the same fit count. Under the inherited C MLP
recipe A, each purpose/mask package has its own neural head; each head's
internal epoch selection and full-role refit are counted separately. Under
the source-style joint task recipe B, one masked encoder is trained per
mask/seed using the two permitted task labels, then both task heads are fitted
on that encoder; thus the eight lead packages share four encoder fits, and
the twelve remaining packages share six additional encoder fits. Both tasks'
head, calibration, forward-pass and serialization work still counts. For
recipe B, every mask gets the same five-value logistic-regularization search
opportunity as F, using only the same frozen FIT internal group split and
task NLL selection rule. This is ten tuning fits and two final logistic fits
per mask/seed, with no INNER_SELECTION adjustment. Across both seeds, the
lead stage therefore has 4 encoder fits, 40 tuning fits, 8 final logistic
fits and 8 calibrations; bank completion adds 6 encoders, 60 tuning fits,
12 final logistic fits and 12 calibrations. The exact queued stage count and
timing must follow whichever recipe R1 selects.

No old assessment or HEAD_VALIDATION label arrays are admitted. All outputs
remain exploratory because source models and Adult development outcomes were
previously exposed.
