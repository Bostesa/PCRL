# Research decision: utility-reference repair and conditional method screen

**Decision:** `UTILITY_FEASIBILITY_ESTABLISHED` at the predeclared point gate; `UTILITY_PRESERVATION_UNRESOLVED` under simultaneous development bounds; **no exploratory competitive privacy lead and no joint-design lead**. Do not promote a third seed or call this confirmation.

## Reference and utility

The old focused validation comparison was asymmetrically exposed: U0 trained on the FIT rows used to validate its replacement F, while that F trained on only 11,574/11,575 other FIT rows. This is an established role mismatch, not proof of how much of the gap it caused. U0/Ucal inference was restored bitwise on 48 admitted vector checks. The new common INNER role has zero direct row/group overlap with FIT and CAL; historical Ucal family selection on INNER remains a known exposure. The unchanged U0/Ucal definitions, caps, natural-log NLL and summed multiclass Brier were retained.

FIT-only recipe selection chose A (summed validation NLL 3.17304844) over B (3.20561911). After full FIT refit and fixed CAL calibration, A's F met all 24 point costs across two seeds, two tasks, two references and three metrics. The strongest favorable task comparison was seed 1 income NLL, **0.0211500 nats below U0**. The tightest point margin was seed 1 occupation Brier, **0.0038394 above U0** under the **0.005** cap. Its simultaneous upper bound was **0.0099956**, so that clause is unresolved. Across all 24 bounds: 10 PASS, 14 UNRESOLVED, 0 VIOLATION. Point feasibility justified the conditional screen; it does not establish the full confidence conjunction.

The old validation F, scored separately on exactly the same INNER people, also met all point caps versus U0 and Ucal. Its task tradeoffs against new F were mixed. Its 11,574/11,575-row training exposure differs from new F's 15,434; neither a learner improvement nor a causal exposure effect is isolated. There was no demonstrated U0/Ucal implementation or metric bug. The corrected part of this study is the prospective comparison role and full-role fitting convention.

## Mask and recovery result

Dropping the whole native-country or marital-status feature group preserved both tasks' point utility against U0, Ucal and F in both seeds. These are capacity-distinct masks (42 and 76 encoded columns retained). Other bank arms, controls and exact aliases remain in `MASK_UTILITY.csv` and `MATCHED_PRIVACY_RESULTS.csv`; a qualifying single mask alone was not treated as privacy evidence.

The complete feature track transmitted selected raw values, task probabilities and decisions, masks, weights and metadata. Fresh ATTACK_FIT_NEW linear/nonlinear, raw-union, defense-aware and ignore-recipient readers were scored on INNER; null, shuffled-label and planted-leak controls passed. R3 reproduced all 12 selected pair views and 120 reader prediction instances (maximum AUC difference **2.22e-16**, model reforward difference zero), then independently replayed the frozen strongest-control selection.

J and task-only T are the same pair in **both seeds**. J's paired mean coalition AUC was **0.8972768264**; strongest eligible sequential S21 was **0.8959778086**. The predeclared benefit is control AUC minus J AUC, **−0.0012990178**, below the required **+0.020**. The attribution pool selected I_U at the same control AUC. In seed 0 J and S21 transmit the same full 83-column raw union, so their 0.002598 finite-reader difference is not ideal-reader information removal. In seed 1 their pair is identical. The complete-view evidence does not support a competitive or joint advantage. A third-seed pilot would not rescue the already exact J=T identity and was not launched under the frozen promotion rule.

## Scope and next step

All results are exploratory on repeatedly used Adult people. The two paired model seeds share the same 2,235 INNER people; they are not two populations. The attack slate measures recovery by fitted readers, not a population privacy guarantee. Full task interval coverage is unresolved. No selection-aware privacy interval was computed after the observed control shortfall; the exact J=T pair identity already rules out a joint-design gain in this screen. The prior study remains `INCOMPLETE_MATCHED_COMPARISON`; its no-go has not been rewritten as a privacy failure.

**Next step:** freeze a genuinely different feature-track method and its complete interface, controls and inference before testing on a separately authorized, unexposed population. Repeating mask selection or adding another seed on this reused role is not an independent confirmation step.
