## Independence and limits

The independent namespace uses its own feature construction, functional model forwarding, reader refits, AUC, nomination and interval arithmetic. It imports the runner queue authenticator, and does not import the runner metric, selection or interval implementations. ROOT ran all numerical work through its queue.

Final endpoint arithmetic replays stored curves; separate actual model forwards and actual reader refits establish the model-level verification scope. CONSTRAINED, ORDINARY and LOCAL_TWIN nominate the unchanged epoch-0 RAW_HYBRID witness. Trained privacy checkpoints at epochs 5, 10 and 20 fail the prospective task proxy gate. This evidence does not show a successful selected nonzero privacy update.

Aliases and repeated targets are not extra independent evidence. This is exploratory fixed-model, fixed-reader coverage on an inherited, repeatedly exposed development population, with two source seeds averaged inside shared group bootstrap draws. It supplies no fresh-population or causal generalization claim.
