# Branch B state refinement

The registered 64-state child-partition attempt **did not create a new ACS state** on any of the three 2018 anchors. Each completed Branch B unit records `SUPPORT_LIMITED_ALIAS_A`, 32 realized T32 states, zero split rules, and a selected 32×17 channel bitwise identical to its same-anchor Branch A release. The fixed support rule required at least 100 unique fitting households and 100 effective weighted households in **each** child. On anchor 0, the largest parent held only 198 distinct coefficient households, so no binary split could meet the 200-household necessary condition; the requirement was not weakened. The other two immutable B receipts also report no admissible split. The matched task-only, joint-risk, and seeded random partition controls likewise realized 32 states under the same floor. The registered 128-state escalation and split shrinkage fallback were not triggered because no first split was admissible.

This is a **support limitation**, not empirical evidence that richer legal encoder observations cannot help. The B mechanism, same-partition deterministic optimization, and split policy cannot earn an independent ACS component attribution from an exact A alias. Its named label remains in the audit matrix for traceability, but an aliased channel contributes one physical release and one original-person sample. The three B fit archives were independently read back from versioned encrypted S3, hash checked, and inspected: each receipt points to the intended A completion hash, its partition has identity T32 parents and no rules, and its Q array hash equals the saved A-selected array hash. Linux x86 runtime parity additionally checked stored T0/child codes and service bytes on 5,257, 5,317, and 5,334 coefficient people for anchors 0, 1, and 2, respectively. The exact receipts and archive versions are in `RUN_STATE.json` and the private archive manifests.

The two preregistered **synthetic** fixtures still establish why the hypothesis was meaningful. In B1, four observable states with masses `(2/5,1/10,1/10,2/5)` have sensitive labels `(0,1,0,1)` and task probabilities `(1/10,1/10,9/10,9/10)`. T is the exact task posterior. Exact-fraction enumeration of all 15 partitions shows every deterministic exactly-private map has zero task information, and a private T-only channel must have the same row for both T values. The richer stochastic X-channel `P(Z=1|X)=(0,0,1,1/4)` has equal `P(Z=1|S)=1/5` and task information `0.08972125227425051` nats. B2 uses independent fair H,T bits, with task and sensitive relevance switching by H: the context-blind private T-channel loses task information, while the deterministic contextual map releases T only when H=0, has zero conditional sensitive information, and retains `(log 2)/2 = 0.34657359027997264` nats task information. These are exact-law counterexamples to a general T-only access restriction, not ACS findings or new nullspace mathematics.

The fitted nuisance predictor was frozen from the global nuisance-training role and computes full-schema SEX/RAC1P risk probabilities from allowed `X_A,H_A`; it cannot use a person's observed protected or task label. Synthetic tests verify child routing, parent-row lift, fixed-price split-score signs, forbidden-input rejection, and release replay. The `100`-household floor and current 2018 coefficient allocation prevented these otherwise legal internal distinctions from being assessed empirically here. Any future change to that support/design tradeoff is a **new development stage**, not a retroactive success for this completed center panel.
