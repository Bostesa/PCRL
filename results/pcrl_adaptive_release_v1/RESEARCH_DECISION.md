# Adaptive PCRL: 2018 development decision

**Verdict: `EXPERIMENTAL_NO_ADVANTAGE`.** A trained, callable 17-token release was fitted on all three anchors, with an unchanged public service and a private one-token interface. Neither registered competitive route passed the locked independent development assessment. This is used 2018 data, not new confirmation. The completed 2016 prospective result remains unchanged.

The utility-route nominee was the reference-calibrated, decoder-adapted T32 channel (`A_selected`). Relative to D17, its residence cross-entropy difference (candidate minus control; negative is better) was **+0.001450 nats** under U, simultaneous 95% interval **[-0.003063, +0.005964]**, and **+0.000914** under PWGTP, interval **[-0.004355, +0.006183]**. The route required an upper confidence bound at or below **-0.003** in both weightings and protected-recovery guards at or below **+0.001**. It passed **2 of 30** registered primary interval clauses. It also did not establish a task advantage over the locked task-only or historical-Q representatives. Its task benefit over H was 0.036146 U and 0.038830 PWGTP nats; the separate H-capability intervals cleared 0.01. Capability is not a route pass.

The privacy-route nominee was the separately fitted `PrivacyFirst_selected` channel. Its AB/SEX measured-recovery difference versus D17 (control attack loss minus candidate attack loss; negative is less recovery) was **-0.003672 U**, interval **[-0.008799, +0.001456]**, and **-0.003325 PWGTP**, interval **[-0.009239, +0.002589]**. Those favorable point estimates did not establish the required **-0.002** bound. More decisively, its residence loss was **+0.009539 U**, interval **[+0.003539, +0.015538]**, and **+0.009835 PWGTP**, interval **[+0.003758, +0.015912]**, versus D17: both demonstrate violation of the registered **+0.001** task guard. The same task guard failed against task-only, matched gradient, and historical Q. It passed **5 of 40** primary interval clauses. Its H benefit was 0.028058 U and 0.029909 PWGTP nats, separately exceeding the capability floor.

The two candidate slots were explicitly locked as **diagnostic only** because their inner point rules did not nominate a success. All 70 primary contrasts and four separate H-capability endpoints were scored without final-data selection. Confidence bounds are two-sided 95% Bonferroni within the registered families, from 10,000 common-household paired bootstrap draws across the three overlapping anchors. The intervals condition on the selected fitted objects; they do not cover the full adaptive research history or the ACS survey design. The [endpoint table](ENDPOINT_TABLE.json), [full results](FULL_RESULTS.csv), and [frontier](OUTER_FRONTIER.svg) give all comparisons and per-anchor estimates. Continuous J is shown as a contextual operational reference with a different interface and is not a matched 17-token control.

The repaired same-row min-bank reference made D17 a constructive feasible witness for every nonnegative training allowance; it fixed the historical impossible H-only cuts. Branch A completed its three `.001` center fits and independent audits. Branch B's legal child-state rules reached **no admissible split** under the frozen 100-household support floor on any anchor, leaving exact Branch A aliases. A fixed-bank stochastic-versus-deterministic numerical objective gap on two anchors is a statement about those frozen LP/MILP inputs, not an independently measured advantage or a population privacy guarantee. No randomization or new algorithmic component earned a competitive claim.

Independent replay matched all 70 primary and four capability point estimates to at most **6.17×10⁻¹⁶ nats**, and a separate 10,000-draw bootstrap replay matched every interval decision.
