
It passed **174/174** tests after the scorer correction and **181/181** after the separate artifact-packaging CLI and reviewer-requested channel-shape guard were added. Meaningful fixtures cover the cross-pool H-only contradiction and same-row D17 witness; reference rebasing and probability-floor signs; exact per-token loss and U/PWGTP coefficient replay without multiplying cell mass twice; one-state LP/MILP bounds; allowed-input routing and hidden-row exclusion; household-disjoint roles; frozen inner routes and hash inventories; private contribution replay; three-anchor alias resolution and common-household inference; and tampered/partial merged-panel rejection. These are software and finite-object checks, not empirical power or a population privacy guarantee.

Branch B's attempted 64-state refinement remained at 32 states on all three anchors because the registered 100-unique/100-effective-household support floors blocked eligible child cells. Its recorded status is `SUPPORT_LIMITED_ALIAS_A`, and Linux runtime parity receipts were verified during the run. **Those external parity receipts were not included in the final unit archives.** The restored deployable package therefore uses the hash-pinned T32 channels and the original T32 release API, with no child-state deployment claim. The packaging CLI checks Linux x86, the frozen encoder hash, the locked channel and restored-unit inventories; a newly provisioned host must also reproduce archived T0 codes on an allowed-input parity fixture in the pinned environment before claiming historical bitwise equivalence. The partition controls consequently realized the same 32 leaves. This is a support-limited result, not evidence that richer legal internal state cannot help on a different supported sample. The privacy-first fits completed on all three anchors; fitted-bank `tau` was positive on anchors 0 and 2 and exactly zero on anchor 1. Their independent inner audits used the same standard slate and copied, hash-verified H-only predictor trees from the main panels. The derived combined panels include `PrivacyFirst_selected` without refitting or reselection; all three merged archives were read back and extraction-hash verified, with 5,547, 6,807 and 6,807 files respectively.

Outer scoring needed a documented technical correction after unlock. Independently deserialized object identifiers had been compared as pointer bytes, and a fifth historical pool had extra task-label keys beyond the three required study labels. Failed attempts are retained in [SCORER_CORRECTION.json](SCORER_CORRECTION.json). They did deserialize outer labels, but produced **no scores or predictions** before repair. The correction compares identifiers by typed value, retains numeric H/weight byte comparisons, and requires the three study labels as a subset. It did not change the locked candidates, endpoints, models, channel laws, routes, or margins; the completed 17-token scores were preserved. The correction record pins old/new scorer hashes and the failed SSM invocations. Continuous J remains a differently accessed contextual reference.

All outer inference remains development evidence on previously used 2018 data. The upstream supervised teacher had historical overlap with some globally assigned outer households, and household bootstrap intervals condition on fitted models and adaptive study design. Exact service parity, finite-bank feasibility, independent fitted-risk comparisons, algorithmic advantage and population privacy are distinct claims; the recorded checks establish the first two under their stated scopes and do not by themselves establish the others.

The a0 AB/race revealing positive control was detected under both weightings; registered separate a1/a2 positive-control units were not run, as disclosed in `UNIT_LEDGER.json`. The independent score replay establishes consistency of the fitted slate's frozen predictions, not that its attack families are universally optimal. No external continuous eraser was silently treated as a matched 17-token control.
