# Registered adaptive-release 2018 development protocol

Registered before any new ACS candidate fit or outcome inspection. This is a separate study, not an amendment to the completed 2016 or task-aligned-cuts evaluations. All source objects, failed arms, phase-I records, technical retries, and aliases remain traceable by immutable unit ID. The controller is `restore → reference/integrity → headroom → A centers → B fixtures/refinement → matched controls → independent inner audits → bounded repair → selection lock → one outer development assessment → replay → archive/closeout`. The absolute deadline is 2026-09-24 22:33:31 UTC, with scientific dispatch ending by 20:33:31 UTC and $50 maximum incremental AWS expenditure including reserve, storage and requests. Optional units are removed in this order if a measured center-unit forecast requires it: extra anchor-1/2 allowance, 128-cell refinement, noncenter anchor-0 allowances, then outer training rounds above three. The three-anchor A and B centers, matched controls, both branch diagnoses, and one locked diagnostic assessment have priority.

## Data and roles

Use only admitted, hash-verified 2018 rows and stored Linux x86 T0 codes. 2016 person rows and all 2017 rows are forbidden. Across all three anchors and five historical 2018 pools, a household is assigned exactly one role by the first 64 bits of `SHA256(UTF8("pcrl_adaptive_release_v1|" + household_id)) / 2^64`: intervals `[0,.20)` nuisance/decoder/encoder training, `[.20,.40)` independent audit fitting, `[.40,.65)` coefficient and split search, `[.65,.75)` inner selection, `[.75,.85)` inner checking, `[.85,1)` locked outer development assessment. No role uses a household assigned another role in any anchor. Person IDs remain private and are never counted twice within an anchor. Until lock, outer labels are unavailable through the new role loader, even if an old prepared object contains them. The restored historical teacher saw some assigned outer households upstream; disclose that residual dependence. All fitting support uses the full SEX=2 and RAC1P=9 schemas, with absent classes disclosed and probability floors fixed across reference/candidate scoring. U and PWGTP are separately normalized on the same eligible original people; all bootstrap units are households, shared across anchors, methods and endpoints.

Training, split search and auditing may use authorized 2018 residence and sensitive labels in their allocated roles. Deployment sees only permitted `X_A,H_A` and frozen training-derived nuisance objects; never the person's Y/S, H_B, loss, household assignment or private key as predictive inputs. The released wire has unchanged H service values and one of 17 tokens. A sees `(H_A,Z)`, AB sees `(H_A,H_B,Z)`, B sees its unchanged service. The internal cell, predicted risk vectors, Q row, seed and unused draws are private. Exact risk is `sum_i w_i sum_z q(z|x_i)[-log p(label_i|H_view_i,z)]`, never loss of averaged probabilities. One persistent draw per record is the release contract.

## Reference and optimization

Historical D17 is the fixed Q_ref; every child cell copies its parent row to embed it. For each role/view/target/weighting and retained fixed attack a, coefficients aggregate `L_a(Q)=sum_i w_i sum_z Q[cell(i),z](-log a(s_i|H_view_i,z))`. They already include cell mass. On the identical coefficient people, weights, class definitions and clipping, set `rho_r=min_{a in B_r} L_a(Q_ref)` and require every `L_a(Q)≥rho_r−delta`. Recompute all rho values and rebase every old cut after each bank expansion. Delta is common across roles/weightings in `{0,.001,.003}` nats; center is `.001`. Before every solve, independently replay Q_ref feasibility and phase I. The new target is relative fitted attack risk, not true MI or a population guarantee. An optional paired-cut ablation is labeled separately and cannot replace the main target. The fixed decoder channel step minimizes equal U/PWGTP average task coefficients under row-simplex and all bank cuts. Its LP/dual residuals and a same-bank deterministic MILP lower bound are numerical finite-bank certificates only.

Branch A begins with the common coverage decoder and T32/17-token code. Fit center `.001` on all three anchors, then optional allowances. Refit task decoders on the exact expected-token training law, retaining earlier decoders; independently fit sensitive best responses on the audit-fit role, evaluate cuts on coefficient rows, rebase reference, and re-solve candidate and equal-opportunity controls. At most six outer rounds; stop a branch arm for exact aliases, three rounds without the preregistered inner-selection improvement, or fixed budget. The joint decoder/channel problem is nonconvex; a fixed-bank LP step is not global joint optimality. If old fixed and fresh decoder rankings disagree, this branch still runs.

Branch B retains T32 parents and the same 17-token wire, using up to 64 then 128 child cells. Candidate legal split features are H_A, frozen residence residual/posterior, and frozen SEX/full-RAC1P nuisance probabilities trained solely on the nuisance role. No fitting/checking/coefficient household trains its own nuisance predictor. Child support floor is **100 unique fitting households** and effective weighted support at least 100 per child, fixed before split outcomes. Parent fallback handles unseen/empty routes. Every old Q lifts exactly to children. Rank bounded training-quantile splits by the fixed-price score `min_z Σ_{i∈parent} g_i(z) − Σ_child min_z Σ_{i∈child} g_i(z)`, with `g_i(z)=u_i(z)−Σ_j λ_j a_ij(z)` and already weighted contributions. Check split stability on inner checking. The score is nonnegative for exact partitions at fixed duals, but does not guarantee feasible or held-out gain. At most four split batches and six outer updates; preserve 32/64/128 checkpoints. Exact B1 rational and B2 contextual fixtures precede ACS fit. No extra-Y-signal gate cancels B. Compare against no refinement, task-only, nonadaptive joint-risk and seeded random eligible partitions at matched leaf support, supervision and tuning work, including a same-partition deterministic optimizer.

Strong controls are H alone, historical D17/Q, compatible same-host J, newly trained task-only predictor and deployable 17-token quantization, unconstrained rowwise deterministic objective checks, same-final-bank deterministic MILP/search, and a matched exact-token gradient adversarial adaptation with D17 and one alternative initialization. Constant-token replacement and 17-token randomized response on D17 and task-only predictor use rates `.50,.75,.90,1.00`, with no visible 18th missing symbol. Operational historical continuous methods are context unless freshly adapted/audited under a compatible contract. An optional privacy-first LP maximizes AB/SEX tau in both weightings, retaining all other role floors at `rho−.001` and both fixed-decoder task losses within +.001 of D17. A zero tau forced by H-only cuts is retained as a result.

## Selection and assessment

Before assessment, fit the same standard and up to two predeclared calibrated boosted-tree/residual-logit audit additions to every compatible arm if positive-control leakage is missed. Select attacks and fresh task probes on inner selection only; inner checking supplies development comparisons. Include H-only and legal A/B/AB ancestors; token predictors see H and token one-hot only. Keep all eight sensitive endpoints, both weightings, absolute recovery versus H, and task CE. No final-outcome attack maximum or per-person Q row reaches a predictor. A deliberately revealing release and nonlinear interaction fixture test audit sensitivity. Weak audits limit privacy claims, never move an endpoint.

For differences, task is `CE_Y(candidate)−CE_Y(control)` and sensitive recovery is `CE_S(control)−CE_S(candidate)`; negative sensitive difference favors protection. Route U requires task ≤−.003 in **both** weightings and all eight sensitive differences ≤+.001. Route P requires task ≤+.001 in both, AB/SEX sensitive difference ≤−.002 in both, and all other sensitive differences ≤+.001. Both require residence benefit over H ≥.01 at the point estimate in both weights, legal inputs, H parity and audit integrity. The U/P margins do not depend on training delta. At most one nominee per route, chosen by equal-anchor-averaged inner-selection rule and evaluated unchanged on all three anchors. Every independent baseline family includes a clearly labeled D17 fallback and locks one U representative among points with max sensitive difference ≤+.001 by lexicographic `(max task difference, mean task difference, max sensitive difference, stable unit ID)`. It locks one P representative among points with max task difference ≤+.001 and max sensitive difference ≤+.001 by `(max AB/SEX difference, mean AB/SEX difference, max task difference, stable unit ID)`. Exact aliases remain named but one distinct comparator. A broad competitive route must pass against D17 and every locked strong simple/task-only, gradient-adversarial and independently constructed deterministic representative. Same-partition deterministic equality blocks a stochastic component claim; independent algorithm ties still block superiority. J and continuous-method reference curves have their own disclosed scope.

If no nominee passes its inner point rule, lock at most one explicitly **diagnostic** candidate per branch and still run the mandatory controls on the outer development role. Before opening it, `SELECTION_LOCK.json` enumerates all candidate slots, comparator points, routes, endpoints, anchor aggregation, exact hashes, model selection, and multiplicity. The default is family-wide simultaneous two-sided 95% Bonferroni intervals over the locked comparisons, with a common 10,000-draw household bootstrap across anchors/methods/endpoints. Intervals condition on selected fitted objects and omit full design/model-selection uncertainty. Outer labels open once; no route, split, hyperparameter, attack, threshold or candidate changes afterward. A scorer-only bug preserves both records and rescoring of the unchanged lock. This is independently audited **development** evidence, not untouched confirmation.

## Bounded controller and integrity

The first complete center benchmarks RSS, disk, duration, and forecast before optional units. A and B both execute despite the other's negative result. Corrected Q_ref infeasibility is a stop-and-repair defect; a prior historical target remains failed. H-only audits trigger positive-control and declared attack additions, while U continues. No extra task signal beyond T32 does not stop B. Split overfitting gets one fallback: previous smaller nested partition with quadratic shrinkage toward copied parent Q, coefficient from `{0,.01,.1}` chosen on inner selection, and matched control opportunities. At most two independently reviewed prelock design amendments for demonstrated defects; a third substantive change ends the current design. Same-science technical retries preserve failure evidence and are bounded. No result is accepted without simplex, source, bank, model, service and output hashes. No new scientific dispatch after the science cutoff or when projected disk falls below 5 GiB. The final stage archives, reads back, restores a fitted unit, shuts down only task-owned resources, and reports measured costs/retained storage. A working losing model remains labeled `EXPERIMENTAL_NO_ADVANTAGE`.
