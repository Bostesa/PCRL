## Scope statements carried into the paper

1. **Four bodies of work, no connecting pipeline.** The repositories contain:
   - (i) the original purpose-specific PCRL encoder work (LoRA heads on a seeded random frozen backbone, Rounds 1–7);
   - (ii) attribute-removal and isolate/noise work (erase layer, LEACE / INLP / LAFTR / SPLINCE comparisons);
   - (iii) the later ACS finite-release studies (durable-guarantees and the ACS evidence branches);
   - (iv) the new stored-model/output analyses (pilot, matched benchmark, output-aware, diagnosis, this study).

   No code path connects them into one evaluated architecture, and the paper never describes them as one.
2. **The backbone is seeded and random, not pretrained.**
3. **No R²-to-accuracy guarantee.** The retired guarantee is not used. Recovery numbers are measured properties of fitted attackers on these rows.
4. **The `main` branch API is not claimed fixed.** The claims audit found that main ships the retired API.
5. **Best-validation vs final-checkpoint counts stay separate.** The Round-4/5 evidence has two families of counts (22→8 and 26→19 in the evidence inventory). They are reported in separate rows, never merged.
6. **Absolute vs additional recovery.** Cross-purpose (coalition) recovery is reported both as an absolute pair AUC and as the increment over the strongest individual release. The two are never conflated.
7. **Reused rows.** Every Stage-C number is development evidence on already-used assessment rows.
8. **"Not significant" is never used** for an interval that excludes zero but fails a larger target (row 1).

## Do any decisions change?

**No.** Every registered PASS / NOT_ESTABLISHED decision in the earlier studies is unchanged. Two changes are interpretive:
- **FARE.** "FARE contributed nothing beyond compression" becomes "FARE contributed a small amount beyond compression (+0.007, interval [0.002, 0.013]), below the 0.02 materiality target".
- **The offset effect.** It is not seed-uniform on HMDA, and it holds on 5 of 14 pairs.

Both make the empirical paper more careful. Neither changes the recommendation.
