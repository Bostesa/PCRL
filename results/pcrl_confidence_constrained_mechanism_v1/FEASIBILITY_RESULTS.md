## 5. What these results do not show

- They do not show any privacy result on Adult: no attacker was fitted and no label was read.
- They do not show that the average-loss problem (hcal's 0.010 / 0.005 allowances) is infeasible. G is stricter.
- They do not show that other capacities or constants fail. None was searched, by design.
- A finite-law optimum is not a population certificate.
