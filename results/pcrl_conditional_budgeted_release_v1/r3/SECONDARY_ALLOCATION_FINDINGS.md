Both ORIGINAL cells in both packages were infeasible before protected-label
objective optimization. Independent Phase-I replay verified positive dual
certificates for the declared families and safeguards. These cells provide
no fresh attack result. They do not establish infeasibility for all mechanisms.

The wide package retained every declared column: 10,600 parameters for seed
0 and 10,863 for seed 1. The narrow package used 432 and 352. Context-shared
neighbor-position templates and context-shared global-code templates differ
in capacity and parameter sharing. The package comparison does not isolate a
support-only effect or prove one family contains the other.

Wide improved the equal-seed fitted composite over G_STRICT by
0.008783534276. Its difference from narrow was only 0.000008000146 on the
same fitted composite, with opposite seed directions. Neither number is a
fresh recovery gain or evidence that a joint component caused the difference.

lookup and injective integer encoding of actual output pairs. The full-shape
engineering preflight measured 0.93988 GiB peak RSS. Both admitted WIDE V2

actual-law replay passed for both seeds.
