For seed 0, C fails the unchanged INNER point gate on occupation Brier excess versus U0 (0.005176977955143592 > 0.005) and occupation NLL excess versus Ucal (0.010696750169764834 > 0.010). ORIGINAL is not automatically a relaxation of STRICT on these fitting rows. P0 already has negative remaining budgets. This does not establish infeasibility of other supports, held-out service requirements or population utility.

Independent primary replay passed in both seeds, including all utility witnesses, ORIGINAL Phase-I certificates, fitted kernels, objective calculations and constraint matrices. The largest primary primal residual was about 4.1e-10 and largest dual residual about 5.1e-9. Caps remain unchanged; boundary clauses are explicitly marked numerically unresolved.
