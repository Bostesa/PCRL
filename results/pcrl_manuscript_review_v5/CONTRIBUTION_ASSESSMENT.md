# CONTRIBUTION_ASSESSMENT — v5

**Strongest supported claim.** On a locked, previously unused survey year (ACS 2017, Study 1), a penalty
built from the coalition's joint view lowers measured sex/race recovery relative to both an equal-strength
and an equal-total-mass local control: 14 of 16 sensitive cells better under simultaneous 95% intervals,
none worse, two unresolved. The residence cost is bounded only by a point rule (interval noninferiority at
.001 holds in 0 of 4 comparisons).

**Primary contribution.** The release problem (an immutable published output plus an appended channel for
one recipient) and its measurement contract: per-recipient and coalition audit roles, independently fitted
attackers, H-only subtraction with absolute recovery alongside, unclipped negative increments, per-endpoint
reporting, denominators beside counts, both weightings.

**Method status after Study 5.** Not competitive. Five development studies, all negative against the
internal channel J:

| study | what was tried | outcome |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | nonlinear penalty, rank | 0/8 coordination cells; one endpoint significantly worse |
| 3 | exact invariant repair | 8/48 cells significantly more disclosure, 0 less |
| 4 | adversarial refinement from A0 | 189 worse / 18 better (all residence) |
| 5 | J-initialised factorial; coalition projection of J | no prospectively selected release met the conjunction; selection returned J; projections trade residence like LEACE on J; no advantage over matched local projections |

**What Study 5 adds that is scientifically useful.**
* J initialisation is no longer untested; under this objective and selection rule it does not improve on J.
* Teacher strength matters for A0-initialised training (a coefficient finding, not a method).
* An exploratory coalition-specific race effect exists on the weak channel A0 at k=2 (family X only).
* The coalition-projection construction is formally an adaptation (limit of an existing eigensystem).

**Not claimed.** Any bound on I(S; Z | H); equivalence from nonsignificance; stronger-attack survival;
fresh-year confirmation (2016 sealed, unscored); coalition-specific benefit on J; novelty of the eigensystem.

**Remaining scientific gap.** Whether a release beside J can add authorised capability at bounded added
disclosure (the utility-first direction), and whether the A0 coalition race effect has any analogue on a
channel with residual race signal comparable to A0 but utility comparable to J. Neither has evidence yet.
