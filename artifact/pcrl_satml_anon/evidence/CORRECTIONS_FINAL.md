## Boundaries restated in the paper

* A finite-table conditional-information budget is not a full-continuous-$H$ population guarantee.
* Publishing one sampled token differs from publishing the channel row or redrawing per query.
* Negative finite-attacker increments do not demonstrate negative conditional mutual information.
* Unresolved intervals are neither equivalence nor harm.
* A supervised task result is not an unseen-task result.
