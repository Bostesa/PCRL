# Frozen C2 objective and gradient diagnostic

The predecessor already measured nonzero protection gradients and noncollapsed coordinate variances. Neither zero gradients nor collapse is a default explanation. This campaign distinguishes arithmetic on its saved logs from genuinely regenerated parameter-gradient tensors.

## Fixed descriptive comparisons

For each family/strength/seed path, use the available ordered 0/5/10/20 checkpoints; do not invent missing epochs. Report consecutive checkpoint differences for occupation NLL, summed Brier, accuracy, mean confidence and fresh ATT-validation SEX envelopes. Compute descriptive Spearman ranks along the fixed path between the saved sensitive objective and fresh validation recovery, and between task loss and recovery. Keep each seed separate; report the paired signs and whether they recur. With fewer than three valid ordered checkpoints, ranks are unavailable. Ties use average ranks. Do not search extra lags, transformations or selective correlations.

Confidence decline precedes observed protection when the first saved transition with worsening NLL/Brier or decreasing confidence occurs before the first transition with declining fresh recovery. This is coarse checkpoint timing, not the exact within-epoch causal onset. Report both transitions and all intermediate differences. A source nomination's exclusion is attributed to its actual frozen rule and actual utility values, never inferred from its absence alone.

Conditional-kernel power, online RAW critic risk, and an external SEX AUC are different objectives. Rank agreement is descriptive, not a population guarantee. A fresh reader disagreement may reflect capacity, training exposure, finite selection, numerical scaling or surrogate mismatch. Unsupported conditioning strata remain a plausible explanation until separately measured.

## Real FIT regeneration

Queue entrypoint: `python -m occupation_bottleneck.gradient_worker --spec PRIVATE_SPEC.json` with one CPU thread and stage `admission`. The spec must bind the source manifest/hash, checkpoint/hash, source seed, operation `regenerate_fixed_fit_gradients`, explicit full-role FIT row indices, private output directory and safe aggregate output path. Freeze one to four batches, each one to 256 distinct rows, entirely within inherited FIT folds 0/1. The batch indices themselves stay private; aggregate results bind their hashes. No runtime sampling, optimizer step or critic training occurs in this operation.

Use actual checkpoint encoder and current own heads. Compute uniform mean-row summed task CE, occupation CE, occupation reported NLL with the 1e-12 floor, and summed multiclass Brier. A source RAW checkpoint with saved actual neural critics permits regeneration of its sensitivity penalty `sum_views[1−min(critic_CE,entropy)/entropy]`, with its exact saved normalization, source prior and parameters. Freeze those critics during encoder differentiation and perform zero critic updates. The constant entropy envelope can yield a locally zero gradient; that is a measured state, not evidence that all training failed. A JOINT or U0 state without saved neural critics is `MISSING_SAVED_NEURAL_CRITIC`, never a zero sensitive gradient. The fixed-batch worker does not label a new surrogate or new critic fit as the original source objective.

The low-level `batch_gradients` interface additionally accepts an explicitly restored conditional-kernel callback. A legitimate callback must bind the saved kernel reference, actual source objective, FIT0/1 strata and registered fixed draw recipe; a new draw is a regenerated gradient at that state, not replay of a historical logged minibatch. The default worker records conditional-kernel regeneration unavailable separately from its valid task and RAW gradients.

Record vectors for all actual model parameters and for the occupation encoder/current head subset in named-parameter order. Reduce squared norms and pairwise dot products in float64; cosine is undefined when either norm is exactly zero. Losses are mean-row losses; gradients are not independently unit-normalized before norm comparisons. Cosine<0 means conflicting minimization directions. Sensitive adversarial penalties are minimized; the underlying critic CE is maximized by that direction. Do not reverse this sign when discussing conflict.

Private `gradients.pt` retains actual flattened tensors by batch/block/loss. Safe summaries bind its hash, parameter ordering/sizes, losses, norms and cosines. Saved source `GRADIENT_LOG.json` norms are independently listed as source logs, never promoted to regenerated tensors. Independent replay must recompute norms and dots from these tensors and regenerate at least one fixed batch from the actual artifact when runnable.

## Runnable update mechanism

`project_displacement(p,A,max_norm)` minimizes distance to the proposed parameter displacement under two homogeneous NLL/Brier halfspaces and a Euclidean step bound. Normalize every strictly nonzero task-gradient row, including tiny gradients; exactly zero rows are vacuous. Enumerate active sets and certify the normalized residuals with tolerance 1e-10. The zero displacement is always a feasible witness. A numerical solver failure returns a logged conservative zero fallback, never an uncertified step.

`finite_step(parameters,p,A,evaluator,max_norm)` applies the actual displacement to the actual parameter dtypes. It checks the realized, rounded displacement against halfspaces and the norm bound, and evaluates real finite-step FIT-batch NLL and Brier. Guarded arms accept only nonincrease up to 1e-10 per metric at fractions 1/.5/.25/.125; unsuccessful attempts restore exact original parameters and log a zero-step rejection. Float32 updates that round to zero are separately logged. These safeguards concern an update batch, not held-out usefulness.

`occupation_refinement_step` combines a real occupation task CE update with the sensitivity update, so task learning is retained. It uses bounded SGD with no momentum/preconditioning, and certifies after dtype casting. Its signature supports constrained, ordinary, exact local twin and task-only arms. Income encoder/head tensors are snapshot-checked bitwise after every step. This is an implementation-ready conditional prototype, not evidence that the pilot trigger has been satisfied.
