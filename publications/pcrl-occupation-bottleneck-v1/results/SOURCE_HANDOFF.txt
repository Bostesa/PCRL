Complete all three diagnostic parts. Each conclusion needs a specific measurement and an independent check.

C1. Exact own-head geometry

For the current linear head, let centered logits be A z + c, where A is the class-centered head matrix. Construct the orthogonal row-space projector P = A^+ A in float64 under a declared SVD rank rule.

Check on real authorized inputs that:
A P = A within tolerance;
centered logits from Pz match those from z;
probabilities match;
decisions match on every checked row.

A rank rule, tolerance, and treatment of near-ties must be frozen before real results. If exact decision preservation fails, report it and do not advertise that package as decision preserving.

Attack Pz plus its actual own outputs on both recipients and the pair for U0, RAW, and JOINT anchors. Release no discarded residual or raw z alongside it. Make the projection public to the reader.

This is a diagnostic compression control. It protects the current head's computation, not arbitrary future tasks. In exact arithmetic Pz is reconstructible from the centered logits and fixed head parameters. An invertible re-expression of the same scores removes no information.

If this ordinary compression captures the gain, credit ordinary compression. It is not a new privacy-training or joint-design result. A lower-dimensional effective disclosure is not an equal-capacity representation win.

8. MEASURE THE GAP WITHOUT A FALSE PROGRESS PERCENTAGE

For every fixed nominee and diagnostic checkpoint report:

- Pair benefit against the current matched RAW anchor.
- Required additional benefit to reach the original 0.020 target.
- Income and occupation local worsening against that anchor.
- Utility slack or excess for each original task/seed/reference clause.
- The interval classifications separately from the point comparisons.

For a fixed candidate C and matched comparator R:
pair benefit = AUC(R_pair) - AUC(C_pair);
point target deficit = 0.020 - pair benefit;
local guard excess = AUC(C_recipient) - AUC(R_recipient) - 0.010.

Positive deficit or excess means the point requirement is missed. These quantities are not interchangeable and cannot be combined into a meaningful "percent of the algorithm completed."

The predecessor's RAW pair AUC 0.856824 implies a nominal target around 0.836824, before uncertainty, if that exact reader/control comparison is retained. Fresh matched readers may change it. Recompute, do not hard-code an old AUC into a new claim.

Show a discrete observed Pareto frontier. Do not claim that interpolating between two trained models creates a deployable feasible method. Do not promote a historical model that fails the current utility requirements.

Point-feasible but interval-unresolved is a precision issue for that clause. A pair-benefit interval entirely below 0.020 is an effect-size shortfall. Missing comparisons are missing, not negative evidence.

Preferred intervention when the evidence indicates occupation update conflict or prediction leakage:

Keep the current RAW income encoder and head frozen as an identical paired anchor in every pilot arm. Modify only the occupation encoder and its own head. This keeps the existing strong income protection and tests the main remaining bottleneck.

The substantive change should concern update direction or an explicit task-feasibility safeguard, rather than another weight sweep:
- propose a sensitive-loss update;
- constrain its local direction against occupation NLL and Brier gradients on authorized FIT-update batches;
- bound the step and check the declared task safeguard;
- record accepted/rejected steps and active constraints.

One possible implementation is a small gradient projection/QP against task-gradient halfspaces plus a bounded step. The designer and math reviewer must specify the exact update, sign conventions, objective, tolerances, and finite-step safeguard before fits. Constrain the actual proposed parameter displacement: Adam momentum or preconditioning can invalidate a constraint applied only to an earlier raw gradient. Confirm a zero displacement is a feasible witness, log zero-step stalls, and require a real finite-step task check rather than claiming the local linearization guarantees usefulness. First-order constraints do not guarantee held-out usefulness. This is an empirical constrained-training mechanism, not a privacy or optimization theorem.

Preserve task-training updates needed to maintain or improve the real occupation head. Evaluate accuracy and every class recall even when the safeguard uses differentiable NLL/Brier. Safeguards may use FIT folds 0/1 only; FIT fold 2 remains internal selection.

- unchanged RAW incumbent;
- ordinary adversarial occupation continuation with the same starting weights, critics, update count, optimizer and candidate budget, without the new task safeguard;
- exact local-only twin of the candidate, removing only the pair term;
- task-only occupation continuation with the same training exposure and selection budget.

Keep disclosure, head class order, source initialization, effective feature dimension, and reader budget matched. Charge extra critic training and constraint solves. If a controller, new attacker family, or extra distillation term is added, it needs a separately isolated control; default omit it.

Use a single predeclared recipe across both seeds. At most three common recipes may be considered if engineering requires a small fixed step-size ladder; the ladder, training lengths, and ranking rule must be frozen before any pilot fit. Do not append recipes after an adverse result.

Default maximum training length: 20 epochs with checkpoints at 0/5/10/20, subject to the remaining resource budget. Nominate one common configuration per arm using FIT-fold-2 utility and ATT-development evidence under a fully recorded exploratory selection rule. Match access and selection effort across arms.

A mechanism that merely changes the gradient magnitude, refreshes critics, or tunes the old kernel is not this repair. The ordinary continuation control must be strong enough that a new safeguard earns its benefit.
