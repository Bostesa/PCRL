# Research decision: a different competitive release mechanism (campaign `pcrl_new_release_mechanisms_v1`)

Florida ACS 2019 development cohort (admitted public-use PUMS records), 2026-10-20. Development evidence on adaptively used
MODEL_SELECT records, for the declared readers only. Lower recovery for these readers is not protection against every reader.

## Decision

**Finite negative result.** There is no frozen development candidate, no useful component, no joint-design result and no novelty
claim. Both mechanism families closed by their own registered rules. The coordinator launched no replacement family (decision A4, not
a rule outcome). Neither the statistical reviewer nor the challenger objected.

**Headline** (the statistical reviewer's required wording, `roles/statistical_reviewer/CLOSING_STATEMENT.md`):

> No new release mechanism met this campaign's frozen development criteria, and no development candidate was selected. Family A
> (propensity-routed finite cells) closed at its internal capacity stage; only its task-only twin was evaluated. Family B's
> channel-trained learner failed the original utility limits. Its revision, a SEX-targeted Gaussian logit channel applied post hoc to
> the strongest admitted task-only base, kept both tasks within the original limits at two strengths, but did not lower linked-pair
> SEX recovery beyond its same-form, SEX-blind control at the same strength (benefit +0.0027 and +0.0019 AUC, each with SE 0.0089,
> not resolved). It was not separated from task spending on either SEX-blind control path, so its registered falsifier F1 holds and
> the family closed. The recovery reduction it did achieve against its base (about 0.011 pair AUC at the higher strength) was matched
> by untargeted noise of the same cost. No superiority over the nine admitted references, no 0.020 material improvement, no
> useful-component result, no joint-design contribution and no novelty is claimed. All results are development evidence on adaptively
> used MODEL_SELECT records, for the declared readers only; lower recovery for these readers is not protection against every reader.

Reading note (coordinator): the headline's "about 0.011 pair AUC" is the base-instance reading (registered-cell mean pair AUC 0.6605 for
the base against 0.6496 for FB2_SEX_S5). The Monte Carlo pooled reading that the claims and their uncertainties use is +0.0120 (se
0.0073, not resolved; `TABLES.md` section 2). The quoted wording itself is unchanged.

## Selection and frontiers

- **Selection** (`nrm.rules.select_candidate`, claims/FINAL_V2): `NO_DEVELOPMENT_CANDIDATE`.
- **Original benchmark:** FB2_SEX_S3 and FB2_SEX_S5 are G4 `NOT_SUPERIOR` against all nine admitted references and G5 `NOT_MET`
  against NEURAL_RAW and H_LJ. Their pair AUC (0.6536, 0.6486, Monte Carlo pooled) is above that of six of the nine references (RETIRED_B3CTL 0.6162,
  SUCC 0.6184, B2TWIN 0.6267, H_LJ 0.6327, NEURAL_RAW 0.6361, TWJ 0.6461) and below only W, TR100 and TT100 (0.6565-0.6609), against
  which no benefit is resolved either.
- **Reserve-qualified frontier** (`nrm.rules.reserve_frontier`): FB2_SEX_S3 has the lowest pair AUC among the six reserve-qualified
  points, but its benefit is a point estimate only against each of the other five (FB2_BASE, FB2_MINF_S3, W, TR100, TT100):
  `NO_RESERVE_FRONTIER_IMPROVEMENT`. So: **neither** the original benchmark **nor** the reserve-qualified frontier.
- **Robustness readers** (descriptive; never a decision input): on 16 new cells (repetitions R3/R4, reader seeds k2/k3) every
  comparison is `ROBUSTNESS_NOT_CONFIRMED`. The SEX channel's benefit over its twin keeps its sign but is never resolved, and its edge
  over the same-strength SEX-blind channel stays near zero and changes sign (`roles/reader_robustness/PHASE2_REPORT.md`). Two seed labels
  apply: the registered grid passes its validity check (A-SC1, `READER_SEEDS_EFFECTIVE`), while the aggregate's reader-seed qualifier is
  `READER_SEEDS_PARTLY_EFFECTIVE` (identical k0 and k1 cells in some blocks), so no "across reader seeds" statement is made.

## What is closed and what stays open

**Closed by this campaign's rules:** FA-PRC at the capacity stage; family B v1 (utility); FB2 by F1, with no strength, coupling or
penalty tuning (the card's stop rule); MEAN and FB2P not triggered.

**Routes not tested** (the challenger's closing list; "no replacement" does not mean these were exhausted): propensity-routed finite
cells beyond the twin (HQ, ORD and SHUF never ran); continuous-mixture and temperature-matched controls; randomized response on the
decisions; input-side recoding or erasure of the six fields or of the 145-column encoding; training-time SEX-balanced reweighting; the
MMP mixture (not authorized; it awaits the advisor's target decision of 2026-10-16); an isolated coupled-versus-decoupled randomness
contrast.

## What the evidence suggests for the next decision (descriptive, not a rule outcome)

- The strongest admitted base is 0.024-0.028 pair AUC above the target references. Post-hoc noise at affordable spend lowered recovery
  by about 0.01, and SEX-blind noise of the same cost did about as much (not separated). For this base and the declared readers, this is
  consistent with the gap lying in what the task outputs themselves transmit rather than in how they are post-processed; it was not
  tested directly.
- In Designer B's fit-time records, the targeted channel cut in-sample linear SEX discriminability by 36-40% at S5 (SEX-blind: 11-14%),
  yet the readers' pair AUC fell no more than under SEX-blind noise of the same cost (about 0.012 either way). This is consistent with
  the readers using SEX information outside the single targeted direction; that, too, was not tested directly.
- Measured for the declared readers, the occupation view's SEX information sits mostly in the non-argmax remainder (occupation-view
  AUC 0.643 to 0.560 when the remainder is replaced by fixed shares). Removing it failed the occupation NLL clauses (1.8-2.2 times the
  cap) and the men's guard (2.7-3.0 times), and the linked pair stayed at 0.646.
- Designer B and the challenger both recommend a label-light diagnostic before any new output channel: on fixed readers, how much SEX
  information sits in decisions, in each recipient's confidence, in the remainder and in the linked residual.
- This campaign does not show that no mechanism can close the gap (decision A4 is not such evidence).

## Limits of this evidence

- MODEL_SELECT was used adaptively by this and every earlier campaign; FB2 was designed after family-B screening on the same people.
- Effects of 0.002-0.012 AUC carry standard errors of 0.007-0.009, about 40% of the 0.020 target; "not separated" is not "equivalent".
- One released instance per person; repeated-query protection is not claimed.
- Six admitted raw fields; two task heads; one cohort; the declared reader grid only.
