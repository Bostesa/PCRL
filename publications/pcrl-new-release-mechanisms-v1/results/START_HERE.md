# Start here: PCRL new release mechanisms (campaign `pcrl_new_release_mechanisms_v1`)

A bounded research-and-development campaign, run on 2026-10-20 on the existing Florida ACS 2019 development cohort (admitted
public-use PUMS records). It looked for a different competitive release mechanism after the leaf-score correction line was closed.
Two independent designers built two mechanism families; a challenger built the simplest SEX-blind controls; a statistical reviewer
froze the rules before outcomes; an independent verifier replayed the numbers with separate code.

**Outcome: a finite negative result.** No development candidate was selected, and neither family met the frozen development criteria.
The statistical reviewer's required headline wording is in `RESEARCH_DECISION.md`.

## Read in this order

1. `ADVISOR_BRIEF.md`: the eight questions of the brief answered in two pages, with the frontier figure.
2. `RESEARCH_DECISION.md`: the decision, what closed each line, what stays untested, the limits of the evidence.
3. `TABLES.md`: utility and recovery per release, matched-control comparisons, frontier against the declared control paths and the
   reserve-qualified frontier (generated by `nrm.tables` from `claims/FINAL_V2/CLAIMS.json`).
4. `figures/FRONTIER.png` (table view `figures/FRONTIER.csv`).
5. `CLOSEST_WORK.md`: closest published work and earlier PCRL attempts for each operation.
6. `VERIFICATION.md`: the independent verifier's replay and its final report.
7. `HANDOFF.md`: for the manuscript and claims owners.

## What this campaign does not claim

No superiority over the admitted references, no 0.020 material improvement, no useful component, no joint-design contribution and no
novelty. All results are development evidence on adaptively used MODEL_SELECT records, for the declared readers only. Lower recovery
for these readers is not protection against every reader. One released instance per person; repeated-query protection is not claimed.
