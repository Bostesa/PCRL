# Start here: an input-side family and a joint pair-release family (campaign `pcrl_input_and_pair_release_v1`)

A bounded development investigation, run on 2026-10-22 (start 01:19:06Z) on the Florida ACS 2019 development cohort (admitted
public-use PUMS records).

**What the service does.** It predicts an income class and an occupation group from six fields (AGEP, WKHP, COW, SCHL, MAR, POBP).
Each of two recipients receives only its own probability vector and argmax decision.

**What the privacy evaluation measures.** How well classifiers ("readers") infer the dataset's recorded SEX attribute from what the
recipients receive: from each output alone, and from the linked pair.

**The task.** Develop and test a materially different release method that keeps the predictions useful while reducing that inference.
It was to be compared against an equally trained task-only twin, matched controls, the simplest alternative explanation and the strong
inherited references.

**Result: no method is established.**
- **The designs.** Two designers proposed operations in disjoint spaces, and a challenger reviewed both before any card was committed:
  - **IA**, shared SEX-aware field generalization (SFG, Designer A): a SEX-ordered ladder of coarser raw-field levels; both task models
    are refitted on the coarsened record.
  - **IB**, coordinated keyed selective flattening (CSF): for a keyed share of people, both vectors are collapsed to their decision-cell
    means. Designer B proposed a SEX-targeted version (CSF-T); the challenger redirected it to the uniform, SEX-blind collapse, and
    CSF-T became its targeting ablation.
- **Utility.** Every candidate met all 28 original utility conditions at the point (12 clauses and 2 guards in each version). Point
  feasibility is not confidence preservation.
- **The registered readings.** IA_SFG and IB_CSF are both NOT_SEPARATED from their matched controls under the frozen rules, and both are
  TASK_ONLY_FEASIBLE_NO_PROTECTION (scopes in `RESEARCH_DECISION.md`).
  - IA_SFG: +0.0016 (se_agg 0.0058) against the twin. Its S3 budget stopped the ladder at its first informative step (married / not
    married), so it is a near-identity release. Its higher income-view AUC is a reader-selection sensitivity in one repetition, not
    resolved leakage.
  - IB_CSF: +0.0069 (0.0057) against the twin and +0.0007 (0.0081) against its SEX-blind spending bracket (which spends 0.0037 nats
    more), so it is not separated from SEX-blind spending.
- **One adaptive revision of IA.** IA_OSG (ADAPTIVE_R1) generalizes only the occupation recipient's input; the income recipient keeps
  the twin's output.
  - It was diagnosed from the registered reading: the twin's pair recovery sits in its occupation view, and income held back the shared
    ladder.
  - It lowered the occupation view at the point (0.6351 against 0.6443), but the linked pair hardly moved (+0.0017, se 0.0062).
  - It is not separated from any of its controls. At the point it is more recoverable than its occupation-restricted release-only tilt
    (-0.0072, se 0.0064, against a bracket point that fails the utility limits in one version; not resolved).
  - D2: ADAPTIVE_R1__NOT_SEPARATED. It is never IA's result.
- **The count.** D2 was met in 0 of 2 registered and 0 of 1 adaptive readings, 3 looks in total; one AGG_POSITIVE comparison passes a
  true null in about 0.07-2% of replicates under six moderate NRM1 known laws and about 8% when one version or split dominates, and
  with 3 looks the chance that at least one passes by chance is at most 3 times the per-look rate.
- **References and criterion.** No release is competitive with the strong references (D3 NOT_SUPERIOR) or meets the 0.020 material
  criterion (D4 NOT_MET). The best results against NEURAL_RAW and H_LJ are -0.0158 and -0.0225 (IB_CSF).
- **Robustness.** Matched reader refits (new household splits and reader seeds of the same people) were genuinely effective and confirm
  nothing.
  - The family readings are IA_R1_D2R and IB_REG_D2R. IA_OSG became IA's strongest release by 0.0001 over IA_SFG, a tie in substance
    and a winner's-curse selection: +0.0028 (se 0.0059) against the twin on the refit cells.
  - IA_REG_D2R is an extra reading of the registered candidate.

The statistical reviewer's required headline is quoted verbatim in `RESEARCH_DECISION.md`.

## What this campaign does not claim

- **No method, candidate or component.** D2 was not met in any reading, and no point-only difference is a reduction.
- **No income-view leakage for IA.** IA_SFG's higher income-view AUC rests on one repetition in which the selected reader switched. The
  verifier's fixed LR and HGB readers in that repetition, the data-rich readers and the MODEL_FIT readers show no comparable difference.
- **No SEX targeting for IB.** IB_CSF is SEX-blind by construction: SEX entered only through the MODEL_FIT strength guards, which fixed
  pi* together with income NLL. Its gain over the twin is not separated from SEX-blind dithered quantization (its SPEND bracket).
  "Not separated" is neither "equal to" nor "is spending".
- **No information bound for IA.** IA's RO comparisons are against the generic release-only tilt (the declared fallback). They are not
  an information bound, and the operation driven only by the twin's release was not built.
- **IA_OSG is not IA's result.** It is an adaptive revision chosen after the registered reading, and its selection as IA's strongest
  release (by 0.0001) is a winner's-curse selection on the same cells.
- **Nothing beyond the declared readers and data.** No improvement over the strong references, no 0.020 margin, no joint-design result
  and no novelty.
- **No confirmation.** All evidence is development evidence on previously and adaptively used records, for the declared readers only.
- **Nothing about the closed roles.** The reserved populations and the sealed assessment stayed closed.
