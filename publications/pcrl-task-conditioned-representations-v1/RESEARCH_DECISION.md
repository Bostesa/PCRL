# Research decision

## What was tested

Separate 83→64→64→16 income and occupation encoders, with correctly folded useful scaler/logistic task heads, were continued jointly. All predictions come from the actual released features. The conditional kernel method matches SEX groups within each task label and within aligned task-label pairs, retaining the published target-power utility objective. The official variance proxy is retained as code; it is not a calibrated power or privacy guarantee. Unsupported pair strata cover 9.5666634% of optimization mass and remain outside the pair penalty.

Forty-six two-seed configurations were fitted under the fixed three-strength ladder. FIT-only nomination selected strength 0.3 and epoch 5 for protected arms. Utility-only and utility-target-power continuation selected epoch 0, exact U0 aliases. The required local twin, stronger local family, unconditional joint, ordinary adversarial joint and both freeze-first orders were completed. Sequential search had 37.5% extra search compute, favoring those controls and explicitly charged.

## Separate verdicts

- Useful baseline: point-qualified. U0, U_NEW and U_TASKPOWER are exact source aliases at nomination; no improved baseline is credited. Their simultaneous comparison against Ucal remains precision unresolved on three clauses.
- Useful joint features and predictions: all 24 point clauses pass against both references. Primary K51 utility intervals: 13 PASS, 11 UNRESOLVED, zero VIOLATION. This is not a completed confidence conjunction. The wider all-arm K240 table gives 7 PASS and 17 UNRESOLVED for JOINT.
- Competitive method: SHORTFALL. The coalition benefit upper bound against task-only is below 0.020, and every required matched-control benefit is a shortfall. A local cap violation against RAW_JOINT independently prevents success.
- Conditioning contribution: UNRESOLVED / not established; joint conditional minus unconditional recovery benefit is −0.000635 [−0.006684, 0.005414].
- Coalition contribution: UNRESOLVED / not established; benefit over the exact local twin is 0.004826 [−0.002662, 0.012315]. The stronger local control also does not establish an advantage.
- Kernel versus adversarial contribution: not established. RAW_JOINT has the lowest point coalition AUC among eligible primary controls.
- Joint versus sequential contribution: not established against either order. Benefits are 0.001114 [−0.004744, 0.006971] against SEQ12 and 0.005394 [−0.001500, 0.012288] against SEQ21.
- Novelty: established conditional fairness/kernel tools plus a tested recipient-pair adaptation; no new mechanism/solver or competitive joint-design claim earned.
- Missing scientific dependency: none among required comparisons. Optional CFair was not registered or run. Oracle-label diagnostics are interpretation only, not independently verified endpoints.
- Historical raw reference: actually restored, deployed and freshly attacked, but passed 16/24 and failed 8/24 original point utility clauses. Its lower recovery is diagnostic and cannot be promoted.

## Strongest favorable and adverse comparisons

Joint versus task-only full-wire pair benefit: 0.008252 [0.000111, 0.016393], a positive exploratory difference but a confirmed shortfall of the target. Joint's local shifts versus task-only pass their caps. Against RAW_JOINT, coalition benefit is −0.005067 [−0.013430, 0.003295]; income local worsening is 0.063252 [0.043198, 0.083306], a violation. RAW_JOINT's point gain versus task-only is about 0.013319; it is retained as the strongest matched benchmark, without claiming a new complete competitive result for RAW.

All intervals are conditional on fitted models/readers, paired on duplicate groups, with fixed multiplicity and approximate development coverage. They are not post-selection confirmation. Score-only historical codes are separate services and never equal-capacity feature controls.

## Next decision

Retain RAW_JOINT as the current matched representation benchmark and stop this recipe. Do not launch another strength/kernel/critic sweep from these results. Preserve runnable packages and this negative comparison for the algorithm-paper ledger; a later confirmation needs separate authorization, an unexposed population and a frozen prospective protocol.
