# Compressed income safeguard: read this first

The new corrected trial ran both seeds, four matched training arms, fresh complete-view attacks and independent numerical replay. **No competitive method was established.** This is separate from the old closed occupation campaign, which still had zero defense fits.

The frozen selector chose the untrained hybrid start for constrained, ordinary and local-only training. Later privacy checkpoints lowered recovery but lost too much occupation accuracy and confidence on FIT2. Task-only selected epoch 5. The safeguard made 516 and 578 nonzero updates out of 800 proposals per seed; it ran, but its batch checks did not preserve the selection proxy.

The strongest eligible control was fully projected RAW. Against it, the selected candidate's coalition benefit was-0.001854, interval [-0.011110,0.007402], short of 0.020. The candidate passed all 24 utility point caps, but18 interval clauses remain unresolved, including all 12 frozen-income clauses. This is not a confidence violation or a complete utility pass.

Income projection improved individual recovery by 0.062452 against full RAW, interval [0.035017,0.089888], while preserving source decisions exactly. This is known compression evidence, not a learned safeguard contribution.
