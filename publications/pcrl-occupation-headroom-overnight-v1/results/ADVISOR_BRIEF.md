# Advisor brief: occupation headroom and robust release (overnight campaign, 2026-10-18)

**Bottom line.** A better occupation learner exists, and some of its extra quality can be traded for lower linked-pair SEX recovery
by our declared readers. But nothing gets below the inherited JOINT_TREE release (SUCC) on both seeds while keeping the 2-SE reserve.
No configuration passed the frozen nomination gates, DEV_REPORT stayed closed, and nothing was confirmed. This is development evidence
on previously used Florida ACS 2019 records.

**What worked.** Coding place of birth as its ordinal census code, instead of as a 106-level category, repairs gradient boosting for
occupation.
- The repaired learners beat the teacher on NLL on both seeds (−0.0026 to −0.0046 vs T_TREE).
- They improve the condition that bound every earlier task-only release, the men's guard: B2_SINGLE −0.0041 / −0.0025 and A1_PO_16
  −0.0039 / −0.0023, against the teacher's −0.0010 / +0.0006 (limit +0.005). The women's guard stays near the teacher's level.
- With them, the occupation side no longer binds; H3's seed-1 income accuracy does (2.86 SE).
- Caveats: the gain over the equally exposed TASK_REFIT is small (0.5 to 1.5 SE). The two seeds are not independent fits: family B's
  base learners are identical across seeds, and family A's seed-1 model is its seed-0 boosting path truncated (154 of 168 trees).

**What the protection step showed.** The coalition-aware occupation correction is a critic-trained leaf correction against SEX readers
of the income-occupation pair, with H3's income frozen.
- At the B80 level it reads lower than a matched continuation twin and a cost-matched compression code in every grid cell, on both
  witnesses and both seeds, under 17-18 of 18 fixed reader recipes per seed. Example: P2A_PAIR_B80 vs its twin, +0.040.
- Its best seed-0 point (pair AUC 0.581) is below every required comparator in every seed-0 cell, with better occupation NLL, Brier
  and men's guard than SUCC (accuracy and women's guard slightly worse). On a third household split, though, the frozen winner puts it
  behind SUCC (−0.0025).
- On seed 1 the same recipe lands at 0.612, behind SUCC. The projection chose a smaller scale there for two reasons. The seed-1 men's
  slack is smaller (0.0041 vs 0.0054). And the out-of-fold projection's men's-cost error flips sign: about +0.002 on seed 0, −0.001 on
  seed 1. So the seed-0 point over-spends men's quality and breaks the reserve, while the seed-1 point under-spends and lowers
  declared-reader recovery less.
- At reserve-keeping levels, the best is the local critic P2_LOC_B80, still 0.010 above SUCC. The PAIR B50 arms are 0.011-0.015
  above.

**Why promotion failed.** The gates are checked in order. All 18 admissible configurations pass the first, the utility point gate,
and fail the next, the recipient point gate; each would fail later gates too.
1. **Recipient gate.** H3's own income view reads +0.031 above H_LJ in two seed-0 cells (limit 0.010). This is consistent with split
   noise: under a normal-noise model even a true zero difference fails one comparator with p ≈ 0.81. Every candidate's occupation view
   is also more SEX-readable than the JOINT_TREE comparators (+0.019 to +0.082 per cell).
2. **Stability.** No candidate has a stable positive pair benefit against all six required comparators. The best score is −0.003,
   bound by RETIRED_B3CTL.
3. **Reserve.** Six candidates miss the reserve on one seed. The strongest protection points miss it on seed 0, the compression codes on
   seed 1. The required comparators fail this reserve themselves (SUCC 0.32 / 0.62), so it constrains candidates only.
4. **Income.** Income accuracy still binds the complete release. The income-branch repair did not move it.

**Components to keep (development stage only).** The repaired occupation learner, packaged beside H3, and the coalition-aware
correction with its matched controls and frontier. Both run from the six raw fields, and their packages are checked bitwise, including
from a restored backup.

**If a successor continues.**
1. Choose the budget level on a held-out training role, so a nominal level costs what it claims on both refits.
2. Locate the per-SEX cost balance between the unweighted task loss (P2) and the 2:1 SEX-weighted task loss (P3, seed 0 only).
3. Reconsider a per-cell recipient gate on two household splits: on this grid every H3-anchored release fails it through H3's own
   income view.

None of these was run here.
