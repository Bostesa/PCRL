## B. The threat model is standard

A recipient-access adversary sees only its own released probability vector and argmax, can link the two recipients' outputs by
person, and trains its readers on auxiliary SEX-labelled data from the same population. This is a standard threat model.
- Attackers trained on auxiliary labelled data and applied to released outputs: Song & Shmatikov 2020 (an auxiliary set D_aux of
  labelled (x, s) pairs plus black-box access) [P16]; Jia & Gong 2018 (AttriGuard) [P16]; Wang et al. 2019 (attacker on model
  outputs) [P16]; Yeom et al. 2018 [P16]; Jayaraman & Evans 2022 (imputation baseline; varying D_aux size and distribution) [P16];
  Stadler et al. 2024 (label-only adversary) [P16].
- Post-hoc probes of protected models: Elazar & Goldberg 2018 [P16]; Ravfogel et al. 2020.
- Linkage and composition across releases: Sweeney 2002; Narayanan & Shmatikov 2008; Ganta et al. 2008 [P16]; Wang & Fung 2006
  [P16]; Li et al. 2012 [P16]; Garfinkel, Abowd & Martindale 2019 (reconstruction by combining published census tables); Taylor et
  al. 2026 [P16]; Tian et al. 2025 (FD-AIA: one attack model trained on two models' predictions for the same person) [P16].
The campaign's pair reader is an instance of a composition attack combined with auxiliary-data attribute inference.

## C. Closest work to a "coalition-aware occupation correction"

The step: an occupation output o' = o + delta(x) trained against a critic that reads the pair (H3 income release i(x), o'(x)), with
income frozen. This is adversarial training against a multi-input (two-view) adversary, not a two-output joint design.
1. Closest training mechanics, conditional adversarial debiasing (the adversary receives the trained output plus a fixed extra
   input): Zhang, Lemoine & Mitchell 2018 (the equalised-odds adversary reads (Y-hat, Y)) [P16]; FAGTB's equalised-odds variant reads
   (sigma(F(x)), y) (boosted trees) [P16]; LAFTR's equalised-odds adversary reads (Z, Y) [S, search summary only]. Here the frozen
   income release replaces the label. Because the pair reader can always ignore o', the reachable floor is the recovery from income
   alone: the step targets the incremental leakage of o' given i, the conditional-independence structure of equalised odds
   (o' independent of SEX given i), not "pair protection" from scratch.
2. Closest problem formulations (designing one release given an earlier release the colluders already hold): Erdogdu & Fawaz 2015
   (an online scheme bounding the incremental leakage of step t given earlier releases; Def. 3 online-versus-batch "regret") [P16];
   Wang & Fung 2006 (the current release generalised given a fixed previous release linked by a global identifier) [P16]; Taylor,
   Vippathalla & Coon 2026 (arXiv preprint; adaptive release under collusion with constraint I(R_k, R^{k-1}; X) <= delta_k; designed
   sequentially because future requests are unknown) [P16]; Sankar et al. 2013, Thm 3 (an "informed" encoder that knows the user's
   side information) [P16].
3. Closest attacker: Tian et al. 2025 FD-AIA (links two models' predictions for the same person) [P16].
4. Inside this lineage it mirrors an existing family: source family P trained income-only leaf corrections against SEX critics on the
   income wire and the linked-pair wire with the JOINT_TREE occupation output frozen
   (`results/pcrl_income_headroom_successor_v1/MECHANISM_CARDS.md`); the occupation version swaps the roles; JOINT_TREE itself is the
   lineage's two-output joint design.
5. Why it is not a joint design: a joint design optimises both outputs (or both heads of a shared model) against the pair adversary
   (JOINT_TREE; correlated multi-copy designs, Li et al. 2012; multi-task fairness, Oneto et al. 2019 and Wang et al. 2021, which
   address per-task fairness rather than coalition privacy). The repository analysis states that for deterministic releases a
   sequential designer with full look-ahead can realise every jointly designed pair [P16 section 8.1]. Freezing H3 is a restricted
   sequential procedure: a single-output (block-coordinate) best response, never "joint" or "coalition-optimal".

## E. Joint versus local protection: facts usable in claims, with provenance

1. Cap on any occupation-only step: pair recovery is never below single-view recovery for a reader that can ignore an input; with
   income frozen at H3, an occupation-only step can at best bring pair recovery down to the H3 income-only recovery; the benefit
   available to any such step is bounded by (pair recovery before) - (H3 income-only recovery) under rich readers. Elementary; the
   reviewer's statement.
2. Local protection of SEX does not certify the pair: the XOR construction [P16 section 8.3]; Ganta et al. 2008; for fairness, Dwork
   & Ilvento 2019.
3. Only all-attribute maximal leakage is subadditive over releases (Issa et al. Lemma 6) [P16]; it does not apply to SEX alone.
4. Linear guardedness composes under concatenation for linear readers only: if each block has zero covariance with SEX, so does the
   concatenated pair (follows from LEACE's covariance characterisation; the reviewer's derivation, not stated in the papers); it
   holds only for readers affine in the erased coordinates; nonlinear pair readers can exploit cross-moments.
5. Bijective calibration changes nothing about optimal recovery (row 3.8).
6. For deterministic releases, joint and sequential design are equivalent in what they can reach [P16 section 8.1]; any measured
   "joint advantage" comes from a restricted sequential procedure (here the frozen H3), from computation, or from optimisation
   quality.
