## 1. Sign conventions

NR's, unchanged. Pair benefit b = comparator AUC - candidate AUC (positive favours the candidate). Recipient difference r = candidate AUC - comparator AUC (positive = the candidate's single view is more recoverable; allowance 0.010 per version at the aggregate point). Paired costs = loss(candidate) - loss(comparator) (negative favours the candidate; accuracy enters as a loss). A utility-condition difference CAND - START is the paired cost on the matching metric (the U0 / Ucal reference cancels on the same people): men's guard `guard|sex0` -> `occupation|men_brier`, women's guard -> `occupation|women_brier`, a clause `<task>|<ref>|<metric>` -> `<task>|acc_loss / nll / brier`. The binding-condition improvement in UM is therefore a negative paired cost.

## 2. Household resampling

- Recovery: household bootstrap of SCORE (B = 400, readers fixed, paired: the same weights for both releases), seed 2026102400 + 100 v + repetition index; cell SEs are the SDs of the paired bootstrap differences. Repetitions R1-R4 are SHA-256 household deals of the same 8,001 people (FIT 0-4 / VALIDATION 5-6 / SCORE 7-9); SCORE sets of different repetitions share about 460-490 of 1,590 households. Disclosed: SAR1's v0 bootstrap seeds 2026102400-2026102403 equal IPR1's v1 seeds; with the same SCORE size (1,590) the weight matrices coincide but attach to different household sets, so the SE estimates are not shared; not a validity condition.
- Utility: household-clustered SEs on MODEL_SELECT (5,306 households; within-SEX guards by subgroup cluster SE), MC SE in quadrature for randomized releases; versions pooled by NR's rule (mean of versions, SE = mean of version SEs: a perfect-correlation bound).
- Nothing is resampled at the level of the MODEL_FIT training rows; refitting variability of the releases themselves is not in any interval (versions are two procedure repeats on shared rows, never replication).

## 3. Uncertainty reporting

Every recovery comparison is reported as the aggregate mean, se_agg with its components (se_hh, se_rs, se_mc), the per-version means, the block means, n_eff (distinct seed cells) and the reader-seed qualifier; every utility comparison as the value, its household-clustered SE and the one-sided 2-SE bound. Labels never replace numbers. Intervals are asymptotic, model-based, conditional on the reused MODEL_SELECT people, and do not correct the adaptive search; they are not confirmation. The confidence conjunction is 28 one-sided per-condition bounds, not a simultaneous statement. A few-valued view's seed equalities count once (n_eff) and do not bias se_agg (se_rs is computed from block means over reader seeds).

## 5. Adaptive-search limitations

- **Looks.** Each reading whose D2 reaches its conjuncts is a D2 look: at most 2 registered + 2 x 2 revisions + 1 replacement (+ its revisions within budget), so K is about 3-7. Each D3 / D4 reading is a look at the references too. No multiplicity correction is applied or claimed; the campaign statement carries K and the registration status.
- **Per-look false pass under known laws** (inherited NRM1 calibration, `SCREEN_CHECKS.json`, 20,000 replicates per law): one AGG_POSITIVE comparison passes a true null in 0.07-2.0% of replicates under six moderate laws (below the one-sided normal 2.3% because se_hh is a worst-case bound and both version means must agree) and about 8% when one version or split dominates (SD 0.008 against <= 0.002). The 0.020 target screen at a true 0.020 passes 0.02-2% under the moderate laws. A D2 look (2-3 comparisons) is an intersection-union conjunction, so its per-look rate is at most its weakest member's; with K looks the chance of at least one chance pass is at most K times that (about 0.5-14% for K = 7 under the moderate laws, up to about 56% if every look were version-dominated, which is why such readings carry the caveat).
- **Not covered by the calibration**: the anchors were chosen on MODEL_SELECT (an informed, adaptive choice); START's binding condition was identified on the same people (its level is selection-biased upward); every earlier campaign's development on the same people; reader-bank choices made after seeing earlier grids; a revision designed after a MODEL_SELECT outcome.

D2 (START se 0.006, CTRL 0.008, correlation 0.5): 0.15 / 0.38 / 0.66 / 0.86 at a true benefit 0.010 / 0.015 / 0.020 / 0.025 against both; with CONT 0.09 / 0.30 / 0.59 / 0.83; when CTRL reproduces half the gain 0.22 / 0.33 at 0.020 / 0.025.
