## Scientific limits

This is exploratory development on repeatedly used Adult people. Fixed-law
optimization, exact integration and repeated seeds do not provide population
privacy or independent confirmation. Intervals are approximate conditional
development intervals with fixed fitted kernels/readers. Exact integration
scores actual emitted probabilities, not their average. The cached 53-bit
HMAC deployment is distinguished from the ideal continuous-uniform law;
fresh repeated-query protection is not claimed. No feature-sharing claim is
made. Correct implementation and restoration do not resolve the confidence
conjunction or create a competitive result.
