# Conditional budgeted releases: research decision

## Decision

The frozen nominee is W_STRICT. Its coalition SEX AUC is 0.8008045112; P0 is 0.8027627488. The reduction is 0.0019582376, with simultaneous development interval [-0.0037199809, 0.0076364561]. ## What actually ran

Two paired source seeds; 17 registered arms and all 552 primary slots retained. Candidate/witness/feasibility work used 48 LP calls. There were 600 fresh reader/probe fits across 20 eligible arm/seed banks, followed by 20 exact-law reports and 2,048 paired duplicate-group bootstrap replicates. No teacher, task head, partition, decoder or temperature was refitted. There was no fresh population or assessment access.

Four ORIGINAL-budget families, G/C/N/W, have independently replayed finite-law infeasibility certificates in both seeds. Their eight protected kernels and dependent recovery results were not run. C_STRICT, N_STRICT and LOCAL missed the paired original utility point gate; their utility arrays remain in the inference family, while their fresh attacks are untriggered. These missing dependencies are not negative privacy findings. All requested controls were actually fitted; eligible ones were freshly attacked. All original attempts and skipped bounded solver slots remain recorded.

## Attribution, novelty and limits

Evidence supports the correctness of conditional allocation, full finite-law optimization, exact sampled-law scoring, source/deployment custody and matched comparisons. It supports no competitive protection component, no joint advantage, no randomness advantage and no support-only contribution. The deterministic arm is genuinely trained with integrality; independent LP bounds leave valid gaps 0.0051135229 / 0.0053798949. Solver status alone is not a certificate of global MIP optimality. Local/product procedures are bounded alternating optimizers; sequential procedures freeze the first stage. Their inherent input/capacity differences are disclosed.

UT_SELECTED minimizes task NLL under the same frozen constraints and protected-reader CE guards. Its objective is utility-only; the entire procedure is not free of sensitive-label-derived information. All empirical guarantees concern fixed fitted laws and declared readers, not population privacy. This is a score-release study and supplies no feature-sharing privacy guarantee. LP release design and conditional allocation are established techniques; a repository implementation is not a new training principle.

"This study is exploratory development research designed from prior outcomes on repeatedly used Adult people. A new branch, optimizer, inference rule or lock does not make these people fresh confirmation."

All intervals condition on fitted kernels/readers and use approximate Bonferroni-t construction with K552, 2,048 paired group resamples and df2233. Reader-training uncertainty, reused-person bias and portfolio selection do not vanish. Exact integration removes release-draw Monte Carlo error only; the cached HMAC deployment uses a disclosed 53-bit grid and independent-distinct-person PRF assumption. Old outcomes and locks remain unchanged; fresh refits and the exact-law reporting contract do not make the repeated population independent evidence.
