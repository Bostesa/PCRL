## Utility and comparisons available at this handoff

Both seeds of D_SELECTED, P_SELECTED, S12_SELECTED and S21_SELECTED pass the INNER point utility gate against both U0 and Ucal. Both local policies fail it. These are point-estimate gates, not simultaneous confidence-interval passes. Structurally preserved decisions establish only the decision identity relative to P0; they do not establish mean confidence retention or population privacy.

The fitting objective is empirical Bayes pair recovery plus half of each recipient's empirical Bayes recovery. Lower is better. It is neither AUC nor a fresh attack endpoint.

## Solver evidence and limits

The deterministic arms were trained with genuine binary mixed-integer programs, not rounded stochastic policies. Independent archive arithmetic reports PASS for both final seeds. The remaining global optimum gap upper bounds from certified continuous relaxations are 0.005113522935801029 and 0.00537989494427471, respectively. These are independent bounds, not independent proofs of integer global optimality. A solver-reported zero gap does not replace that qualification.

Source receipts: [seed 0 arithmetic replay](../r6/R6_CONTROL_DETERMINISTIC_S0_01.json) and [seed 1 arithmetic replay](../r6/R6_CONTROL_DETERMINISTIC_S1_V3_ARCHIVE.json). Product and sequential coordinate/stage certificates concern their serialized subproblems; they do not certify a global optimum or whole-family infeasibility.

Two failed scientific attempts are preserved: deterministic seed 1 refused an integer/primal residual above 1e-8; product seed 0 failed strict serialization because an accepted LP factor contained a tiny negative probability. Published amendment Q fixed solver numerical accuracy before one corrected fit each. Acceptance thresholds, banks, objectives, starts, rounds, support and scientific ceilings were unchanged. No post-fit clipping, projection or normalization was used.

## Complete call accounting

The original ten-policy plan allowed 74 LP calls and two MIP calls, or 76 total. The two authorized corrected fits added at most 17 LP calls and one MIP call. Thus the amended maximum was **94 calls: 91 LP and 3 MIP**.

Actual scientific optimization was **59 calls: 56 LP and 3 MIP**. This includes all eleven calls in the two preserved failed attempts. The ten final policies used 48 calls. The remaining 35 possible LP calls were skipped by the predeclared bounded procedures; they are unused capacity, not hidden fits. Metadata refusals before worker launch and engineering fixtures are reported separately. R4 fitted no new task models, calibrators or attackers. Immutable original banks were reused.

## Attribution and deployment

The evidence supports implemented finite-channel controls, strict executable probability laws, complete coordinate archives and runnable original-bank deployment adapters. Local and product use independent recipient coins; sequential laws condition on the actual first emitted global code; deterministic policies emit one exact categorical choice. Recipients receive the actual original-bank code, probability vector and decision. Internal contexts, template positions and coins are not emitted. Public bank parameters remain available to defense-aware attackers.

These are established optimization constructions adapted to this contract. No new solver, differential privacy guarantee, population information theorem or feature-sharing protection is demonstrated. Randomness and joint design earn attribution only if the fresh complete-view comparisons support it beyond deterministic, product and both sequential controls. Historical restored S21 remains a distinct source control; the new generic sequential controls are not called Taylor's method.

All runtime source bytes are held through queued fresh attacks and deployment replay. Independent executable-law, utility/guard, raw-input and reader replays are separate evidence boundaries. Their final statuses belong in root's shared verification and decision files.
