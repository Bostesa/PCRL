## Discrepancies

### Decision record (check 3). None changes the outcome.

- **D1. The S2 "fail in substance" is a ruling made after the proposals were read, not a mechanical application of S2 as committed.** Read literally, S2 is arguably met: both mechanisms change which input distinctions are merged, and S2's exclusions name frozen outputs, while both proposals refit their teachers. The reviewer says they escape "the letter of the closed lanes". The ruling draws on the brief's hypothesis wording and on a requirement that the structure be "learned … jointly" (§6), which is not in S2. §3 discloses the ruling, but line 53 ("rests on S2 and S3 as committed") overstates it for S2.
- **D2. Ruling 1 misstates the brief.** The brief says "instead of only modifying continuous scores on frozen representations, frozen tree partitions or historical code banks". The decision writes "instead of modifying the outputs of frozen representations, partitions or code banks". Dropping "only" and changing "continuous scores" to "outputs" widens the exclusion in the direction of the ruling.
- **D3. The S4 "pass" for SCM is not supported as written.** SCM §5 says NEURAL_RAW "merges nothing beyond the encoder's OTHER pooling". That is an unqualified injectivity claim. CORRECTIONS §1(e) and §2 say injectivity is unknown ("Existing proof of population injectivity for any actual model: none"), and S4 forbids injectivity claims. Neither review checked the proposals against S4's no-injectivity rule, yet the decision cites "both reviews" as S4 evidence. SCAR §5 qualifies injectivity as unknown but then says NEURAL_RAW "keeps every distinction SCAR merges" (minor).
- **D4. "Both arrived independently … hashed before either was opened by the other designer" is asserted, not evidenced, for designer 2.** Designer 1 states it did not open `designer_2/`. Designer 2's proposal contains no such statement, and proposal 1 sat on disk 18 minutes before proposal 2 was written. (I traced the service-model v1 and lookup-reader text that designer 2 cites: it came from the adversary's SERVICE_REVIEW, created 21:10:58, before proposal 1. No SCM content reached designer 2 that way.)
- **D5. "This is the closed output-channel family" compresses the reviewer.** The reviewer wrote "inside the output-channel family", "escapes the letter … not their substance", the "strongest overlap" with frozen-bank channels, and only "partial overlap" with frozen-code rescue.

- **D7. "The pair reveals exactly what the occupation recipient sees"** drops the adversary's condition, "whenever R2's vectors are distinct" across cells.

### Mathematical scope (check 8)

- **M1. Approximate constraints fail worse than "no exact composition".** With Z1 = A + εS and Z2 = A − εS, the best single-release linear AUC is 0.505, while the pair reader Z1 − Z2 reaches AUC 1.0.
- **M2. "Linear guarantee" here is the convex-loss notion.** Zero covariance does not make every linear score's AUC 0.5 (an exact law with equal class means gives a linear score AUC 0.75), and the benchmark reports AUC.
- **M3. The guarantee covers readers that are linear in the guarded coordinates.** LOG_LR (log-probabilities), DEC_LR (one-hot decisions) and OUT_LR with its argmax column are linear in transformed features, so they are not covered. Composition on a fitting sample is exact on that same sample; it fails if the two constraints were fitted on different samples or are evaluated off-sample. In the table row "LEACE; INLP", exact zero covariance is LEACE's property only.
- **M4. The lookup reader adds power only for byte-identical finite releases.** On continuous releases almost every key is unseen and scores π (AUC 0.5). −0.0 and +0.0 give distinct keys, and −0.0 passes the bank's p ≥ 0 check, so canonicalised stored vectors matter (as the adversary noted).
