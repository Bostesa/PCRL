# Incumbent and complete-view objective protocol

## Scope and exposure

This is exploratory development work on repeatedly used Adult data, designed using earlier development outcomes. A new lock, branch, objective or incumbent does not create fresh confirmation. The original assessment and new HEAD_VALIDATION labels remain closed. No new population is opened.

The exact historical `U|JOINT|i8o64|l0.1|H-CLASS-TEMP` from the locally pinned bank source is P0. Its router, teacher, code table, decoder, metadata and original global IDs remain frozen. All learned channel inputs are the two private P0 codes and their fixed decoded values, or the own-recipient subset for V-L. No new calibration, representation or partition fit occurs. ## Exact costs and selection

Expected task NLL/Brier is sum over the action law of the loss of the emitted probability, never loss of mean probability. Stable float64 accumulation and independent precision checks determine a numerical safety tolerance before policy fits. FIT and existing internal validation caps are P0-relative: accuracy loss≤.002, NLL excess≤.001, summed Brier excess≤.0005 for each task/seed. Frozen-reader local CE may not worsen relative to P0 beyond numerical tolerance on identical internal rows. This guard does not establish the final AUC cap.

Exact executable identities are archived for every family. Nonzero eta .05 exploration with fixed geometric backtracking prevents a zero-gradient identity initialization. Backoff remains within each family: product/local independent coins, deterministic no randomization, sequential second factor only after first-stage freeze. Whole-P0 sequential fallback is separately reported. Approximate optimization has residuals and no privacy certificate.

Original INNER limits remain, per seed/task and BOTH U0/Ucal: accuracy loss≤.010, NLL excess≤.010, summed Brier excess≤.005. All 24 point clauses admit an arm to final attacks; a crossing interval is unresolved. One checkpoint per arm is chosen only on sealed FIT/internal quantities. Overall new-family nomination among eligible V arms minimizes equal-seed mean constant-prior CE minus strongest retained pair-reader CE, with tie order V-J,V-P,V-D,V-L,V-S12,V-S21. V-J has its own diagnostic nominee if eligible. No post-attack checkpoint search.

Bonferroni two-sided simultaneous intervals use 2048 group bootstrap replicates with shared paired people across arms/seeds and whole-release draw-index resampling. Source seeds are not independent populations. A: preselected new family versus fresh P0 requires pair benefit lower bound≥.020, every utility bound and local worsening upper bounds≤.010. Ties use fixed arm order. Identity ties receive no mechanism credit.
