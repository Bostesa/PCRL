# Start here: two independently designed release families (campaign `pcrl_dual_design_release_v1`)

A bounded development investigation, run on 2026-10-21 (start 16:37:33Z) on the Florida ACS 2019 development cohort (admitted
public-use PUMS records).

**What the service does.** It predicts an income class and an occupation group from six fields (AGEP, WKHP, COW, SCHL, MAR, POBP).
Each of two recipients receives only its own probability vector and argmax decision.

**What the privacy evaluation measures.** How well classifiers ("readers") infer the dataset's recorded SEX attribute from what the
recipients receive: from each output alone, and from the linked pair.

**The task.** Develop and test a materially different release method that keeps the predictions useful while reducing that
inference. It was to be compared against an equally trained task-only twin, matched controls, the simplest alternative explanation
and the strong inherited references.

**Result: no method is established.**
- Two designers proposed two different operations:
  - **DA**, SEX-standardized task posteriors (STD);
  - **DB**, propensity-tilt cancellation (PTC).
- A challenger reviewed both. Each family was fitted, evaluated against its matched controls and revised once.
- **Both families test one idea family.** STD and PTC each subtract the propensity-proportional part of the released task posterior,
  and the revisions stay inside it. This joint negative closes one idea, not two.
- **Utility.** Every release met all 28 original utility conditions at the point (G1: 12 clauses and 2 guards in each of its two
  version-specific configurations; all 48 configurations pass). Point feasibility is not confidence preservation.
- **D2.** None reduced recovery beyond its matched controls under the registered rule. D2 was met in 0 of 2 registered and 0 of 2
  adaptive readings, 4 D2 looks in total. One AGG_POSITIVE comparison passes a true null in about 0.07-2% of replicates under six
  moderate NRM1 known laws, and about 8% when one version or split dominates. With 4 looks, the chance that at least one passes by
  chance is at most 4 times the per-look rate.
- **D3 and D4.** None is competitive with the strongest references (D3). None meets the 0.020 material criterion (D4).

**Registered readings, the family results.** DA_STD is PARTIAL_SBC_FRONTIER_ONLY and DB_PTC is NOT_SEPARATED. Both are
TASK_ONLY_FEASIBLE_NO_PROTECTION.

**Beside them, never as a family result:** DB's adaptive revision DB_PTC_R1 (ADAPTIVE_R1; recipient-split strengths 0.35 / 0.65). The
registered rule selected it afterwards, on the same registered cells, as DB's strongest feasible nonzero release (winner's curse;
descriptive).
- **Registered cells.** For the declared readers on the registered cells:
  - its linked-pair AUC is below the twin's by 0.0239 (se_agg 0.0101; lower 2-SE bound +0.0037);
  - it is below the SPEND bracket's by 0.0245 (se_agg 0.0122), which passes the SPEND frontier comparison with a lower 2-SE bound
    of only +0.0001;
  - it is not separated from its SEX-blind-target (SBC) bracket (+0.0265, se 0.0139).
- **Refit cells.** On new household splits and reader seeds of the same 8,001 people, its benefits over the twin, the SBC bracket and
  the SPEND bracket stay resolved (descriptive; D2 unchanged; not a replication). The refit estimates are below the registered ones.
- **Release-only control.** By the reading declared before that control's fit, it is not separated from DB_ZTILT_R1, a tilt whose
  amplitude is estimated from the twin's released vectors alone: +0.0128 (se 0.0081) on the registered cells, +0.0066 (se 0.0095) on
  the refit cells. So its gain is not separated from release-only information. That control overspent (0.0169 against 0.0099 nats),
  which is disclosed and does not change the declared reading.
- **References.** It stays above four references at the point (their pair AUC is 0.600-0.622; it is 0.627). It is resolved worse
  only against RETIRED_B3CTL.
- **Reserve.** It is point-feasible but not reserve-qualified in version 1.

The statistical reviewer's required headline is quoted verbatim in `RESEARCH_DECISION.md`.

## What this campaign does not claim

- No method, candidate or component: D2 was not met in any reading, and no partial outcome is a claim.
- No information removal for any release, and no gain that needs propensity information beyond the release. Neither DB release is
  separated from its release-only control.
- DB_PTC_R1 is not "DB's result": the registered DB reading is DB_PTC, NOT_SEPARATED. DB_PTC_R1's refit confirmation is descriptive,
  on the same people, and does not change its D2.
- No improvement over the strong references, no 0.020 margin, no joint-design result and no novelty.
- No confirmation. All evidence is development evidence on previously and adaptively used records (MODEL_SELECT was read for every
  decision), for the declared readers only.
- Nothing about the closed roles, the reserved populations or the sealed assessment, which stayed closed.
