## What the completed controls show

The missing controls do **not** explain L_TREE's advantage on this exposed development role:
- the advantage persists against the task-only continuation at the same epoch (C1);
- it persists against a freshly fitted, equally exposed neural RAW-J (C2).

The neural RAW-J control could not reduce recovery much without violating the internal utility caps. The access-honoring official FARE bank (fair tree on UPDATE only) reaches much lower recovery (coalition AUC 0.694) but is not utility-feasible on FIT2 (best normalized violation 6.7×) or on INNER (4/24 points). It is therefore reported descriptively: its recovery is lower than L_TREE's (C4 established shortfall for L_TREE, −0.091 [−0.121, −0.061]), but it is not utility-feasible, so it is not counted as beaten.

The gap to the historical score codes (0.014–0.018, unresolved) is untouched by this completion.
