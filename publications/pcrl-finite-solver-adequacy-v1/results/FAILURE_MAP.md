## What this campaign tests

- Whether the original seven-tilt, first-improvement Lloyd step misses better deployable solutions of its own objective (exact
  references; cached MODEL_FIT statistics).
- Whether the documented approximations matter for the actual release. These are: shrunk routing tables against unshrunk acceptance
  information, the cell-mean codeword refresh against the prototype-dependent guard, and B1 emission against the search prototypes.
- Whether a better solver changes actual usefulness or recovery (exact laws; MODEL_FIT nested). If, and only if, the advancement
  condition is met, it also tests that on MODEL_SELECT against an equally optimized HQ.
