## What must not be said

- Not "the revised solver improves privacy", "improves protection" or "improves recovery". The held-out recovery difference is a
  point estimate (-0.0018 / -0.0038 pair LOOKUP AUC) that the registered 2-SE condition does not resolve, and it was not examined on
  MODEL_SELECT.
- Not "the original optimizer was buggy". The four documented approximations are approximations, not defects (route D).
- Not "a lower objective is better protection". The objective is a surrogate on label-derived training statistics. On two invented
  laws its optimum violates the utility limits.
- Not "propensity adds information beyond HQ". Task scores identify the support states for about 99% of people, and the descriptive
  PRC-HQ nested difference is not a registered comparison. A larger nested gap of this kind did not carry to the MODEL_SELECT
  readers in the previous campaign.
- Not "an engineering result", "a bounded negative result" or "a component" in the rules' sense. No pilot outcome class was computed;
  the verified engineering findings are descriptive.
- Not "SV1 finds the optimum". It is path-dependent and falls short of the best known points, both on the exact laws and on the
  source.
- Not "finer resolution does not help". The equal-effort-per-step control ran into its step cap while still improving.
- Not "a new privacy algorithm". A better line search of an existing objective is an optimizer improvement.
- Not "no finite-cell method can work". The closed hypothesis concerns repairing this optimizer.
