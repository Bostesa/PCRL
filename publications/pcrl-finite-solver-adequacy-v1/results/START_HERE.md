# Start here: solver adequacy of the finite-output benchmark (campaign `pcrl_finite_solver_adequacy_v1`)

A bounded investigation, run on 2026-10-21 on the Florida ACS 2019 development cohort (admitted public-use PUMS records). It asked
whether the optimizer of the propensity-routed finite-cell release (`nrm/fa/prc.py`, used by the finite-routing comparison,
publication 04ac9161f) is adequate for its own stated objective. It kept three questions apart:
- A, optimization;
- B, whether a better optimum improves actual usefulness and recovery;
- C, whether person-level propensity adds anything beyond an equally optimized task-score-only control (HQ).

A development pilot was conditional on a prospectively defined advancement condition.

**Rule outcome: `OPTIMIZER_REPAIR_CLOSED`. The conditional pilot did not run, so no pilot outcome class was computed, and no fitted
method is claimed.** Descriptively, the optimizer-repair hypothesis closes on MODEL_FIT.
- The original seven-tilt search misses deployable improvements of its own objective: exactly on invented laws, and on the real
  cached statistics.
- A revised solver (SV1, an exact tilt line search with best improvement) lowers PRC's objective at every registered strength in both
  versions, and HQ's in 11 of 12 cells. It is not optimal.
- On MODEL_FIT nested held-out data, its recovery gain is not resolved under the pre-registered rule (`S_PROXY_ONLY`): pooled pair
  LOOKUP change -0.0028, 2-SE interval -0.0065 to +0.0009.
- No implementation defect was found.
- The previous campaign's P1 evidence reproduces bitwise, and the verifier recomputed it (`P1_REPLAY_VERIFIED` under the statistical
  reviewer's estimator-identity ruling).

Both readings of the previous campaign stand: `INCOMPLETE` under its frozen rule, `ENGINEERING_REPAIR` under its post hoc ER1.

## What this campaign does not claim

- No fitted method, component or candidate.
- No usefulness or protection gain from the better solver.
- No improvement over the strong references, no 0.020 margin, no joint design and no novelty. A better line search is not a novel
  privacy algorithm.
- The closed hypothesis is not an impossibility result about other mechanisms.
- Everything is development evidence on previously used records, for the declared readers only.
