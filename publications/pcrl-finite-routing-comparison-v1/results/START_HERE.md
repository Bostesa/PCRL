# Start here: PCRL finite-routing comparison (campaign `pcrl_finite_routing_comparison_v1`)

A bounded research-and-development campaign, run on 2026-10-20/21 on the Florida ACS 2019 development cohort (admitted public-use
PUMS records). It continued family A, propensity-routed finite cells (card FA_PRC_v1), of `pcrl_new_release_mechanisms_v1`
(publication 682068d52), which had closed at its capacity stage before any protected routing was fitted. That campaign's negative
result is preserved unchanged. This one asked:
- did probability estimation explain the twin's lost margin?
- does a repaired task-only finite release qualify?
- does the actually fitted propensity-aware routing lower SEX recovery beyond matched controls?

The roles were: coordinator (implementer), challenger, statistical reviewer, engineering reviewer and independent verifier.
Registration (protocol, access rules, comparisons, selection rules) was committed and its sanitized receipt verified on the GitHub
remote before any label was read.

**Outcome: `ENGINEERING_REPAIR`, under the post hoc grid-validity repair ER1.** Under the frozen strict rule, D2-D4 are not
computed and the outcome class is `INCOMPLETE`.
- The probability repair is resolved: the out-of-fold teacher-mean tables lower held-out occupation NLL against the label-refit
  tables on identical partitions. The repaired 16 x 64 twin qualifies as a finite task-only release.
- The fitted protected routing (one protected partition per version) lowered registered-grid linked-pair recovery against the
  twin by 0.007 (se 0.004, not resolved).
- That reduction was not separated from task spending on any matched-control path. On the registered grid it stays 0.018-0.023
  above both target references.

The statistical reviewer's required headline is in `RESEARCH_DECISION.md`.

## What this campaign does not claim

- No useful component, no superiority over the admitted references, no 0.020 material improvement, no joint-design contribution and
  no novelty.
- Everything is development evidence on adaptively used MODEL_SELECT records, for the declared readers only. Lower recovery for these
  readers is not protection against every reader.
- One released instance per person; repeated-query protection is not claimed.
